## Identity and versions

* **Venom — Premium / Limited Edition** — Stern Pinball, 2023; SPIKE 2. Lead design: **Brian Eddy**; rules/software: **Dwight Sullivan, Raymond Davidson**; art: **Jeremy Packer (Zombie Yeti)**. [S1][S2]
* Premium and LE share the gameplay layout. Compared with Pro, Prem/LE adds mechanically changing host layouts, the physical **Doppelganger 3-bank**, **Quick Doppelganger Multiball**, and **Million Plus Pops**. [S1]
* **Rules basis: `code`.** Stern released the major feature-complete **1.0** update in April 2024. [S3] The detailed current community rulesheet identifies its basis as **1.06**. [S1] I could not verify a newer exact downloadable revision/date from Stern's currently indexed code pages, so I would **not invent a “latest” version**.
* Major competitive multiballs: **Mayhem Multiball, Carnage Multiball, Quick Doppelganger Multiball (Prem/LE)**, plus **Toxin Team-Up** as the key mini-wizard mode. [S1]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** start **Eddie Brock**. He begins with two Mayhem locks lit; use his left-loop skill shot to spot a mini-mode, finish his three mini-modes, then change host to **Flash Thompson**. [S1][S4]
2. Complete mini-modes to light Mayhem locks. Where possible, choose which containment vessel receives each ball because lock placement grants Mayhem perks; three right-side locks give **+1× playfield** during Mayhem. [S1]
3. Build **playfield X** by repeatedly looping the center ramp and then hitting the Bell Tower. Timed PF X can reach **7×**, with additional untimed +1× sources. [S1]
4. Start **Mayhem + Carnage**, defeat a Doppelganger, then cash those prerequisites at **Toxin Team-Up**. Toxin becomes progressively more valuable if the player survives repeated Super Jackpots. [S1][S4]
5. The giant late-game alternate is **Host Hurry-Up** after Toxin: its escalating 20M-base awards can produce hundreds of millions normally and potentially billions under a large PF multiplier. [S1][S4]

## Core rules and persistence

* Each host has three colored **two-shot mini-modes**. Complete a mini-mode → light a Mayhem lock; complete the host's full column → qualify host change and, at level 10+, that host's battle. [S1]
* **Important obstruction:** while a Battle is lit at the center ramp, Mayhem locks cannot be lit/collected and Carnage modes cannot start. A player may therefore deliberately avoid completing the Battle qualification. [S1]
* **Mayhem lock placement matters.** Balls can be assigned between the two containment vessels; their positions determine multiball perks, including boss credit, 2× XP, or +1× PF. Holding the action button can abort the start and continue locking, up to six balls. [S1]
* Mayhem begins with a controlled **Quick Jackpot** phase, then releases the balls for normal jackpots. Completing all lit jackpots lights Super Jackpot; subsequent rounds require double, then triple jackpots. A Mayhem Super can also defeat a boss brought into the multiball. [S1]
* **PF X:** repeated center-ramp loops light Bell Tower; Bell Tower adds +1× timed PF scoring, up to 7×. Bell Tower hits also extend the active multiplier. Grid and Mayhem perks can supply additional untimed +1×. [S1]
* **Toxin Team-Up:** requires Mayhem started + Carnage Multiball started + one Doppelganger defeated. Six jackpots light a 25M Super at the scoop; collecting it adds a ball, and later Supers grow by 25M. Carnage can temporarily multiply Toxin jackpots 2×/3×. [S1]
* **Shot multipliers:** combos can raise an individual shot as high as 5×. [S1]
* Insider Connected can persist XP/level and unused Team-Ups **between games**. Those persistent advantages are inappropriate for equal-start tournament play unless specifically allowed. [S1][S4]

## Skill shots

Every host also has a **standard rollover skill shot**: plunge through the flashing top lane for **+2 Bonus X**. The right flipper cycles skill-shot choices. [S1]

Host-specific competitive choices include: [S1]

| Host           | Useful skill shots                                        | Why choose it                                             |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| Eddie Brock    | Left loop: spot mini-mode; right orbit: points            | Spotting a mini-mode accelerates the common Eddie opening |
| Flash Thompson | Scoop: light Mystery; right orbit: spot mini-mode         | Mini-mode spot accelerates Flash grid progress            |
| Gwen Stacy     | Left loop: spot mini-mode; right loop: start Doppelganger | Useful for fast Doppelganger/Quick MB progress            |
| Peter Parker   | Left loop: spot mini-mode; scoop: light Mystery           | Progress versus utility choice                            |

**Secret Hideout Skill Shot:** plunge directly into the orbit-apex saucer for **3M + 500k per prior skill shot and +4 Bonus X**. It forfeits the other skill-shot opportunities. [S1]

After changing hosts during a ball, that host's listed skill shots can instead be collected as **Bonus Shots**. [S1]

## Secondary features

* **Spider-Sense:** once per ball during an active feature, hold the action button; a random white shot lights briefly. Making it scores a 25M-starting hurry-up **and makes major progress**, e.g. lights Carnage/Toxin Super, completes a Mayhem jackpot set, lights Battle Special Attack, or defeats Doppelganger. This is a major tournament resource. [S1]
* **Sleeper Team-Up:** available immediately when Flash is the initial host, and later via Infected progress. During multiball it can **add a ball**; during Battles it adds time. [S1]
* **Mystery:** qualified from bottom-lane completions. It can award major progress such as three locks, +1× PF, grid completion, team-up, or Carnage progress. **Competition mode fixes the award order** rather than leaving it random. [S1]
* **Scream:** video mode, normally qualified from Infected progress; flipper buttons defeat enemies on the display. Starting it alone awards 10M. [S1]
* **Extra Ball:** lit at the scoop through Infected progress or Mystery. Availability/conversion is tournament-configuration sensitive. [S1]
* **Prem/LE Million Plus Pops:** with Flash, eight qualifying target hits start a timed mode where pop hits score **1M × current PF X**. At large multipliers this is a legitimate score-swinging side feature. [S1]

## What to watch

1. **Host selection/display + physical layout:** on Prem/LE the chosen host can physically alter shot geometry. Eddie/Flash/Gwen/Peter therefore tells you both the rules package and some available shots. [S1]
2. **Grid above the flippers:** colored completed inserts show mini-mode progress; completed four-insert boxes activate powerful perks including +1× PF and Extra Ball Save. [S1]
3. **Bell Tower / PF-X display:** repeated center ramps followed by Bell Tower means the player is deliberately building the game's major scoring multiplier. [S1]
4. **Containment vessels:** where the Mayhem balls are being locked is strategic, not cosmetic; watch for three balls accumulated on one side to identify major perks. [S1]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** **Strategic inference:** Eddie into a straightforward Mayhem/Carnage route is attractive because it converts attainable mini-mode progress into protected multiball scoring. Take obvious Supers rather than repeatedly risking single-ball center-ramp/Bell-Tower sequences solely to build a huge multiplier. [S1][S4]

**Playing behind:** Build PF X aggressively and preserve Spider-Sense for the most valuable stack. Toxin repeated Supers and especially **multiplied Host Hurry-Up** offer enormous comeback ceilings. Million Plus Pops with Flash is another Prem/LE-specific leverage opportunity when PF X is already large. [S1][S4]

**Key recurring decision:** **cash progress now versus engineer the multiplier.** Venom rewards deliberately arranging host, grid perks, lock placement, Spider-Sense and PF X before the valuable feature starts; simpler Mayhem progress is substantially safer. [S1][S4]

## Danger zones

* **Left-loop return:** expert guidance specifically recommends nudging and attempting a right-flipper live catch after this feed. [S4]
* **Containment-vessel releases:** balls can emerge rapidly and unexpectedly while another ball is already in play; watch for instant multiball chaos. [S4]
* **Center ramp / Bell Tower sequence:** lucrative multiplier building forces repeated center-playfield shots and dangerous rebounds. [S1][S4]
* **Carnage captive ball:** repeated direct hits are required for major progression but return energy directly toward the player. [S1]
* **Prem/LE Doppelganger bank:** unlike Pro's virtual Doppelganger, the physical flyaway targets create an additional real rebound/target interaction. [S1]

## Trivia

* Venom marked the return of **Brian Eddy**, designer of *Attack from Mars* and *Medieval Madness*, to a highly experimental modern Stern design. [S2][S4]
* The rules draw heavily from Marvel's **Absolute Carnage** and **King in Black** storylines. [S4]
* The two vertical containment vessels can collectively hold **six balls** for Mayhem Multiball. [S1][S4]
* Prem/LE can physically alter portions of the shot layout according to the selected host, unusually literal implementation of “choose your host, change the game.” [S1][S2]
* Mark Tremonti contributed music to the game. [S1]
