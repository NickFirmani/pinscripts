## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** start an Island Scene and work the **Spider Pit** early; Pit multiballs are valuable utilities for safely progressing modes. Avoid stacking the precision-heavy Cross the Chasm or Stegosaurus Encounter. [S4]
2. Then clear the **sweepable drop targets** to light physical Kong locks at the center ramp and start **King Kong Multiball**. [S3][S4]
3. In Kong Multiball, prioritize drops to light Jackpots; the **Cliffs Ramp is the 2× Jackpot** shot on later rounds. Complete green awards and relock a ball for **20 seconds of 2× jackpots/Super Jackpots**. [S3][S4]
4. Build the four orange **X targets** and bring **2× playfield scoring** into a valuable multiball/mode when possible. [S3]
5. High-ceiling alternate: play strong Island Scenes toward **Crash the Gate**; its Super Jackpot multiplier can escalate **2× → 4× → 8×…**. Deep Treasure Hunts also contain a fixed **500M** fifth-map award. [S3][S4]

## Core rules and persistence

* **Island Scenes:** gong starts one of five 60-second scenes. Completing a scene awards a map segment. Playing all five enables **Crash the Gate**; completion quality matters because Crash the Gate's hurry-ups are based partly on those earlier mode totals. [S3][S4]
* **Island Lock:** climbing to specified heights lights a lock at the punchback/right-orbit route. Starting the next scene with a collected Island Lock makes it a **2-ball multiball**, enables Island Jackpot, and blocks progress toward other multiballs until back to one ball. [S3]
* **King Kong Multiball:** first qualification requires drop-bank work, then three center-ramp locks. Premium physically stores them in the train. Jackpots must be lit; after a Jackpot, the **side ramp** collects Super Jackpot. [S3]
* Completing all green awards allows a **relock** at the center ramp. On Premium the ball is physically held; all Jackpots/Super Jackpots become **2× for 20 seconds**. [S3]
* **2× playfield:** hit all four orange X targets, then the roving lit orange target. Further lit X hits extend the timer, up to four extensions per activation. [S3]
* **Crash the Gate:** five hurry-ups feed a side-ramp Super Jackpot. Players may keep cycling the hurry-ups before collecting, exponentially raising its multiplier **2×, 4×, 8×…**. This is one of the game's clearest match-swinging features. [S3][S4]
* **Treasure Hunts persist as strategic perks.** The second treasure doubles end-of-ball bonus for the rest of the game; the fourth makes playfield X easier; the fifth treasure hunt awards **500M**, explicitly not multiplied by 2× playfield. [S3]
* Height climbed and **KING KONG letters persist across balls**; switch-count contribution to bonus and Bonus X reset each ball. [S3]

## Skill shots

Every **unique** skill shot made during the game immediately earns a **KING KONG letter** and, on default settings, adds initial ball-save time. [S3]

* **Skill Shot:** plunge into the Spider Pit targets.
* **Super Skill Shot:** plunge through the River lane near the mini-flipper.
* **Anti-Skill Shot:** plunge into the unusual left outlane.
* **Secret / Super Secret:** plunge into the inner / outer left inlane.
* **Mega Secret:** feed the upper-left flipper, reflex the **Cliffs Ramp**; 1.75M + 8 seconds ball save. Follow with Biplane or U-turn for 2×/3× value.
* **Ultra Secret:** Mega Secret sequence, then shoot the River lane from the upper-right flipper; current rulesheet lists the exact value as **uncertain (~7M)**, plus 8 seconds save. [S3]

Tournament implication: learning repeatable plunge strengths is unusually valuable because skill shots accelerate both survival and KING KONG/T-Rex progression. **Observed strategy.** [S4]

## Secondary features

* **Log Bridge:** action button activates a hidden diverter at the River to feed the mini-flipper for drops/Cliffs. Starts with one use; more are earned during play. Competition Mode restricts automatic activation to strategically relevant states. [S3]
* **Cliffhanger:** after enough Cliffs shots, a left-outlane drain triggers a **12-second rescue challenge**: hit the Power-Up target to save the ball. Operator settings can instead make this a conventional virtual kickback or 50M consolation. [S3]
* **Island Mystery:** broad random award pool normally; in **Competition Mode it is reduced to 10M or Add-A-Ball when applicable**. [S3]
* **Extra Balls:** several routes qualify one at the Kong Cave. If disabled/limited, the award becomes **15M**, multiplied by 2× playfield to 30M. [S3]
* **Deadeye:** Premium lights it later in KING KONG than Pro. It gives 30 seconds of unlimited saved plunges/skill shots, with repeated Deadeye Jackpots. [S3]

## What to watch

1. **Balls in the train:** physical Kong locks; three means King Kong Multiball is imminent.
2. **Orange X lamps:** all four completed plus the roving X means **2× playfield** is ready.
3. **Log Bridge/action button flashing:** player is intentionally routing the River feed to the mini-flipper for drops or Cliffs.
4. **Island Lock steady / scene ready:** the next Island Scene will become 2-ball play with Island Jackpot/Victory Challenge enabled. [S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** take the reliable **scene + Pit Multiball** value, then a controlled King Kong Multiball. Cash available Jackpots/Supers rather than risking a perfect relock or prolonged Crash the Gate buildup.

**Playing behind:** bring **2× playfield** into Kong Multiball, earn the 20-second Kong relock multiplier, or push deep Island Scenes into Crash the Gate's exponentially multiplied Super Jackpot. Treasure Hunt #5's **500M fixed award** is also unambiguously match-changing if reachable. [S3]

**Key recurring decision:** **stack a mode with multiball, or play it cleanly first?** Pit/Kong multiballs make many modes safer, but Cross the Chasm and Stegosaurus demand precise upper-playfield shots and are explicitly recommended for single-ball play. [S4]

## Danger zones

1. **Gong forehand:** can rebound directly into the two nearby X standups; a viable backhand is often easier to recover from. [S4]
2. **Punchback target:** intentionally fires the ball back across the playfield toward the upper flipper; mistiming the recovery turns success into danger. [S4]
3. **Mini-flipper/drop area:** missed sweeps or Cliffs attempts can dump the ball unpredictably back to the lower playfield.
4. **Left outlane between two left inlanes:** unusual geometry makes lateral feeds difficult to read; Cliffhanger only helps when qualified. [S3][S4]
5. **Weak center ramp:** failure to climb the ramp returns the ball centrally; the factory center post can sometimes rescue it, but don't count on it. [S4]

## Trivia

* Keith Elwin designed the game; Stern explicitly markets it as coming from the creative lineage behind **Jaws, Godzilla, and Jurassic Park**. [S1]
* The theme uses **original Stern-created King Kong material** inspired principally by the original 1930s story rather than a specific movie license. [S3]
* Data East developed an unreleased King Kong machine around 1990; Stern's 2025 game therefore arrived roughly **35 years after that abandoned project**. [S3][S4]
* Premium Kong is an **animatronic mechanism** that moves his torso and arms and physically destroys/releases the train lock at multiball start. [S1]
* The hidden **“2.42 Million Bananas”** lane jokes about Data East's unreleased game's “1 Million Bananas” insert, adjusted for inflation. [S3]
