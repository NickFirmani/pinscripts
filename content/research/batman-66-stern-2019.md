## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy — high ceiling:** complete **Bookworm → Mr. Freeze → repeated King Tut**. Bookworm makes minor-villain perks persistent, Freeze stops important timers, and later Tut completions raise the **Main Channel multiplier**. [S5][S7][S9]
2. Layer a **major villain mode** with **Villain Multiball**, then add **2×/3× playfield scoring** and a valuable **shot multiplier**. Batman ’66 rewards multiplicative stacking heavily. [S5][S7]
3. Before taking the first major-villain shot, hit **Commissioner Gordon twice** for +1× Villain Multiball scoring; the **Batcave Combo** can also double that villain mode’s base scoring. [S5][S6]
4. At the end of a villain phase, choose between **cashing the Super Jackpot at the Penguin scoop** or continuing the villain for a higher mode multiplier and bigger future payoff. [S5]
5. **Alternate tournament line:** on a hard/short-playing game, use **Catwoman + Shame + Villain Multiball** for faster, safer protected scoring rather than investing a whole ball in Bookworm/Freeze/Tut. [S8][S9]

## Core rules and persistence

**Confirmed rules:** the four major villains are qualified differently—**Catwoman via the ramps, Joker via Villain-Vision targets, Penguin via the scoop, Riddler via the orbits**. Once qualified, the Bat-Phone hurry-up starts; hitting the phone begins the selected villain. [S5]

Major-villain phases feed a **Super Jackpot**. Cashing it takes the value; continuing instead increases that villain’s scoring multiplier for subsequent phases. Villain progress is documented by competitive guides as surviving drains, making a partially developed villain strategically valuable on later balls. [S5][S8]

During a major villain, the targets around the turntable qualify **Villain Multiball locks** at the right orbit; three locked balls start multiball. Qualification difficulty is operator-adjustable and became explicitly configurable in v1.02. [S5][S6]

The major multiplier layers are:

* **Shot multiplier:** Commissioner Gordon progression assigns 2×, then progressively higher multipliers up to **6×**, lasting for the ball. [S5]
* **Playfield multiplier:** the Batmobile/Atomic Pile area lights **2× for 30 seconds**; repeating can produce **3×** and reset the timer. [S5]
* **Main Channel multiplier:** repeated completed **King Tut** modes raise scoring on whichever mode occupies the large TV/Main Channel. [S5][S7]
* **Villain-mode multiplier:** continuing a major villain instead of cashing it raises that villain’s subsequent scoring. [S5]

Easy misconception: several modes can run simultaneously, but **only the large-TV mode is the Main Channel** and receives the King Tut Main Channel multiplier. Holding the action button changes channels. [S5]

## Secondary features

* **Gadgets / action button:** completing GADGET earns uses. Pressing the button can spot a lit mode shot; holding it switches the Main Channel. Gadgets can also affect physical-lock access in appropriate states. [S5]
* **Shame:** first play is a two-ball multiball. Completing Shame improves later multiballs with **+1 ball and +10 seconds of base multiball ball save**, making it disproportionately valuable in tournament play. [S5][S7]
* **Mr. Freeze:** completion freezes game timers, including minor-villain timers, Bat-Phone, playfield-X and other timed opportunities. [S5]
* **Mystery:** context-sensitive; during multiball it awards **Add-A-Ball**. Other awards include lock/progress, multiplier and time-related benefits. Competition-mode normalization was not verified. [S5]
* **Extra balls:** several qualifications exist, but Stern’s current **Competition install sets “NO EXTRA BALLS.”** No universal fixed points conversion was verified. [S6]
* No material standalone video mode found.

## What to watch

1. **Bat-Phone exposed / hurry-up running:** a major villain has been qualified and the player is trying to start it. [S5]
2. **One mode on the big TV, others on smaller screens:** that is the **Main Channel**; King Tut’s multiplier applies there. Channel-switching may explain an action-button hold. [S5]
3. **White multiplier inserts:** Commissioner Gordon progression is assigning increasingly valuable shot multipliers; a 5×/6× on a key cash-out can dominate the game. [S5]
4. **2×/3× playfield indication + active mode/multiball:** a major scoring stack is live; with Freeze completed, that normally temporary leverage may remain indefinitely while Freeze’s perk applies. [S5]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** **Strategic inference:** take completed villain cash-outs rather than repeatedly continuing them, use Shame/Villain Multiball for ball protection, and avoid sacrificing control solely to build an elaborate 3× PF / 5× channel / 6× shot stack.

**Playing behind:** the high-variance route is Bookworm + Freeze + repeated Tut, then a major villain/Villain Multiball stack with 2×/3× playfield scoring and a strong shot multiplier. **Egghead** can also create an enormous end-of-ball bonus if its frenzy has been developed. [S5][S7][S9]

**Key recurring decision:** **build the long-term multiplier/perk engine, or score immediately.** On friendly Batman ’66s, elite players can invest heavily in Bookworm/Freeze/Tut; tournament commentary also documents Shame + a major villain as a practical survival/scoring line on difficult machines. [S7][S9]

## Danger zones

* **Bat-Phone/turntable rejects:** observed to rebound sharply or, on some setups, release awkwardly toward center. Machine-sensitive. [S11]
* **Right-orbit return:** some machines send it toward the slings instead of providing an easy cradle. [S11]
* **Atomic Pile/Batmobile area:** closely packed spinner/targets can produce slow dribbles or unpredictable rebounds into the middle. [S11]
* **Ramp rejects:** Batman ’66 flows quickly when made, but a partial climb can return much faster than the intended ramp feed. **Strategic inference from shot geometry; machine-sensitive.**

## Trivia

* Batman ’66 marked Stern’s move into its **SPIKE-2 / full-color HD LCD** era and uses clips from the original television series. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][2])
* **Adam West and Burt Ward** supplied voice material for Batman and Robin. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][2])
* The design pairing is notable: **George Gomez** designed the game and **Lyman F. Sheats Jr.** programmed its unusually deep rules. [S1][S6]
* Stern’s 2019 Catwoman Signature Edition included a **Julie Newmar-autographed collectible card**; Newmar played Catwoman in the original series. [S2] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Catwoman Signature Edition received its own Catwoman-focused art package but retained the underlying Premium gameplay package. [S2][S10] ([Stern Pinball][1])
