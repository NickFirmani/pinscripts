## Identity and versions

* **Solar Fire** — Williams Electronics, **July 1981**, game/model **507**, Williams **System 7** solid-state with 7-digit numeric displays. Design by **Barry Oursler**; art by **Constantino Mitchell and Jeanine Mitchell**; software and sound by **Ed Suchocki**. Production: **782**. [S1][S4] 
* Four flippers across a **two-level playfield**, two ramps, three eject-hole locks, player-controlled Magna-Save, a magnetic Solar Gun captive ball, and **2-ball / 3-ball Multiball**. No pop bumpers. [S1][S4] 
* Latest verified factory software revision is **L-2**. [S3] ([Planetary Pinball][1])
* Solar Fire was the **fourth and final** Williams split-level/Magna-Save game after *Black Knight*, *Jungle Lord*, and *Pharaoh*. [S6] ([PinWiki][2])
* Scope follows the supplied commentator-reference specification.

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** get upstairs and repeatedly attack the timed **Flash Bank**—the upper **4-bank**. Hit the first target when the cycling value is near **100K**, then finish the remaining targets before the timer expires. [S1][S2] 
2. Build **F-I-R-E** with the upper 3-bank. A qualifying completion starts **20 seconds of 2× playfield scoring**; combine that with a high-value Flash Bank collect. [S1] 
3. Take Multiball when safely available: lower 3-bank completions light random eject-hole locks; two locked balls start **2-ball**, three start **3-ball**. Flash Bank pays **2× in 2-ball and 3× in 3-ball Multiball**. [S1] 
4. The blow-up stack is **100K Flash Bank × 3-ball Multiball × FIRE 2× = 600K**. Williams' contemporary announcement explicitly promoted a possible 600K Flash Bank collect. [S1][S5] ([World Radio History][3])
5. Alternate long-ball route: spell **S-O-L-A-R** for up to **6× Bonus X** and build both 63K bonus ladders; expert documentation gives a maximum combined bonus of **756K**. [S1][S2] 

## Core rules and persistence

* **Flash Bank:** the upper playfield has four drops. Hitting the first target freezes the cycling **20K / 40K / 60K / 80K / 100K** value; clear the bank before its adjustable timer expires to collect it. Failure resets the bank and restarts the cycling value. [S1] 
* **FIRE:** complete the upper 3-bank while its qualification lamp is flashing to spot a FIRE letter **and immediately start 20 seconds of 2× scoring**. Complete FIRE to light the lower-right bullseye for Special. [S1] 
* **Multiball:** each lower 3-bank completion lights one random eject-hole lock. Two lit locks + two locked balls starts 2-ball Multiball; all three locks + three balls starts 3-ball. [S1] 
* **Bonus:** normal bonus and a separate Multiball bonus each build to **63K**. SOLAR letters give **2× through 6× Bonus X**. [S1][S2] 
* The manual explicitly lists **lock lamps/locked balls, Magna-Save lamps, and Solar Gun progress as memory features**. SOLAR/Bonus-X memory and FIRE-letter memory are separately operator-adjustable; factory settings are **memory on**. [S1] 
* Multiplayer physical locks have **soft lock-stealing interaction**: another player's Multiball can clear a physically held ball, while the original player's earned lock state remains to be re-established later. Tournament treatment matters. [S7] ([Tilt Forums][4])

## Secondary features

* **Magna-Save:** completing any 3-bank advances Magna-Save availability. Separate cabinet buttons energize the magnet above the corresponding return lane. It is player-controlled and valuable, but expert guidance warns that a catch is **not guaranteed to settle safely into the inlane**. [S1][S2] 
* **Drain Shield:** repeated upper 3-bank completions advance the horseshoe from **25K → 50K → Drain Shield**. Collecting the horseshoe at Drain Shield protects one alternating outlane; a drain there returns the ball to the shooter trough. [S1] 
* **Mystery:** left inside rollover flashes Mystery at the right-ramp rollunder. It can randomly spot Magna-Save, Bonus X, Drain Shield, or points. There is no modern competition-mode normalization. [S1]
* **Extra Ball:** completing SOLAR awards an Extra Ball or **200K**, depending on configuration. [S1] 
* **Solar Gun:** qualifying lower 3-banks advance 3/5/10-second timing; hitting the Solar Gun standup makes its magnetic captive ball oscillate and score. Verified, but the main tournament guide says **ignore it** relative to Flash Bank scoring. [S1][S2]

## What to watch

1. **Flash Bank value lamp:** if it freezes at **100K**, every remaining upper-bank target becomes urgent.
2. **FIRE / 2× scoring:** a FIRE qualification immediately creates a 20-second scoring window; this is where a 100K bank or Multiball becomes explosive.
3. **Balls in eject holes / lock arrows:** two locks mean 2-ball is close; three enable the biggest Flash Bank multiplier.
4. **SOLAR / Bonus-X lamps:** 5×–6× plus developed normal and Multiball bonus means a large end-of-ball score is already banked.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** stay upstairs and farm Flash Bank. A clean 80K–100K collect is preferable to returning to the hazardous lower playfield solely to manufacture 3-ball Multiball. Use Magna-Save/Drain Shield resources rather than chasing marginal side features. **Observed strategy.** [S2] ([Silverball Rules][5])

**Playing behind:** build the full leverage package—**FIRE 2× + 3-ball Multiball + 100K Flash Bank**. The resulting **600K single collect** is the clearest comeback shot. Developing 6× bonus adds another large delayed swing. [S1][S5]

**Key recurring decision:** **remain on the relatively productive upper playfield or deliberately return to the lower playfield to build Multiball?** Bob Matthews explicitly recommends “Flash Bank first; Multiball if you can get it safely.” [S2]

## Danger zones

1. **Balls falling down either upper-playfield ramp:** expert guidance identifies these uncontrolled descents as the game's defining danger. [S2]
2. **Lower drop-bank shots:** almost every deliberate lower-playfield target shot carries substantial rebound risk. [S2]
3. **Magna-Save catches:** the magnet may fail to stop the ball or may release it somewhere other than the desired inlane. [S2]
4. **Upper-right-flipper exit:** balls slipping below it require awareness of the upper-left flipper; missing the recovery sends them downstairs. [S2]
5. **Center post:** tournament difficulty changes dramatically if the post between the lower flippers is bare or removed. Check before play. [S2]

## Trivia

* Solar Fire was the **last of Williams' four early-1980s two-level Magna-Save games**: *Black Knight*, *Jungle Lord*, *Pharaoh*, then *Solar Fire*. [S6] ([PinWiki][2])
* Only **782** were produced, making it dramatically scarcer than *Black Knight*. [S4] ([Pinball Serial Number Database][6])
* It was the only game in that four-game split-level sequence with **sound but no speech**. [S6]
* The magnetic **Solar Gun** repeatedly propels a captive ball across a scoring switch rather than launching a player's ball. [S1][S4]
* Williams' 1981 trade announcement called the upper target feature the **“Flash Bank”** and explicitly advertised a possible **600K** score from it. [S5] ([World Radio History][3])

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **4-bank versus 5-bank:** Bob's Guide/Kineticist repeatedly call the Flash Bank a **5-bank** [S2], but the Williams factory booklet, technical chart, contemporary Williams announcement, PinWiki and surviving playfield all document **four targets** [S1][S5][S6]. Use **4-bank**. 
* Secondary mirrors disagree about what happens to Flash Bank scoring after a ball drains from 3-ball Multiball. The primary manual only guarantees **2× while in 2-ball play and 3× while in 3-ball play**; avoid announcing the transition behavior until observed on the copy. [S1][S2]
* **Lock stealing is unusual:** factory memory preserves earned lock state, but tournament/player reports document soft physical-ball stealing between players. [S7]
* PinWiki reports an end-of-game **Bonus Ball for the highest-scoring player** in multiplayer games [S6], but the factory pages reviewed here do not clearly document its exact trigger/timing. Verify that this feature is disabled or understood before tournament use.
* Drop-bank timing, Mystery timing, SOLAR timing, and SOLAR/FIRE memory are operator-adjustable. [S1]
