## Identity and versions

**Cyclone** — Williams, 1988. Designed by **Barry Oursler**; artwork by **Python Anghelo**; software by Bill Pfutzenreuter; music by Chris Granner. Williams **System 11B**, alphanumeric display, two flippers. Approximately **9,408** units were produced. [S2][S7] ([PinMAME Machine Reference][1])

* **Rules basis: `rom` — L-5.** PinMAME treats L-5 as the parent/latest production set, with L-4 and L-1 as earlier revisions; System 11 service references likewise identify L-5 as the latest Cyclone CPU revision. [S2] ([PinMAME Machine Reference][1])
* **No multiball.** Competitive scoring is entirely single-ball repeat-shot play. [S3] ([Pinball.org][2])
* No edition split or materially different production trim was found.

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Make the 100K skill shot.** Its value is multiplied by ball number/plunge count, so later-ball plunges can be worth up to **500K**. [S3][S4]
2. **Grind the Comet ramp.** Repeated shots advance 20K → 40K → 60K → 80K → 100K → **1M**; factory settings require five qualifying ramp shots to reach the Million. Relight Comet with the left Cat/Ball Toss targets. **Observed strategy:** this is the safest repeatable tournament scoring route. [S1][S3][S6] 
3. **Take Cyclone when lit.** Three timed right-ramp shots award 50K, 100K, then the progressive **Gate Jackpot**, starting at 500K. Relight it with the right Duck/Shooting Gallery bank. [S3][S4]
4. **Exploit Double Score rather than chasing it blindly.** Completing 1-2-3 starts 20 seconds of 2x scoring; a Comet Million becomes **2M**, Ferris Wheel doubles, and draining while 2x is running also doubles end-of-ball bonus. The Cyclone Gate Jackpot itself is *not* doubled. [S3][S5]
5. Alternate strategy: build Bonus X early with the Boomerang; a well-developed bonus combined with Double Score can itself become a match-swinging cashout. [S5]

## Core rules and persistence

**Comet:** the center ramp advances one step per successful shot. Factory adjustment is **five shots to the Million**, but this is operator-adjustable from 3–6. Once the Million is reached, it is timed; expiration or collection means Comet must be relit via the three left-side targets. [S1][S3] 

**Cyclone:** the right ramp must be made three times during its timer; the third shot awards the Gate Jackpot. The jackpot begins at **500K**, and the factory manual sets each 1-2-3 rollover hit to increase it by **5K**. The Gate Jackpot is a machine-wide progressive and can persist until collected, including across games. [S1][S3][S4] 

**Double Score:** completing all three top lanes starts a factory-default **20-second 2x playfield** timer. Completing them again while active **adds another 20 seconds** rather than merely resetting it. It doubles Comet Million, Ferris Wheel and end-of-ball bonus, but not the Cyclone Gate Jackpot. [S3][S5] ([Pinball.org][2])

**Bonus:** a lit Boomerang advances Bonus X. Factory settings retain Bonus Multiplier progress between balls; completing both left and right target banks can hold accumulated bonus. Strong-player guidance therefore values Bonus X more on early balls than late ones. [S1][S5] 

Factory settings also remember the lit/unlit state of the **Comet and Cyclone** across balls (“Mem Lit”). [S1] 

## Skill shots

* **Five-hole Skill Shot:** soft-plunge the shooter lane into one of five holes. Bottom-to-top values are **10K / 25K / 100K / 25K / 5K**; the center 100K hole is the clear competitive target. [S3]
* The award is multiplied by the current plunge/ball count, capped at **5x**. Extra balls advance that multiplier too, so an extra-ball plunge can be worth more than the printed ball number suggests. [S3]
* The audible **“We have a winner!”** accompanies the 100K make. [S3]

No separate super or secret skill shot was verified.

## Secondary features

* **Mystery Wheel / Spook House:** knock down the drop target, then enter the scoop. The mechanical backglass wheel awards from **Zilch to 200K**, plus Extra Ball or Special. It is genuinely random; Cyclone has no Competition Mode to normalize it. [S3]
* **Extra Ball:** repeated Duck-bank completions can light Extra Ball on the alternating outlanes; Mystery can also award one. Factory settings allow the operator to vary how readily extra balls are earned. [S1][S3]
* **No conventional ball save** was found in the factory rules or adjustment table.
* **No action button, video mode, or player-activated save** applies.

## What to watch

1. **COMET 1 MILLION lamp / “Million” callout:** the next successful Comet ramp is the main repeatable cashout—**2M if Double Score is running**. [S3]
2. **1-2-3 lanes complete / Double Score music:** the player has a 20-second 2x window; subsequent completions extend it. [S5]
3. **Cyclone ramp lit + Gate Jackpot shown on backglass:** three timed ramp makes are now worth pursuing, especially if the progressive is unusually large. [S3][S4]
4. **Advance X lit at Boomerang:** the player may be deliberately building a persistent Bonus X rather than taking incidental points.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:**
**Observed strategy:** stay on Comet Millions, take a lit Cyclone opportunity when convenient, and avoid deliberately shooting the Spook House. The repeatable ramp game is comparatively controllable and does not require random awards. [S5][S6] ([Tilt Forums][3])

**Playing behind:**
Engineer the high-ceiling combinations: **Double Score + Comet Million = 2M**, chase a large carried-over Gate Jackpot, or finish a strong ball with Double Score active so the entire end-of-ball bonus doubles. [S5]

**Key recurring decision:**
**Keep grinding Comet or divert to the Cyclone progressive.** Comet is repeatable and relatively safe; Cyclone can be much larger, but its timer and right-flipper return force faster, less comfortable transfers.

## Danger zones

* **Spook House:** competitive players explicitly call it a “sucker shot”; low expected scoring plus an uncontrolled Boomerang kickout. [S5]
* **Boomerang kickout:** documented to feed left, right, or occasionally straight down the middle. [S3]
* **Duck targets:** stand directly above the right outlane; relighting Cyclone can be expensive.
* **Cyclone repeat:** the ramp returns to the right side while the same shot is naturally a left-flipper shot, forcing a timed transfer. [S6]
* **Pop-bumper exits:** under-shot Comet attempts and top-lane play can surrender controlled possession to the central pops.

## Trivia

* Cyclone is the middle game in Barry Oursler’s carnival trilogy: **Comet (1985) → Cyclone (1988) → Hurricane (1991)**. [S7] ([Kineticist][4])
* Its working **Ferris wheel physically carries the ball** before returning it to the left side. [S3]
* Williams promoted its rotating backglass **Mystery Wheel as an industry first**. [S8] ([World Radio History][5])
* Despite arriving deep into the System 11 era, Cyclone has **no multiball whatsoever**. [S3]
* Williams produced roughly **9,408** machines, making it a comparatively high-production System 11 title. [S7] ([Kineticist][4])
