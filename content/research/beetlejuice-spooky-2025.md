## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** make the skill shot/Super Skill Shot, then favor the repeatable **Left Ramp/Dante’s Inferno** and **Upper Knock Loop** to acquire a useful 5× Wheel award and work toward the relatively accessible Couch multiball. [S4] ([Pinball Genie][4])
2. Build **Returning Souls** before the payoff: pops spawn Lost Souls; returning them builds a playfield multiplier up to **5×**. Do not activate it casually. [S3] ([Tiltforums][3])
3. **Cash-out:** start the multiplier immediately before a multiball/Super Jackpot window and, ideally, combine it with the Wheel’s matching **5× Super**. This is the largest documented general-purpose scoring leverage. [S3][S4] ([Tiltforums][3])
4. **Alternate progression:** play Beetlejuice modes toward **Singing in the Stairwell**—complete two modes or merely start four—or deliberately delay starting a qualified mode to collect yellow **SHOWTIME** letters for It’s Showtime Multiball. [S1][S3] ([Spooky Pinball][5])

## Core rules and persistence

* Three purple Beetlejuice shots qualify a story mode. The rebound **Mode Change** switch selects it; Launch can lock the selection; the **Right Orbit** starts it. Modes cannot be qualified/started during multiball or a Juno mode. [S3] ([Tiltforums][3])
* While a mode is qualified but **not yet started**, yellow shots spell **S-H-O-W-T-I-M-E**. Completing it qualifies It’s Showtime, creating an intentional reason to postpone a mode start. [S3] ([Tiltforums][3])
* **Returning Souls:** pops spawn a Lost Soul; make its lit shot, then the front of the Center Scoop to bank multiplier progress, maximum **5×**. The multiplier is started through the **back entrance beneath the upper flipper** and then steps downward over time. [S3] ([Tiltforums][3])
* **Afterlife Couch:** Upper Loop “Knocks” light locks; lock three at the raised Right Ramp. Green shots score jackpots, Knock raises jackpot value, and re-locking lights Super Jackpot at Spinner/Juno. [S3] ([Tiltforums][3])
* **Singing in the Stairwell:** official code says **complete 2 modes or start 4** to light it. It contains add-a-balls; community documentation places its Super Jackpot at Juno, requiring an Action Button press to collect. [S1][S3] ([Spooky Pinball][5])
* Detailed between-ball persistence for several newer progress systems is **not sufficiently documented**; do not confidently call resets/carryover on stream unless visible.

## Skill shots

* **Standard Skill Shot:** time Launch to the lit plunge value; a successful plunge feeds the right flipper. A miss deliberately sends the ball through the right outlane and the under-flipper magnet automatically returns it **without consuming the normal Magna-Save**. [S3][S7] ([Tiltforums][3])
* **Super Skill Shot:** after making the normal skill shot, immediately complete **Left Ramp → Right Ramp → Spinner**. [S3] ([Tiltforums][3])
* **v2026.07.20 sensitivity:** skill-shot difficulty now advances only after successfully completing the previous skill shot; later successful skill shots additionally award a **+1× score multiplier**. [S1] ([spookypinball.com][2])

## Secondary features

* **Magna-Save:** Action Button fires the magnet below the flippers to rescue center/outlane drains. It begins qualified each ball; after use, hit the **Handbook** target to spawn the fly and press the green-lit Action Button to swat it and requalify. Maximum uses are operator-adjustable; Spooky added an explicit cap setting in v2026.06.11. [S1][S3] ([Spooky Pinball][2])
* **Dante’s Inferno Wheel:** repeated Left Ramps qualify a spin; Action Button stops it. Material awards include **5× Super Pops/Ramps/Spinner/Worm**, Extra Ball, points, or “You Lose.” [S3] ([Tiltforums][3])
* **Mystery:** coffin progress → right Mystery Scoop. Verified pool includes Lite Lock, up to **50M**, Advance Now Serving, **15-second ball save**, and **Shooting Gallery** video mode. Competition-mode normalization was not verified. [S3] ([Tiltforums][3])
* **Shooting Gallery:** flippers steer and Action Button fires. [S3] ([Tiltforums][3])
* **Extra balls:** Wheel and Sandworm progression can light one. No authoritative stock tournament point-conversion rule was found.

## What to watch

* **Purple faces / white mode inserts:** three purple shots qualify a mode; white indicates the player has locked the chosen mode. Right Orbit is next. [S3]
* **Yellow SHOWTIME arrows:** player is intentionally sitting on a qualified mode and building It’s Showtime rather than starting it. [S3]
* **Center 1–5 inserts:** visible measure of Returning Souls playfield-X preparation; a high number makes the next major scoring window much more dangerous to the opponent. [S3]
* **Raised Right Ramp / balls on couch:** physical indication of Couch locks and impending Afterlife Couch Multiball. [S3] ([Tiltforums][3])

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:**
**Strategic inference:** favor repeatable Left Ramp/Knock progress, Couch multiball, and keeping a Magna-Save charged. Cash an already-built multiplier rather than spending several risky shots maximizing it.

**Playing behind:**
Build **Returning Souls toward 5×**, acquire the relevant Dante 5× Super, then expose both during a multiball/Super Jackpot. It’s Showtime and Singing in the Stairwell provide higher-variance deeper routes. [S3][S4] ([Pinball Genie][4])

**Key recurring decision:**
**Start the qualified Beetlejuice mode now, or leave it qualified so yellow SHOWTIME shots remain available?** The latter sacrifices immediate mode progress for It’s Showtime Multiball qualification. [S3]

## Danger zones

* **Sandworm wall bank:** centrally placed standups; **strategic inference:** failed direct hits can return quickly toward the middle.
* **Polaroid camera targets:** another central standup-bank objective used for Strange & Unusual; missed control is the price of qualification.
* **Spinner:** documented to sometimes rattle into the pops/upper playfield rather than completing cleanly—control can disappear suddenly. [S3]
* **Magna-Save return:** a save means survival, not necessarily a cradle; the magnet throws the ball back into active play. [S7]
* **Right-outlane plunge:** looks exactly like an immediate drain, but during a missed skill shot it is intentional and automatically rescued—do **not** prematurely call the ball over. [S7] ([Zencastr][6])

## Trivia

* Spooky limited Beetlejuice to **999 Collector’s Edition games**, originally priced at **$9,999**. [S2][S6] ([Spooky Pinball][1])
* The game uses footage/audio from the **1988 Tim Burton film**, Danny Elfman’s theme, and Harry Belafonte’s **“Day-O.”** [S2] ([Spooky Pinball][1])
* This was the **fifth game** designed together by Luke Peters and Corwin Emery. [S2]
* The bizarre shooter-lane behavior is deliberate theme integration: designer commentary explains that the ball can “die” through the right outlane and be magnetically brought back, echoing Adam and Barbara’s immediate death/revival in the film. [S7] ([Zencastr][6])
* v2026.04.10 added **Singing in the Stairwell** months after launch; Beetlejuice has therefore changed materially through downloadable code rather than shipping with a fixed ruleset. [S1] ([spookypinball.com][2])
