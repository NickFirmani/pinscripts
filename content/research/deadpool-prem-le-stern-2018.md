## Identity and versions

* **Deadpool — Premium**, Stern, **2018**; SPIKE 2 platform. Lead design **George Gomez**; principal rules/software **Tanio Klyce**; hand-drawn art by **Jeremy Packer / Zombie Yeti**. [S1][S5] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Premium/LE add **8 extra drop targets, motorized disco ball, molded Wolverine/Dazzler figures and an actuated left-orbit ramp that feeds the right flipper**. Premium/LE also get **Disco Loops**, absent on Pro. [S1][S5] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* **Rules basis: `code` — v1.16.0, released 2026-07-07.** That revision only fixes the Player-4 score display; the latest material gameplay addition was v1.11.0 (2024-07-30), which added **Berserker BOOM** and increased Berserker Rage scoring. [S2][S3][S4] ([Tiltforums][2])
* Important multiballs: **Lil’ Deadpool Multiball, Ninja Multiball, Mechsuit Multiball, Sauron Multiball**. Premium also reaches Disco Multiball through its spinner progression, interleaved with Disco Loops. [S5]
* Research scope follows the supplied commentator-reference specification.

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** prepare **Lil’ Deadpool Multiball**, but do **not** start it yet. Start a Battle first—especially risky **Juggernaut**—then release Lil’ Deadpool to play the Battle under multiball/ball-save protection. [S5][S6] ([Tiltforums][3])
2. Before tougher Battles, qualify useful **Team-Ups**. Wolverine doubles damage; **Colossus doubles Battle scoring** and can make a healthy Final Shot extremely valuable. [S5]
3. Whenever scoring gets big, attack **SNIKT → right Katana ramp** for temporary **2×–5× playfield scoring**. This is Deadpool’s biggest recurring leverage mechanism. [S5][S6] ([Tiltforums][3])
4. **Ninja Multiball** is the longer setup: build Ninja Stars first if practical, then lock three balls; its Super Jackpot equals the jackpots already collected. [S5]
5. **Alternate route:** build/cash **Weapons** toward 45 for Mechsuit Multiball or 100 for a permanent +1 base Playfield X for the rest of the game. [S5][S4] ([Pinside][4])

## Core rules and persistence

**Battles:** Battle is available at the scoop at each ball start, then requalified by completing DEAD. Flashing shots damage Juggernaut, Mystique or Sabretooth; after defeating one, the scoop is lit for a **10-second Final Shot**. Battle progress survives a drain, and draining with Final Shot lit still grants completion credit; tilting does not. [S5] ([Tiltforums][3])

**Team-Ups:** qualify them before a Battle during otherwise-normal single-ball play. Dazzler adds health/time, Colossus doubles Battle points, Wolverine doubles damage, Domino adds villain-specific super scoring. Qualification gets harder after reuse. [S5]

**Stacking:** Lil’ Deadpool can be started during a Battle but not once another multiball is already running. Ninja Multiball can stack **into an already-running Battle or Quest**. This start order is strategically important. [S5][S6] ([Tiltforums][3])

**Playfield X:** SNIKT ricochets into the right ramp raise temporary Playfield X up to **5×**; higher X expires faster, while further SNIKT hits extend time. Completing Lil’ Deadpool Bounce adds +1 base X for that ball; each 100 Weapons adds +1 base X **for the rest of the game**. [S4][S5] ([Pinside][4])

**Persistence:** Battle/Quest progress, Weapons, Ninja Stars and Chimichangas persist. End-of-ball bonus uses those three resources; the bonus multiplier itself resets each ball. [S5]

## Secondary features

* **BOOM button:** spell BOOM through the return lanes; pressing the lit button spots the highest-valued lit shot other than the scoop. Banking four uses lights **Big BOOM**, which collects all lit shots. During Berserker Rage it also pays Berserker BOOM; Super BOOM pays that value at **4×**. [S4][S5]
* **Regeneration:** every third completion of DEADPOOL lights the **left outlane ball save**. [S5]
* **Mystery:** completing DEADPOOL lights the scoop. During multiball it normally prioritizes **Add-A-Ball** if available; otherwise awards include Ninja lock/progress, Weapons and other benefits. Competition-mode exact award sequencing was not found in current Deadpool-specific documentation. [S5]
* **Extra balls:** current Stern Competition install sets **NO EXTRA BALLS**. The exact Deadpool tournament points conversion was not reliably verified. [S4] ([Pinside][5])
* **No true video mode found.**

## What to watch

1. **Lil’ Deadpool insert green / ball physically trapped:** multiball is primed; if the player heads to the scoop instead, they are probably setting up a Battle stack. [S5]
2. **Green Team-Up icons:** identifies which allies are prepared before the Battle—Wolverine and Colossus are especially consequential. [S5]
3. **SNIKT shot succeeds into the Katana ramp:** Playfield X just increased; a player may immediately change from setup to cash-out mode. [S5]
4. **Right ramp lock / three balls locked:** Ninja Multiball is imminent; watch whether another mode is started first for the stack. [S5][S6]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** **Strategic inference:** take the easy Battle progress and prepared Lil’ Deadpool stack rather than repeatedly forcing SNIKT. Cash Battle Final Shots promptly, use BOOM to avoid dangerous final mode shots, and take Ninja Multiball once qualified rather than over-building its Ninja-Star value.

**Playing behind:** attack SNIKT aggressively during multiball, bring **Colossus** into Battles, and stack Battle + Lil’ Deadpool or Battle/Quest + Ninja MB. A near-full-health Battle Final Shot starts around **30M before multipliers**, so Colossus plus high Playfield X can produce a match-swinging collect. [S5] ([Tiltforums][3])

Also watch **Colossal Jackpot**: spinner, bumper and DEADPOOL-target activity builds it; a full U-turn collect can double it, and Playfield X multiplies it again. It can become significant in a long game. [S5]

**Key recurring decision:** **start the multiball now or use it as protection for a Battle/Quest?** Strong players routinely preserve Lil’ Deadpool/Ninja readiness until another valuable mode is running. [S6]

## Danger zones

* **Lil’ Deadpool drop-bank/standup:** direct hits and release feeds can head toward the middle; particularly dangerous during Juggernaut without multiball protection. **Observed, machine-sensitive.** [S6][S8]
* **Katana ramp reject:** weak right-ramp attempts can return abruptly toward the center/flippers. **Observed.** [S8]
* **SNIKT reject:** even apparently good hits can fail to complete the ricochet ramp and return unpredictably. **Observed/setup-sensitive.** [S8] ([Pinside][6])
* **Full plunge / left-orbit return:** can arrive at the lower flippers with substantial speed; players often loop-pass or otherwise absorb it. **Observed.** [S8] ([Pinside][7])
* **Juggernaut target work:** requires attacking DEAD/POOL standups/drop targets; this is precisely why competitive players commonly bring Lil’ Deadpool MB into it. [S6]

## Trivia

* Deadpool was designed by **George Gomez**, with its distinctive hand-drawn comic treatment by **Zombie Yeti**. [S1]
* **Nolan North** voices Deadpool in the game; Stern deliberately built an original pinball story around Marvel’s comic version rather than simply adapting one film. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Premium/LE’s actuated left-orbit ramp creates a repeatable feed to the right flipper specifically for **Disco Loops**. [S1]
* The **SNIKT** stand-up is intentionally angled so a clean hit ricochets the ball onto the Katana ramp—a shot that simultaneously demonstrates the layout and activates its central multiplier rule. [S5]
* Stern revisited this six-year-old title in **July 2024** for *Deadpool & Wolverine*, adding Wolverine speech and Berserker BOOM while increasing Berserker Rage scoring. [S4] ([Pinside][4])
