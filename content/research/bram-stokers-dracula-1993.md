## Identity and versions

* **Bram Stoker’s Dracula** — Williams Electronic Games, April 1993; model **50001**, Williams **WPC Fliptronics II**. Design: **Barry Oursler and Mark Sprenger**; software: **Bill Pfutzenreuter**; art: Sprenger; music/sound: **Paul Heitsch**. Approximately **6,801** produced. [S1][S4] ([Kineticist][1])
* Three major multiballs: **Castle Multiball, Mist Multiball, Coffin Multiball**. They can run simultaneously; stacking two gives a **2× multiball jackpot multiplier**, and all three gives **3×**. Do **not** describe this simply as universal “3× playfield”: the documented multiplier applies to jackpots plus features such as Mist, Bats, Rats, and Carriage Bonus. [S2] ([GameFAQs][2])
* **L-1 is the verified final Williams production ROM.** P-11/P-12 prototype code behaves differently in several scoring rules. A later **L-2C community Competition MOD** (2016) patches L-1, notably fixing Mystery at 500K and changing ball-save behavior around pop bumpers. [S2][S5] ([PinMAME Machine Reference][3])
* Scope follows the supplied commentator-reference specification. 

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** prepare all three multiballs before cashing any one. The **left Castle ramp is the game's key shot**: 3 loops starts Bats, 5 normally lights Mist, and recurring 6-loop bonuses can light Castle locks. [S2][S3] ([Kineticist][4])
2. Use the **right Coffin ramp** to light Coffin locks before an upcoming left-ramp bonus; if Coffin is already ready/open, that bonus can instead be steered toward the harder-to-obtain **Castle lock**. [S2][S3] ([Kineticist][5])
3. Ideally reach roughly **Mist lit + two Castle locks + Coffin nearly ready**, then start and overlap the multiballs rather than wasting one in isolation. Exact preferred start order varies among expert guides, but both emphasize building the stack first. [S2][S3][S6] ([Kineticist][5])
4. With all three running, prioritize **Castle Multiball**: relock one ball on the left ramp to light Jackpot, or two for **Double Jackpot**, then collect at the Tunnel. Triple-stacked Double Castle can reach roughly **480M**, easily a match swing. [S2][S3] ([GameFAQs][2])
5. **Safer alternate:** if only a modest comeback is required, starting **Mist Multiball alone** gives 10M on each major shot and is substantially easier than constructing the full triple stack. **Observed strategy.** [S3] ([Kineticist][4])

## Core rules and persistence

* **Castle:** light a temporary Castle Lock via target-bank completion, Mystery, or loop awards; collect three locks at the left ramp. During Castle Multiball, relocking one ball lights the Tunnel Jackpot for about **20 seconds**; a second relock with 3+ balls alive doubles it and resets the timer. Base Jackpot advances from roughly 20M to an L-1 maximum of 80M. [S2] ([GameFAQs][2])
* **Mist:** typically lights at 5 Castle-ramp loops. Shoot the Tunnel; a magnet carries another ball across the playfield, which must be knocked free. During Mist, major shots score **10M Mist Jackpots**, multiplied to 20M/30M when two/three multiballs overlap. [S2][S3] ([GameFAQs][6])
* **Coffin:** advance/open the right ramp, collect three virtual locks, then spell **D-R-A-C-U-L-A** through Coffin shots. Letters persist across Coffin multiballs. Jackpot progresses 20M→50M; with 3+ balls alive it is doubled, and Multi-Multiball can multiply it again. [S2] ([GameFAQs][2])
* **Bats is enormous in tournament context:** normally starts at 3 Castle loops. Clear 15 switches against a 50M countdown; the completed value **persists and is awarded again at the end of every remaining ball**. If Bats begins during triple multiball, its award can reach 150M. [S2][S3] ([GameFAQs][2])
* **Rats** similarly persists as an end-of-ball super bonus. Its exact stacked-multiball scoring is unusually ROM-dependent, so it is worth recognizing but not quoting a precise large value unless the ROM is known. [S2] ([GameFAQs][2])
* On production **L-1**, Bonus X advances to 10× and is held ball-to-ball; Bats/Rats are displayed separately and are **not multiplied by Bonus X**. [S2] ([GameFAQs][2])
* Easy commentary trap: once multiple multiballs overlap, only **two balls need remain** to keep all currently active multiball modes alive. [S2] ([GameFAQs][2])

## Skill shots

**Gargoyle Skill Shot** — the autoplunger sends the ball toward the upper-left drop target while three gargoyle faces cycle on the display.

* Left face: **100K**
* Right face: **500K**
* Center: **1M**, increasing by 1M per successful center award to **5M**
* Center additionally spots a **V-I-D-E-O letter** and normally advances the Coffin ramp one step. [S2] ([GameFAQs][6])
* The timing speeds up after success and can be inconsistent because of the launch geometry. For tournament play, the center award matters far more for **Coffin progression** than for its raw points. [S2]

No separate verified super or secret skill shot found.

## Secondary features

* **Love Never Dies ball save:** extremely short. Stock adjustment range is **Off–15 seconds**, with 2 seconds documented as the default; it also activates briefly when multiballs begin. The L-2C Competition MOD pauses its timer during pop-bumper activity. [S2][S5] ([VPForums.org][7])
* **Altar rescue:** immediately after an Altar eject, Love Never Dies briefly protects the **left outlane only**; a center drain is still live. [S2] ([GameFAQs][2])
* **Video Mode:** spell V-I-D-E-O via the right orbit/skill shot, then shoot the right orbit into the upper sinkhole. Flipper buttons shoot approaching werewolves. It can award useful points and typically an extra ball for completing its second wave, but it is strategically secondary to multiballs. [S2][S3] ([GameFAQs][2])
* **Mystery:** may award locks, Video Mode, Bonus X, Extra Ball, or points. Stock L-1 retains randomness even with Tournament Play; **L-2C instead fixes Mystery at 500K**, a meaningful tournament-code difference. [S2][S5] ([GameFAQs][6])
* **Extra balls:** notably lit through reflexed Castle-loop thresholds, Mystery, and Video Mode. Tournament treatment must be checked. [S2] ([GameFAQs][2])
* Flipper buttons steer the top rollover lanes and control Video Mode. [S2]

## What to watch

1. **Coffin / Castle / Mist lamps above the flippers:** tells exactly which multiballs are active; adjacent green **2×/3×** lamps show the current stacked jackpot multiplier. [S2]
2. **Ball visibly moving sideways across the playfield:** Mist has been triggered; the player must strike that magnet-carried ball to actually begin Mist Multiball. [S2]
3. **Castle Jackpot lamp at the Tunnel:** a Castle ball has been relocked; if another ball is also relocked, the player has set up the much larger Double Jackpot. [S2]
4. **Bats on the display:** an early completed Bats is not just the displayed award—it becomes recurring end-of-ball scoring for the rest of the game. [S2]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** take **Mist alone** if it provides enough points, or enter a prepared two-way stack rather than continuing to expose the ball while constructing the perfect triple. Controlled left-ramp loops are preferable to attacking Castle standups. **Observed strategy.** [S3]

**Playing behind:** build the full **Castle + Mist + Coffin triple stack** and attack Castle Double Jackpots. A single maximum triple-multiplied Double Castle Jackpot approaches **480M**, while Mist simultaneously makes major shots worth another 30M. [S2][S3]

**Key recurring decision:** **start a ready multiball now, or keep single-ball play alive to prepare the others?** Expert sources differ somewhat on the ideal starting order—one modern guide favors preparing everything and starting Castle first; an older tournament guide favors Mist first to exploit its ball-save window—but they agree that **stacking is more important than the exact order**. [S3][S6] ([Kineticist][5])

## Danger zones

1. **Castle-ramp reject:** one of the game's defining drain shots; a weak left-ramp attempt commonly returns toward the center. [S3] ([Kineticist][4])
2. **Pop-bumper exits:** strong horizontal feeds combine badly with Dracula's short “lightning” flippers and generous outlane exposure. [S3]
3. **Short flippers:** conventional flipper passes and recovery catches have less margin than on standard WPC flippers. [S2][S3]
4. **Altar kickout:** should feed the right flipper, but setup matters; a poor eject can head centrally. [S2]
5. **Late tilt warning with big super bonus:** production L-1 has a documented bug where a warning near the drain can cause **the entire end-of-ball bonus—including accumulated Bats/Rats—to be skipped**. [S2][S3] ([GameFAQs][2])

## Trivia

* Dracula uses **lightning flippers**, approximately **1/8 inch shorter** than standard contemporary Williams flippers, contributing materially to its reputation for brutality. [S3] ([Kineticist][4])
* The famous **Mist** effect uses an under-playfield magnet to carry a ball horizontally across the playfield for the player to knock loose. [S1][S4] ([Pinside][8])
* The game is based on Francis Ford Coppola's **1992 Bram Stoker’s Dracula** film. [S4] ([Games Database][9])
* Production L-1 became the final Williams rules release because programmer **Bill Pfutzenreuter left Williams as production began**. [S2] ([GameFAQs][6])
* Its display presentation is distinctive for the **blood-red DMD filter/panel treatment**, rather than the normal orange appearance of most contemporary Williams games. [S4] ([Pinball Adventures][10])

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Multiball starting order:** [S3] recommends preparing all three and generally beginning with Castle because Castle is hardest to start; [S6] advocates Mist → Castle → Coffin, exploiting Mist's initial protection and control opportunities. This is a genuine **strategy preference**, not a rules conflict.
* **Prototype versus production scoring:** P-11/P-12 differ substantially from L-1 in Rats, Bonus X and other behavior. Do not use prototype scoring values on an L-1 tournament game. [S2]
* **Tournament Play does not fully eliminate randomness on stock L-1.** The detailed rulesheet specifically notes random Mystery scoring remains; L-2C changes Mystery to deterministic 500K. [S2][S5]
* The exact Coffin jackpot behavior has conflicting historical documentation: the manual/rulesheet disagree on some bumper-based advancement details. For commentary, use the verified L-1 **20M→50M progression** rather than describing a bumper formula. [S2]
