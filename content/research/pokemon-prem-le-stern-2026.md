## Identity and versions

* **Pokémon — Limited Edition**, Stern, **2026**; Stern **SPIKE 3** platform. Lead design: **George Gomez and Jack Danger**; software/rules: **Tanio Klyce, Andrew Wilkening, Joshua Henderson**; art: **Jeremy Packer / Zombie Yeti**; sound: **Jerry Thompson**. [S1][S3] ([Stern Pinball][1])

* **Rules basis: `code` — v0.85.0, released 2026-08-07.** v0.85 added **Pokémon Arena** and **Pikachu vs. Lucario**. [S2] ([Pinside][3])
* Important multiballs: **Pikachu vs. Raichu**, **Team Rocket Multiball 1 & 2**, **Pikachu vs. Charizard**, and **Pikachu vs. Lucario**. An optional Magikarp topper adds a separate **Magikarp Multiball**. [S2][S3][S6] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** start with the top-lane skill shot; it builds bonus and sends the ball toward the bumpers, which also advance Pokémon discovery. [S4] ([Kineticist][5])
2. **Pikachu vs. Raichu is the safest early power play:** three right ramps start a 2-ball multiball; during its ball save, work BATTLE to qualify the action-button add-a-ball. Completing the story also earns a permanent-for-the-ball **shot multiplier**. [S3][S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
3. Prepare **2× Playfield** with the Berry targets, then stack a single-ball Story Mode—**Squirtle is a documented strong choice**—with **Team Rocket Multiball**. [S3][S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
4. In Team Rocket MB1, collect six jackpots → center Super Jackpot → a 15-second unlimited-Super window. Completing that first Super also qualifies another shot multiplier. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
5. **Alternate long route:** Discover → Catch → Train → Battle through all four biomes to reach the new **Pokémon Arena** mini-wizard. Rival Battles themselves can score heavily and now end in a timed Victory Bonus. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][3])

## Core rules and persistence

Each starter owns one major shot: **Bulbasaur = left orbit, Squirtle = left ramp, Pikachu = right ramp, Charmander = right orbit**. Three hits in single-ball play start that Pokémon’s Story Mode. Completing a Story Mode qualifies a **2× shot multiplier**: the *next* major shot made receives 2× scoring for the rest of that ball. Once every shot has X, subsequent awards can raise a shot to 3×. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

**2× Playfield:** hit both outer Berry targets twice, then the flashing center Berry. It lasts 20 seconds; center-Berry hits add 5 seconds, capped back at 20. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

**Team Rocket MB:** spell BATTLE, hit lowered Meowth three times, then shoot the center lane. It can run with single-ball Story Modes. Completing BATTLE during multiball lights an **action-button add-a-ball**. [S3][S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

**Catch/Battle path:** discover in the bumpers, scan at the Pokédex captive ball, complete the purple CATCH sequence and finish at the left ramp. Captures qualify Rival Battles at the town scoop. Winning a battle lights a **10-second Victory Bonus at the left ramp**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

v0.83 added **Travel**: complete Discover, Catch, Train and Battle in the current biome to choose the next biome at the scoop. Doing this in all four biomes qualifies v0.85’s **Pokémon Arena**. [S2] ([Pinside][3])

Easy commentary trap: **TEAM boosts and shot multipliers are ball-scoped**, while the four-biome journey is longer-term game progression. Optional topper MAGIKARP letters are even stranger: they carry **between players and between games**. [S3][S6] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

## Secondary features

* **Pokédex Assist:** build assists by hitting the captive ball; store up to three. Red action button spots a CATCH shot or valuable Battle attack, but cannot collect the final Catch or Victory Bonus. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][3])
* **Outlane save:** complete the town/Chansey targets; flippers move the save between outlanes. Each subsequent save requires another target-bank completion. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
* **Psyduck save — Premium/LE:** Sneak-In scoop starts a short ball saver on eject, with no grace period. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
* **Mystery:** TEAM lanes light it at the town scoop. Awards include points, discovery progress, Poké Balls, outlane-save progress and possibly Extra Ball. Exact current Competition-install normalization is not documented in the sources reviewed. [S3]
* **Extra balls:** three Story Modes, 50 Poké Balls, 25 Pokédex hits or Mystery can light one. When unavailable/maxed they score **2.5M**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

## What to watch

1. **Pikachu’s cheeks glowing:** 2× Playfield is running; expect immediate jackpot/mode aggression. [S3]
2. **X insert on a major shot:** that lane is doubled for the rest of the ball; this often explains repeatedly targeting one ramp/orbit. [S3]
3. **Meowth balloon lowered:** Team Rocket MB is being qualified; during MB, a green action button means add-a-ball is ready. [S3]
4. **Purple CATCH shots → Poké Ball flashing:** the player is completing a capture; once caught, the town scoop may light Rival Battle. [S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** **strategic inference:** favor Pikachu vs. Raichu and Team Rocket MB for ball protection, collect available Supers, and place shot-X on comfortable high-utility lanes—especially the left ramp—rather than forcing the Pokédex repeatedly.

**Playing behind:** combine **2× Playfield + shot-X + Squirtle/another Story Mode + Team Rocket MB**, then chase repeated Supers. Well-played Rival Battles are another high-ceiling line, especially with trained Pokémon and TEAM-boosted shots. [S3][S4]

**Key recurring decision:** **score the accessible multiball now, or keep setting up multipliers and another mode first?** Current expert strategy explicitly favors stacking Squirtle with Team Rocket, but Pokémon’s code is still young enough that there is not yet a settled v0.85 tournament consensus. [S4]

## Danger zones

* **Pokédex captive ball:** strong required hits can rebound off the right-orbit guide or sling toward an outlane. [S4]
* **Town scoop eject:** fast, awkward return; a reflex left-flipper shot is safer than assuming a dead bounce. [S4]
* **Bulbasaur orbit return:** approaches near the left-flipper tip; some copies have had notably dangerous center-drain feeds. [S4][S7] ([Kineticist][5])
* **Pikachu/right-ramp return:** very fast feed with little settling time before the next shot. [S4]
* **Battlefield:** Premium/LE magnet plus six standups makes direct center work inherently less controlled than the outer fan shots. [S1][S4]
