## Identity and versions

* **Volcano** — D. Gottlieb & Co., **September 1981**, model **667**, four-player **System 80** widebody with three flippers, four pop bumpers, two five-bank drop banks, a four-hole “skee-ball” crater ramp, three-ball multiball, speech, and a player-controlled left-outlane save. **3,655 produced.** [S1][S2]  ([Pinside][1])
* Design credits in modern databases: **John Osborne and Tom Szafransky**; mechanics John Osborne; art primarily **David Moore**. [S2] ([Pinside][1])
* No useful numbered stock ROM revision was verified; Pinside lists latest software as **unknown**. [S2] ([Pinside][1])
* **3-ball versus 5-ball matters:** only 3-ball play gives **2× playfield scoring during multiball**. [S3] ([Pinball Nirvana][2])

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Tournament-conversion route:** complete both drop banks to light Extra Ball at the **Fire Pit**. On the current competitive 50K-conversion setup, the Fire Pit stays lit and can be farmed repeatedly for **50K per shot**. **Observed strategy.** [S4] ([JLP's Pinball Cards][3])
2. While working drops, hit the **individually lit target**: it advances the Seismic bonus multiplier and earns the player-controlled left-outlane Ball Save. [S3]
3. Alternate route: aim the moving crater lights and capture three balls to start **Eruption / 3-ball multiball**. In a 3-ball game, playfield scoring is doubled during multiball. [S1][S3]
4. Higher craters are worth more and trigger timed **Lava Flow 2×/3×/4× playfield multipliers**; craters 3 and 4 also light the spinner. [S3]
5. Central decision: **farm the deterministic Fire Pit conversion or pursue crater/multiball variance.** Which is best depends heavily on Extra Ball handling.

## Core rules and persistence

* **Crater locks:** only the currently lit crater captures the ball; an unlit crater ejects it back through the Volcano shaft. Captured balls remain physically underground until the third capture starts multiball. [S1][S3] ([GameFAQs][4])
* Captured balls are documented as retained **ball-to-ball and player-to-player** until used. That makes physical lock state a multiplayer concern, although I did not find Volcano listed in the maintained lock-stealing wiki and would verify the exact behavior on the venue copy before calling it “lock stealing.” [S3][S7] ([Pinball Nirvana][2])
* **3-ball multiball:** all three captured balls erupt one at a time from the shaft; playfield scoring becomes **2×** until two balls have drained. Craters are not lit during multiball. **5-ball play does not get this 2× incentive.** [S3] ([Pinball Nirvana][2])
* **Lava Flow X:** crater 2 lights timed 2×, crater 3 timed 3×, crater 4 timed 4× playfield scoring. Crater values are 1K / 4K / 9K / 16K and advance bonus by 1 / 2 / 3 / 4 units respectively. [S3]
* **Seismic bonus multiplier:** hitting the individually lit drop target advances it. When base bonus reaches its maximum **19 units**, a Collect Seismic arrow lights; collecting bonus resets the Seismic multiplier to 1×. [S3]
* **Extra Ball:** completing both drop banks lights the Fire Pit. A closed-gate right-outlane drain can also light it for one shot if operator switch 31 is enabled. [S3]
* **Special:** visit all four craters, then collect at the top hole. Crater visits carry ball-to-ball; the Special lamp itself carries ball-to-ball only in 3-ball play. [S3]

## Skill shots

* **Moving Shooter Guide skill shot:** on the plunge, the shooter-lane guide and lit drop-target lamps sequence. Press the **right green cabinet button** to freeze both, then plunge toward the selected lit target. Gottlieb explicitly marketed this as the player's aiming challenge. [S1][S3] ([GameFAQs][4])
* Hitting the individually lit target earns **Ball Save** and advances the Seismic multiplier, so this is useful setup rather than just plunge points. [S3]
* The same right button later **freezes the moving crater lights for about one second**; another scoring switch must be hit before it can freeze them again. [S3]
* No separate super/secret skill shot found.

## Secondary features

* **Player-controlled Ball Save:** hit the individually lit drop target to light the left-outlane save; when the ball heads left outlane, press the **left green cabinet button** to fire the kicker at the right time. This is a genuine manual save, not automatic. [S1][S3] ([GameFAQs][4])
* **Extra Ball / tournament conversion:** stock rules award EB at the Fire Pit after both banks. The current Pinball Cards tournament strategy assumes EB is worth **50K**; under that setup the Fire Pit apparently **remains lit**, enabling repeated 50K collections. [S4] ([JLP's Pinball Cards][3])
* **Right-outlane rescue opportunity:** with switch 31 enabled, a ball through the closed right gate lights Fire Pit EB for **one shot only**. [S3]
* No video mode, mystery feature or automatic ball saver.

## What to watch

* **Fire Pit / Extra Ball lit:** if the event uses the 50K conversion, this is the game's dominant repeatable scoring state. [S4]
* **Lava Level 1 / 2 lamps:** one flashing means one ball captured; both means two captured and the next successful crater lock starts multiball. [S3]
* **Crater 2/3/4 light:** tells you whether the player is aiming for a 2×, 3× or 4× timed Lava Flow multiplier.
* **Left Ball Save lamp:** an apparent left-outlane drain is still live—the player must manually hit the green button.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** If the tournament uses the **50K Extra Ball conversion**, finish the banks and farm the Fire Pit rather than taking unnecessary crater risk. Keep the left Ball Save armed. **Observed strategy.** [S4] ([JLP's Pinball Cards][3])

**Playing behind:** Pursue crater locks and 3-ball multiball, where all playfield scoring doubles on a 3-ball setup. Timed Lava Flow multipliers provide another large swing opportunity before multiball. **Strategic inference** from confirmed rules. [S3]

**Key recurring decision:** **Drops/Fire Pit versus craters/multiball.** The first route is deterministic and, under 50K conversion, highly exploitable; the second offers multipliers, multiple balls and more variance.

## Danger zones

* **Volcano shaft eject:** owner reports describe the under-playfield launcher as erratic and sometimes capable of firing the ball essentially straight between the flippers. [S5] ([Pinside][5])
* **Pop-bumper exits:** multiple owners call out direct center-drain launches from the unusually fast lower pop arrangement. [S5]
* **Right lane/outlane:** notoriously dangerous even with its gate; failed attempts to recover/control there can simply drain. [S5]
* **Drop-target rebounds:** unavoidable because the main deterministic strategy requires clearing both five-banks.
* **Multiball release:** three balls are launched sequentially from the shaft; immediate control can disappear before the player has settled the first return.

## Trivia

* Volcano's four-hole crater assembly is essentially a miniature **skee-ball ramp**, one of the strangest physical scoring devices of the early solid-state era. [S1][S2] ([GameFAQs][4])
* Its shooter lane uses a **motorized, player-stoppable guide** so the player physically aims the plunge. [S1]
* The game has a **double-layer backglass/light effect** intended to make the volcano appear to erupt. [S2]
* Volcano was Gottlieb's **second speech game**, following *Mars God of War*. [S2] ([Pinside][1])
* Contemporary trade coverage said Gottlieb's location tests had Volcano earning strongly enough to compete with leading video games of the period. [S6] ([World Radio History][6])

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Dominant tournament scoring is configuration-sensitive.** The July 2026 Pinball Cards guide [S4] says the preferred strategy is both banks → repeat Fire Pit for 50K because the converted Extra Ball remains lit. Factory rules [S3] simply describe an actual Extra Ball. Verify the event setup before making Fire Pit farming the headline strategy.
* **Shared locks:** the detailed rules transcription [S3] explicitly says captured balls persist player-to-player, but Volcano is absent from the current Tilt lock-stealing list [S7]. Treat the exact multiplayer consequence as unresolved until tested on the venue game.
* **Seismic multiplier maximum:** available rule transcriptions state how it advances and resets, but I did not find a reliable source giving the exact maximum X value. Do not print one.
* **Multiplier stacking:** timed Lava Flow X and 3-ball multiball's 2× playfield scoring are both confirmed, but I did not verify whether an already-running Lava Flow multiplier stacks multiplicatively with the multiball 2×.
* Physical crater capture, Fire Pit eject, shaft launch and pop exits are strongly copy-dependent.
