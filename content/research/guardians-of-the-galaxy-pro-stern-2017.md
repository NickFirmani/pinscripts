## Identity and versions

**Guardians of the Galaxy — Pro**, Stern Pinball, **2017**, SPIKE 2 with LCD. Design: **John Borg**; software: **Lonnie D. Ropp, Mike Kyzivat, Waison Cheng**; artwork: **Christopher Franchi**; animation: **Zac Stark**; sound: **Ken Hale**. [S1][S2]

The Pro has the same core rules and physical Groot lock as Premium/LE, but only **one center playfield magnet**, a **static open Orb**, static Rocket figure, conventional pop lighting, and no sculpted Groot arms. Premium/LE have three magnets and the motorized Orb; this makes their multiball chaos physically different even though the scoring architecture is shared. [S2]

Latest verified Pro software: **v1.14.0, November 26, 2024**. Recent releases are mainly SPIKE/system support; the final major gameplay rules were established years earlier. [S1]

Major features: **Groot Multiball, Orb Multiball**, Quill's Quest 2-ball multiball, and wizard multiballs **Cherry Bomb, Immolation Initiative, Save Xandar**. [S3]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Prioritize completing character modes.** A completed mode permanently makes its associated shot worth **5X jackpots in Groot Multiball**, adds persistent bonus value, and advances toward Cherry Bomb. [S3][S4]
2. Prepare **Groot Multiball**, then start a compatible mode first and shoot Groot to stack them. **Yaka Arrow + Groot** and **Knowhere → Groot** are documented competitive routes. [S4]
3. During Groot MB, re-lock a ball in Groot for **20 seconds of 2X playfield**; do it again for **3X**. Combine that with the 5X character jackpots and permanent shot multipliers. [S3]
4. Use the **Hadron Enforcer** action button to spot hard mode/jackpot shots instead of risking dangerous targets. [S3][S4]
5. **Alternate high-variance route:** Orb Multiball can score heavily with controlled multiball play; late-game Quill's Quest is another enormous lever because its Level-2 hurry-up starts at **10% of current score** and can be multiplied. [S3][S4]

## Core rules and persistence

A character mode can be chosen at the **start of each ball** or started later at the right scoop. Completing it immediately begins its untimed **Level 2 / Super Mode**, which can coexist with later modes for the rest of that ball. Completed modes persist as game-level progress. [S3]

**Groot Multiball:** open Groot's mouth, then lock balls inside. First start requires three locks; later starts require more work. During MB, every major shot has three jackpots. Completed character modes make their associated shots **5X jackpots**; after jackpot milestones, Groot lights for Supers, eventually leading to the Double Super Jackpot. [S3]

The critical scoring lever is Groot re-locking: hit Groot to open him, then lock a ball during MB for **2X playfield for 20 seconds**; another re-lock can raise this to **3X**. These playfield multipliers combine with the game's other shot/mode multipliers. [S3]

**Orb Multiball:** hit the Orb drop then the target behind it three times. At start, quickly hit the Orb drop to lock in the hurry-up value and release the third ball; failure means only a 2-ball start. Orb hits can temporarily double the next jackpot, and completing the jackpot set leads to a Super Jackpot hurry-up. [S3]

**Rocket's Rampage:** spell RAMPAGE at Rocket to start **45 seconds of 2X playfield scoring**; Rocket hits extend it. Separately, completing the five Nova Corps rollovers lights permanent **2X shot multipliers for the rest of that ball**, later progressing to 3X opportunities. [S3]

## Skill shots

* **Normal:** plunge through the flashing top lane for **250K +50K per prior Skill Shot**.
* **Hands-Free:** make the normal plunge without touching either flipper; scores **2X the normal Skill Shot**.
* **Super Skill Shot:** hold the left flipper and full-plunge around the orbit, then quickly shoot the flashing **Orb, right ramp or right orbit**; starts at **1M +250K per success**. [S3]

The normal top lanes are flipper-selectable; touching the flippers forfeits only the Hands-Free bonus.

## Secondary features

**Hadron Enforcer:** complete the five scattered yellow standups to earn three action-button charges. Pressing the button spots the leftmost eligible lit mode/multiball shot and awards its multiplied value. Some finals/Supers cannot be spotted. Competitive guidance specifically recommends using Hadron rather than intentionally attacking every standup. [S3][S4]

**Right-outlane save:** hit the two targets flanking Groot to light a virtual kickback. Requalification gets progressively harder. [S3]

**Mystery:** spell GUARDIANS at the center target to light the right scoop. During either normal multiball, the first Mystery is effectively an **Add-A-Ball** opportunity. [S3]

**Extra Balls:** normally light at the scoop after three major modes/MB starts and again after sufficient completions. Current **Competition Install sets NO EXTRA BALLS**. When EBs are disabled outside that install, the rulesheet documents a **15M compensation**, multiplier-sensitive under some multiplier systems. [S1][S3]

## What to watch

* **Groot mouth open:** lock is available; if a mode is already running, expect a deliberate stack.
* **Character insert strobing during Groot MB:** that mode was completed earlier, so this jackpot shot is **5X**.
* **2X/3X playfield timer:** a ball has been re-locked in Groot; high-value mode shots and Supers become immediate priorities.
* **Hadron button lit:** the player has banked automatic shot completions and may use them to finish a difficult mode or jackpot instead of shooting it physically. [S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** finish manageable modes, bank Hadron charges, and bring a mode into Groot Multiball rather than repeatedly shooting dangerous standups. Take 2X Groot playfield when available instead of requiring the extra re-lock for 3X if ball control is deteriorating. **Strategic inference**, consistent with documented tournament play. [S4]

**Playing behind:** exploit the multiplicative game. Complete **Knowhere** or another favorable mode first for its 5X Groot jackpot shot, stack Groot MB, re-lock to 3X playfield, and attack that character shot. A late **Quill's Quest Level 2** is even more volatile because its hurry-up begins at **10% of the player's current total** and accepts applicable multipliers. [S3][S4]

**Key recurring decision:** **start Groot now, or finish/setup a mode first?** Groot is easy enough to stumble into, but its scoring ceiling rises dramatically when completed character modes and shot/playfield multipliers are already prepared.

## Danger zones

* **Orb shot:** narrow and close to the flippers; competitive guidance specifically calls the Orb/magnet area dangerous. [S4]
* **Rocket:** another tight close-range shot; a brick can come back almost immediately. Experienced owners commonly avoid forcing it single-ball. [S5]
* **Groot eject:** the Pro's single center magnet is intended to disrupt/redirect releases, so feeds can be unpredictable and setup-sensitive. [S2][S5]
* **Left outlane:** repeatedly reported as the dominant drain on difficult examples, especially from spin/backspin off nearby shots. [S5]
* **Hadron standups:** useful resource qualification, but intentionally bashing them gives up control; strong-player guidance usually lets them accumulate naturally. [S4]

## Trivia

* *Guardians of the Galaxy* is a **John Borg** design on Stern's early SPIKE 2 generation. [S1]
* Stern built a real **four-ball lock inside Groot's head** on all three editions. [S2]
* The Pro has a **static open Orb** while Premium/LE physically open and close theirs to reveal the Infinity Stone. [S2]
* Premium/LE use three central magnets; the Pro deliberately retains only **one**, so the Pro is physically less chaotic during magnet events. [S2]
* Marvel documented the game's development at Stern with Borg, programmer Lonnie Ropp and designer/programmer Waison Cheng discussing how the movie became a pinball ruleset. [S5]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Mode bonus percentage:** the current community rulesheet describes roughly **10% of completed-mode total** being added to recurring end-of-ball bonus [S3], while the 2019 Pinball for Mortals guide says **half the mode points scored on that ball** [S4]. Because this is not central to live tournament shot choice and the sources conflict, avoid quoting a precise bonus percentage on the final one-pager.
* **Groot physical vs virtual locks:** physical locking is stock; Stern added a tournament Virtual Locks adjustment in v1.06 and later included it in the Competition-install workflow. Confirm venue configuration before describing balls as being physically stolen/shared between players. [S1]
* **Pro feeds versus Premium/LE:** strategy transfers between editions, but the Pro has one magnet rather than three and lacks the moving Orb, so magnet/eject behavior should not be generalized from Premium footage. [S2]
* Some public strategy material predates the final 1.xx rules. The key mode/Groot/Orb rules above are taken from the post-v0.95 ruleset and current archive, not early launch-code strategy.
