## Identity and versions

* **Foo Fighters (Pro)** — Stern Pinball, 2023; **SPIKE 2**, LCD, 3 flippers. Lead design: **Jack Danger**; software/rules: **Raymond Davidson and Tanio Klyce**; mechanical engineering: **Harrison Drake**; art: **Zombie Yeti**. [S1][S4] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Scope is specifically the Pro requested in the supplied brief. 
* Important Pro differences: no Premium/LE Area 51 upper playfield, no physical Deadpost Overdrive mechanism, and no Overlord magnet; Pro instead starts Area 51 from repeated **right-ramp shots** and uses a virtual left-outlane Overdrive save. [S2][S3] 
* **Latest public Pro code verified: V1.03.0, November 14, 2024.** The current archive still lists that version as of September 2026. [S4] ([Pinside][2])
* Major scoring multiballs: sequential **Overlord Multiballs** (*All My Life*, *Monkey Wrench*, *The Pretender*), **Area 51**, **Combotron**, and deeper **FooBot Multiball**. [S2][S5] 

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Prepare a Van Mode and first Overlord Multiball together.** Spell VAN with white arrows, but ideally get Overlord close to multiball before starting the city at the left ramp. **Observed strategy.** [S5][S6] ([Tilt Forums][3])
2. **Start the Van Mode, then start Overlord Multiball inside it.** New Orleans is especially forgiving because all shots begin lit; first Overlord is often used as a utility multiball to finish a lucrative mode safely. [S2][S6] 
3. **Bring in Playfield X.** Fill the Rock-O-Meter for **2×**, then refill it while running for **3×**. This multiplier applies broadly and is one of the game's biggest scoring levers. [S2] 
4. On Pro, **five right-ramp shots start the first Area 51 Multiball**; it can even be started during an Overlord multiball. Eight jackpots then light right ramp → UFO captive-ball combo for the Super Jackpot. [S2][S5] 
5. Alternate high-variance route: combine **Bot Frenzy / spinner scoring** with multiball and multiplier. With all four unique Bot targets hit, Bot Frenzy spinner scoring can reach an **8× mode multiplier before Playfield X**. [S4][S6] ([Pinside][4])

## Core rules and persistence

* **Van Modes:** white arrows spell VAN; left ramp selects a city. Mode progress survives a failed attempt, and current code leaves the mode ready to restart rather than requiring VAN again after a failure. Van Mods—Engine/time, Speakers/scoring, Bomb/progress—carry between balls. [S2][S4][S5] 
* **Overlord:** first multiball requires qualifying the lock, capturing a ball, then bashing that captive ball enough times. During multiball, re-locking at Overlord starts a **20-second 2× jackpot window**. [S2] 
* **Area 51 Pro:** first start = 5 right ramps; later starts require **9**. It may progress/start during Overlord Multiball. Eight jackpots light the Super Jackpot sequence. [S5][S6] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Playfield X:** fill Rock-O-Meter for 2×; refill before expiry for 3×. Competition Install shortens the timer from the normal setting. [S5] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Ray Gun multipliers:** an inlane-adjacent Ray Gun target multiplies the **next shot 2×**; immediately hit the opposite one for **4×**. They stack with Playfield X and permanent/active FooBot shot multipliers. [S5] ([Tilt Forums][5])
* **FooBot parts:** collecting a part gives its corresponding major shot **2× for the current ball**; upgrading the part makes that 2× persist for the rest of the game. [S2] 
* Easy commentator trap: the **UFO action button can spot a valuable lit shot but cannot start a mode or multiball**. [S2] 

## Skill shots

* **Rock-O-Meter:** soft plunge through the right crossover/Rock-O-Meter lane; timing the flashing level can make substantial progress toward Playfield X. [S2] ([Stern Pinball][6])
* **Van Mod-ulator:** plunge into the three drops to award the currently flashing Van Mod level—often the best tournament plunge if ball save is short/off. [S2][S6] ([Stern Pinball][6])
* **Sonic Radio:** plunge to the flippers, then hit a radio target for 1M, dial progress and Mystery. [S5]
* **Combo Hi-Five Super Skill Shot:** hit the left crossover, then continue the lit combo chain for up to six shots; each successive shot extends ball save. [S2]
* **Secret Skill Shots:** plunge directly into the **left inlane** for 2.5M plus save time, or left outlane for **5M + light Overdrive**. [S5] ([Tilt Forums][3])

## Secondary features

* **UFO action button:** captive-ball hits charge 1–3 uses. Pressing it finds the highest-value eligible shot and awards/advances everything at that shot except starting modes/multiballs. [S2] 
* **Overdrive, Pro:** once lit, a left-outlane drain is automatically saved; hitting the left flipper promptly adds an Overdrive score award. Unlike Premium/LE, there is no physical Deadpost. [S2] 
* **Missile save:** first Bot Award lights a separate right-outlane ball save. [S2]
* **Mystery:** Sonic Radio target completions briefly light a target for an award. In **Competition Mode the sequence is deterministic**; first Mystery during a multiball is Add-A-Ball when available. [S2][S5] ([Tilt Forums][5])
* **Extra Ball:** after two completed Van Modes or bot thresholds. If EBs are disabled/capped, the game awards **10M**, and that consolation score is affected by Playfield and Ray Gun multipliers. [S5] ([Tilt Forums][5])

## What to watch

* **White arrows / left ramp flashing:** VAN is qualified; the player is about to select or resume a city.
* **Overlord ball captured:** Multiball is close; continued hits to the captive ball are deliberate qualification shots.
* **Rock-O-Meter filled / 2× or 3× display:** expect immediate aggressive cash-outs—mode finals, supers, or high-value spinner/ramp shots. [S2]
* **Right ramp Area 51 insert flashing:** on the Pro, the next right ramp starts Area 51; if another feature is already running, a stack is imminent.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** Use the first Overlord or Area 51 as a **utility multiball inside an already-running Van Mode**. Take 2× when convenient rather than forcing a difficult 3× refill, and use the UFO button to remove hard mode shots. **Strategic inference** from confirmed rules and observed tournament play. [S6]

**Playing behind:** Build the stack first: Van Mode + Overlord, optionally Area 51, then turn on **2×/3× Playfield**. Bot Frenzy or New York spinner scoring can be another explosive path. Ray Gun 2×/4× on a major collect can multiply this further. [S5][S6]

**Key recurring decision:** **Start the prepared scoring feature now, or spend more single-ball shots building multiplier / multiball / Van Mods first?** Foo Fighters rewards stacking heavily, but its center shots and fast returns punish greed.

## Danger zones

* **Overlord / adjacent targets:** hard direct hits can rebound straight into the lower playfield; expert strategy often favors a controlled right-flipper backhand. [S6]
* **Right ramp:** steep rejects are fast; owner reports also document some copies throwing ramp returns into the shooter lane/right-outlane area. [S8] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Right-orbit Rock-O-Meter return:** speed-sensitive; can skim the right sling/outlane instead of feeding cleanly across. [S8] ([Pinside][7])
* **Mod-ulator/drop area:** misses and paddle rebounds feed directly into other center geometry.
* **Upper-flipper crossover:** missing the side loop/ramp frequently turns a controlled feed into fast uncontrolled lower-playfield action.

## Trivia

* Foo Fighters was **Jack Danger's first full Stern production design** after joining Stern from his Dead Flip streaming career. [S1][S7] ([Pinball Archive][8])
* The theme is an original fictional Saturday-morning cartoon universe, **“Foo Fighters Saturday Morning Action Time!”**, not a recreation of a real tour. [S1]
* Stern collaborated directly with the band and included **15 full-length songs**. [S1][S3] 
* The Pro has only **three flippers** versus four on Premium/LE, but retains the unusual upper side-loop/side-ramp geometry. [S3]
* **V1.03.0** substantially buffed Combotron Multiball and changed Bot Frenzy's unique-bot spinner multiplier to **1×/2×/4×/8×**. [S4] ([Pinside][4])

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Code metadata conflict:** the maintained Tilt rulesheet currently labels itself “based on Code Rev 1.04” [S5], but the current public Pro software archive lists **V1.03.0 (November 14, 2024)** as the latest release [S4]. No public V1.04 Pro release was found, so use **1.03.0** unless the physical machine reports otherwise. ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Rulesheet age:** Stern's official detailed rulesheet is dated March 2024 [S2], while V1.03 added later scoring changes, especially Combotron and Bot Frenzy [S4]. Those changelog changes supersede older values.
* **Competition Install:** it materially tightens Overlord qualification, Playfield-X timing, and Van Mode timing; therefore strategy can differ from standard play. [S5]
* **Physical feeds:** right-ramp and Rock-O-Meter returns are documented as highly copy/setup sensitive. [S8]
