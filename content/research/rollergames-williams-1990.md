## Identity and versions

**Rollergames** — Williams, 1990. Design: **Steve Ritchie**; software: **Mark Penacho**; artwork: **Pat McMahon and Linda Deal**; music/sound: **Dan Forden**. Williams **System 11C**, three flippers, single multi-path ramp, physical three-ball lock and “Deep Freeze” magnet. Approximately **5,000** produced. [S5]

* **Rules basis: `rom` — L-2 / LA-2**, the latest standard English factory revision. Williams also produced later localized L-3 ROMs for some European markets. [S1][S6]
* The unofficial **L-2C Competition MOD** removes random Sudden Death starts, makes lock credit player-specific and deterministic, and changes other tournament behavior; this brief does **not** assume it. [S7]
* One major feature: **3-ball Multiball**.

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Complete WAR to qualify locks**, ideally qualifying all three before physically locking anything so opponents have fewer opportunities to steal your setup. **Observed strategy.** [S3]
2. Shoot **The Wall** from the upper-right flipper to lock three balls and start Multiball. [S2][S3]
3. In Multiball, keep shooting **The Wall for repeatable Jackpots**. The magnet is automatically available to tee up the first upper-flipper attempt. [S2][S3]
4. If **Sudden Death** randomly starts, immediately pivot to The Wall: every successful Wall shot is **1M** and resets its timer. This can swing a tournament game despite requiring no player qualification. [S2][S4]
5. Alternate payoff: spell **WILLIAMS** to start a **4M→1M Scoring Cycle** hurry-up, then collect it at The Pit. [S2]

## Core rules and persistence

Completing the three **WAR** targets qualifies a lock. Lock qualifiers can be accumulated before balls are physically locked, and three locked balls start Multiball. Factory multiplayer uses shared physical locks, so another player can remove/use balls you previously locked; compensation logic exists but is imperfect. [S3][S7]

During Multiball, **The Wall** is lit for Jackpot as long as at least two balls remain. The first Jackpot inherits the current progressive; after collection it resets to **1.5M**, allowing repeatable 1.5M Wall shots while Multiball survives. There is **no multiball restart**. [S2][S3]

Normal factory play uses a shared Jackpot that carries across balls, players and games until collected. **Contest Mode instead fixes the Jackpot at 1.5M**, sets WILLIAMS progress player-specific, disables saved locks between games, and disables automatic EB/Special percentaging. It does **not** eliminate Sudden Death. [S1][S4]

**Sudden Death** begins pseudorandomly during single-ball play or can appear from Roller Motion. It starts a timer; each Wall shot scores **1M and resets the clock**. Factory code has no setting to disable its random appearance, although its timer speed is adjustable. [S1][S4]

Completing **WILLIAMS** starts Scoring Cycle: **4M counting down to 1M**, collected at The Pit. Factory default WILLIAMS progress can persist across players and games; Contest Mode changes it to player-specific. [S1][S2]

The Deep Freeze magnet catches the Pit feed at the upper-right flipper. Wait through **“Don’t flip!” → 3-2-1 → “Flip!”**, then shoot The Wall. Flipping early releases the magnet prematurely. [S2]

## Skill shots

**None found.**

A full plunge travels up The Wall and scores **60K**, but contemporary detailed strategy explicitly describes Rollergames as having no meaningful dedicated skill shot. Plunged balls cannot be locked. [S3]

## Secondary features

* **Roller Motion:** semi-random Pit award. Possible results include Instant Lock, WILLIAMS letter, team advance, Bonus X, Extra Ball, Special, points—or essentially nothing. Factory Contest Mode does not fully convert this into a deterministic tournament feature. [S2][S4]
* **Atomic Whip kickback:** left-outlane save, relit by completing ABC and available through some awards. [S2]
* **Jetway teams:** lit orbit/spinner shots collect six teams; completing all six lights Extra Ball at the difficult center turnaround lane. [S2]
* **Power Plays:** Wall shots with no major Wall award lit build toward Extra Ball/Special thresholds. [S2]
* **Bonus X:** JET top lanes advance multiplier to 7x; normal bonus/multiplier resets each ball. [S3]
* No video mode or player action button.

## What to watch

1. **Green LOCK arrow at The Wall:** the player has WAR progress ready to convert into a physical lock.
2. **“Don’t flip!” / countdown:** the magnet has caught the ball; expect a controlled Wall attempt when “Flip!” is called. [S2]
3. **“It’s Sudden Death! Go for the Wall!”**: immediately important—each Wall shot is now 1M and refreshes the timer. [S2]
4. **“Go for the Pit!” / WILLIAMS completed:** Scoring Cycle is counting down from 4M and The Pit is the cashout. [S2]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:**
**Observed strategy:** qualify locks before taking them, then use Multiball and controlled Pit→magnet feeds rather than deliberately hunting random Roller Motion. If Sudden Death appears, take the free millions rather than ignoring it. [S3][S4]

**Playing behind:**
Multiball offers unlimited repeatable 1.5M Wall Jackpots while two balls remain. A simultaneous or conveniently timed **Sudden Death** creates even higher Wall-shot leverage. Scoring Cycle's 4M maximum is another credible comeback opportunity. [S2][S3]

**Key recurring decision:**
**Lock now or qualify more locks first.** Physical locks are stealable on factory software; qualifying all three first minimizes the amount of valuable setup left sitting in the shared lock for opponents. [S3][S7]

## Danger zones

* **Multiball release:** all three balls are dumped toward the **right inlane/right flipper together**, frequently costing a ball before control is established. [S2][S3]
* **Weak Wall shot:** can climb, stall and roll back down the steep ramp entrance.
* **Pop exit:** the central pops can release directly down the middle beside WAR. [S2]
* **ABC/right side:** useful kickback progress sits beside an **unprotected right outlane**.
* **Magnet timing:** flipping before “Flip!” shuts the magnet off and converts a controlled Wall setup into a live upper-flipper feed. [S2]

## Trivia

* Rollergames was based on the short-lived **1989–90 syndicated RollerGames TV series**, which mixed roller derby with pro-wrestling-style presentation. [S8]
* The show itself featured a **Wall of Death** and even an alligator pit during Sudden Death; both concepts are referenced by the pinball rules/theme. [S8]
* The production game replaced the prototype **SKATE drop targets with standups**, leaving some original instruction-card language inaccurate. [S3]
* The game is a classic Steve Ritchie flow layout: left orbit → upper-right flipper → steep Wall ramp is its signature combo. [S3]
* Williams produced about **5,000** games. [S5]
