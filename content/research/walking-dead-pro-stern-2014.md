## Identity and versions

* **The Walking Dead (Pro)** — Stern Pinball, **2014**, model **I-00F5**, Stern **SAM** DMD platform. Design by **John Borg**, software/rules by **Lyman F. Sheats Jr.**, art by **Greg Freres and Kevin O’Connor**, animation by **Mark Galvez**, sound/music by **Brian Schmidt and Vince Pontarelli**. Pro production total is **unknown**. [S1][S5] ([Kineticist][1])
* Latest verified original-game software: **v1.60.0, November 30, 2017**. It substantially rebalanced modes, added/fixed Competition behavior, improved Horde and made Crossbow scores carry ball-to-ball. [S4] ([Pinball Revolution][2])
* Three main multiballs: **Prison** (3-ball), **Well Walker** (2-ball), and **Blood Bath** (starts 2-ball, with add-a-balls). Deep multiball wizard modes include **Horde** and **Last Man Standing**. [S2]
* **Pro-specific:** no physical Crossbow cannon, no Well Walker magnet/guts, no raised Bicycle Girl target; Crossbow is instead a timed flipper-shot feature. Core rules are otherwise substantially shared with Premium/LE. [S1][S6] ([Stern Pinball][3])
* Scope follows the supplied commentator-reference specification. 

## Thirty-second game plan

1. Clear the **left 3-bank drops** to light a Dead Feature. **CDC** is the strongest documented single-ball scoring mode; **Riot** is especially good to stack with multiball. [S3] ([Kineticist][4])
2. While clearing drops, deliberately collect all three **Supplies**—First Aid, Weapons, Food—to qualify **Blood Bath**, the only main multiball that stacks with either Prison or Well Walker and the only one with add-a-balls. [S2][S3]
3. Build **Prison** or **Well Walker**, then combine one of them with Blood Bath and an already-running mode. Bare multiballs are useful protection; the **mode + multiball stack** is where the scoring gets large. [S3]
4. Bank Walker kills instead of wasting them: every two kills builds an **inlane shot multiplier**. Save a large X for a CDC cash-out, Well Super, or other huge collect. [S2][S3]
5. For maximum leverage, also qualify **2× playfield** at the Prison-side stab targets and Tower. Shot X and 2× playfield multiply together. [S2][S3] ([Google Docs][5])

## Core rules and persistence

* First Dead Feature requires **one drop-bank completion**; later ones normally require two. Five main modes are **Barn, CDC, Riot, Tunnel, Arena**. On normal settings, modes can be lit/started during multiball; **Competition Install forbids lighting new modes during multiball**, materially weakening some stacks. [S2] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][6])
* **CDC:** loop the left ramp to build a cumulative value, then shoot other lit arrows to repeatedly score that accumulated value; returning to the left ramp ultimately cashes the phase out. Expert strategy is to milk CDC rather than finish it quickly. [S3] ([Kineticist][4])
* **Prison:** bash center to spell PRISON, clear the Prison Yard, then complete PRISON again to start 3-ball Multiball. Six phase-one jackpots light a Super at Riot; deeper Prison progress toward **Siege carries across Prison Multiballs**. [S2][S3]
* **Well Walker:** repeated bash hits start 2-ball MB. Switches relight W-E-L-L jackpots; completing the set lights a **Super worth the sum of prior jackpots**. Unlike Prison, unfinished Well-MB progression does **not** carry to the next attempt. [S2][S3] ([Kineticist][4])
* **Blood Bath:** all three Supplies + another drop-bank completion starts it. Switches build the jackpot; completing the drops cashes it. The **first two bank completions during Blood Bath add a ball**. [S3]
* **Shot X:** every two Walker kills lights/increases the inlane X. Rolling through the lit inlane arms the multiplier for the **next shot**. If unused, more kills can build it well beyond 2×; it resets when used or at drain. [S2][S3]
* **2× playfield:** complete both stab targets flanking Prison, then hit the Tower through Riot. Detailed v1.60 documentation gives **30 seconds**, extendable before activation. It multiplies with shot X. [S2] ([Google Docs][5])

## Skill shots

**Top-lane / star-rollover skill shot** — Stern marketing calls the star-rollover system a **Super Skill Shot**. [S1][S6]

* Flippers select the flashing top lane before/during plunge.
* Making the selected lane scores the main award; hitting the flashing star rollovers on the way up adds substantial extra points.
* The first completed skill shot starts at **250K**, with later successful plunges increasing sharply; first-shot maximum including both rollovers is documented as **550K**. [S2] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][6])
* No separate alternate or secret plunge was verified.

## Secondary features

* **Blood Bath add-a-balls:** unique among the three primary multiballs and strategically important to long stacks. [S2]
* **BIT:** a lit outlane awards points; **it is not a ball save or kickback**, despite looking like one. [S3] ([Kineticist][4])
* **Woodbury:** right-ramp/Fish Tank progression eventually lights the far-right return-to-shooter-lane feature. Flippers choose a lane/rollover award before replunging. Useful awards include Extra Ball and Start Multi-Kill, but ordinary Woodbury scoring is modest. [S2][S3]
* **Crossbow — Pro:** Weapons Supply lights Crossbow at the right ramp; then a brief timed window lights major shots. Three successful Crossbow shots qualify **Crossbow Frenzy**. Premium/LE use a physical cannon; Pro uses normal flippers. [S2][S6] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][6])
* **Extra Balls:** several routes exist. Competition Install converts Extra Ball awards to points. [S2]

## What to watch

1. **Flashing Dead Feature arrows:** a mode is ready; left ramp = CDC, Riot/Tower = Riot.
2. **Three Supply inserts solid:** the next required drop-bank completion can start **Blood Bath**.
3. **Inlane X value:** a large banked multiplier explains why the player may avoid an otherwise easy shot and wait for a huge collect.
4. **2× playfield/Tower lit:** expect the player to delay activation until a strong mode or multiball is ready.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** favor controlled **CDC** scoring or a straightforward mode + Prison stack. Spend a modest X on a known good collect rather than risking several dangerous standups to manufacture the perfect 2× stack.

**Playing behind:** build **Blood Bath + Prison/Well + Riot**, bring 2× playfield into it, and preserve a large inlane X for the biggest Super/cash-out. A multiplied Well Super or CDC payout can change the entire match. **Observed strategy.** [S3]

**Key recurring decision:** **take protection now or keep building the stack?** Prison/Well can rescue a dangerous ball, but Blood Bath, a good mode, 2× PF and a banked X dramatically raise the ceiling.

## Danger zones

1. **Prison:** center bash plus magnet can throw the ball straight down the middle. [S3] ([Kineticist][4])
2. **Well Walker:** backhands can ricochet toward the left outlane or SDTM. [S3]
3. **Supply drops:** strategically essential but direct standup/drop rebounds are one of the game's major drain sources. [S3]
4. **Pop-bumper / left-orbit exit:** highly unpredictable—either flipper or straight down the middle. [S3]
5. **Stab targets for 2×:** excellent leverage, but expert guidance specifically calls these targets **deadly**. [S3] ([Kineticist][4])

## Trivia

* TWD reunited **John Borg and Lyman Sheats**, the design/rules pairing behind *TRON: Legacy* and *Metallica*. [S5] ([Ars Technica][7])
* Special-effects artist and *Walking Dead* producer **Greg Nicotero** designed the game's sculpted Walker characters. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][3])
* Stern later added the **Premium in 2015** after initially launching Pro and a 600-unit LE. [S5] ([Kineticist][1])
* The 2025 **Walking Dead Remastered** is based on the original Premium/LE layout—not this mechanically simpler Pro. ([Tilt Forums][8])
* v1.60 was an unusually late major update, arriving more than three years after the original game. [S4]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **2× playfield timer:** the maintained v1.60 rulesheet and current expert guide give **30 seconds** [S2][S3], while one older rulesheet mirror still says **20 seconds**. Use the actual v1.60 machine/adjustment.
* Competition Install materially changes stacking: modes cannot be **lit** during multiball and Extra Balls become points. [S2]
* Competitive copies sometimes keep the **Prison doors closed** because the exposed head can fail to register reliably. [S3] This is a venue modification, not the normal rule.
* Pro Crossbow is a timed flipper-shot feature; Premium/LE use the motorized cannon. [S2][S6]
* Production quantity for the Pro is not reliably documented; IPSND/Pinside list it as **unknown**. [S5]
