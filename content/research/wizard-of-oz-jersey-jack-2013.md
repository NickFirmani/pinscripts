## Identity and versions

* **The Wizard of Oz** — Jersey Jack Pinball, **2013**; widebody, five-flipper, LCD-era solid-state game. Design by **Joe Balcer**; software by **Keith P. Johnson and Ted Estes**; art by **Jerry Vanderstelt, Greg Freres and Matt Riesterer**; animation by **Jean-Paul de Win**; music/sound by **Chris Granner and Rob Berry**. [S2][S4]
* Editions include **Standard, Emerald City Limited Edition, 75th Anniversary, and Yellow Brick Road LE**. Their core game/rules are substantially the same; differences are primarily cosmetics, trim, toys and included hardware. [S2][S4]
* Latest verified software is **8.02, March 25, 2025**; JJP's current support page distributes 8.02 for the game. [S1][S5]
* Major multiballs/features useful on commentary: **Emerald City Multiball, Rescue Multiball, four Crystal Ball 2-ball modes + Flipper Frenzy, Munchkin Multiball, Haunted Forest Multiball, Battle the Wicked Witch**, and final wizard mode **Somewhere Over the Rainbow**.
* Scope follows the supplied commentator-reference specification.

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** build several features simultaneously rather than rushing one multiball. *Wizard of Oz* rewards stacking more than isolated cash-outs. [S2][S3]
2. An accessible foundation is **Emerald City Multiball (ECM)**: complete Lion, Tin Man and Scarecrow rollovers, then shoot the center ramp after each character to lock three balls. [S2]
3. Also spell **B-A-L-L** for a Crystal Ball mode. The first four are **2× scoring**; Flipper Frenzy is **3×**. Stack one with multiball whenever practical. [S2]
4. During multiball, put balls onto multiple active playfields to engage **Super X**. Main + Munchkinland = 2×; adding the Castle = 3×, and this multiplies with Crystal Ball's 2×/3× scoring. [S2]
5. Alternate high-ceiling route: **Rescue Multiball**. Building extra balls beforehand increases its jackpots; completing the Mega Jackpot earns one of eight jewels toward **Somewhere Over the Rainbow**. [S2]

## Core rules and persistence

* **ECM:** qualify each of the three characters with its rollover, then immediately shoot the ramp to lock that character. Three locks start multiball. Completing the full gift sequence earns a jewel toward Somewhere Over the Rainbow. [S2]
* **Rescue Multiball:** qualify Capture Dorothy at the Winged Monkey targets, then make the right orbit to physically capture her. Enter the Castle, spell **R-E-S-C-U-E**, break the doors, and shoot the saucer. Additional pre-start RESCUE/Search completions can add balls and increase jackpot values. [S2]
* Rescue jackpots progress to **Super Jackpot**; getting all six jackpot shots to 3× lights **Mega Jackpot**, collected via Search Orbit → Castle-door combo. Mega awards its SOTR jewel. [S2]
* **Super X:** during multiball, the number of simultaneously active playfields determines an additional multiplier. The documented insert states include **2×, 3×, 4×, 6× and 9×**; Crystal Ball scoring multiplies on top of this. [S2]
* **Crystal Ball:** B-A-L-L → spinner/VUK starts a 2-ball mode. Lights Off, Lights On, Weak Flippers and No-Hold Flippers all score **2×**; fifth mode Flipper Frenzy gives **3×** with reversed flipper controls. [S2]
* **Battle the Wicked Witch:** becomes available after starting ECM, Rescue MB, a Munchkin Mode and Fireball Frenzy. It is a 2-ball mini-wizard mode. [S2]
* **Somewhere Over the Rainbow:** requires eight jewels covering essentially every major rules branch, including completed ECM/Rescue, Munchkin/Haunted/Crystal-Ball progress, Yellow Brick Road, Fireball Frenzy and Wicked Witch. [S2]
* Important persistence: Emerald City and RESCUE progress gained persistence in later code; Horse of a Different Color progress also carries ball-to-ball. [S2][S6]

## Skill shots

**O-Z Lane Skill Shot**

* Plunge through the lit O-Z lane.
* Starts at **5K**, increasing 1K per success, and awards an OZ multiplier. [S2]
* Competition rules fix the skill lane rather than randomizing it. [S6]

**Witch Skill Shot**

* Soft plunge to the upper-right flipper, then shoot the Witch.
* Starts at **5K** and immediately starts a Wicked Witch Hurry-Up. [S2]

**Haunted Forest Skill Shot**

* Feed the upper-right flipper and hit the Haunted Forest skill target.
* Starts a **Haunted mode**, making it a strategically useful progression choice. [S2]

**B-A-L-L Skill Shot**

* Note the flashing B-A-L-L target before plunging; feed the upper flipper and hit that target.
* Spots **all four B-A-L-L letters**, immediately qualifying a Crystal Ball mode. This is usually the highest-leverage tournament choice when available. **Strategic inference.** [S2]
* In Competition Mode, the chosen BALL target is deterministic. [S6]

## Secondary features

* **There’s No Place Like Home:** left-outlane rescue. After entering the left outlane, correctly activating the five nearby targets starts a timed recovery sequence. Qualified target state can carry between balls; difficulty/timing is adjustable. [S2][S6]
* **Toto Escape:** complete T-O-T-O, then a right-outlane drain starts a timed escape challenge rather than immediately ending the ball. Later saves require more shots. [S2]
* **Extra Balls:** collected at the Throne Room from several progression routes. JJP supports a **No Extra Balls** setting / limits; tournament handling must be checked. [S2][S6]
* **Glinda/Wizard awards:** normally semi-random utility awards. Competition Mode makes major random sequences deterministic, including BALL skill shot, lane skill shot, Fireball order, Glinda, Wizard, HOADC and Haunted modes. [S6]

## What to watch

1. **Character rollover + ramp Lock lamp:** the player is building Emerald City Multiball.
2. **B-A-L-L complete / Crystal Ball glowing:** 2× scoring—and therefore a potentially huge stack—is one spinner/VUK shot away.
3. **Super X insert color:** immediately tells how many scoring multipliers are active through multiple playfields.
4. **Eight jewel status / SOTR ramp lit:** the player has completed virtually the entire rules tree and is about to start the final wizard mode.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** use **ECM + a favorable Crystal Ball mode** rather than forcing Castle/Witch shots. Preserve control and take the multiplier stack you already have. **Strategic inference.**

**Playing behind:** build a larger stack—Crystal Ball 2×/3× plus multiple active playfields for Super X—and pursue **Rescue Mega Jackpot** or completed ECM gifts. These multipliers can turn otherwise modest values into match-changing scoring. [S2][S3]

**Key recurring decision:** **start a ready feature now, or delay it until another mode/multiball is qualified?** PAPA-era tournament guidance explicitly recommends stacking, while warning that **Fireball Frenzy can make a stack worse** because its “bad” red shots interfere with normal scoring. [S3]

## Danger zones

1. **Winkie/Castle target:** required for Rescue but a direct center-right drop-target bash.
2. **Witch magnets:** deliberately alter ball motion; a Witch attempt may not return predictably.
3. **Center-ramp reject:** the game's busiest progression shot can come back fast toward the flippers.
4. **Upper mini-playfield exits:** Castle/Twister play creates uncontrolled re-entry to the main playfield.
5. **Tilt with Munchkin scoring:** Munchkin-mode scoring is applied in bonus, so a tilt can erase substantial accumulated points. [S2]

## Trivia

* *Wizard of Oz* was **Jersey Jack Pinball's first production game**. [S4]
* It was the first new American pinball in years to make a **full-color LCD** central to the presentation, helping establish the modern LCD era. [S4]
* The game has **three simultaneously playable levels**: main playfield, Munchkinland/Twister and Witch's Castle—used directly by the Super X rules. [S2]
* **Dennis Nordman** built the original spinning-house model and conceived the original Witch mechanism. [S2]
* Version **3.0** was a landmark rules update, adding Somewhere Over the Rainbow, Super X, Haunted Multiball and persistent ECM/Rescue progression. [S6]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* The commonly available detailed rulesheet is **v1.25 from 2015** [S2], while current production code is **8.02 from 2025** [S1][S5]. The major rules architecture remains recognizable, but exact scoring/timing should defer to the current machine.
* Several early rulesheet values are explicitly marked uncertain by its author—especially some skill-shot increments and minor Munchkin values. They are omitted here rather than treated as confirmed. [S2]
* Competition Mode changed substantially after early code: by 6.06 it explicitly normalized numerous random features. [S6]
* Edition differences are primarily cosmetic/hardware; no material rules difference between Standard, ECLE, 75th and YBR LE was verified.
* Ramp, Castle, magnets, upper-playfield exits and VUK ejects are highly copy-dependent.
