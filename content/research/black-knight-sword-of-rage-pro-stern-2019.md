## Identity and versions

**Black Knight: Sword of Rage — Pro**, Stern Pinball, 2019. SPIKE 2, LCD, two flippers. Lead design **Steve Ritchie**; rules/software **Tim Sexton**; artwork credits include **Kevin O’Connor**; music by **Scott Ian and Brendon Small**. [S1][S2][S3] ([Stern Pinball][1])

**Pro matters:** unlike Premium/LE, it has **no transparent upper playfield, third flipper, Catapult lock, Catapult Multiball, or Last Chance feature**. The core Monster Battles, Triple Knights Challenge, KNIGHT modes, Magna-Save, Supers and Black Castle are shared. [S1][S3] ([Stern Pinball][2])

Latest software verified: **Pro v1.18, November 26, 2024**. [S2] ([Pinside][3])

Major normal-play multiballs useful to commentary: **Triple Knights Challenge**, **KNIGHT Multiball**, **The King’s Ransom** and, after Black Castle, **RAGE Multiball**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Work toward a Monster Battle and Triple Knights Challenge together.** Hit the three dangerous center areas to qualify a battle; hit the small Light Lock target and shield scoop to build three virtual locks. Strong players commonly arrange to bring a Battle into multiball. [S3][S4] ([Tilt Forums][5])
2. During a Battle, charge **Sword of Rage POWER** with right drops/center hits and collect POWER for **2X battle scoring plus easier rules**. [S3] ([Pinball Genie][6])
3. Build **K-N-I-G-H-T** through Battles, WAR Hurry-Ups and the Triple Knights Super Jackpot. Completing KNIGHT lights the left spinner lane for a 3-ball retro multiball with **playfield scoring multiplied by balls in play, up to 3X**. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][5])
4. The huge leverage play is to have valuable **Super Features** running before KNIGHT Multiball, then cash their **Super Jackpot at up to 3X** with Magna-Save/right-return-lane logic. [S3][S6] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
5. **Alternate:** grind WAR completions/Hurry-Ups. They can reach **25M each**, award KNIGHT letters, and avoid some repeated direct mode-qualification bashing. [S3][S5] ([Tilt Forums][5])

## Core rules and persistence

**Monster Battles:** qualify the ramp with the three center shots; make the ramp to raise the shield, then shoot behind the shield to start a battle. Modes begin around **65 seconds** and **carry between balls**; the game restores some time after a drain when necessary. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][7])

**Sword of Rage / POWER:** right-drop-bank completions and center-target hits significantly fill the sword meter. During a Battle, collecting lit POWER at the center target gives **2X scoring for that Battle and changes its rules to make completion easier**. Default code starts the first Sword substantially pre-charged; **Competition Install changes Sword difficulty to HARD**. [S2][S3] ([Tilt Forums][8])

**Triple Knights Challenge:** three virtual locks start 3-ball multiball. Complete the left spinner lane, ramp and shield; then a ramp hurry-up sets Phase 2 jackpot value. Complete all lit jackpots to relight the ramp for a **3X Super Jackpot plus a KNIGHT letter**. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][5])

**KNIGHT Multiball:** KNIGHT letters persist through the game. Normal settings initially spot K and N, but **Competition Install sets KNIGHT difficulty to Extra Hard, spotting zero letters**. During KNIGHT MB, scoring is multiplied by balls in play, max **3X**; Super Features may score but cannot be further boosted once multiball begins. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][3])

**Supers:** spinner activity lights SUPER at the center target. Starting it awards a random Super category; Supers are untimed and last until drain or collection. The left VUK levels all active Supers during single-ball play, up to purple. [S2][S3] ([Epicenter For All Things Pinball][9])

Easy-to-miss rule: on current Super rules, the displayed Super Jackpot is collected when the player **activates Magna-Save and then crosses the right return-lane switch within five seconds**. It therefore does not strictly require an actual outlane rescue. [S2] ([Epicenter For All Things Pinball][9])

## Skill shots

* **Standard Skill Shot — Pro:** plunge the lit **W-A-R** top lane for **750K**, increasing 250K for successive skill shots. Flippers can lane-change. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
* **Locked-lane Super Skill Shot:** hold the left flipper and plunge the already-lit WAR lane **without changing it**; documented at **3X the normal Skill Shot value**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
* **Center-shot Super Skill Shot:** hold the left flipper through the plunge and hit the **flail, center target or shield** for **1.5M +500K for each subsequent Super Skill Shot**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

The two latter outcomes are both described as Pro “Super Skill Shots” in the community rules documentation.

## Secondary features

**Magna-Save:** lockdown-bar button fires the magnet above the **right outlane**. Completing the right drop bank relights it. Besides survival, Magna-Save is the Super Jackpot cash-out mechanism, making it strategically much more than a kickback. [S1][S3] ([Stern Pinball][10])

**Left-outlane Ball Save:** complete the two Add Time standups enough times to light a save at the left outlane; subsequent saves become harder to requalify. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][5])

**Extra Balls:** winning a Battle, enough U-Turns, King’s Ransom or some Mystery outcomes can light Extra Ball at the shield scoop. Current **Competition Install sets NO EXTRA BALLS**. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][3])

**Mystery:** spell R-A-G-E on the return/outlanes. Under current Competition Mode, the randomized award is normalized to alternate between **3M and Advance WAR Lanes**. [S2] ([Pinside][3])

## What to watch

* **POWER lit at the center target:** the player can convert the current Battle to 2X/easier rules; delaying it may be intentional. [S3]
* **Colored Super icons on the LCD:** green → yellow → red → purple shows increasingly valuable Supers; purple means maxed. [S2][S3]
* **K-N-I-G-H-T spellout:** once complete, the left spinner lane starts the enormous balls-in-play multiplier window. [S3]
* **Magna-Save lamp:** if lit while high-value Supers are active, a right-return sequence can suddenly create a massive Super Jackpot. [S2][S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** cradle and **backhand the center shots** rather than firing uncontrolled forehands; keep Magna-Save lit and take the reliable Battle + Triple Knights stack instead of extending Supers indefinitely. This “backhand, backhand, backhand” approach is repeatedly documented for the Pro because the rubber-post geometry punishes misses. **Observed strategy.** [S4][S6] ([Pinball for Mortals!][11])

**Playing behind:** build multiple/high-level Supers, push toward KNIGHT, and defer the Super Jackpot until the **3X balls-in-play window**. Successful modern-code cash-outs can dwarf ordinary mode scoring. WAR Hurry-Ups are another aggressive route because individual collects can reach 25M while also advancing KNIGHT. [S3][S5][S6] ([Tilt Forums][5])

**Key recurring decision:** **use Magna-Save now for survival/cash-out, or preserve and build toward a larger Super Jackpot—ideally inside KNIGHT Multiball?**

## Danger zones

* **Forehanding central shots:** close rubber posts create brutal rejects; tournament players heavily favor backhands. [S4]
* **Light Lock target:** a miss/rebound can head quickly toward the right drain. [S4]
* **Shield:** mechanically blocks/bashes the ball and can return it with substantial speed. [S1]
* **Flail ramp:** incomplete entries interact with the motorized flail and central posts, producing unpredictable returns. [S3]
* **Right drop bank:** strategically desirable for Magna-Save/POWER but exposes the ball to direct target rebounds.

## Trivia

* *Sword of Rage* is the **third Steve Ritchie Black Knight game**, following Williams’ *Black Knight* (1980) and *Black Knight 2000* (1989). [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Stern described the project as the Black Knight returning after roughly **40 years**. [S1]
* The player-controlled **Magna-Save** deliberately continues one of the franchise’s signature mechanics. [S1]
* The soundtrack was written/performed by **Scott Ian of Anthrax and Brendon Small**. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Premium/LE add the transparent upper playfield and Catapult; the **Pro’s simpler two-flipper lower playfield produces a materially different tournament game**. [S1][S3]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Super Jackpot terminology changed with code.** Very early 2019 discussions describe the left VUK itself as the Super cash-out. Stern's v1.01 changelog changed the rule: the left VUK **boosts** Supers, while collection requires Magna-Save activation followed by the right return-lane switch within five seconds. Use the latter on current code. [S2][S3] ([Epicenter For All Things Pinball][9])
* The public community rulesheet is explicitly based on **v1.14**, while current code is **v1.18**. Later releases appear predominantly system/tournament-install updates rather than a major scoring rewrite, but v1.18 should govern venue configuration. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][3])
* The exact value ceiling of a Super Jackpot is inherently state-dependent because it combines active Super scoring and, during KNIGHT Multiball, the balls-in-play multiplier. Avoid quoting a fixed jackpot amount.
