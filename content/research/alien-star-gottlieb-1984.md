## Identity and versions

**Alien Star** — Gottlieb/Mylstar, June 1984. System 80A solid-state game; 4-player, 2 flippers, 3 pop bumpers, one physical lock/saucer and **2-ball multiball**. Design: **John Trudeau**; art: **Larry Day**; sound: **Craig Beierwaltes**. Production is documented at **1,065 units**. [S2][S7] ([Pinball Serial Number Database][1])

Two game PROM revisions are documented: **689** and later **689A**. The known 689A changes are associated with production wiring/fusing changes; I found **no verified scoring-rule change** between them. [S2] ([Pinball Serial Number Database][1])

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Complete A-L-I-E-N** on the dangerous right-side standups to light the left capture saucer for multiball. [S1][S3] 
2. Before locking, shoot the **left orbit/spinner** until the multiball playfield multiplier is **5X**, rather than settling for 2X or 3X. [S3][S5] ([Silverball Rules][2])
3. Shoot the left saucer; the multiplier freezes and 2-ball multiball begins. [S1][S3] 
4. The scoring bomb: roll through the **right inlane**, which temporarily raises the spinner from 1K to **10K per spin**, then rip the spinner. At 5X multiball that becomes **50K per spin**; a clean rip can swing the game immediately. [S3] ([Silverball Rules][2])
5. **Observed tournament alternate:** in head-to-head play, skip the risky multiball build and repeatedly shatz the right inlane → spinner for 10K/spin single-ball scoring. [S3][S6] ([Silverball Rules][2])

## Core rules and persistence

**Confirmed rule:** ALIEN lights the capture hole. Each ALIEN completion also enables another of the four top-lane awards; pop-bumper hits rotate which award is currently selected. To collect one, both its award lamp and corresponding S-T-A-R lane must be lit. [S1][S4] 

**Confirmed rule:** spinner action selects the **2X / 3X / 5X** multiball multiplier; capturing the ball freezes that value. The multiplier operates during 2-ball multiball and ends when multiball becomes single-ball play. [S1][S3][S5] 

**Confirmed rule:** the right inlane temporarily lights the spinner at **10X its normal value**. Combined with 5X multiball, that produces 50K/spin. This is the dominant scoring leverage on the game. [S3] ([Silverball Rules][2])

**Persistence:** detailed rules documentation says ALIEN progress and a lit capture/lock carry ball-to-ball, while the upper award lamps do not. Technical PI-80 documentation adds an important qualification: ALIEN/capture persistence reflects normal **3-ball** behavior but not 5-ball behavior. [S3][S4][S8] ([Silverball Rules][2])

Bonus can reach 29K × 5X, but is normally secondary to lit-spinner scoring. [S3] ([Silverball Rules][2])

## Skill shots

**None found as a distinct rules feature.** The plunge enters the top S-T-A-R rollover area and lane selection can matter, but I found no reliable manufacturer/rules source documenting a separate normal/super/secret skill-shot award. Do not describe an ordinary STAR-lane plunge as a formal skill shot.

## Secondary features

**Extra Ball:** when the **Extra Ball** award is lit, scoring the corresponding **R** top rollover awards it. Because ALIEN completions add available upper awards, repeated ALIEN progress can eventually expose it. [S1][S3] 

**Special:** completing ALIEN and STAR can light Special; a lit outlane scores it. Whether that has tournament value depends on operator/event settings, so do not assume it scores useful points. [S1] 

No stock ball save, action-button control, mystery award, video mode, Magna-Save, kickback, or comparable player utility was verified.

## What to watch

* **CAPTURE WHEN FLASHING / left saucer lit:** ALIEN is complete; multiball is ready. [S1][S4] 
* **2X / 3X / 5X inserts beside the saucer:** tells you whether the player should lock now or risk another spinner shot. [S3][S4] ([Silverball Rules][2])
* **Right inlane → immediate left-orbit attempt:** the player is exploiting the short 10K/spin window. [S3][S5] ([Silverball Rules][2])
* **Ball captured, second ball plunged:** multiball has begun; now watch specifically for right-inlane-to-spinner combinations. [S3][S5] ([Silverball Rules][2])

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** favor the repeatable **right-inlane → spinner** single-ball strategy rather than exposing the ball to five ALIEN standups and a hazardous saucer eject. If multiball is already qualified, accepting 3X—or sometimes even 2X—can be preferable to dying while gambling for 5X. **Strategic inference**, consistent with documented match-play strategy. [S3][S6] ([Silverball Rules][2])

**Playing behind:** build ALIEN, insist on **5X**, start multiball, then manufacture right-inlane → spinner opportunities. A single strong 50K/spin rip can erase a large deficit. [S3][S5] ([Silverball Rules][2])

**Key recurring decision:** **lock now or shoot spinner again for 5X?** That risk/reward decision—and the alternate choice to ignore multiball entirely—is what most clearly distinguishes conservative from aggressive play. [S3] ([Silverball Rules][2])

## Danger zones

* **ALIEN standups:** direct hits can rebound violently toward the center or outlanes. [S3]
* **N target:** partially blocked by the top of the right slingshot; especially awkward. [S3]
* **Left saucer eject:** notorious for clipping the left sling and heading straight toward the left outlane. [S3][S5]
* **Pop-bumper exits:** orbit/top shots can return unpredictably through the pops. [S3]
* **Shatzing the right inlane:** too much energy can turn the scoring setup into an inlane-to-outlane drain. [S3] ([Silverball Rules][2])

## Trivia

* Alien Star was released in **June 1984** during Gottlieb's Mylstar/System 80A period; documented production is **1,065 machines**. [S2] ([Pinball Serial Number Database][1])
* It was designed by **John Trudeau**, later designer of *Creature from the Black Lagoon*, *Judge Dredd*, *The Flintstones* and others. [S7] ([Kineticist][3])
* **Larry Day** supplied the artwork; **Craig Beierwaltes** is credited for sound. [S7] ([Kineticist][3])
* Two ROMs, **689 and 689A**, are documented; 689A accompanied hardware/wiring revisions rather than any verified major rules rewrite. [S2] ([Pinball Serial Number Database][1])
* Alien Star has appeared at elite-level events including **PAPA** and the **2023 INDISC High Stakes** tournament, where it was used for the first-place tiebreaker. [S5][S9] ([Tilt Forums][4])

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Ball-to-ball persistence:** [S3] states ALIEN progress/lock carry over; [S8]'s technical game definition says that is normal for **3-ball** play but not 5-ball play. The venue configuration therefore matters.
* **Skill shot:** some informal player commentary describes aiming the non-lane-changeable top rollovers as a skill shot, but I found no sufficiently authoritative source establishing a distinct skill-shot rule or award. Treat as **unverified terminology**.
* **689 vs 689A:** two revisions are verified, but no competitively meaningful rule difference was found. The documented changes concern hardware/wiring. [S2]
