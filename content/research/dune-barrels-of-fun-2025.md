## Identity and versions

**Dune** — Barrels of Fun, 2025. Game design: **David Van Es and Travis Moseman**; rules: **Bowen Kerins and Phil Grimaldi**; software: **Eric Priepke**; artwork: Jonathan Bergeron; mechanics: Moseman. FAST Pinball platform, three flippers, six balls, four magnets, four physical ball locks, dual LCDs, and the signature rising **ball-eating sandworm**. [S4][S6]

- One production trim; no edition split affects gameplay. Barrels announced production as limited to **up to 1,000 units**. [S6]
- **Rules basis: `code` — v2026.05.13, released 2026-05-13.** This remains Barrels of Fun's current downloadable build; its changes are principally fixes/mechanical refinements rather than scoring changes. [S1]
- Two core multiballs: **Desert Power Multiball** and **Fedaykin Multiball**. Later DUNE mini-wizard modes also contain multiball phases. [S4]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Collect Thumpers and save them for leverage.** One/two/three Thumpers activated with the action button give **2x/3x/4x playfield scoring**; current strategy guidance favors spending them during Desert Power or Fedaykin Multiball. [S4][S5]
2. **Build Desert Power Multiball:** activate and make two orbit locks, inflate the jackpot in the Desert Power Field, then start at the Worm. Move jackpots outward for up to **5x**, collect three, then attack the captive ball and collect the Super—**2x if entered from the front**. [S4]
3. **Use the day/night cycle for progression:** Harvester Battle by day, Prophecy by night. Prophecy's midpoint is the key decision—cash accumulated Spice safely, or continue and risk it for a **5x final hurry-up plus a permanent perk**. [S4]
4. Current-code alternate: **pre-lock the Sand Trap before a Harvester Battle**, then bring **Fedaykin Multiball** into the battle; keep using the trap for add-a-balls and the upper-right ramp for jackpots. [S2]
5. Long game: build strong Desert Power/Fedaykin values before **Liberate Arrakis**, because that mini-wizard explicitly reuses your best values from both multiballs. [S4][S5]

## Core rules and persistence

The game cycles between **day (orange)** and **night (blue)**, normally after 200 qualifying switch hits. One Harvester Battle can be played per day and one Prophecy per night. Starting that cycle's mode accelerates time progression; at 80%, the clock can hold until the active mode finishes. [S4]

**Water and Spice are strategic resources.** Water is consumed as the timer during Harvester Battles; Spice is consumed at a Prophecy midpoint for **1M per Spice, 5M minimum**. Uncollected resources carry between balls by default, while **tilting wipes both Water and Spice to zero**. [S4]

A failed Harvester Battle does **not reset**: remaining Harvester health, soldiers and thopters persist to the next day cycle. [S4]

Prophecies cannot run during multiball. At their midpoint the player can cash out immediately or continue; successful completion turns the final opportunity into a 5x payout and earns a permanent game perk. [S4]

**DUNE letters** come from meaningful accomplishments—including destroying a Harvester, continuing a Prophecy, first Desert Power Super, first Fedaykin Jackpot and side features. Four letters qualify a mini-wizard. Tournament Mode removes selection among the first three DUNE modes and plays them in prescribed order. [S4]

End-of-ball bonus can matter on a long ball: each day/night transition that ball adds +50% bonus multiplier, up to **5x**. [S4] The final wizard **Voice From the Outer World** contains a verified **1B→500M hurry-up**, easily large enough to decide a match. [S4]

## Skill shots

- **Front Scoop:** selectable; 2.5M + 10 resources. If no flipper is used before collection, both become **5M + 20 resources**.
- **Sneak / Rear Scoop:** selectable; 5M + 5 resources, doubled to **10M + 10** with no flip.
- **Left Spinner:** selectable; 7.5M and lights all resource dots; **15M** if doubled. Resource type is Water or Spice according to current day/night state.
- **Tunnel secret:** direct plunge; **10M** and improves spinner resource generation for the rest of the ball.
- **Left-outlane secret:** direct plunge; **5M**, immediately loads a **Sand Snork** ball save and autoplun­ges another ball.
- **Center-spinner dribble / Side Ramp secrets:** direct plunges worth 2.5M / 5M respectively; verified but primarily point awards rather than major strategic setup. [S4]

No verified ball-number scaling applies; the useful choice changes primarily with **day/night resource state**.

## Secondary features

- **Sand Snork:** collect from the Fremkit drop bank, then load at the yellow left ramp. It saves a left-outlane drain; unused Snorks can be reloaded next ball and up to three may be stored by default. [S4]
- **Pain Box:** available from Ball 2 by default or via Bene Gesserit. A left-outlane drain is captured; hold the action button continuously while playing one-handed and make the left ramp to earn the ball back. [S4]
- **The Voice:** a right-outlane save can be earned from Bene Gesserit and permanently through completing *Survive*; these saves can stack for later balls. [S4]
- **Bene Gesserit:** purple left-ramp award at day/night transitions. Common awards normally shuffle; **Tournament Mode skips that shuffle**, reducing randomness. [S4]
- **Extra balls:** normally from the second Harvester victory and the Bene Gesserit rare pool. Once the configured EB limit is reached, further EBs score **25M**; I did not verify a universal Tournament-Mode EB conversion. [S4]

## What to watch

1. **Orange vs. blue cabinet lighting:** instantly identifies day/Harvester versus night/Prophecy context.
2. **Thumper inventory / flashing action button:** 2x–4x playfield scoring is available; expect the player to wait for a high-value feature before firing it. [S4][S5]
3. **Orange Desert Power jackpot arrows moving sideways:** the player is steering them toward the outer **5x** positions rather than merely shooting whatever is lit. [S4]
4. **Ball physically locked in the Sand Trap as daytime arrives:** strong evidence the player is preparing current-code Fedaykin Multiball inside the next Harvester Battle. [S2]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:**  
**Strategic inference:** prioritize Sand Snork/Pain Box protection, take a ready Desert Power Multiball, and **cash out Prophecy at the midpoint** rather than risking the Spice jackpot for the 5x finish. Avoid spending extra single-ball time maximizing a multiball build when the existing value is enough.

**Playing behind:**  
**Observed strategy:** bank three Thumpers for **4x playfield**, use them in Desert Power or Fedaykin, steer Desert Power jackpots to 5x and take the 2x Super. At night, the Prophecy **continue** choice trades the guaranteed Spice cashout for the 5x finish. If strong MB values are already established, Liberate Arrakis has the much larger ceiling. [S5]

**Key recurring decision:**  
**Prophecy cashout versus continue.** It is a literal risk/reward fork: bank all accumulated Spice now, or risk that value to pursue 5x and a permanent perk.

## Danger zones

- **Sand Trap front shot:** misses into the surrounding targets are repeatedly described as capable of going straight down the middle; the rear upper-flipper entry is safer but harder. **Observed physical behavior.** [S8]
- **Left outlane:** notably hungry on location machines, which is why Sand Snork and Pain Box are so strategically valuable. **Observed physical behavior.** [S8]
- **Scoop eject:** highly setup-sensitive; some machines kick toward the center rather than a clean flipper feed. [S8]
- **Tilt with resources banked:** a tilt destroys **all Water and Spice**, not merely the normal end-of-ball bonus. [S4]

## Trivia

- Dune was Barrels of Fun's **second production title**, following *Jim Henson's Labyrinth*. [S6]
- Its signature sandworm physically **rises, magnetically captures a ball, and “eats” it** before later releasing it. [S6]
- The game has **four magnets and four physical ball-lock locations**, unusually mechanism-heavy for a standard-width modern game. [S6]
- Barrels' **Horizon Atmospheric Lighting** turns the entire cabinet orange or blue to make the day/night rules state visually obvious. [S4][S6]
- The fourth and final DUNE wizard mode, **Voice From the Outer World**, was not added until the 2026-03-30 update, nearly a year after launch. [S3]
