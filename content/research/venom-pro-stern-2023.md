## Identity and versions

* **Venom (Pro)** — Stern Pinball, **2023**, SPIKE 2, LCD, two flippers. Lead design: **Brian Eddy**; rules/code: **Dwight Sullivan and Raymond Davidson**; mechanical engineering: **Tom Kopera and Kevin Kolodziej**; art: **Jeremy Packer / Zombie Yeti**; music by **Mark Tremonti**. Stern announced the game July 18, 2023. [S1][S3]  ([Stern Pinball][1])
* **Latest verified Pro code: V1.06.0, May 6, 2025.** The current maintained rulesheet is also based on 1.06. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][2])
* Pro has **two ramps** and fixed shot geometry. Premium/LE add transforming shot geometry, the physical Doppelganger target mechanism, **Million Plus Pops**, and Quick Doppelganger Multiball. All editions retain the two three-ball vertical containment locks, Carnage captive ball and Bell Tower. [S1][S4] 
* The two tournament-important multiballs are **Mayhem Multiball** and **Carnage Multiball**; they **can be stacked**. Toxin Team-Up and Klyntar Multiball are later wizard-level scoring opportunities. [S3][S5] ([Tilt Forums][3])

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Start Eddie Brock.** He begins with two Mayhem locks lit and scores more in Mayhem. Use the skill shot to **spot a mini-mode**, then finish Eddie's three mini-modes. **Observed strategy.** [S3][S5] ([Kineticist][4])
2. Change to **Flash Thompson**: he gives access to Sleeper and makes Bell Tower / playfield-X progression easier. Continue mini-modes and build toward a large Mayhem. **Observed strategy.** [S5] ([Kineticist][4])
3. After ball 2 is locked, **hold the action button to postpone Mayhem** and keep locking—up to six balls. Three balls in the **right** tank adds +1× playfield during Mayhem. [S2][S5] ([Stern Pinball][5])
4. During Mayhem's protection, hammer **Carnage**, start Carnage Multiball on the center ramp, then use **Spider-Sense** to accelerate both multiballs toward their Super Jackpots. **Observed strategy.** [S5] ([Kineticist][4])
5. Keep looping the center ramp → Bell Tower to bring **playfield scoring as high as 7×**. This multiplier is the major score-blowup mechanism. [S2][S3] ([Stern Pinball][5])

## Core rules and persistence

* Each host has three color-coded **mini-modes**. Each is essentially a two-shot combo: first shot untimed, second under a short timer. Completing mini-modes lights Mayhem locks and fills that host's Grid column. [S3][S5] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Mayhem locks:** first lock normally takes one completed mini-mode, second takes two, third and later three. Host choice modifies starting locks. At two locked balls the game attempts to start Mayhem; holding the action button defers it so the player can lock as many as six. [S2][S3] ([Stern Pinball][5])
* Left-tank locks bring **Agony → Lasher → 2× XP**. Right-tank locks bring **Riot → Phage → +1× playfield during Mayhem**. [S2] ([Stern Pinball][5])
* Mayhem begins with a single-ball **Quick Jackpot** sequence, then releases the locked balls. Complete all major jackpots → scoop Super Jackpot; later rounds require each shot twice for Double Jackpots, then three times for Triple Jackpots. A Super also defeats one boss brought into Mayhem. [S2][S3] ([Stern Pinball][5])
* **Carnage Multiball:** bash Carnage fully back into Ravencroft, then center ramp starts it. Re-lock Carnage during MB to light the scoop Super; other jackpots increase that Super. It stacks with Mayhem. [S2][S3] ([Stern Pinball][5])
* **Playfield X:** center-ramp loops light the Bell Tower; a lit Bell hit raises timed playfield scoring, up to **7×**. An unlit Bell hit extends the timer. Grid and right-tank Mayhem perks can raise the base multiplier. [S2][S3] ([Stern Pinball][5])
* **Web Combos** independently multiply the *next shot* up to **5×**; a 10-way combo reaches 5×. Do not confuse Shot X with the global playfield multiplier. [S2] ([Stern Pinball][5])
* Important lockout: while a **Battle is lit at the center ramp**, Mayhem locks cannot be lit/collected and Carnage modes cannot start. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* Competition Mode disables loading persistent Insider Connected progress, so tournament players normally begin with the four default hosts rather than unlocked Venomized hosts. [S3][S6] ([Tilt Forums][3])

## Skill shots

At each ball, choose among three host-specific skill shots. The strategically dominant choice is usually **Spot Mini-Mode**. A host change during a ball can award the same style of choice as a bonus shot. **Observed strategy.** [S2][S5] 

* **Eddie Brock:** top lanes = +2 Bonus X; **left loop/180 shot = Spot Mini-Mode**; right orbit = big points.
* **Flash Thompson:** top lanes = +2 Bonus X; scoop = Light Mystery; **right loop/orbit = Spot Mini-Mode**.
* **Gwen Stacy:** top lanes = +2 Bonus X; **left loop = Spot Mini-Mode**; right loop = Start Doppelganger.
* **Peter Parker:** top lanes = +2 Bonus X; **left loop = Spot Mini-Mode**; scoop = Light Mystery. [S2][S3] ([Stern Pinball][5])

No separate verified super/secret skill shot for the four tournament-default hosts.

## Secondary features

* **Spider-Sense:** once per ball, hold the yellow/red action button for >¾ second, then release; a random white shot becomes a hurry-up. Completing it advances the active feature dramatically—for example, lights Carnage Super, completes a Mayhem jackpot set, lights a Battle Special Attack or Toxin Super. [S2][S3] ([Stern Pinball][5])
* **Sleeper Team-Up:** selecting Flash as first host unlocks Sleeper; in multiball its action-button use gives an **Add-A-Ball**, or adds time during Battles. [S2][S3] ([Stern Pinball][5])
* **Scream:** actual flipper-controlled video mode; stop Infected approaching from either side. [S2] ([Stern Pinball][5])
* **Mystery:** scoop award after qualifying it; Competition Mode removes randomness and supplies a fixed award sequence. [S2] ([Stern Pinball][5])
* **Extra Ball:** defeating enough Infected lights an EB at the scoop; **Competition Install disables Extra Balls**. [S2][S6] ([Stern Pinball][5])
* Grid **Extra Ball Save** perk lengthens ball saves at ball start and during multiball. [S2] ([Stern Pinball][5])

## What to watch

* **Blue action button after lock 2:** player is deliberately refusing Mayhem to build a larger multiball.
* **Three balls in right containment tank:** upcoming Mayhem receives +1× base playfield scoring.
* **Bell Tower lit / multiplier timer running:** center scoring leverage is active; expect aggressive Bell refreshes.
* **Yellow/red action button:** Spider-Sense is ready; during a Mayhem/Carnage stack it can instantly push both toward Supers.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** Start a **smaller Mayhem** rather than risking several extra locks, use Eddie's favorable Mayhem scoring, and cash available Supers. Avoid unnecessary Carnage captive-ball hits outside ball save. **Strategic inference.**

**Playing behind:** Build toward **six-ball Mayhem**, prioritize three right-side locks for +1×, stack Carnage, and run Bell Tower X. A Spider-Sense completion during that stack can light both Super Jackpots. Deeper games can explode through **Rampage (~300M strong performance)** or Host Hurry-Up, where a clean sequence can reach hundreds of millions before playfield X. **Observed strategy.** [S5] ([Kineticist][4])

**Key recurring decision:** **Start Mayhem now or keep locking balls?** More balls add safety, bosses and perks, but every extra single-ball lock attempt risks losing the whole setup.

## Danger zones

* **Carnage captive ball:** tournament strategy explicitly calls the recovery dangerous; attack it preferentially during ball save/multiball. [S5] ([Kineticist][4])
* **Outer Infected-target rubbers:** sit directly above the outlanes; balls entering those areas often need an immediate nudge. [S5] ([Kineticist][4])
* **Containment-vessel releases:** balls can eject before the incoming lock reaches the vessel, creating sudden two-ball chaos. [S5] ([Kineticist][4])
* **Left-loop return:** competitive advice recommends nudging and attempting a right-flipper live catch rather than assuming a safe feed. [S5] ([Kineticist][4])
* **Bell Tower standup:** repeated PF-X attempts place a hard rebound directly in the busy center of this exceptionally fast layout.

## Trivia

* Venom marked **Brian Eddy's second Stern design** after his return to pinball; his Williams résumé includes *Attack from Mars*, *Medieval Madness* and *The Shadow*. [S5] ([Kineticist][6])
* The two vertical containment vessels each physically accommodate **three balls**, enabling six-ball Mayhem. [S1][S2] ([Stern Pinball][7])
* Host choice was designed to change not just rules but, on Premium/LE, the **physical routing of shots**. Pro keeps fixed geometry. [S4] 
* **Mark Tremonti** composed/performed original music for the game. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* A 2025 topper update added **Ghost Rider** and the optional **Symbiote Mania** wizard mode, but those persistent/unlocked features are normally outside tournament-regulation play. [S2][S7] ([Pinside][2])

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Latest software:** the latest public Pro build I could verify is **V1.06.0, May 6, 2025** [S2]. Stern was still discussing Venom development in 2025, but no later Pro package surfaced in current official/current archive searches through September 2026. ([Pinside][2])
* **Original Stern PDF versus current code:** Stern's downloadable rules PDF is labeled **0.90** [S2]; the maintained Tilt rulesheet is explicitly updated through **1.06** [S3]. Use Tilt for later rule refinements while retaining Stern as the primary baseline. ([Stern Pinball][5])
* **Skill-shot control wording changed:** the older Stern PDF says the left flipper cycles skill shots; current tournament documentation describes left-flipper host choice and right-flipper skill-shot selection. Follow the installed 1.06 UI on the actual game. [S2][S5] ([Stern Pinball][5])
* Physical ramp, loop, captive-ball and lock-release feeds are highly setup-sensitive.
