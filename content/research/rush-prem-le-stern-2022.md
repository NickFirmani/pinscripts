## Identity and versions

* **Rush — Premium/LE**, Stern, **2022**, SPIKE 2. Design: **John Borg**; software/rules: **Tim Sexton and Raymond Davidson**; mechanics: **Elliot Eismin**; art: **Michael Barnard**; animation: **Chuck Ernst and Paul Chamnankit**. [S1][S2] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Per catalog convention, **Premium and LE are gameplay-equivalent**. Both add the motorized/electromagnetic Time Machine, working Clockwork Angels clock, Dead End VUK/wireform, subway system for Far Cry locks, and instrument-bank secret lock for Freewill. LE adds collector treatments/Expression Lighting and was limited to **1,000**. Pro is physically different. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* **Rules basis: `code` — v1.19.0, released 2026-07-01.** v1.19 fixes record/mode qualification, lock and clock/ramp issues and makes Bastille Day decay about half as fast; it does not substantially rewrite the mature scoring structure. [S2] ([Pinside][2])
* Six core multiballs matter for progression: **Red Barchetta, Subdivisions, Fly By Night, Far Cry, Freewill, Headlong Flight**. Playing all six qualifies **Cygnus X-1 Book 1**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][3])

Research scope follows the supplied commentator-reference specification. 

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** put three setup shots into the **Time Machine** so the first multiball is one shot away, then qualify a song mode with Records. [S4][S5] ([JLP's Pinball Cards][4])
2. Ideally earn the song’s matching **Album** first. Start the song, then start a Time Machine Multiball underneath it. Holding the flashing **Weapon** button can then spot every mode shot or add a ball. [S3][S5] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][5])
3. Current strategy sources differ on the favorite song: PinballCards favors **Limelight / Spirit of Radio**; Kineticist favors **Tom Sawyer / Working Man** as easy multiball stacks. The common strategy is **song + multiball + matching Weapon**. [S4][S5] ([JLP's Pinball Cards][4])
4. Completing songs earns permanent **2× rainbow-record shot multipliers** and years; every ten years upgrades the Time Machine and makes later multiballs stronger/easier to reach. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][5])
5. **High-variance alternate:** build the Drum Clock to midnight and start **Headlong Flight during another multiball**—the one normal multiball explicitly allowed to stack with another. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][3])

## Core rules and persistence

Collect flashing colored Records to qualify a song at the Time Machine. Matching-colored shots become **2× during that song**; getting all six record shots to one color gives **3× all shots**. Extra records before mode start add 10% to mode scoring. Completing a song adds at least five years and gives a **rainbow record** that permanently makes one chosen shot 2× until 2112. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][5])

The first Time Machine Multiball takes **three qualifying Time Machine shots plus the start shot**. Every ten years permanently reduces later Time-Machine-MB requirements by one and increases their jackpot values. Some decade perks last only for that ball; the qualification reduction persists. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][5])

Modes may be running when a multiball starts, but **a new song cannot normally be started during multiball**. Most multiballs exclude one another; **Headlong Flight is the exception** and can be started during another MB. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][5])

The three Time Machine MBs have different cash-outs: **Red Barchetta** builds RPM toward bumper Supers, **Subdivisions** expands jackpots outward then returns to Time Machine for Super, and **Fly By Night** dims the playfield through jackpots/switches until a blue Super appears. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][5])

Albums come from unique **1-2-3 combos → Combo Jackpot at Time Machine**. A matching Album arms **The Weapon**: blue action button spots mode shots; green adds a ball during its associated multiball. If both are active, add-a-ball takes priority. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][5])

## Skill shots

All programmed skill shots require a **short plunge to the upper-right flipper**. Base value starts at 750K, rises 250K per success, and successful skill shots extend ball-save time. [S3][S5] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][5])

* **Side Loop:** 1× skill shot.
* **Side Ramp:** 2×.
* **Upper Scoop:** 3×.
* **Super Skill Shot:** Side Loop first, then Side Ramp or Upper Scoop; doubles the second award. [S3][S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][5])

**Observed strategy:** avoid the full plunge; it puts the ball into dangerous bumpers and gives up these upper-flipper awards. [S5] ([Kineticist][6])

## Secondary features

* **The Weapon:** hold Action for three seconds when blue/green; spots all lit song shots or adds a ball to the matching multiball. A quick red press instead changes the ramp diverter. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][5])
* **Roll the Bones Mystery:** complete the three inlanes, then left scoop. It prioritizes contextual awards such as MB add-a-ball. **Competition Mode uses an ordered deterministic award list.** [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][5])
* **Outlane save:** two RUSH-bank completions light one outlane; flippers move the lit side. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][5])
* **Extra Balls:** ramp progress, five Albums, or reaching 2062 can light one. Disabled EBs score **10M**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][5])
* **Video mode:** None found.

## What to watch

1. **Time Machine color:** red flashing = song ready; green = multiball ready; yellow = Combo Jackpot/Album ready. [S4] ([JLP's Pinball Cards][4])
2. **Blue/green Action button:** blue means a matching Album can sweep the mode shots; green means an **add-a-ball** is available. [S3]
3. **Rainbow Record / doubled shot:** a completed song has left permanent 2× value on that shot; repeated targeting may be multiplier-driven rather than objective-driven. [S3]
4. **Drum Clock at midnight:** Headlong Flight is ready at the right orbit and can be stacked into another multiball. [S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** **strategic inference:** take the straightforward song + first Time Machine MB stack, use the Weapon for protection, and cash accessible Supers rather than deliberately shooting the bumpers/right orbit to force Headlong Flight.

**Playing behind:** improve the stack before starting it—matching Album, boosted Records, persistent shot-X—and add **Headlong Flight** if midnight is already close. A well-executed **La Villa Strangiato** is also a genuine stand-alone match swinger because its 12-shot combo multiplies successive values. [S5] ([Kineticist][6])

**Key recurring decision:** **start the prepared multiball now, or keep setting up its song/Album first?** Starting early buys safety; waiting can make the Weapon and mode multiplier transform the same multiball into much larger scoring.

## Danger zones

* **Full plunge / pop bumpers:** explicitly called out by current expert strategy as dangerous; short-plunge instead. [S5] ([Kineticist][6])
* **Right orbit:** feeds those same bumpers; experts often ignore it unless a rule specifically requires it. [S5]
* **Steep right ramp:** essential Far Cry/Bastille shot, but a weak attempt comes back rapidly.
* **Upper-flipper side shots:** missing the Side Loop/Ramp/Scoop drops the ball into less-controlled upper-playfield traffic.
* **Left orbit:** a precise lane between the bumpers; inaccurate attempts can turn immediately into chaotic bumper action. [S5]

## Trivia

* Rush was unveiled on **January 4, 2022**, with **16 Rush songs** spanning much of the band’s career. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* **Geddy Lee and Alex Lifeson** recorded custom callouts; fellow Canadian musician **Ed Robertson** of Barenaked Ladies also recorded speech for the game. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Premium/LE’s motorized **Clockwork Angels Clock** is modeled after Neil Peart’s Time Machine Tour bass drum; reaching midnight triggers Headlong Flight. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Premium/LE add three major mechanisms absent from Pro: the **Far Cry subway**, **Dead End VUK/wireform**, and physical **Freewill secret lock**. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* The LE was limited to **1,000 units** and added Stern’s Expression Lighting System; its gameplay mechanisms match Premium. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
