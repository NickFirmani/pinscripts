## Identity and versions

* **Harlem Globetrotters On Tour** — Bally Manufacturing, **1979**, model **1161-E**, Bally **AS-2518-35** solid-state platform. Design by **Greg Kmiec**, art by **Greg Freres**. Archival production figure: **14,550 units**. [S1][S4]
* Three flippers, three spinners, four inline drops and the distinctive **right-outlane Free-Ball Gate**. **No multiball.** [S4]
* No named production software-revision history was found. A later unofficial **7-digit display conversion ROM** exists and should not automatically be assumed rules-identical to stock. [S6]
* Archival databases disagree on whether to date the game to **1978 or 1979**; Bally's manual is game #1161-E, while IPSND/Pinside place production/release in September 1979. “Bally, 1979” is safest for commentary. [S1][S4]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** attack the four **Free Throw inline drops** on the right. They simultaneously build Bonus X to **2× → 3× → 5×** and raise the **Fast Break spinner** to **100 → 200 → 1,000 → 2,000 per spin**. [S1][S2]
2. Consider hitting the far-right **Slam Dunk** target early to open the right-outlane gate; it trades a risky standup shot now for a potential drain save later. **Observed strategy.** [S2]
3. Once the drops are finished, switch priorities: repeatedly rip the **2K Fast Break spinner**. This is the dominant sustained scoring shot. [S2][S3]
4. Build base bonus toward **20K/30K/40K Super Bonus hold thresholds** if memory is enabled. A 49K base bonus at 5× is worth **245K at drain**. [S1][S2]
5. Alternate route: the **top GLOBE saucer** and inline drops spell G-L-O-B-E for **50K** completions; this becomes more attractive when GLOBE is set to sequential rather than scanning/random selection. [S1][S2]

## Core rules and persistence

* Base bonus runs from **1K to 49K**. The Free Throw drops add bonus and the last three award **2×, 3× and 5× Bonus X**. [S1]
* The Fast Break spinner grows with those same four drops; once maxed it scores **2K per spin**, making long spinner rips disproportionately valuable. [S1][S2]
* **Super Bonus memory is adjustment-sensitive.** With switch #15 liberal, reaching 20K, 30K or 40K preserves that threshold as the starting bonus on later balls; conservative mode does not. [S1]
* The inline drops and their multiplier progression **reset each new ball**. The Free Throw saucer behind them scores 25K on its first collection; depending on switch #8, it either leaves the drops down or resets them for another completion/Special opportunity. [S1]
* **G-L-O-B-E progress carries ball-to-ball.** The top saucer or Free Throw drops spot the currently lit letter; completing GLOBE scores **50K**, resets the letters, and advances its outlane/Special progression. [S1]
* GLOBE can use a **scanning** letter selection or the easier **one-at-a-time sequential** behavior. This materially changes the value of repeatedly shooting the top saucer. [S1]
* The five left **Players targets** advance the value available at Slam Dunk: Extra Ball → Special → 5K. Their progress itself can be configured to carry between balls. [S1]
* Tilt forfeits the entire end-of-ball bonus. [S1]

## Skill shots

**Top GLOBE Saucer Skill Shot** — plunge into the top-center saucer.

* Scores **3K**, advances bonus three steps, and spots the lit G-L-O-B-E letter. [S1][S3]
* Completing GLOBE is worth 50K, so this has substantially more strategic value than merely making a top rollover. [S1]
* With sequential GLOBE selection, repeated saucer plunges are especially effective; with scanning selection, timing is less deterministic. [S1][S2]
* Visible cue: watch the cycling G-L-O-B-E lamp at the saucer.

No separate verified super or secret skill shot.

## Secondary features

* **Free-Ball Gate:** when lit, hitting Slam Dunk opens the right-outlane gate. A ball entering the right outlane is diverted back to the right flipper and closes the gate. This is a true outlane rescue and a major survival resource. [S1]
* **Extra Ball:** completing all five Players targets advances Slam Dunk to Extra Ball. Original operator settings can instead use replay/novelty scoring, and tournament treatment therefore matters. [S1]
* **GLOBE/Special awards:** original DIP settings can substitute Extra Ball or fixed point values for Specials. [S1]
* **Center spinners:** normally score only 100, but every fifth spin advances bonus when active. They are useful near a Super Bonus threshold but generally inferior to the Fast Break spinner. [S1][S2]
* **Pop bumpers:** all are lit for 1K on the standard 3-ball setup; in 5-ball play their lit states alternate. [S1]
* No conventional timed ball save, video mode, mystery award, lane change, or action button.

## What to watch

1. **Inline drops remaining:** this simultaneously shows Bonus-X and Fast Break spinner progress; three drops down means the spinner has already reached **1K/spin**. [S1]
2. **2× / 3× / 5× Bonus lamps:** 5× plus substantial base bonus means a huge score is already sitting at end of ball.
3. **Open right-side gate:** the right outlane is temporarily survivable; once used, the gate closes. [S1]
4. **20K/30K/40K Super Bonus lamps:** if memory is enabled, crossing one of these thresholds protects that amount for subsequent balls. [S1]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** get the Free-Ball Gate open, establish at least **3×–5× bonus**, then prefer repeatable Fast Break spinner shots over continuing to attack dangerous standups. Reaching a Super Bonus hold threshold is particularly valuable if memory is enabled. **Observed strategy.** [S2][S3]

**Playing behind:** finish all four inline drops and aggressively rip the **2K spinner** while pushing base bonus toward 49K. A long ball can combine hundreds of thousands in spinner scoring with a **245K maximum end-of-ball bonus**. [S2]

**Key recurring decision:** **open the gate first or attack the inline drops immediately?** The gate costs a dangerous standup shot but protects the right outlane; the drops accelerate scoring much faster. Competitive guidance explicitly recognizes both approaches. [S2]

## Danger zones

1. **Inline-drop rebounds:** perhaps the game's defining danger; individual targets can fire the ball directly toward the center drain. [S2]
2. **Gap between the two left “scissor” flippers:** trapping with both raised can let the ball slip cleanly between them. Dead-bouncing toward the right flipper can be safer. [S3]
3. **Pop-bumper exits:** expert guidance identifies the upper bumpers as the most common source of uncontrolled drains. [S2]
4. **Soft center-spinner exits:** balls dribbling through the center spinners can angle toward the center; active nudging is often required. [S2]
5. **Right outlane with gate closed:** unlike a normal kickback, the gate must already be opened by Slam Dunk before the ball enters the outlane. [S1]

## Trivia

* Harlem Globetrotters On Tour was designed by **Greg Kmiec**, later designer of games including *Paragon*, *Xenon* and *Vector*. [S4]
* The artwork was an early Bally assignment for **Greg Freres**, who later became one of pinball's most prominent artists and art directors. [S4][S5]
* Bally produced approximately **14,550**, making it a comparatively high-volume late-1970s solid-state title. [S4]
* The game uses unusual **stacked/scissor left flippers**, creating both unique passing opportunities and a notorious gap drain. [S3]
* Original Bally code only has six-digit scoring; a later unofficial **seven-digit conversion** exists, appropriate for machines where skilled players frequently roll the score. [S6]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Year:** current Pinside/IPSND records date production to **September 1979** [S4], while OPDB/Silverball label the game 1978. Use **1979** unless venue documentation establishes otherwise.
* **Super Bonus memory is not universal.** The Bally manual explicitly makes it an operator adjustment, so commentary must not assume 20K/30K/40K persists. [S1]
* **GLOBE behavior is adjustment-dependent.** The manual distinguishes scanning versus one-at-a-time selection; this can materially alter the preferred right-flipper strategy. [S1]
* **Free Throw reset behavior is adjustment-dependent.** On one setting the drops stay down after the first saucer collect; on the other, the saucer resets them and requires another dangerous completion. [S1]
* A non-stock **7-digit conversion ROM** exists [S6]. I did not verify that every rules behavior is identical to the original six-digit code; identify it if installed.
* Physical inline-drop, spinner and bumper feeds are strongly copy-dependent.
