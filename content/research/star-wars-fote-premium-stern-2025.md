## Identity and versions

* **Star Wars: Fall of the Empire (Premium)** — Stern Pinball, released **September 12, 2025**; two flippers, LCD, and Stern’s new **SPIKE 3** generation. Lead design: **John Borg**; rules/software: **Raymond Davidson, Andrew Wilkening, Mike Vinikour**; mechanics include John Rotharmel; art includes Jeremy Packer/Zombie Yeti and Vincent Proce. [S1][S3]  ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Premium/LE add the **motorized AT-AT**, **motorized Hyperspace lift ramp**, and most importantly the player-controlled magnetic **Jedi Save** below the flippers. Premium also gets the exclusive **Mynock Multiball** rule through the lowered center ramp. [S1][S2] ([Stern Pinball][2])
* **Latest verified code: V0.97.0, July 1, 2026.** Stern’s release notes chiefly fix Ion Cannon 3× behavior, multiplayer Death Star lock state, mission bugs, and Death Star II logic. [S2] 
* Commentary-relevant multiballs: **Lightspeed, Battle of Hoth, Death Star, Death Star II**, and Premium-exclusive **Mynock**. Lightspeed + Hoth is the important legal multiball stack. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][3])

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Prepare Battle of Hoth and Lightspeed together.** Hit the Droids/light-lock target toward three Hoth locks while advancing the Falcon at the center ramp. **Observed strategy.** [S5] ([Kineticist][4])
2. **Start Lightspeed Escape before taking the third Hoth lock.** Then start Hoth; this is the notable multiball-on-multiball stack the game permits. [S4][S5] ([Tilt Forums][3])
3. Before multiball, play/complete useful **Rebel Missions** and make character combos. Missions give persistent **2×/3×/4× jackpot Force Boosts**; combos make those same shots count as 2 or 3 jackpots of progress during multiball. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][3])
4. Use the **Ion Cannons** to place 2× shot multipliers on likely cash-out shots—especially Luke, Droids, Death Star or center ramp. [S4][S5] ([Tilt Forums][3])
5. Alternate route: **Death Star Multiball** has an excellent standalone ceiling—15 jackpots, then build a timed Super Jackpot up to 3× before collecting it. [S4]

## Core rules and persistence

* **Rebel Missions:** spell FORCE to light missions at the eight character shots. Missions get harder as more are played; completing one gives that character a permanent **3× multiball jackpot Force Boost**, while merely playing it gives 2×. Completing the later Bonus Mission upgrades that character to **4×**. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Rebel Combos:** two-shot natural combos reset each ball. One combo at a character makes its multiball jackpot progress count **2×**; two combos make it count **3×** and start that character’s Super Mode. [S4]
* **Battle of Hoth:** Droids target lights locks at Luke. During MB, jackpots build a Letter Award at the AT-AT; spell AT-AT, then shoot Luke for the Super. Competition Install removes the post-multiball 10-second “last chance” Super. [S4][S6] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Death Star:** open the Death Star, lock three. Fifteen jackpots qualify the Super phase; during its 30-second build window, 3 additional jackpots make the Super 2× and 7 make it 3×. Super progress survives failed Death Star MB attempts. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Ion Cannon multiplier:** bumper hits charge a 2× multiplier; make a pulsing shot and that shot stays 2× for the **rest of the ball**. Uncollected pulsing opportunities carry between balls. After all major shots have been multiplied, a **roving 3×** becomes available. V0.97 fixed its interaction with existing 2× shots. [S2][S4] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* Easy commentator trap: **Force Boost, Rebel Assistance and Ion Cannon Shot X are three separate systems**—one changes jackpot score, one changes jackpot qualification progress, and one multiplies the actual shot.

## Skill shots

* **Regular Skill Shot:** after the plunge, hit any flashing major shot within **6 switch hits**. Base award starts at **1M and rises 1M per successful skill shot**. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Super Skill Shot:** make it within the first **3 switches** for **2×** the regular award. [S2][S4]
* Each shot also gives a utility perk. Tournament-relevant choices include:

  * **Droids:** spots one Hoth lock.
  * **Death Star:** spots one Death Star lock.
  * **Center ramp:** +2 Falcon advances.
  * **Luke:** +3 FORCE letters.
  * **Left orbit:** +2 JEDI letters.
  * **Right ramp:** +3 TIE Fighters. [S2][S4] 
* Those perks will not illegally start a multiball or mode; if unavailable, the game substitutes scoring. [S2]

## Secondary features

* **Jedi Save — Premium/LE:** spinner hits spell JEDI. With partial progress, the action button can pulse the under-flipper magnet to steer an outlane ball; with JEDI complete, the magnet can **grab and fling the ball back into play**. Multiple button presses can cancel the full save, so timing matters. [S4][S5] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Multiball Add-A-Ball:** 10 Sarlacc spins during multiball lights the action button for one Add-A-Ball; subsequent activations require +10 spins. [S4]
* **Dark Side Mystery:** return lanes light the Vader scoop. Awards include mission/MB progress, JEDI, Ion Cannon charge, Stormtrooper hurry-up and Extra Ball. On Premium/LE, the **third eligible Mystery starts Mynock Multiball**. [S4]
* **Mynock Multiball:** lowered center-ramp shots score 10M; ten shots light Extra Ball. Premium/LE only. [S4]
* **Extra Balls:** multiple routes exist; when no more can be awarded, compensation is **25M**, and that value can be multiplied by Ion Cannon Shot X. Competition Install disables Extra Balls. [S4][S6] ([Tilt Forums][3])

## What to watch

* **Third Hoth lock lit + Falcon/Lightspeed ready:** expect the player to start Lightspeed first and deliberately create the legal Hoth stack.
* **Character portrait/name inserts:** portrait = Force Boost to jackpot value; name = Rebel Assistance to jackpot progress. This explains why one jackpot may suddenly be worth/count far more.
* **Pulsing Rebel Alliance shot icon:** next make locks a **2× Shot X** there for the rest of the ball.
* **JEDI letters / glowing action button:** on Premium, an apparent outlane drain may become a player-controlled magnetic save.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** Take Hoth or Lightspeed once one is safely prepared; don't spend extra single-ball time optimizing every character multiplier. Keep JEDI charged and use the relatively controllable orbit feeds. **Strategic inference.**

**Playing behind:** Prepare **Lightspeed + Hoth**, build character Force Boosts/combos first, and place Ion Shot X on Luke/Droids/center ramp. Death Star's 2×/3× Super build is another excellent high-variance comeback path. **Observed strategy.** [S5]

**Key recurring decision:** **Cash the ready multiball now, or keep building permanent jackpot leverage first?** Mission completion and combos can massively strengthen the eventual multiball, but every extra single-ball setup shot risks losing the ball.

## Danger zones

* **Death Star:** expert strategy specifically calls it a severe drain-risk shot; controlled right-flipper attempts are preferred. [S5]
* **Vader scoop eject:** fast return toward the right flipper; some copies allow a drop catch, others demand an immediate bailout flip. [S5]
* **Ion Cannon bumpers:** positioned beside the slings/outlanes; useful for Shot X but physically hazardous. [S5]
* **Sarlacc exit:** a leftward nudge is recommended on many copies to obtain a controlled catch. [S5]
* **Premium moving center ramp/AT-AT:** mechanical timing can change the return compared with Pro; verify the actual copy before calling a feed predictable.

## Trivia

* Fall of the Empire brought **John Borg back to Star Wars more than 30 years after his 1992 Data East *Star Wars*, his first pinball design**. [S3][S5] ([Kineticist][5])
* It was Stern's first game built on the **SPIKE 3** generation, with a larger **18.5-inch HD display** and upgraded audio architecture. [S3] ([Primetime Amusements][6])
* Premium's AT-AT is genuinely **motorized** and works with a spiral loop to recreate the tow-cable takedown. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][7])
* C-3PO callouts were recorded by **Chris Bartlett**, who has portrayed the character in modern Star Wars productions. [S1]
* V0.96 added **Death Star II Multiball** in April 2026; V0.97 followed in July primarily as a rules/bug-fix release. [S2]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Latest software listings conflict:** Pinside's machine page still displays **V0.96**, but Stern's own July 1, 2026 release notes explicitly document **V0.97**, and the current maintained rulesheet is based on 0.97. Use **0.97**. [S2][S4] ([Pinside][8])
* **Skill-shot scoring:** the current rulesheet gives a base starting at 1M and rising 1M per success [S4], while the April 2026 Kineticist guide summarizes it as 3M/6M [S5]. The structural rule—regular within six switches, Super within three, plus shot-specific perks—is verified; exact displayed value should follow installed code.
* **Competition Install** specifically raises Hoth difficulty, sets Jedi Save difficulty to Medium and disables Extra Balls. [S6]
* Premium magnet behavior and Vader/Sarlacc feeds are strongly physical-copy dependent.
