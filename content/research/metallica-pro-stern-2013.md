## Identity and versions

* **Metallica (Pro)** — Stern Pinball, April 2013; **S.A.M. system**, DMD, 2 flippers, 2 ramps. Scope is the original 2013 Pro requested in the supplied brief.  [S2] ([Pinside][1])
* Design: **John Borg**; software/rules: **Lyman F. Sheats Jr.**; art: **Donny Gillies (“Dirty Donny”)**; music/sound: Vince Pontarelli. [S2]
* Pro-specific hardware: fixed illuminated Grave Marker, non-animated Snake jaw, **virtual Coffin locks**, and no Premium/LE ball-smashing hammer or under-trough physical three-ball lock. A later **Pro LED** uses LEDs/metal backbox but no material rules difference was verified. [S1][S2] 
* **Latest verified original-game code: V1.80.0, November 16, 2018.** Later versions substantially lowered default Crank It Up requirements to **12 of each item** and completed the deep ruleset. [S2] ([Pinside][2])
* Four main multiballs: **Electric Chair, Grave Marker, Snake, and Coffin**. Coffin is the unusual one that can bring another multiball in with it. [S3]
* Do **not** confuse this with **Metallica Remastered (2024)**, a newer SPIKE 2 game with significantly expanded/rebalanced rules. [S6]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Start toward Electric Chair or Grave Marker Multiball while deliberately using the Spider.** Move the Spider to a useful ramp/orbit before hitting Sparky, Grave Marker or Snake; that shot then starts awarding extra copies of that item. **Observed strategy.** [S3][S4] ([Kineticist][3])
2. During multiball, **finish the four Pick targets**, then shoot Snake: first time adds a ball, gives roughly 10 seconds of save, and starts **20 seconds of 2× playfield**. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][4])
3. If practical, spell **FUEL** and hit the Dead End for another 20-second 2×. During multiball the two multipliers combine for **4× scoring**. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][4])
4. Keep farming items—especially Coffins—until **12 Electric Chairs + 12 Grave Markers + 12 Snakes + 12 Coffins** lights **Crank It Up** at the scoop. On Pro, **For Whom the Bell Tolls** is the preferred tournament mode. [S3][S4] ([Tilt Forums][4])
5. In Crank It Up, build the mode jackpot, then decide whether to **escape/cash at the scoop** or continue. Ideally cash while FUEL 2× is active. **Observed strategy.** [S4] ([Kineticist][3])

## Core rules and persistence

* The four “items”—**Electric Chair, Grave Marker, Snake, Coffin**—drive both multiballs and Crank It Up. The first Crank It Up needs 12 of each on current default code; later ones require incrementally more. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][2])
* The **Spider** sits on a major shot. Hit Sparky, Snake or Grave Marker and the corresponding item insert lights at the Spider shot; subsequent makes there collect that item. These temporary inserts normally last for the ball. Completing all three at one shot qualifies a Coffin Hurry-Up; collecting that hurry-up permanently locks those three inserts at that shot across balls. [S3][S4] ([Kineticist][3])
* **Electric Chair MB** progress persists and normally starts after repeated Sparky hits. **Grave Marker MB** is faster to qualify—clear the three inline drops then hit the marker—but that qualification sequence resets if the ball drains. This explains the common tournament choice between the two. [S3][S4] ([Tilt Forums][4])
* **Coffin MB:** captive-ball hits qualify virtual locks on Pro: **5 hits for lock 1, then 10 and 10**. By default, locks cannot be collected during another multiball, though qualification progress can continue. Once started, Coffin MB can bring one other major multiball into play. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][4])
* Most ordinary multiballs are mutually exclusive; **side modes** such as Lady Justice, FUEL and Seek & Destroy can run underneath multiballs and Crank It Up. [S3]
* Crank It Up is fundamentally a **single-ball risk/reward mode**. Hit all four physical features to light the scoop for **collect or continue**. Continuing lets the jackpot grow but risks losing the uncaptured value on a drain. [S3][S4] ([Tilt Forums][4])
* End-of-ball bonus uses accumulated items × Bonus X from the inline drops; it can become significant on very long games. [S3]

## Skill shots

* **Normal Skill Shot:** plunge the flashing top rollover. Base is **200K**, +50K per prior successful skill shot; the **right lane scores 2×**. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][4])
* **Super Skill Shot:** hold the **left flipper** during plunge to disable the orbit post, then shoot a major feature. General award is roughly 2× the normal skill-shot value, but several shots have utility bonuses:

  * **Dead End** → immediately qualifies FUEL 2×.
  * **Scoop** → Mystery.
  * flashing major shot → instantly qualifies that shot's Coffin Hurry-Up; in Competition Mode this is deterministically the **left orbit**.
  * top rollover → 2× normal skill-shot award, thus 4× on the right lane. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][4])

## Secondary features

* **Snake multiball save/add-a-ball:** during any multiball, complete all four Pick targets and shoot Snake. First collection adds a ball, starts about **10 seconds of ball save**, and activates 20 seconds of 2× playfield. [S3]
* **FUEL 2×:** each FUEL completion lights the dangerous far-left Dead End for **20 seconds of double playfield**; combines with Snake 2× for 4×. [S3]
* **Mystery:** complete all five top/inlane rollovers, with flippers changing lanes. Repeated completions improve Mystery level; awards include points, items, Snake letters, METALLICA letters and Bonus X. During Crank It Up, Mystery instead spots useful mode progress. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][4])
* **Extra Ball:** lit at scoop from captive-ball thresholds, combos, or Blown Pistons. V1.80 default first captive-ball threshold is **10 hits**. If the EB cap has already been reached, another EB scores **2.5M**; tournament treatment of disabled EBs should be confirmed. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][2])
* No video mode or player action button was verified.

## What to watch

* **Four item lamps above the flippers:** all four pulsing means Crank It Up is ready.
* **Spider arrow + item inserts at a ramp/orbit:** reveals exactly why a player is repeating that shot—they are farming Crank It Up items, not merely scoring the ramp.
* **Snake flashing during multiball:** Pick targets are complete; expect Add-A-Ball + temporary 2× immediately.
* **Crank It Up scoop flashing after the four physical features:** this is the **cash-or-continue decision**, often the largest strategic moment of the ball.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** Favor the multiball route that is already closest—usually Electric Chair or Grave Marker—and use the safety to accumulate items. Take a healthy Crank It Up escape instead of insisting on completion or extra FUEL setup. **Strategic inference.**

**Playing behind:** Optimize Spider placement, build Crank It Up, choose **For Whom the Bell Tolls**, and delay the escape long enough to build a very large mode jackpot. Turning on FUEL 2× before the collect can create a massive comeback. A rarer high-variance alternative is Coffin MB + another multiball + side modes. **Observed strategy.** [S3][S4] ([Kineticist][3])

**Key recurring decision:** **Take the score now, or keep preparing leverage?** Metallica repeatedly asks whether another risky item, multiplier or stack is worth exposing a controlled single ball before the payoff.

## Danger zones

* **Left inlane/outlane complex:** exceptionally hungry; ricochets off the double-inlane rail/slings can hop directly out. [S4]
* **FUEL/Dead End:** strategically valuable but specifically called out by competitive guides as a left-outlane danger. [S4]
* **Sparky:** central funnel makes direct Electric Chair hits among the game's most dangerous shots. [S4]
* **Grave Marker/drop bank:** safer as a left-flipper backhand; frontal attempts can rebound center. [S4]
* **Coffin captive ball:** repeated hits are mandatory for Crank It Up/Coffin MB, so an apparently small standup rebound can end a high-value setup.

## Trivia

* Metallica was designed by **John Borg** with rules by **Lyman Sheats**, whose long post-release development transformed the game considerably after launch. [S2] ([Kineticist][5])
* Stern released the original game with multiple price tiers; Premium/LE have a moving Grave Marker, animated Snake and ball-smashing hammer that the Pro lacks. [S1]
* A later **Pro LED** appeared in 2014 with LED lighting and a metal backbox while retaining the Pro layout/rules. [S2][S4]
* **Brendon Small** of *Metalocalypse* voices Sparky and the Snake; James Hetfield and Lars Ulrich also supplied custom speech. [S2] ([Pinside][6])
* The final **V1.80** arrived more than five years after the machine's 2013 release. [S2]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **“Coffin” versus “Casket” Multiball:** community rulesheets often call it **Casket Multiball** [S3], while Stern's later code notes explicitly removed “casket” text from the game and use **Coffin Multiball**. [S2] For commentary, “Coffin Multiball” best matches current stock code. ([Pinside][2])
* Early rulesheets list **15 items each** for Crank It Up; later code changed the factory default to **12 each**. [S2] A hard tournament setup can therefore look different from the current default.
* Competition Mode makes at least some randomness deterministic—for example the Super Skill Shot hurry-up always selects left orbit—but I did not verify a complete V1.80 list of Competition-preset changes. [S2][S3]
* Pro and Pro LED have physical presentation differences, but no gameplay-rule difference was verified.
* Left-outlane, Sparky, Coffin and eject behavior are highly setup-dependent.
