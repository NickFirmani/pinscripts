## Identity and versions

* **Rush (Pro)** — Stern Pinball, January 2022; **SPIKE 2**, LCD, three flippers. Scope is the Pro requested in the supplied brief. 
* Design: **John Borg**; code/rules: **Tim Sexton and Raymond Davidson**; art: **Michael Barnard**; animation: Chuck Ernst and Paul Chamnankit; sound credited in current archives to Bob Baffy. [S1][S4] ([Kineticist][1])
* Pro differs materially from Premium/LE: its **Time Machine entrance is stationary**, Dead End is a standup rather than a VUK, and Freewill uses software/add-a-ball behavior rather than the Premium/LE physical hidden lock. Premium/LE additionally have the motorized Clockwork Angels clock and physical Far Cry lock hardware. [S1][S2] ([Kineticist][2])
* **Latest verified Pro code: V1.18.0, November 26, 2024.** V1.18 itself is essentially system/support work; current tournament-relevant changes include the modern Competition Install and later completed wizard-rule set. [S3] ([Pinside][3])
* Six principal multiballs: **Red Barchetta, Subdivisions, Fly By Night, Far Cry, Freewill, Headlong Flight**. Headlong Flight is the unusual one that can stack with another multiball. [S2][S4] ([Kineticist][4])

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Prepare a Song Mode and Time Machine Multiball together.** Collect colored records to qualify a useful song—commonly **Tom Sawyer or Working Man**—while bashing the center Time Machine toward multiball. **Observed strategy.** [S4] ([Kineticist][4])
2. **Start the Song first, then the Time Machine multiball.** A Time Machine MB can start during a Song, but starting the MB first prevents a Song from starting. [S2] 
3. Build **1-2-3 combos → Album → The Weapon**. If the Album matches the running Song, the button spots every lit mode shot; if it matches the running multiball, it **adds a ball**. This is the game's biggest practical stack enhancer. [S2][S4] 
4. Complete Songs and multiball goals to collect **years**. Each 10-year Time Machine upgrade improves later play; 10 years already adds a ball and 10 seconds of save to Time Machine MBs. [S2] 
5. Alternate swing strategy: **La Villa Strangiato** as a precision single-ball mode, where chaining its 12-shot sequence at speed creates escalating combo multipliers. **Observed strategy.** [S4] ([Kineticist][4])

## Core rules and persistence

* **Song Modes:** collect flashing colored records; the dominant color determines which Song starts at the Time Machine. More matching records boost the mode. Matching shots are **2× mode scoring**, and locking all six to the same color/rainbow can make all six **3×**. Modes are untimed and end on completion or drain. [S2] 
* Current competitive advice strongly favors **Tom Sawyer / Working Man inside multiball** because their many required shots become much easier to finish. [S4] ([Kineticist][4])
* **Time Machine Multiballs:** bash the center enough times; Dead End changes which of Red Barchetta / Subdivisions / Fly By Night will start. Early Time Machine MBs are generally two-ball until year upgrades add balls and save time. [S2][S4] 
* **Far Cry:** qualify locks via the two green targets beside the right ramp plus the ramp; two locks start MB. Jackpots carry timed multipliers from **1× through 5×**; 15 jackpots light the Time Machine Super, which begins **One Little Victory** victory laps. [S2] 
* **Freewill:** collect all three instruments. During MB, chosen jackpot shots become Supers; on Pro, repeated instrument-lane shots add a ball and trigger **20 seconds of 2× jackpots**. [S2] 
* **Headlong Flight:** advance the Drum Clock to midnight. It can stack with another multiball and features 3× Super Jackpot combos plus 2×/4× Mega awards. [S2] 
* Several values normally reset each ball, but ramp **Hold Awards** can preserve spinner, combo, pop, ramp, instrument, record or Bonus-X progress. [S2] 

## Skill shots

* **Regular Skill Shot:** short-plunge to the upper flipper; side loop = **1×**, side ramp = **2×**, side scoop = **3×**. Each successful skill shot adds ball-save time. [S2] 
* **Super Skill Shot:** side loop followed quickly by side ramp or side scoop; scores **2× the corresponding regular value** and doubles the added ball-save time. [S2]
* **Observed tournament choice:** avoid a full plunge. The pops can produce immediate uncontrolled danger; short-plunge possession plus extra save time is considerably more valuable. [S4] ([Kineticist][4])

## Secondary features

* **The Weapon / action button:** Albums come from completed 1-2-3 combos. Matching Song → hold button to score all lit shots; matching multiball → **Add-A-Ball**. A quick press otherwise controls the ramp-return diverter. [S2] 
* **Roll the Bones Mystery:** complete all three return lanes; collect at scoop. During multiball, the first eligible Mystery is always **Add-A-Ball**. Competition play gives the remaining awards in a fixed sequence. [S2] 
* **Outlane saves:** repeated RUSH-bank completions light a movable left/right outlane save; flippers choose the side. [S2]
* **Extra Balls:** ramp count, five Albums, a Time Machine upgrade and Mystery can qualify them. Current **Competition Install sets Extra Ball Limit to No Extra Balls**. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][3])
* Competition Install also limits randomness/difficulty and currently sets **Freewill MB Easy, Freewill AAB Hard, maximum two wildcard records**, among other settings. [S3] ([Pinside][3])

## What to watch

* **Red Time Machine lamp flashing:** Song Mode is qualified; if green/multiball progress is also nearly ready, expect the player to start Song first.
* **Action button flashing blue/green:** blue = Weapon can sweep Song shots; green = Weapon can add a ball to the active multiball. [S2]
* **Colored record arrows:** reveal the intended Song and its shot multipliers; a board heavily concentrated in one color means the player has deliberately boosted that mode.
* **Time Machine year total at 10/30/60/90:** those thresholds add balls/save time to major multiballs, so later multiballs can be dramatically stronger than the first. [S2]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** Start the prepared Song + Time Machine MB rather than overbuilding Albums or years. Use Mystery for Add-A-Ball and prioritize straightforward mode completion. **Strategic inference.**

**Playing behind:** Prepare the **matching Album** before starting Song + multiball, so The Weapon can either sweep mode shots or prolong multiball. Far Cry → Super → One Little Victory and a stacked Headlong Flight offer very high variance. La Villa Strangiato is another large single-ball comeback route. **Observed strategy.** [S4] ([Kineticist][4])

**Key recurring decision:** **Start the ready stack now, or spend more single-ball shots preparing Albums/records/years first?** Rush increasingly rewards preparation, but its dangerous pops and tight upper-flipper shots punish greed.

## Danger zones

* **Full plunge into pops:** specifically discouraged by competitive strategy; the pop exits can immediately find an outlane or center drain. [S4]
* **Right orbit:** generally avoided unless rules require it because it sends the ball into the dangerous pop area. [S4]
* **Dead End on Pro:** unlike Premium/LE's VUK, this is a standup; direct hits can rebound immediately downfield. [S1]
* **Right-ramp reject:** a steep shot and major Far Cry bottleneck; weak attempts return fast.
* **Upper-flipper side shots:** valuable for skill shots and combos, but missing them relinquishes controlled upper-flipper possession.

## Trivia

* Rush was designed by **John Borg** and shares obvious layout DNA with his *TRON: Legacy*, though its rules and shot purposes differ substantially. [S4]
* **Geddy Lee and Alex Lifeson** recorded custom speech; Barenaked Ladies' **Ed Robertson** also supplied callouts and helped coach the recording sessions. [S1][S3] ([Pinside][3])
* The machine includes **16 Rush songs**, including both books of *Cygnus X-1* and *2112*. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][5])
* Stern tied the game's launch to support for the **Neil Peart Research Award** through the Glioblastoma Foundation. [S1]
* The Pro keeps virtually the same deep rules architecture while replacing several Premium mechanisms with simpler software/standup equivalents. [S1][S2]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Rulesheet revision:** Stern's downloadable detailed rulesheet [S2] predates final V1.18, while the maintained Tilt rulesheet currently describes **V1.14**. Later V1.17/V1.18 release notes mostly add wizard/competition/system changes rather than rewriting the fundamental Song, Album, Weapon or multiball structures. [S3]
* **Mode year threshold:** older factory documentation says the extra five years require a **100M** completed Song; current competitive documentation says **80M**, reflecting later code. Use current-code **80M** if printed. [S3][S4] ([Kineticist][4])
* **Shot multiplier cap:** later code added an adjustable maximum; Competition Install has been documented at **3×**, so a standard-location game may allow stronger multiplier states than a tournament install. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][6])
* Pro/Premium physical feeds differ enough that strategy reports mentioning the Dead End VUK, physical Freewill lock or Clock mechanism should **not** be copied directly onto the Pro page.
