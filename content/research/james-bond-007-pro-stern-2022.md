## Identity and versions

* **James Bond 007 (Pro)** — Stern Pinball, 2022. Lead design **George Gomez**; rules/code **Lonnie Ropp and Mike Vinikour**; art **Kevin O’Connor**; sound **Ken Hale and Jerry Thompson**. The cornerstone game covers the six Sean Connery Bond films from *Dr. No* through *Diamonds Are Forever*. [S1][S3]
* Latest verified code: **1.06, July 1, 2026**. The detailed community rulesheet identifies itself as based on **1.01**; Kineticist’s strategy guide was updated April 29, 2026 and says it reflects current code. [S4][S5]
* Important Pro differences: **Bird One locks are virtual** rather than physical; Pro has no moving magnetic Jetpack and uses a **roving Super Jackpot shot** in Jetpack Multiball. Premium/LE also have Swipe-a-Ball; Pro does not. [S3]
* Main competitive multiballs: **Jetpack Multiball**, **Bird One Multiball**, and the **Bond… James Bond** 2-ball mini-wizard mode. Four Q Branch modes can also become 2-ball multiballs. [S3]
* Do not confuse this with the mechanically and rules-wise separate **James Bond 007 60th Anniversary** machine.
* Scope follows the supplied commentator-reference specification.

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** qualify **Jetpack Multiball** at the left eject, start a Villain mode at the right ramp, then start Jetpack at the left ramp so the mode runs during multiball. [S4]
2. In Jetpack, hit the **center loop immediately** to lock the jackpot value, collect five jackpots, then left ramp for Double Jackpot and the Pro's roving **Super Jackpot + add-a-ball**. [S3]
3. Meanwhile build **Bond Women**, collect them at the upper loop, and activate **007 Scoring** with the action button during a lucrative multiball. It can reach **7× playfield scoring**. [S3]
4. Next build **Bird One Multiball** and ideally bring a Henchman mode into it. Starting one Villain, one Henchman, Jetpack, and Bird One qualifies **Bond… James Bond**. [S3][S4]
5. Alternate high-value route: Q Branch **Power Pack Multiball** or a heavily built Bird One can score enormously, especially under 007 Scoring. [S4]

## Core rules and persistence

* Villain modes start from the **right ramp**; Henchman modes from the upper-flipper **side ramp**. Normal Villain/Henchman play can be brought into Jetpack or Bird One, which is central to tournament strategy. [S3][S4]
* **Jetpack:** left eject lights left ramp. At multiball start, center loop locks in a countdown jackpot value. Five jackpots → left ramp Double Jackpot → Pro-only roving Super Jackpot, initially **10M**, which also adds a ball the first time. [S3]
* **Bird One:** complete the three Osato drops to light a virtual lock at the center loop; three locks start multiball. Later locks each require another bank completion. Three quick upper-flipper Double Jackpots at multiball start earn a **one-time 2-ball multiball extension**. [S3]
* Bird One jackpots progress through **single → double → triple** waves. Rocket Super Jackpot eventually caps at **300M base** and is itself multiplied by 007 Scoring. [S3]
* **Bond Women:** various playfield tasks light women at the upper loop. Collected women become a timed playfield multiplier: one produces 2×; additional women add +1× and time, up through **7× for 70 seconds**. Starting 7× also grants a 30-second ball save. [S3]
* Qualified-but-uncollected women generally **carry between balls**; once collected, draining usually loses them unless special film progression protects them. [S3]
* **SPECTRE Weapon countdowns and Q Branch 2-ball multiballs cannot stack with other multiballs.** This is easy to misread when the player deliberately delays one feature. [S3]
* **Bond… James Bond:** start Villain + Henchman + both main multiballs. After the current feature ends, left eject/right ramp lights for a 70-second 2-ball mode. Complete ten lit shots, then side ramp Super Jackpot, capped at 100M base. [S3]

## Skill shots

* **Classified:** plunge the naturally flashing MI6 top lane without moving it — points + **+1 Bonus X**.
* **MI6:** use flippers to move the flashing top lane, then plunge it — smaller points + **+1 Bonus X**.
* Missile-paying Super Skill Shots, normally worth 2.5M-plus and **one Smart Missile** the first unique completion: **Undercover** (right ramp), **Double “O”** (side ramp), **Licensed to Kill** (left eject), **Eyes Only** (DB5 eject), and **Top Secret** (upper-flipper bumper lane).
* **Commander Bond:** medium plunge, then left ramp; points but no missile.
* **MXV Super Skill Shot:** right orbit → upper loop → side ramp after the plunge; awards **two Smart Missiles**. [S3]

For tournament play, the left-eject or side-ramp super skill shots are especially attractive because they also advance Jetpack/Henchman setup. **Observed strategy.** [S4]

## Secondary features

* **Smart Missiles:** blue action button automatically collects a lit mode shot, prioritizing harder shots; up to four can be stored. If a Super Jackpot is lit, missiles are disabled. Flippers select between Smart Missile and 007 Scoring when both are available. [S3]
* **Outlane save:** five Dragon Tank hits light a save that alternates between outlanes with slingshot hits. Additional tank hits can queue another. Strategically useful, but the target itself is dangerous. [S3][S4]
* **Extra Ball:** normally collected at the left eject. If disabled, the game awards **25M instead**, and that 25M is multiplied by 007 Scoring—potentially **175M at 7×**. [S3]
* **High Stakes:** every second B-O-N-D return-lane completion lights a random award at the left eject. Competition Mode is intended to normalize random/global behavior, but I found no authoritative Bond-specific fixed award sequence. [S3][S6]
* **Competition Install** currently sets Competition Mode on and **No Extra Balls**; it also selects virtual locking. [S6]

## What to watch

1. **Pink action button / solid Bond Women:** 007 Scoring is ready; expect the player to save it for a multiball or wizard state.
2. **Left ramp flashing for Jetpack:** left eject setup is complete; a Villain mode may be started before cashing it.
3. **Bird One lock lamps / Osato drops:** reveals exactly how close the player is to the second major multiball.
4. **Both left eject and right ramp lit after play stops:** the four prerequisites are complete and **Bond… James Bond** is ready.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** take the straightforward **Villain + Jetpack** stack, cash jackpots, and use a modest ready 007 multiplier rather than spending a ball trying to build 6×–7×. Avoid the Dragon Tank and difficult upper-flipper shots unless required.

**Playing behind:** accumulate multiple Bond Women, then unleash **4×–7× 007 Scoring** over Bird One, Power Pack, or Bond… James Bond. Bird One's 300M-cap Super Jackpot makes the multiplier especially explosive. [S3][S4]

**Key recurring decision:** **activate 007 Scoring now, or save the collected women for a better scoring state?** The multiplier is timed and collected women can be lost at drain, so cashing 2×–3× now may be correct while a trailing player may gamble on building 5×–7×.

## Danger zones

1. **Left-eject kickout:** can re-enter the scoop, strike the Dragon Tank, or head toward the center drain. [S3]
2. **Dragon Tank:** valuable for outlane saves but identified by expert strategy as a particularly deadly direct standup. [S4]
3. **Left-ramp rejects:** steep misses return quickly toward the flippers.
4. **Upper-flipper shots:** holding or flipping late on the upper loop/side ramp can send the ball toward the right outlane. [S4]
5. **Osato drops / Bird One rocket:** necessary for Bird One but produce hard central rebounds; backhanding the bank is a common control tactic. [S4]

## Trivia

* The cornerstone game uses footage and music from the **six Sean Connery Bond films** released from 1962–1971. [S1]
* The Pro edition is specifically themed visually around **Dr. No**. [S1]
* All cornerstone editions feature a sculpted **Aston Martin DB5** that ejects balls vertically through its roof. [S1]
* Premium/LE replace the Pro's illustrated Jetpack with the famous **moving magnetic Jetpack**, so Jetpack Super Jackpot rules physically play differently. [S1][S3]
* Bird One is also edition-sensitive: **virtual locks on Pro, physical locks on Premium/LE**, with Swipe-a-Ball exclusive to the latter. [S3]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* Latest verified version is **1.06, July 1, 2026** [S5], but the maintained Tilt rulesheet labels its rule basis **1.01** [S3]. Kineticist's guide was updated April 29, 2026 and states it reflects then-current code [S4]. I found no detailed authoritative 1.06 rules changelog; verify any tournament-critical change introduced after 1.01.
* Current Kineticist instructions describe **two right-ramp shots** to get into the first Villain mode [S4], whereas the rulesheet shorthand simply says “shoot the right ramp to start.” Avoid announcing an exact qualification count unless verified on the venue copy.
* High Stakes is random in standard play. Competition Mode should normalize random features, but I found no reliable published Bond-specific award order.
* Physical danger is highly copy-dependent, particularly the left eject, steep left ramp, upper-loop feed and center drop bank.
