## Identity and versions

* **Avengers: Infinity Quest (Limited Edition)** — Stern Pinball, 2020; requested edition is the LE. 
* Design: **Keith Elwin**; code/rules credits include Rick Naegele, Raymond Davidson, Brett Z Rubin, Mike Kyzivat and Elwin; mechanical engineering: Harrison Drake; art: **Jeremy Packer / Zombie Yeti**. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* The **Premium/LE** adds the motorized Doctor Strange pop-up Portal, visible under-playfield subway capable of staging three balls, sculpted Infinity Gauntlet, Captain Marvel inverse-gravity ramp and Pym Laboratories up-kicker. The LE was limited to **500 machines worldwide**. [S3] ([Stern Pinball][2])
* **Latest software verified:** **LE 1.10.0, July 1, 2026.** The best detailed public rulesheet found is explicitly based on **1.08**, so rules below should be considered verified through 1.08 unless independently noted. [S1][S4] ([Stern Pinball][3])
* The two important readily accessible multiballs are **Thor Multiball** and **Iron Man Multiball**. Portal Locks additionally turn a Gem Quest into multiball and multiply its scoring. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][1])

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Bank a Portal Lock before starting a Gem Quest.** Four combos light the first; ten combos can light another, and Secret Skill Shot #1 can immediately light one. One/two locked balls make the next Quest **2×/3×** while those extra balls survive. **Confirmed rule.** [S4] ([Tilt Forums][1])
2. **Spell STRANGE at the disc, then enter the raised Portal** to choose a Gem Quest. **Observed strategy:** Reality Gem is a common pure-scoring choice because its disc-built multiplier combines especially well with Portal Locks. [S4][S6] ([Tilt Forums][1])
3. **Bring Thor or Iron Man Multiball into the Quest.** First Thor is exceptionally accessible: three captive-ball hits. This gives survival plus simultaneous scoring/progress. **Observed strategy.** [S4][S6] ([Tilt Forums][1])
4. **Finish the Quest while Portal balls remain.** That opens **Victory Laps** at all major shots, still multiplied by balls in play; this is the major tournament payoff. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][1])
5. **Alternate route:** prioritize a useful Gem perk—especially Time for longer multiball saves/timers—rather than maximum immediate Reality scoring. The recurring choice is **start/cash now vs. keep building locks and a multiball stack**. **Strategic inference.** [S4]

## Core rules and persistence

* **Gem Quests:** spell STRANGE, shoot the **Portal on Premium/LE**, choose the Quest with flippers, confirm with action button/both flippers. They have no normal countdown, but a health bar continually falls; draining fails the Quest. Thor/Iron Man Multiball and timed Super Modes may be stacked into an active Quest. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* **Portal Locks:** up to two can be collected before a Quest. They make the Quest 2× or 3× only **while the extra balls remain alive**. Completing the Quest before losing them earns multiplied Victory Laps. Easy commentator mistake: the multiplier is not simply “banked 3× for the whole mode.” [S4] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* After winning a Quest, the **right ramp collects the Gem**, then a flashing shot places it and activates its perk. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* **Reality Gem:** a placed Reality Gem gives a Main Avenger shot 2×, then 3× at level 1 and **4× at level 2**. **Soul Gem:** after another completed Quest, its shot can award **150% of that previous Quest's value** when placed on a level-2 Main Avenger—an enormous later-game swing. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* **Power Gem** makes Avenger advancement count double; importantly, the affected Avenger's level and level-2 shot multiplier then **carry between balls**. Without that protection, Avenger levels are not generally preserved. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* **Thor:** first multiball needs only three captive-ball hits. **Iron Man:** spell IRON at the return lanes, whose lit state advances with disc spins, then lock balls at Avengers Tower. Both offer an add-a-ball by spelling STRANGE and collecting at the Sanctum during multiball. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* End-of-ball bonus mixes per-ball progress with game-long trophies/Gems and can reach **25× Bonus X**. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][1])

## Skill shots

* **Skill Shot:** soft plunge into the top rollover near the pops. **250K +100K per prior success, plus 3 seconds ball save.** [S4]
* **Super Skill Shot:** full plunge, then Avengers Tower. **1M +150K per success, plus 5 seconds save; first successful one also lights Mystery.** [S4]
* **Secret Skill Shot #1:** medium plunge so the ball rolls backward down the upper loop. **3M +200K per success, 10-second save and instantly lights a Portal Lock.** This is strategically excellent because it shortcuts the normal combo setup. [S4]
* **Secret Skill Shot #2:** full plunge, then center drop target. **3M +200K per success, 10-second save and the currently flashing Computer Grid square.** Useful when a specific grid award can be timed. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][1])

## Secondary features

* **Ball save:** factory standard is **5 seconds**, but operator-adjustable. Skill shots add their separate 3/5/10-second saves. [S2][S4] ([Stern Pinball][4])
* **Action button:** confirms Gem selection and later powers several Gem abilities. Most notably, Mind Gem can spot lit shots; Space Gem can move Gems; Soul Gem can replenish balls during Black Order Multiball. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* **Captain Marvel / Binary Hurry-Up — LE-specific leverage:** alternating the Captain Marvel and left ramps scores the hurry-up; every completed Binary Hurry-Up adds **+1× to the Captain Marvel ramp**, up to **5×** on Premium/LE. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* **Mystery:** can award Add-A-Ball, 10M, STRANGE progress, timed modes and other material benefits. In Competition Mode, Mystery and Computer Grid randomness are normalized into fixed behavior. [S4][S5] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* **Extra balls:** several qualifications exist, but **Competition Mode alone leaves extra balls unchanged; full Competition Install disables them.** [S4][S5] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* No conventional Magna-Save or player-operated kickback was found.

## What to watch

* **Balls visible beneath the Portal:** one staged Portal ball means the next Quest can be 2×; two mean 3×, contingent on keeping them alive. [S3][S4]
* **STRANGE complete / Portal rises:** a Gem Quest is ready; expect the player to shoot the Portal and choose a Gem. [S4]
* **Reality Quest's yellow arrows + STRANGE display:** disc spins restore STRANGE letters, which multiply the yellow-shot values; repeated disc shots signal deliberate value-building. [S4]
* **THOR captive-ball progress / balls magnetically held at Avengers Tower:** the player is preparing Thor or Iron Man Multiball, often to bring into a Quest. [S3][S4] ([Stern Pinball][2])

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** Prefer the stack already available: one Portal Lock plus ready Thor is often enough protection. Cash the Quest/Victory Lap opportunity rather than making additional dangerous setup shots solely to reach 3×. **Strategic inference.**

**Playing behind:** **Observed strategy:** chase two Portal Locks, start **Reality Gem**, then bring Thor or Iron Man Multiball into it. Completing Reality with multiple balls still alive opens multiplied Victory Laps; experienced owners repeatedly identify this as a route to very large scores. Later, Soul Gem Hard Mode is another high-variance lever: only 20 flips, but **10× Normal scoring**. [S4][S6] ([Pinside][5])

**Key recurring decision:** **How much setup is enough?** A player can start a Quest immediately, wait for 2×/3× Portal Locks, or additionally stage Thor/Iron Man. More setup raises the ceiling but creates more single-ball drain opportunities.

## Danger zones

* **Captain Marvel ramp reject:** can come back toward the center; highly machine/setup dependent. [S7]
* **Black Widow ramp reject:** another reported SDTM-style reject when the shot is not clean. [S7]
* **Hulk orbit / pop-area return:** a clean orbit can emerge through the Captain America area with little warning and head center or into a sling. [S7]
* **Black Panther orbit reject:** some copies kick failed shots sharply across/down the playfield rather than returning cleanly. [S7]
* **Thor captive ball:** failed/direct rebounds can be severe; the exact behavior should be learned during practice. [S7] ([Pinside][6])

## Trivia

* AIQ was **Keith Elwin's third Stern design** after establishing himself first as an elite competitive player. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* The LE was limited to **500 units globally** and added a mirrored backglass, numbered plaque, shaker, upgraded audio and anti-reflective glass. [S3] ([Stern Pinball][2])
* Stern built an actual **magnetic Avengers Tower** that can hold balls visibly in midair for Iron Man locks. [S3]
* The Premium/LE's Doctor Strange assembly physically rises and sends balls into an under-playfield subway; it is not merely cosmetic edition differentiation. [S3] ([Stern Pinball][2])
* The rules deliberately make Gem placement strategic: the *same Gem* can produce materially different benefits depending on where it is placed and how far that Avenger has been leveled. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][1])

I could not verify a sufficiently detailed official 1.09/1.10 changelog to establish that every rule above is unchanged; installed code should therefore be confirmed. ([Stern Pinball][3])
