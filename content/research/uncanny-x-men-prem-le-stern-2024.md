## Identity and versions

**The Uncanny X-Men — Limited Edition**, Stern Pinball, 2024. Lead design: **Jack Danger**; code/rules: Waison Cheng and Mike Vinikour; art: Jeremy Packer / Zombie Yeti; animation: Chuck Ernst; music includes Charlie Benante. [S2][S3]

* Pro, Premium and LE share the core rules. Premium/LE add the **motorized rising Sentinel body/hands** and elevated Beast's Lab captive-ball mechanism. [S4]
* LE production is limited to **811 games**, referencing Earth-811 from *Days of Future Past*. [S4]
* **Rules basis: `code` — v0.98, released 2026-07-01.** [S1]
* Four major multiballs: **Sentinel Multiball, To Me, My X-Men Multiball, Save the City Multiball, Berserker Multiball.** As of 0.98, Save the City is explicitly a standalone **wizard multiball** rather than a stackable regular multiball. [S1][S2]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Build a useful mission stack.** Train Danger Room to Level 1 for **2x scoring on the next mission**, then start a mission before starting a compatible multiball. [S2]
2. A strong opener is **Sentinel Facility Raid → Sentinel Multiball**. Finishing Raid and surviving Escape Nimrod eventually earns Rogue's **once-per-ball multiball add-a-ball** perk. **Observed strategy.** [S1][S3]
3. In Sentinel Multiball, use the **Danger Room exit** to relight jackpots at **2x then 3x**, then collect the Sentinel Super Jackpot; hitting the open mouth doubles the Super. [S1][S2]
4. Keep destroying Sentinels. **35** lights **Berserker Multiball**, whose Super can commonly be tens of millions and can immediately be doubled at the Danger Room exit. [S1][S2]
5. Alternate route: methodically qualify **Save the City** by completing all six city shots. It is now a standalone 4-ball wizard multiball with very large scoring potential. [S1][S3]

## Core rules and persistence

Missions are qualified with white-arrow progress and started at the blue **Midtown or Subway** shots. A mission can be stacked with a regular multiball **only if the mission starts first**. Multiballs cannot be stacked with each other. [S2]

Completing a mission sends the player to **The Future**. Easy missions use the Sentinel version: defeat the required Sentinels, then escape at Midtown/Subway. Challenging missions use **Escape Nimrod**: clear three red shots, then reach either blue escape shot. Successful escape awards the associated character perk. [S1][S2]

**Danger Room training resets after a mission is played.** Level 1 doubles the next mission; Level 2 reduces Future qualification; Level 3 makes Future Sentinel kills count double and score double. During multiball, Danger Room levels also enhance multiball awards. [S1][S2]

**Gambit** is major scoring leverage: three bumper hits—or an alley pass into the bumper—makes the next mode/multiball award **2x**; after earning Gambit's perk it becomes **3x**. It does not stack multiple uses. [S2]

**Bishop** charges at the left bumper and can store up to three uses. Pressing the flashing-red action button spots the **highest-valued eligible shot** currently lit. [S1][S2]

**Save the City changed materially in v0.98.** It now qualifies only outside multiball, requires 3 completions of each of six city shots on the first cycle, and can only start in main play with nothing else running. Later cycles require 4, then 5 completions. [S1]

## Skill shots

* **Lane Skill Shot:** soft plunge to the switch by the Highway entrance. Awards **200K +1 Bonus X**. Safest low-risk choice. [S2]
* **X-Jet Skill Shot:** plunge through X-Jet into the X-Mansion feed for **+3 Bonus X**. If the feed continues into the Danger Room, the exit ramp completes a **Super Skill Shot**. [S2]
* **Time Loop Skill Shot:** repeatedly loop Downtown, up to five times; completing the sequence then shooting Uptown adds **15 seconds to the initial ball save**. Higher-risk but excellent survival leverage. [S2]
* **Cerebro Skill Shot:** shoot Uptown from the plunge. Spots one Cerebro advance toward **To Me, My X-Men Multiball** and returns favorably toward the left flipper; competitive guidance favors it as an efficient opener. [S2][S3]
* **MXV Super Skill Shot:** officially added in **v0.97**. [S1] The current official notes and 0.98 community rulesheet do **not** document its execution or award, and players were still publicly treating it as a secret after release. **Uncertain; do not infer the sequence.**

## Secondary features

* **Danger Room Abort Save:** Abort is lit from game start and can be requalified through X-Jet/Danger Room entries. Hitting it activates a **5-second whole-playfield ball save**. [S2]
* **X-Mansion Ball Save:** four target hits light a right-outlane save; Jean Grey's perk expands its usefulness in The Future. [S2]
* **Rogue Add-A-Ball:** after earning Rogue's perk, hold the **green action button** during multiball for an add-a-ball, once per ball. [S1][S2]
* **Beast's Lab:** context-sensitive “mystery” awards improve with combo level and can include Bonus X, X-Mansion Ball Save, large points, or Extra Ball/Beast qualification. [S2]
* **Extra balls:** normally light from mission and Beast's Lab progress. **Competition Install disables them**; v0.90 added a consolation score when disabled, but the official changelog does not state its value. [S1][S2]
* **LE Sentinel hand:** operator-adjustable. Factory behavior introduced in v0.92 uses the hand during multiball only; it can physically intercept and relaunch balls. [S1][S4]
* No strategically important conventional video mode was verified.

## What to watch

1. **Danger Room bar turns green/yellow/red:** the player is building the next mission/Future advantage rather than merely taking incidental shots. [S2]
2. **Orange/red jackpots in Sentinel Multiball:** the Danger Room exit has raised that wave from 1x to **2x/3x**. [S2]
3. **Green action button:** Rogue add-a-ball is available; **red** means Bishop can spot the most valuable eligible shot. [S1][S2]
4. **Sentinel counter approaching 35:** expect a pivot toward Uptown for **Berserker Multiball**, one of the game's biggest scoring opportunities. [S2]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:**
**Strategic inference:** take the Cerebro skill shot, start available multiballs rather than over-training the Danger Room, use Bishop to remove difficult shots, and escape The Future as soon as the requirement is satisfied. A ready Sentinel Multiball is generally preferable to risking another dangerous setup shot.

**Playing behind:**
**Observed strategy:** invest in Danger Room multipliers, earn Rogue's add-a-ball, and drive Sentinel kills toward **Berserker**. During Sentinel/Berserker multiball, aggressively use the Danger Room exit and Gambit to multiply already-large Super Jackpots. [S2][S3]

**Key recurring decision:**
**Take safety now or improve the next scoring window.** Danger Room training, extra character perks and delaying a multiball all increase future upside, but this is an unusually drain-prone layout. Good players therefore differ sharply on how much setup they tolerate before cashing a ready feature. [S3]

## Danger zones

* **X-Jet standup:** a near-miss can ricochet dangerously toward the center. [S3]
* **Midtown ramp short-shot:** can dump the ball into the pop-bumper area and toward the right outlane. [S3]
* **Subway rubber:** another route into uncontrolled pops; a timely left nudge can help. [S3]
* **Danger Room:** high-value but genuinely high-risk; an unprepared entry or missed mini-flipper shot can turn into a drain immediately. [S3]
* **Sentinel hands on LE/Premium:** can physically interrupt an otherwise predictable ball path and fire it back into play. [S1][S4]

## Trivia

* The layout was Jack Danger's second Stern cornerstone design after *Foo Fighters* and deliberately uses unusual **crossing ramp trajectories**. [S3][S4]
* The game draws principally from the classic **“Days of Future Past”** and **“Mind Out of Time”** comic stories. [S4][S5]
* LE production is **811**, an explicit homage to Marvel's **Earth-811** future timeline. [S4]
* Premium/LE's Sentinel does more than move: its articulated hands can physically **destroy ramp access or intercept the ball**. [S4]
* Code remains formally **pre-1.0 at v0.98**; the intended **Master Mold** wizard mode is still represented as a placeholder in the current rulesheet. [S1][S2]
