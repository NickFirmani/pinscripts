## Identity and versions

**Captain Fantastic and the Brown Dirt Cowboy — Home Model**, Bally/Midway, **1977**, model **614-2000**. This is a **solid-state, digital, four-player home machine**, not the much more common 1976 commercial electro-mechanical Captain Fantastic. Its layout and rules are fundamentally different; commercial-game advice about A/B lanes, the right-side gate, four flippers, and 15K bonus **does not apply**. [S1][S2][S3] 

It belongs to Bally's **Series II home-pinball platform**, using a Mostek 3870 CPU on board 0614-00913. The home cabinet has no coin door and uses digital scoring. [S2][S4] ([PinWiki][1])

No reliable game-designer credit or production quantity was found. No software revision history was found beyond the fixed original program; replacement CPU hardware exists, so a modified controller is worth checking. No multiball. [S2]

## Thirty-second game plan

There is very little documented tournament play of this rare home version, so the strategy below is **strategic inference from the verified rules**, not established tournament consensus.

1. **Work the numbered 1-2-3 targets**: getting 1+2 earns **Double Bonus**; completing 1+2+3 earns **Triple Bonus**. [S1]
2. **Pick off unmade top rollovers.** Each scores 1K and adds 1K to bonus; completing the entire top set awards **24K**, resets those lamps, and lights the lower outside Extra Ball rollovers. [S1]
3. With **3X Bonus** established, continue building bonus rather than unnecessarily risking the ball—the multiplier is the game's obvious scoring leverage. [S1]
4. If Extra Balls have tournament value, a completed top bank creates a second high-leverage objective: collect the lit **lower outside rollover**. [S1]
5. When ahead, de-emphasize the dangerous free-ball collection and simply preserve a large multiplied bonus; when behind, chase the complete top-bank/Extra-Ball sequence.

## Core rules and persistence

**Confirmed rule:** a top rollover scores **1,000** and advances bonus **1,000**. Completing all top rollovers awards **24,000**, resets the top-rollover lamps, and lights the lower outside Extra Ball lamps. [S1] 

**Confirmed rule:** the numbered **1, 2 and 3 targets** advance bonus; making 1 and 2 establishes **Double Bonus**, and making all three establishes **Triple Bonus**. The multiplied bonus is paid after the ball leaves the playfield. [S1] 

The unusually important rule for commentary is **persistence**. The machine has player-specific memory: partial top-rollover progress, numbered-target progress and Extra-Ball state belong to that player and **carry to their next ball until the relevant feature/bonus is collected**. In multiplayer, another player does not steal or alter that progress. [S1] 

**Tilt is expensive:** tilting disables scoring switches/bumpers and forfeits the current end-of-ball bonus. An Extra Ball already earned before the tilt is retained. [S1] 

## Skill shots

**None found.**

A controlled plunge through an **unmade top rollover** is strategically useful because it scores 1K, advances bonus 1K and advances the 24K completion feature, but the available Bally service documentation does not define a separate skill-shot award. [S1]

## Secondary features

**Extra Ball:** completing all top rollovers lights the **lower outside Extra Ball rollovers**; rolling through one while lit awards an Extra Ball. Multiple Extra Balls can be accumulated and are shown by the “same player shoots again” lamp. [S1] 

A physical **50K/100K “free ball” adjustment switch** exists. The surviving service text documents easy/hard behavior involving 50K/100K score thresholds, but its wording is ambiguous enough that I would verify the installed setting rather than encode its exact threshold behavior into the commentator sheet. [S1][S2] 

No ball save, video mode, mystery award, player-controlled save, lane-change control or similar feature was verified.

## What to watch

* **Top rollover lamps going dark:** each extinguished lamp is progress toward the **24K completion + Extra Ball light**. [S1]
* **Double/Triple Bonus state:** two numbered targets means 2X; all three means **3X end-of-ball bonus**, the principal multiplier. [S1]
* **Lower outside Extra Ball lamps:** the top rollover set has been completed and the player now has a free ball available to collect. [S1]
* **Same Player Shoots Again:** confirms a free ball has actually been earned rather than merely lit. [S1]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** prioritize completing 1-2-3 and protecting a **Triple Bonus** rather than gambling on a lit lower-outside Extra Ball. Nearly completed top rollovers are still attractive because the final lane produces a verified 24K award.

**Playing behind:** pursue the full combination—**3X Bonus + top-rollover completion + Extra Ball**. The 24K completion and an additional ball are unusually large sources of variance on this otherwise simple game.

**Key recurring decision:** once Extra Ball is lit, **is another ball worth intentionally attacking a lower outside rollover?** Match score and the actual venue feed can make two strong players answer differently.

## Danger zones

No reliable competitive feed documentation for the home model was found. These are **strategic inferences**, not established tendencies:

* **Lower outside Extra Ball rollover:** the prize is literally adjacent to drain territory.
* **1-2-3 target rebounds:** mandatory multiplier progress requires shooting direct targets that can return the ball quickly.
* **Upper rollover/bumper area:** useful for the 24K feature but gives less control over the return.
* **Tilt:** especially costly because it erases the end-of-ball bonus payout entirely. [S1]

## Trivia

* The home Captain Fantastic is **model 614-2000**, part of Bally's Series II home range alongside Galaxy Ranger and another home Fireball. [S2] ([PinWiki][1])
* Bally marketed these as cheaper **non-coin-operated home machines** using a common simplified playfield architecture. [S2] ([PinWiki][1])
* The Series II hardware uses a **Mostek 3870** processor—very different electronics from Bally's contemporary commercial MPU platform. [S2]
* A surviving example documented by Propstore has **no coin door**, digital scoring and the 614-2000 model marking. [S4] ([Propstore][2])
* Archival indexes identify dedicated Captain Fantastic home instruction/score cards as Bally **0614-00924 and 0614-00926**. [S5] ([Pinball Rebel][3])

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **50K/100K free-ball behavior:** the Bally service documentation clearly establishes the adjustment and two threshold settings, but the surviving prose is grammatically ambiguous about its exact interaction with the separate rollover-qualified Extra Ball. [S1]
* **Exact bonus ceiling:** the documentation verifies 1K rollover advances, target-related bonus advancement and 2X/3X payout, but the sources reviewed do not state the maximum accumulated bonus clearly enough to quote one.
* **Game-design credit:** secondary sources sometimes associate **Alan Aldridge** with the home game's design/artwork, but I found no authoritative Bally credit sheet establishing him as the pinball designer. Omitted from Identity.
* **Tournament strategy/feeds:** no reliable competitive strategy source specific to model 614-2000 was found. Strategy and physical-risk recommendations above are therefore explicitly inference, not consensus.
