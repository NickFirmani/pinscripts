## Identity and versions

* **Iron Maiden: Legacy of the Beast — Premium**, Stern Pinball, **2018**, SPIKE 2. Lead design **Keith Elwin**; code/rules **Rick Naegele and Keith Elwin**; mechanics **Harrison Drake**; art **Jeremy Packer / Zombie Yeti**; sound/music **Jerry Thompson**. [S1][S3] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Models: Pro, Premium, LE. Premium/LE add the **motorized Pharaoh/Sarcophagus lock** and an actuating center ramp revealing the Underworld scoop. Consequently, Mummy lock/start and Tomb Treasure collection differ physically from Pro. [S1][S3] ([Stern Pinball][2])
* **Rules basis: `code` — v1.18.0, released 2026-07-07.** v1.18 fixed the Premium/LE diverter regression introduced in v1.17; both 1.17 and 1.18 are principally system/service updates rather than new gameplay rules. The detailed public rulesheet is based on gameplay revision **1.10**, so this brief uses its documented mature rules where unaffected by later changelogs. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][3])
* Commentary-relevant multiballs: **Trooper Multiball, Mummy Multiball, Cyborg Multiball**, battle multiballs **Aces High / Rime of the Ancient Mariner**, Premium/LE **Can I Play With Madness**, wizard **Number of the Beast**, and super-wizard **Run to the Hills**. [S3]

Research scope follows the supplied commentary brief.

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** get into an **Eddie Battle** quickly by spelling E-D-D-I-E at white-arrow shots, while simultaneously progressing Mummy/Trooper and Power Features. [S3][S4] ([Kineticist][4])
2. Win battles to collect **Soul Shards**. Soul Shards advance Tomb Treasure progress and eventually unlock **2 Minutes to Midnight**; deep progression leads toward Number of the Beast and Run to the Hills. [S3]
3. Prepare **2× or 3× playfield scoring** with the orange X targets, but save activation for a lucrative battle, multiball, Tomb Treasure, or Power Jackpot. [S3][S4]
4. **Stack when safe:** single-ball battles can continue into Trooper or Mummy Multiball. Flight of Icarus + Mummy and Hallowed Be Thy Name + Trooper are particularly synergistic. [S3]
5. **Alternate tournament route:** exploit **Loop Jackpots** if the inner-loop shot is dialed. Consecutive loops can explode under 2×/3× playfield, but the missed-loop feed is notoriously dangerous. [S3][S4] ([Kineticist][4])

## Core rules and persistence

* White-arrow shots spell **E-D-D-I-E**; completion lights an Eddie Battle at the center Underworld shot. Most single-ball battles can be carried into Mummy/Trooper multiball. [S3]
* Winning each of the five standard battles awards its **Soul Shard**. Each unique Soul Shard also lights a Tomb Treasure. [S3]
* **Playfield X:** complete all orange X targets to light **2×** at an inlane; complete them again while 2× is available/running to qualify **3×**. Lit X is lane-changeable. Orange targets add time while the multiplier runs. [S3][S4] ([Kineticist][5])
* **Trooper Multiball:** complete the drop bank while Light Lock is available, collect three virtual orbit locks, then shoot blue jackpots. Three jackpots light the bullseye Super Jackpot. Jackpot levels **carry between Trooper multiballs**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][6])
* **Mummy Multiball, Premium:** hit the captive ball to spell MUMMY and light lock; lock at the **Left Ramp/Sarcophagus**, then spell MUMMY again. On Premium/LE the final captive-ball blow knocks the physically locked ball loose to begin multiball. MUMMY-letter/Super progress carries between attempts, but switch progress toward an individual jackpot does not. [S3][S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][6])
* **Power Features**—ramps, orbits, spinners, pops and targets—build a Power Jackpot. Completed uncollected Power Jackpots stack their multiplier, up to **5×**, at the Orb. [S3]
* **Loop Jackpot:** repeated inner loops qualify a timed escalating loop sequence. This is one of the game's biggest repeatable scoring exploits when combined with PF-X. [S3][S4]
* End-of-ball bonus includes Soul Shards, Eddie cards, loops and other accumulated progress and is multiplied by Bonus X, but normally remains secondary to multiplied live scoring. [S3]

## Skill shots

* **Standard Skill Shot:** soft-plunge into the lit X target beneath the upper-left flipper. Awards **1M, one E-D-D-I-E letter and +5 seconds ball save**. [S3][S6]
* **Super Skill Shot:** hold the left flipper and plunge through the inner orbit so the ball returns to the upper-left flipper; shoot the Super Jackpot/bullseye target. Awards **5M, lights Playfield X, and +10 seconds ball save**. This is frequently the strategically superior opener because it shortcuts multiplier setup. [S3][S6] ([TiltForums][7])
* **Secret left-outlane skill shot:** a controlled plunge directly through the left outlane is documented. Its availability is **adjustment-sensitive** and can be disabled, so it should not be assumed in tournament commentary. [S5]
* Additional secret skill-shot variants have been reported, but their current exact requirements/rewards are not sufficiently authoritative for this quick sheet.

## Secondary features

* **Revive:** shoot the lower-left spinner to spell R-E-V-I-V-E and light outlane saves. On factory settings the first completion lights both sides and each can save independently; tougher settings may award only one effective save. Lit Revives **carry between balls**. [S3][S4] ([Kineticist][4])
* **Orb mystery:** completing the drop bank when Light Orb is available qualifies the Orb. Re-qualifying before collecting raises its award level to 3. [S3]
* **Can I Play With Madness:** Premium/LE-specific third Tomb Treasure when enabled. Starts 2-ball multiball; flippers move the lit shot, qualifying progressively more green shots and additional balls. It is strategically significant but **operator-adjustable/disableable**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][6])
* **Extra balls:** lit by three Power Features, 30 loops, or the fifth Tomb Treasure, then collected at the center X target. Tournament point conversion depends on event settings; no universal fixed conversion was verified. [S3][S4]
* No player Action Button feature.

## What to watch

* **Orange X inserts / flashing inlane:** 2× or 3× playfield is prepared. If the player delays the lit inlane, they are likely saving multiplier for a larger scoring window. [S3]
* **Soul Shard icons:** show which battles have actually been beaten, not merely started; each unique shard is also Tomb Treasure progress. [S3]
* **Yellow Mummy shots during Mummy Multiball:** completing the current yellow set earns an add-a-ball; up to three are available in one MB. [S3]
* **Inner-loop jackpot insert/timer:** repeated loop shots are about to escalate sharply; under PF-X this can become the match's dominant scoring opportunity. [S3][S4]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:**
Favor battle progression, available multiballs, and Revive qualification rather than forcing a perfect stack. **Observed expert advice explicitly warns against draining while assembling a “golden stack.”** [S4] ([Kineticist][5])

**Playing behind:**
Prepare **3× playfield**, then expose it to a high-value battle, stacked battle+multiball, accumulated Power Jackpot, Tomb Treasure, or Loop Jackpot sequence. Loop Jackpots are particularly explosive if the player owns the shot.

**Key recurring decision:**
**Spend the prepared playfield multiplier now, or preserve the lit inlane until a much larger mode/multiball payoff is ready?** Iron Maiden rewards sophisticated stacking, but the extra setup shots introduce meaningful drain risk.

## Danger zones

* **Inner side-loop miss:** probably the game's signature danger; a weak return can head almost directly to the **right outlane**. [S4] ([Kineticist][4])
* **REVIVE spinner:** ironically the shot used to earn outlane protection can itself send a right-flipper miss toward the **left outlane**. [S4]
* **Drop-bank rebound:** upper-flipper/center shots into the bank can line up a fast center drain. [S4]
* **Left Ramp reject:** especially important on Premium because so many critical features use it; failed shots lose control rapidly. [S4]
* **Greedy PF-X setup:** repeatedly hunting orange X standups before a stack can be more dangerous than simply starting the available scoring feature. [S4]

## Trivia

* Iron Maiden was **Keith Elwin's first commercial Stern design**; its layout evolved from his earlier homebrew **Archer** machine. [S3][S7] ([Pinside][8])
* The Premium/LE Sarcophagus uses a **hit-detecting Newton/captive ball** to physically release the locked Mummy ball. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* All models use **four flippers**, including two upper flippers that create the game's distinctive looping geometry. [S1]
* The game includes **12 selectable Iron Maiden songs**; song selection itself does not alter the rules. [S1][S6] ([Stern Pinball][9])
* Premium/LE's third Tomb Treasure can start **Can I Play With Madness**, while historically the Pro received a Power-Jackpot boost there instead; later Insider Connected support can alter availability, so edition/code matters. [S3]
