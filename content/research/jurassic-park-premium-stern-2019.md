## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Advance Paddocks:** shoot white MAP arrows, then left ramp. Rescue red shots, set the trap, then capture the moving dinosaur. Strong tournament routes generally favor the **outside edges of the map** for easier progress toward Visitor's Center. **Observed strategy.** [S3][S4] ([Tilt Forums][3])
2. After the first dinosaur, **Control Room** lights. **System Boot** is an excellent short-game mode; Restore Power becomes powerful if stacked with multiball. [S3][S4] ([Tilt Forums][3])
3. Build a multiball—especially **T-Rex or Raptor Tri-Ball**—and start **45 seconds of 2× scoring** from the far-right target after collecting a Supply Drop. That's the game's cleanest general scoring multiplier. [S3][S4] ([Tilt Forums][3])
4. **Raptor Tri-Ball is the huge alternate payoff:** progress to six-ball play, where Super Jackpots are multiplied by balls in play; its own 15-second 2× jackpot feature can layer on top. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
5. Use **Smart Missile** aggressively: depending on state, Clear Paddock, +5 Rescues or instant Raptor Multiball can eliminate large amounts of dangerous setup. **Observed strategy.** [S3][S4]

## Core rules and persistence

* **Paddocks:** first Advance Paddock needs one MAP shot; later ones normally need three. Left ramp advances; hitting the truck changes which branch of the island map you take. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* Inside a paddock: rescue staff → hit required trap targets/Helipad → capture the dinosaur at the flashing shot. Captured dinosaurs award permanent perks and advance the route toward Visitor's Center. [S3]
* **T-Rex Events:** spell T-REX at the truck, then start at the left ramp. Feed T-Rex begins as a hurry-up and converts into 2-ball multiball; its collected hurry-up becomes the jackpot base. Five jackpots light a Super equal to their sum. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Control Room:** first dinosaur, Raptor jackpot, two CHAOS spellings, or 15 tower shots can light it. Only one Control Room mode and one multiball can normally overlap; this is a key scoring stack. [S3]
* **Raptor Tri-Ball:** capture a raptor, weaken the fence, then shoot the dead-end lane. Ramp jackpots → CHAOS jackpots → **six-ball Super Jackpot phase**. On Premium, the captured ball can also refresh the 15-second **2× Raptor jackpot** timer. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **2× scoring:** Supply Drop lights the far-right target for **45 seconds of double scoring**. [S3]
* Most major long-term progress—map location, rescues, dinosaurs, fossils, Control Room/T-Rex progress—persists across balls. End-of-ball bonus itself is based heavily on cumulative game progress. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* Easy commentator trap: **Bonus X and the temporary 2× scoring feature are unrelated.**

## Skill shots

* **Main combo Skill Shot:** full plunge → left ramp for base **2M**, then right ramp for Super (2× base), side/tower ramp for Double Super (4×), then right orbit for Ultra Super (6×). Each successful step adds **3 seconds of ball save**; completing all four also lights left-outlane Escape. The base rises by 1M after successful skill shots for later balls. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **MXV Skill Shot:** insert an upper-loop shot between the right ramp and side ramp; the side-ramp award becomes **8× base**. [S3]
* **Secret Skill Shots:** short plunge to a flipper, then immediately shoot:

  * **C**: 6M + 3s save
  * **O**: 8M + 3s
  * pop-area standup: 10M + 3s
  * pop standup **from the right flipper**: 20M + **12s save** and higher Amber value. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Observed tournament choice:** the C secret skill shot is especially attractive because it also advances CHAOS and gives controlled early possession. [S4]

## Secondary features

* **Smart Missile:** enough Rescues lights the left inlane. The ball pauses; action button selects an award, then the player must hit the amber target between the pops. Clear Paddock, +Rescues and Raptor Multiball are especially strong. [S3][S4] ([Kineticist][4])
* **Supply Drop / Super Supply Drop:** three purple targets light Supply Drop; collecting it also lights 2× scoring. Upper-loop progress upgrades later drops into deterministic Amber modes on Competition settings. [S2][S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Escape:** left-outlane virtual save. Current code also has **Nedry Save** on the right outlane, qualified by repeated hits to the 2× target. [S2][S3] 
* **Extra Balls:** several paths exist; if configured to points they score **15M and can be doubled**. Current Competition Install explicitly sets **No Extra Balls**. [S2][S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])

## What to watch

* **Truck direction:** determines the player's next map branch and also changes several feed/award behaviors; a deliberate truck hit often means routing rather than raw scoring.
* **Control Room C lit:** expect a mode start, commonly System Boot, before the player starts a prepared multiball.
* **Far-right 2× target lit:** a scoring stack is imminent; during Raptor/T-Rex/Control Room this can transform the ball.
* **Premium Raptor gate closed with a ball captured:** Tri-Ball setup is underway; once fence integrity reaches zero, one more dead-end shot starts it.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** Take completed paddock progress, System Boot and ready multiballs without insisting on extra setup. Smart Missile can safely skip difficult paddock work. Activate 2× only when a clear cash-out is already available. **Strategic inference.**

**Playing behind:** Build **Control Room + Raptor/T-Rex Multiball + 2× scoring**. Raptor's six-ball phase is particularly explosive: its Super Jackpots already scale with balls in play, then temporary Raptor 2× and general playfield 2× can create enormous leverage. [S3][S4]

**Key recurring decision:** **Keep progressing toward the next big stack, or cash the feature that's already ready?** Jurassic Park rewards layering modes and multipliers, but virtually every extra setup shot is difficult.

## Danger zones

* **O/right-orbit shot:** notoriously tight; bricks leave the ball uncontrolled. [S4] ([Kineticist][4])
* **Raptor Pen:** repeated direct attacks are dangerous; experienced players often favor a left-flipper backhand. [S4]
* **Set-Trap targets / truck:** owner reports document rebounds off this central geometry going essentially straight down the middle. **Setup-specific.** [S5] ([Pinside][5])
* **Ramp rejects:** left/right/Helipad alignment varies significantly between copies and can generate fast center returns. [S5] ([Pinside][6])
* **Premium T-Rex release:** the mechanism physically throws the ball; during Museum Mayhem the rules specifically describe a random-direction throw. [S1][S3] ([Stern Pinball][2])

## Trivia

* Jurassic Park was **Keith Elwin's second Stern production design**, following *Iron Maiden*. [S3][S4]
* Rather than simply reenact the movie, the game tells an original post-breakdown evacuation story focused on rescuing staff and recapturing dinosaurs. [S1]
* Premium/LE's T-Rex is a true **ball-eating, ball-throwing animatronic mechanism**. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][2])
* The physical Premium Raptor Pen uses a **motorized gate and actual ball lock**, unlike Pro. [S1]
* Stern's 2023 V1.10 update added more than 100 speech callouts plus major rule additions including Nedry Save and Trifecta—four years after release. [S2]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* The maintained Tilt rulesheet is explicitly based on **V1.10**, while current official code is **V1.16.0**. [S2][S3] V1.11–1.16 contain mostly hardware/system fixes; no later release note found in this pass supersedes the core V1.10 gameplay described here. 
* **Competition Install versus Competition game mode:** these are distinct. Current Competition Install also sets `EXTRA BALL LIMIT` to **NO EXTRA BALLS**, while the game separately offers Competition gameplay with deterministic randomness. [S2][S3]
* Older tournament references saying Competition Mode merely changes T-Rex order and Amber randomness predate the newer standardized Stern Competition Install; use the actual venue setup. [S2][S3]
* Premium T-Rex throws, Raptor gate timing, ramp rejects and truck rebounds are strongly physical/setup-dependent.
