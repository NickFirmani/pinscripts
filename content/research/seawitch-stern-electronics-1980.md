## Identity and versions

**Seawitch** — Stern Electronics, **May 1980**, model **123**, four-player solid-state game on the **Stern M-200 MPU**. Production: **2,503**. Design: **Mike Kubin**; software: **Bill Pfutzenreuter**; artwork: **Bob Timm**. Four flippers, three pop bumpers, three drop-target banks and one spinner; no multiball. [S1][S4]

No authoritative ROM revision history was found; the archival databases list the software version as unknown. Rules below use the surviving Stern manual/instruction material rather than assuming a later ROM. [S1][S4]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Chase the white X.** Complete the lit bank in sequence: **center → lower-left → upper-right → repeat**, advancing Bonus X toward 7X. [S1][S2]
2. Be careful *when* you max it: Bonus X carries between balls until the configured reset threshold of **6X or 7X**, then drops to **3X** next ball. On a 7X-reset game, stopping at 6X on balls 1–2 can be strategically correct. [S1]
3. Every drop also builds **loop value**. Complete all three banks in one ball to light **2X loop value**, making repeated orbit/Runway shots worth up to **20K each**. [S1][S2]
4. After a bank has already been completed, leave its last target standing and hit it when **SCORE LIT BONUS VALUE** flashes to collect the current base bonus during the ball. [S1]
5. Alternate scoring engine: light all four upper standups to raise the spinner to **1K/spin** and rip it when the feed is favorable. [S1][S3]

## Core rules and persistence

Base bonus runs from **0–39K**. Each drop target adds 1K bonus; a lit upper standup also adds 1K; outlanes and the Runway rollover add 3K. At drain the bonus is multiplied by Bonus X, so the maximum end-of-ball bonus is **273K at 7X**. A tilt forfeits that bonus. [S1]

**Bonus X:** completing the bank carrying the white **INCREASE X** lamp advances X by one. The competitive sequence is center for 2X, lower-left for 3X, upper-right for 4X, then repeat for 5X/6X/7X. [S1][S2]

**Persistence is adjustment-sensitive:** the manual says multipliers below the reset threshold carry ball-to-ball. Switch 8 determines whether reaching **6X or 7X** triggers a reset to **3X on the following ball**. This is one of the most important venue settings. [S1]

Every drop also lights another loop insert, raising loop value toward **10K**. Completing all three banks during the same ball lights **2X loop**, producing up to **20K per completed loop**. [S1][S2]

On a bank already completed that ball, reducing it to one remaining target lights **SCORE LIT BONUS VALUE**; the final target awards the current **base bonus, unmultiplied, plus 1K**. This becomes a repeatable mid-ball cash-out once bonus is large. [S1]

## Skill shots

No manufacturer-defined scored **Skill Shot** was found.

**Observed tournament plunge:** soft-plunge to feed the **upper-right flipper**, then sweep the center drop bank. Alternatives are a full plunge to the upper-left flipper and either flip or hold the flipper to redirect the ball toward center. The soft plunge is preferred when it reliably gives a controlled first shot at the initial X bank. [S2][S3]

## Secondary features

**Extra Ball:** completing all three drop banks in one ball lights Extra Ball at the return lanes. Operator switches can disable it, alternate left/right, or light both; the game can also limit how many EBs are stored. [S1]

**Special:** reaching the configured 6X/7X threshold also controls when Special lights. It can be collected at the Runway rollover or outlanes. The award is unusually important because the operator can set it to **nothing, Extra Ball, 100K points, or replay**; 100K is a genuine tournament swing. [S1]

**3-ball vs. 5-ball:** pop bumpers score **1,000 on 3-ball but only 100 on 5-ball**. [S1]

No ball save, kickback, mystery or player-controlled rescue exists.

## What to watch

* **White X in front of a bank:** that's the bank the player wants for the next Bonus-X advance.
* **1-2-3 all lit:** all three banks have been completed this ball; 2X loop and, if enabled, Extra Ball are available. [S1]
* **Four upper star lamps lit:** spinner is maxed at **1K/spin**. [S1]
* **Pink SCORE LIT BONUS lamp flashing:** one more drop target will cash the current base bonus immediately. [S1]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** preserve a useful carried multiplier. On a machine where only reaching 7X triggers the reset, **6X can be safer than 7X before the final ball**. Once X and bonus are healthy, favor repeatable loops/drop-bank bonus collects rather than forcing dangerous center rebounds. **Observed strategy / strategic inference.** [S1][S3]

**Playing behind:** push all the way to 7X, finish all three banks for **2X loop**, maximize the spinner, and chase repeatable loop/bonus collects. If Special is configured for **100K**, that becomes another legitimate comeback objective. [S1][S2]

**Key recurring decision:** **take the next Bonus-X advance or preserve the current multiplier for the next ball?** The reset-threshold adjustment means what looks like obvious progress can actually cost a player several X on the following ball.

## Danger zones

* **Center drops from upper-left flipper:** documented competitive danger; certain hits rebound straight down the middle. [S3]
* **Bumper-area exits:** balls missing both upper flippers can fall directly toward center or a sling.
* **Lower-left drop rebounds:** common route from an important target directly into left-side sling/outlane danger.
* **Upper-flipper misses:** because so much scoring is upstairs, a failed sweep can turn controlled upper play into an uncontrolled lower feed.
* **Outlanes are physically adjustable:** post position can materially change game length and how aggressively players can nudge. [S1]

## Trivia

* Seawitch was designed by **Mike Kubin**, with software by Bill Pfutzenreuter and distinctive ocean-fantasy art by Bob Timm. [S4]
* Stern produced **2,503** games, and licensed production also occurred in Australia through Leisure & Allied Industries. [S4]
* The game uses **four flippers**, giving the upper flippers meaningful shots at multiple banks rather than functioning as one-shot gimmicks.
* Stern's **2018 Beatles** machine deliberately re-used and modernized the Seawitch layout; its credits explicitly recognize Kubin as designer of the source playfield. [S5]
* George Gomez said one Beatles redesign goal was making the original Seawitch upper orbits more accessible from the lower flippers. [S5]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Bonus-X reset:** the Stern manual is explicit that the reset is to **3X** and that Switch 8 selects a 6X or 7X threshold. [S1] Some secondary rules pages say reaching 7X resets to **1X** [S3]. The primary manual is used here.
* **“Skill Shot”:** current tournament cards call the soft-plunge/upper-right-flipper center-bank sweep a Skill Shot [S2], but the factory manual has no separate scored skill-shot award. Treat it as a **plunge strategy**, not a formal rule.
* **Best post-7X scoring route:** current competitive sources disagree slightly between repeated **loop/orbit play** [S2][S3] and repeatedly collecting base bonus from drop banks [S6]. Both are viable; the better route depends heavily on spinner strength and safe feeds.
* **ROM revision:** no authoritative production revision number was found; venue custom ROMs should be disclosed if present.
