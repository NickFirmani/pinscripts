## Identity and versions

*Research scope and evidence standard follow the supplied workflow brief.* 

* **Playboy** — Bally Manufacturing Co., December 1978; model 1116-E, Bally AS-2518-35 solid-state platform, four players. **Confirmed.** [S2][S6] ([IPDB][1])
* Design: **Jim Patla**; artwork: **Paul Faris**. Production: **18,250**. **Confirmed.** [S6] ([Pinside][2])
* Two flippers, three pop bumpers, five-bank drop targets, six standups, upper-left **Grotto** kickback/eject lane; **no multiball**. [S6] ([Pinside][2])
* No modern software-revision scheme was found. Rules are strongly **DIP-adjustment sensitive**; assume original Bally ROM/rules unless the venue says otherwise. A later unofficial 7-digit conversion exists but is not the factory game. [S7] ([GitHub][3])

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Collect the five Playboy Keys**: four top rollover lanes plus the center #5 Key standup. Each complete set advances the held bonus multiplier: **2X → 3X → 5X → Special**. **Confirmed rule.** [S1] ([Arcade Archive][4])
2. **Build base bonus to 20K early.** Reaching 20K lights **Super Bonus**, causing later balls to begin with 20K bonus already established. That is enormous long-game leverage. **Confirmed rule.** [S1][S3] ([Arcade Archive][4])
3. Completing the Keys also maximizes the **Grotto**: at a full set it is lit for **25K** and advances bonus five steps; the kickout returns the ball toward the top lanes so the player can repeat the cycle. [S4][S5] ([Pinball Zoo][5])
4. **Observed strategy:** strong-player guidance is essentially “up top all day”: repeatedly send the ball through the right orbit/top and Grotto, interrupting mainly to hit the #5 Key target. This is safer than deliberately attacking banks of standups. [S4] ([Silverball Rules][6])
5. **Alternate:** drop targets can light **25K outlanes**, while Playmate targets qualify an extra ball/Special. These are legitimate match-swinging routes but generally higher-risk. [S1][S4] ([Arcade Archive][4])

## Core rules and persistence

* **Keys:** complete all five for 2X, then another set for 3X, then 5X; fourth and later sets score Special. [S1]
* **Bonus:** 1K–39K base bonus. At **20K**, Super Bonus lights and subsequent balls start at 20K. [S1][S3] ([Museum of the Game][7])
* **Multiplier persistence:** expert documentation says 2X/3X/5X progress is held ball-to-ball. [S4] ([Kineticist][8])
* **Grotto:** its value/bonus advance increases with collected Keys; full Keys lights the 25K Grotto and gives the maximum five bonus advances. It ejects back toward the top. [S3][S5] ([Museum of the Game][7])
* **Drops:** first five-bank completion lights a target Special and 25K outlane scoring. [S1]
* **Playmates:** first five-target completion lights the star rollover for Extra Ball; second completion lights it for Special. Maximum one extra ball per ball in play. [S1]
* **Easy commentator trap:** several aspects of Key memory, outlane lighting, and award persistence are operator-adjustable; do not assume every physical Playboy behaves identically. [S5][S8] ([VPForums.org][9])

## Skill shots

**None found.** I found no factory documentation or credible rules source identifying a plunge-based normal, alternate, super, or secret skill shot. The plunge can enter the top Key lanes, but that alone is not enough to label it a skill shot.

## Secondary features

* **Extra ball:** complete all five Playmate standups once to light the star rollover for Extra Ball; one extra ball maximum per ball. Its availability/persistence is adjustment-sensitive. [S1][S8] ([Arcade Archive][4])
* **Specials:** subsequent Key/drop/Playmate completions can produce Specials. Factory documentation describes replay-style awards; tournament-oriented strategy sources describe Specials as potentially **50K when configured for points**. Verify the venue implementation before calling a 50K collect. [S1][S4]
* **25K outlanes:** completing the drop bank lights outlane scoring. Operator settings can make the 25K light **alternate** between sides or light **both**. [S8] ([VPForums.org][9])
* **No video modes, action buttons, Magna-Save, lane-change buttons, conventional timed ball save, or multiball found.**

## What to watch

* **2X / 3X / 5X insert lit:** Key-set progression has reached that multiplier; end-of-ball bonus is now much more valuable.
* **Super Bonus lit / bonus at 20K+:** the player has secured the 20K starting-bonus floor for later balls—especially important if achieved on Ball 1.
* **Five Grotto Key lamps / 25K Grotto lit:** expect the player to hammer the upper-left Grotto and cycle the ball back upstairs.
* **25K outlane lamp(s):** the drop bank is complete; an otherwise painful outlane drain can now add a material 25K.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** Favor the orbit/Grotto/Keys loop. It advances both immediate scoring and end-of-ball value while avoiding repeated direct standup/drop-target attacks. Once 20K Super Bonus is secured, protecting that multiplier/bonus engine is more attractive than chasing secondary awards. **Observed strategy.** [S4]

**Playing behind:** More aggressive completions of the drop bank or Playmates become reasonable, especially where a 25K outlane, Extra Ball, or point-valued Special can swing the game. A deep **5X bonus**—up to 39K base before multiplier—is also major comeback leverage. **Strategic inference from confirmed scoring.**

**Key recurring decision:** whether to continue the comparatively controlled **Keys/Grotto cycle** or leave it to finish a tempting target bank. Expert strategy recommends leaving the main route mainly when only one or two useful targets remain. [S4] ([Kineticist][8])

## Danger zones

* **Playmate/standup shots:** rebounds tend to enter the bumper area rather than return cleanly. [S4]
* **Drop-target bank:** repeated direct attacks trade rule progress for uncontrolled rebounds. [S4]
* **Pop-bumper exits:** the central bumpers are the main source of random lateral/outlane movement. [S4]
* **#5 Key target:** strategically necessary but less controlled than simply looping the top.
* **Grotto/top return:** scoring is safe on entry, but the subsequent pop-bumper descent can rapidly become dangerous.

## Trivia

* Bally produced **18,250** Playboys, an unusually large production run for the era. [S6]
* Jim Patla designed the game and Paul Faris created the artwork. [S6]
* The backglass depicts **Hugh Hefner, Patti McGuire, and Sondra Theodore**. [S9] ([YouTube][10])
* Contemporary trade press said the game begins with a **wolf whistle** and uses the *Playboy After Dark* theme. [S3] ([Museum of the Game][7])
* A December 1978 trade article reported that Hefner was personally involved in creation of the game; treat that as a contemporary industry claim rather than independently documented design credit. [S3]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Special point value:** factory instruction material describes Extra Ball/replay-style awards [S1], while tournament strategy sources describe Specials as worth **50K when set to points** [S4]. Exact venue behavior must be checked.
* **“Held bonus” wording:** the factory rule clearly establishes a **20K Super Bonus floor** for later balls [S1]. One expert rules source additionally describes “up to 100K” being held [S4]; I did not find primary documentation clear enough to use that larger figure in the commentator-facing rules.
* **Grotto wording:** contemporary trade material describes the Grotto as scoring 1K–30K and advancing bonus 1–5 steps [S3]; detailed modern documentation describes a fully qualified Grotto as **25K + five bonus advances** [S5]. Those are compatible in practical strategy but not identical descriptions of the scoring display.
* Operator settings for Key memory, tied Key lanes, outlane lighting, and Extra Ball/Special persistence are documented in emulator-derived DIP tables [S8], but I did not obtain a readable factory adjustment page for independent primary-source confirmation.
