## Identity and versions

**The Munsters — Premium / Limited Edition**, Stern Pinball, 2019. Lead design: **John Borg**; rules/software: **Dwight Sullivan**; art: **Christopher Franchi**; sound/music: Jerry Thompson. Stern SPIKE 2 platform. [S2][S4]

Premium and LE are gameplay-equivalent for this catalog: both contain **Grandpa’s Laboratory**, the playable lower playfield with two mini-flippers, ramps, bash target and two mini pinballs. The Premium uses black-and-white artwork; the 600-unit LE adds cosmetic/presentation upgrades but no verified rule or playfield difference. The Pro lacks Grandpa’s Laboratory and therefore plays materially differently. [S4]

* **Rules basis: `code` — v1.28, released 2026-07-01.** [S1][S5]
* Current detailed rules documentation is based on **v1.24**; no source located documents a material gameplay change between 1.24 and 1.28. Treat that as an evidence gap, not proof that the versions are rules-identical. [S2]
* Major multiballs: **Herman Multiball, Raven Multiball, Grandpa Multiball**, and wizard mode **Munster Madness**. [S2]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Start the five family members**—Spot, Herman, Lily, Grandpa and Raven—to qualify **Munster Madness**. [S2]
2. Every family-member start lights **Kitty**; repeatedly cash Kitty to build the timed playfield multiplier **2x → 3x → 6x**. [S2]
3. Use multiballs to safely start Lily, collect chattering teeth, advance Zap, and qualify multiple **Super Jackpots** at Dragula. [S2][S3]
4. **Do not automatically collect Dragula.** Holding the action button during the Super Jackpot collection cancels it, letting the player keep stacking Super Jackpots for a later **6x** cashout. This is the game's major scoring leverage. [S2][S3]
5. **Observed strategy:** Spot first (often just survive/time it out), then Herman → Lily during Herman MB → Grandpa/Raven → Munster Madness. Raven at 6x is itself a strong tournament payoff. [S3][S5]

## Core rules and persistence

Starting all five family-member modes qualifies **Munster Madness** at the scoop. Starting a member also qualifies Kitty and, after the mode ends, a Raven advance at the left ramp. Lily is unusual because its frenzy **can stack with other features**; most other family modes cannot. [S2]

**Kitty** is the principal multiplier: successful collects advance playfield scoring from **2x to 3x to 6x**, lasting roughly a minute; another Kitty hit while running resets its timer. [S2]

**Herman:** complete the hurry-up and magnet lock, then hit the trapped ball with the auto-plunged ball. Making that shot starts **3-ball** Herman Multiball; missing it still starts multiball, but with only two balls. Four jackpots qualify Herman's Super Jackpot. [S2]

**Raven:** three left-ramp advances start 3-ball Raven Multiball. Four different jackpots qualify Raven's Super Jackpot; if the multiball ends early before doing so, a timed left-ramp restart is available. [S2]

**Grandpa, Prem/LE:** chattering-teeth progress lights the scoop for Grandpa’s Laboratory. On the lower playfield, complete both outer orbits then the Machine to start Grandpa Multiball; collect lower-playfield jackpots, then the Machine Jackpot to light Grandpa's Super Jackpot. [S2]

Up to **11 different Prem/LE Super Jackpots can be stacked** at Dragula. Exact stacked-Super multiplier values in the current rulesheet are explicitly marked **“OUT OF DATE,”** so the large scoring leverage is confirmed but the old numerical table should not be quoted as current. [S2]

**Munster Madness** is a 4-ball wizard multiball, with an additional ball active in Grandpa’s Laboratory on Prem/LE. Level 1 cycles through Herman → Raven → Lily requirements, then offers a scoop hurry-up based on the jackpot scoring just earned. [S2]

## Skill shots

* **Normal Skill Shot:** plunge into the fixed right top lane, **or** short-plunge to the right flipper and shoot the left ramp. Base award is 250K, +1 Bonus X, plus remaining-time value. The short plunge is competitively attractive because it also gives immediate flipper control. [S2][S3]
* **Super Skill Shot:** hold the left flipper during the plunge, then shoot the **right ramp** for 500K. [S2]
* **Double Super Skill Shot — Prem/LE:** hold both left-side flipper buttons, shoot the **scoop into Grandpa’s Laboratory**, then make the lower-playfield saucer. Scores **1M** and collects Mystery if lit. [S2]

No ball-number-specific change to these sequences was verified.

## Secondary features

* **Zap button:** after a standup/purple target, press the flashing action button to charge the Zap Meter. A charged meter adds Zap Jackpots to qualifying jackpot shots; enough Zap Jackpots light another Super Jackpot. [S2][S3]
* **Super-Jackpot cancel:** when Dragula begins collecting stacked Supers, holding the action button aborts the collection and preserves the stack. This is strategically crucial. [S2][S3]
* **Add-A-Ball:** spelling DRAGULA during multiball lights Dragula for add-a-ball plus a short ball save; Raven Multiball lights it immediately. [S2]
* **Mystery:** normal play has random awards, but **Competition Mode removes the randomness**; Mystery becomes “Light Kitty.” Dragula/Kitty randomness is likewise normalized. [S2]
* **Extra ball:** can be lit from family-level progress, 110 chattering teeth or Mystery. No universal tournament point-conversion value was verified. [S2]
* Standard start-of-ball saving is operator-adjustable; competitive strategy sources specifically warn that tournament copies may provide no meaningful opening save. [S3]

## What to watch

1. **Kitty flashing / multiplier callout:** tells you the player can move from 2x to 3x to the critical **6x** scoring state.
2. **Dragula reports multiple Super Jackpots lit:** the player may intentionally refuse the immediate cashout to grow the eventual score.
3. **Spot pops up beneath the left ramp:** the next shot is dangerous; a player ignoring it may deliberately be timing Spot out rather than attacking it.
4. **Grandpa’s Laboratory activates:** on Prem/LE, attention shifts to the tiny lower playfield; Grandpa Multiball also advances the five-family wizard progression. [S2][S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:**
**Strategic inference:** prioritize control and completed family starts over risky Super-Jackpot qualification. Spot is particularly reasonable to start and simply survive/time out. Once a strong 6x cashout is available, taking it is safer than repeatedly refusing Dragula in pursuit of an enormous stack.

**Playing behind:**
**Observed strategy:** build multiple Super Jackpots, advance Kitty to **6x**, then cash during Raven or Munster Madness. Use multiball to attack Lily, chattering teeth and Zap progress simultaneously. [S3]

**Key recurring decision:**
**Cash Dragula now or preserve the Super-Jackpot stack.** Holding the action button can pass up a guaranteed award in exchange for more qualifying Supers and/or a later 6x multiplier. That single decision explains many seemingly strange Dragula plays.

## Danger zones

* **Spot:** expert strategy explicitly calls the exposed Spot target a drain threat; many players start the mode and avoid shooting it. [S3]
* **Herman magnet/bash:** rebounds are unpredictable, especially when the magnet releases rather than captures.
* **Left-ramp/Spot transition:** a normally clean ramp shot becomes a direct bash-target attempt when Spot rises.
* **Dragula eject:** the lane uses a dedicated shooter/kicker to fire the ball back into play rather than providing a gentle return. [S4]

## Trivia

* *The Munsters* was Stern's first new title announced in **2019**. [S4]
* Premium and LE contain a complete **secondary lower playfield** with two miniature pinballs and two mini-flippers. [S4]
* The Premium's black-and-white package deliberately references the original television show's monochrome presentation. [S4]
* Only **600 Limited Editions** were produced worldwide. [S4]
* Herman's sculpt is not merely decorative: a magnet physically captures the ball to set up Herman Multiball. [S4]
