## Identity and versions

* **Dungeons & Dragons: The Tyrant’s Eye (Premium)** — Stern Pinball, 2025, SPIKE 2. Lead design **Brian Eddy**; rules/code team includes **Dwight Sullivan, Corey Stup, Elizabeth Gieske, and David Sullivan**; art by **Vince Proce**; music includes original work by **Cris Velasco**. [S1][S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* Premium/LE add the full **multi-axis Rath the Relentless dragon**, which can eject balls from its mouth, plus the **motorized pop-up Dungeon entrance/trap door**. The Gelatinous Cube magnet and between-flipper Shield are also central physical features. [S1][S2] ([Stern Pinball][2])
* Latest verified software: **1.01, July 1, 2026**. The detailed rulesheet is maintained against **1.00**; a secondary release report describes 1.01 as adding Insider Connected achievements rather than major rules changes, but I did not locate an authoritative detailed Stern 1.01 changelog. [S2][S4] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* Commentary-relevant multiballs: **Dragon Multiball → Town Celebration Multiball**, **Gelatinous Cube Multiball**, and the **Tavern Brawl** 2-ball mini-wizard mode. [S2]
* Scope follows the supplied research specification for the **Premium** model. 

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** build toward **Tavern Brawl**: start a **Dungeon Crawl**, **Dragon Multiball**, and **Gelatinous Cube Multiball** during the game. Once all three are checked off, the center spinner starts Tavern Brawl. [S2][S3] ([Tilt Forums][1])
2. Prioritize **Gelatinous Cube Multiball** for immediate scoring: left orbit lowers its ramp, repeated left-ramp shots trap the ball, and additional locks raise the starting jackpot. Current expert guidance considers Cube more lucrative than ordinary Dragon Multiball. **Observed strategy.** [S2][S3]
3. Qualify Dragon by spelling **DRAGON** at Rath’s 3-bank, preferably while a normal town mode is already running; start it at the right orbit and use the multiball to finish safer mode shots. [S2][S3]
4. If Rath is defeated, Dragon immediately becomes **Town Celebration**, whose jackpots scale from how many jackpots were made during Dragon Multiball. [S2]
5. **Big leverage:** Hidden Temple starts **20 seconds of 2× scoring**. Bringing that timer into Cube jackpots or Tavern Brawl is an attractive high-variance play. **Strategic inference.** [S2]

## Core rules and persistence

* Tournament/Competition play starts **without saved campaign progress**, disables legendary equipment, uses a constant random seed, and excludes campaign-unlocked characters. Standard Insider Connected play can otherwise preserve character level, equipment and campaign location across games. [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* **Dragon Multiball:** spell DRAGON at Rath’s 3-bank, then start at the right orbit or side ramp. Premium uses **8 balls** with an unlimited return/save structure, but each drain moves Rath closer to winning; the player must repeatedly expose and hit Rath before the allowed drains are exhausted. [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* Dragon jackpots progress **1× → 2× → 3×** at major shots. Clearing the initial jackpot set lights a Super Jackpot; defeating Rath starts Town Celebration rather than simply ending the feature. [S2]
* **Gelatinous Cube:** left orbit lowers the ramp; repeat the left ramp with the same ball until the Cube captures it. Once captured, the player can start immediately or risk additional locks to increase both ball count and jackpot value. Jackpots rise by **3M each to 25M**; completing the lit set gives a **20M Super Jackpot**. [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* After playing Cube, it can be requalified by **completing a map mode**. [S2]
* **Town Dungeons lock out multiballs**, which is why score-focused tournament strategy often avoids entering one while pursuing Tavern Brawl. [S2][S3]
* **Tavern Brawl:** after starting Dragon, Cube, and Dungeon Crawl, shoot the center spinner. Jackpots rise throughout the 2-ball mode; repeated spinner shots enable **1×/2×/3× looping jackpots on the left ramp**. [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* Hidden Temple scoring is **2× for 20 seconds**, extendable in 5-second chunks by repeating the Secret Door/Temple sequence. [S2]

## Skill shots

* **Upper Loop Skill Shot:** short plunge, then upper loop. **2M × ball number**; Extra Balls increase the multiplier another step. [S2]
* **Cube-switch Skill Shot:** short plunge to the switch near the Gelatinous Cube, directly or through the shortcut loop. Same normal skill-shot scoring. [S2]
* **Super Skill Shot:** left eject → side ramp → right ramp. Starts at **6M**. [S2]
* **MXV Skill Shot:** shoot the **Hidden Temple lane behind the upper flipper**. Starts at **6M**. [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])

These become progressively more valuable by ball number rather than being fixed first-ball-only awards.

## Secondary features

* **Shield:** when its action button flashes, pressing it raises the metal bar between the flippers and can rescue a straight-down-the-middle ball. Return-lane completions relight uses; normally up to two uses can be stored. Skilled players also **“shield stage”** a ball against it to line up the left orbit/left ramp or right ramp. [S2][S3] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* **Ranged attack:** during battles, hold the action button until the moving blue indicator reaches the desired damage shot, then release to spot it. This lets a player avoid a physically dangerous required shot. [S2]
* **Critical Hit:** hit the captive ball twice within 15 seconds. It can spot a battle/mode shot and also advance DRAGON, Cube, or 2× preparation. [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* **Extra Ball:** normally lit at the right ramp through several routes including two map-mode starts, 50 gems, Fizmo, or repeated Dragon Multiballs. Event treatment should be verified. [S2]
* **Competition mode:** removes saved-character advantages and legendary equipment and normalizes randomness with a constant seed. [S2]

## What to watch

1. **DRAGON letters / Rath lowering:** Dragon Multiball is being built; during multiball the remaining letters also communicate Rath’s remaining health. [S2]
2. **Ball suspended in the Gelatinous Cube:** Cube preparation has reached the important risk/reward point—start now or attempt another lock for a larger multiball. [S2]
3. **Center spinner lit for Tavern Brawl:** Dragon, Cube and Dungeon Crawl have all been started; this is the short-format payoff. [S2]
4. **Flashing Shield action button:** the player has an active center-drain rescue and may deliberately use it to stage a difficult shot. [S2][S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** prioritize Cube jackpots or a safely prepared Dragon Multiball rather than spending the ball building every Tavern Brawl checkbox. Ardor the Paladin makes Rath significantly easier to defeat and is the safer class choice when Dragon reliability matters. **Observed strategy.** [S3] ([Kineticist][4])

**Playing behind:** commit to **Tavern Brawl**, preferably with 2× scoring available. During Brawl, build the looping left-ramp jackpot toward 3× and collect the Premium-specific Super Jackpot qualification through the pop-up Dungeon shot. [S2]

**Key recurring decision:** **cash Gelatinous Cube now or keep adding balls/value?** Once the Cube grabs the first ball, starting immediately protects the feature; further locks substantially raise its ceiling but expose the player to a single-ball drain before the multiball starts.

## Danger zones

1. **Left orbit:** essential to Cube progression but explicitly described as one of the game's toughest shots. [S3]
2. **Right-side congestion:** the right orbit/ramp/shortcut geometry is tight; misses frequently break control. [S3][S5]
3. **Premium Dungeon target:** owner reports document hard direct hits rebounding toward the center drain on some copies. **Setup-dependent.** [S5] ([Pinside][5])
4. **Right-ramp wireform return:** some Premium copies can kick a fast ball off the rail toward an outlane; alignment matters. **Setup-dependent.** [S5] ([Pinside][6])
5. **Late Shield activation:** there is a perceptible delay between pressing the action button and the bar raising, so a truly last-second save attempt may fail. [S3]

## Trivia

* Stern released the game during the **50th-anniversary era of Dungeons & Dragons**, whose original tabletop game debuted in 1974. The LE run of **740 games** deliberately references that date. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][7])
* Premium/LE Rath is a **multi-axis animatronic dragon** with impact sensing and can physically launch balls from its mouth. [S1]
* The Gelatinous Cube uses an **electromagnet to suspend a real pinball** inside the translucent sculpt. [S1]
* Premium/LE hide a genuine **motorized trap door / pop-up ball capture** in front of the center spinner for Dungeon Crawl. [S1]
* The voice cast includes **Michael Dorn, Matthew Mercer, Laura Bailey, Marisha Ray, Kevin Smith, Brendon Small, Gerard Way, and Luke Gygax**. [S1]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* The latest verified code is **1.01 (July 1, 2026)** [S4], while the maintained detailed rulesheet identifies itself as based on **1.00** [S2]. A secondary report says 1.01 principally added Insider Connected achievements, but no detailed authoritative Stern 1.01 readme was located.

* [S3] says Competition Install fixes Gelatinous Cube qualification at **two left-ramp shots**, while [S2] describes the normal Easy/Medium/Hard 1–4-shot adjustment without explicitly restating the Competition override. Verify the event copy.
* Older strategy material sometimes says Cube is effectively once per game; the current rulesheet explicitly says it can be **requalified by completing a map mode**. [S2]
* Right-ramp and Dungeon-shot danger is strongly **copy/setup dependent**. [S5]
