## Identity and versions

* **Harry Potter — Collector’s Edition**, Jersey Jack Pinball, **2025**. Lead design: **Eric Meunier**; rules/code: **Joe Katz**. The game covers all eight Warner Bros. Harry Potter films and uses JJP’s full-feature layout with the rotating **Grand Staircase**, Quidditch upper playfield, Death Eater mechanism, and three-ball Wand lock. [S3][S4] ([Jersey Jack Pinball][1])
* The Collector’s Edition adds MinaLima metallic/gold artwork, gold wireforms and an interactive Hogwarts topper with Hedwig, flying Harry, the Snitch and Dark Mark projection. I found **no documented CE-only competitive rules difference** in current rules/code sources. [S3] ([Jersey Jack Pinball][2])
* **Rules basis: `code` — v1.26, released 2026-04-14.** JJP currently distributes 1.26; the official-changelog archive dates it April 14, 2026. [S1][S2] ([Jersey Jack Pinball][3])
* Five primary progression multiballs matter most to commentary: **Explore Hogwarts, Golden Trio, Death Eater, Quidditch World Cup, and O.W.L.s**. Playing four qualifies **Battle of Hogwarts**. [S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

Research scope follows the supplied commentator-reference specification.

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** strongly consider the **Level-1 left-ramp skill shot** early. It immediately advances Quidditch position to **Seeker** and starts a match. Quidditch position drives the large end-of-game bonus; one Snitch at Seeker represents **45M of final bonus** before any multiplayer MVP award. [S4][S6] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
2. **Fast scoring route:** qualify **Explore Hogwarts Multiball**. The first one can require only two qualifying ramp shots, then the Staircase/right ramp starts it. Four jackpots light a roving Super Jackpot; Hagrid after two jackpots gives add-a-ball. [S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
3. Build **Wizard Travel** through combos and, when **Geminio** is available, time its 30-second playfield multiplier around a valuable multiball or cash-out: 2× initially, later 3× and 4×. [S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
4. **Higher-ceiling route:** improve Golden Trio Multiball through **Ollivander’s**, then lock Harry, Ron and Hermione. Its Wand Jackpots and eventual Super Jackpot are harder but award the Cup Horcrux. [S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
5. **Alternate long-game strategy:** school-year progression → Final Exam → **O.W.L.s Multiball**, preferably after completing Flourish & Blotts spells, each of which adds +1× to all O.W.L.s scoring. Competition mode deliberately reduces its base scoring, so this is less universally dominant. [S4][S7] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

## Core rules and persistence

**Explore Hogwarts** is unusually accessible: lower all eight colored banners with the center and Staircase/right ramps; green/yellow begin spotted, so the first qualification can take only two shots. Four jackpots light the moving Super Jackpot, which awards the Ring Horcrux. On Normal settings an uncollected Super remains lit after multiball; this persistence is difficulty-sensitive. [S4][S5] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

**Golden Trio:** spell HARRY, RON and HERMIONE to light three Wand locks. Character jackpots must be relit through their corresponding shots; Wand Jackpots escalate before a relock/Super phase. Ollivander’s can lengthen ball save, raise jackpots, ease Wand Jackpot qualification and add 2×/3× jackpot combinations. [S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

**Death Eater Multiball does not stack normally:** once it begins, **all other game rules are disabled**. That makes choosing it fundamentally different from choosing a multiball intended as a scoring stack. [S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

Quidditch position and points matter after the final ball: Snitches contribute 150 Quidditch points and goals 10, multiplied by position value—100K Beater through **300K Seeker**. In multiplayer, Quidditch MVP adds **10M per player, maximum 40M**, and current Competition settings default that award to **YES**, though it is adjustable. [S4][S5][S7] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

Deep-game note: **Deathly Hallows** can build its own playfield multiplier as high as 8×; both mode progress and that multiplier survive failed attempts. [S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

## Skill shots

Successful skill shots advance the system **Level 1 → Level 2 → Level 3**; the action button cycles the currently lit choice. They are choice-based rather than one fixed plunge. [S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

## Secondary features

* **Fawkes:** repeated right orbits light the **left-outlane save**. If used, the player must hit three unique shots in 20 seconds to actually save the ball; success then opens victory laps/Super Jackpot. [S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
* **Protego:** spelling HARRY lights the **right-outlane gate**. Weak right-spinner/outlane activity during an existing ball save can fire it without consuming the earned protection. [S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
* **Flourish & Blotts video mode:** memorize and enter button sequences. Every completed spell gives **+1× to all O.W.L.s scoring**; completion also awards the Diary Horcrux. [S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
* **Potions / mystery:** three visible awards are assigned to the left ramp, center ramp and right orbit; choosing one requires that shot, then two repeats. Awards include 30-second ball save, Max Geminio Next, Explore jackpots staying lit, Seeker advancement, Spot Snitch, Protego Maxima and Spot Horcrux. [S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
* **Action button:** changes selectable skill shots/Diagon Alley shops and spends Bombtastic Bombs to finish lessons. [S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
* Extra balls have several qualifications; a universal tournament point-conversion value was **not verified**.

## What to watch

1. **Quidditch position—especially Seeker:** this changes the value of every Quidditch point paid after the game and may explain an apparently aggressive first-ball left-ramp choice. [S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
2. **Colored Hogwarts banners atop the display:** they show Explore Hogwarts qualification; once all eight are gone, the Staircase/right ramp starts multiball. [S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
3. **Trophies filling the display:** Geminio is active; all playfield scoring is currently multiplied for its 30-second timer. [S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
4. **Wand locks / character spellouts:** three character locks mean Golden Trio MB is close; a player delaying may be trying to improve it at Ollivander’s first. [S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** **Strategic inference:** prioritize fast Explore Hogwarts, collect an available Super rather than overbuilding, and preserve Fawkes/Protego. A Seeker position already secured makes later Quidditch play valuable without requiring an all-in World Cup attempt.

**Playing behind:** push the multiplier ceiling—Geminio into a developed Golden Trio/Explore opportunity—or chase Quidditch aggressively. At Seeker, Quidditch end-game accounting plus the possible **40M MVP award** can overturn a displayed-score deficit after the final drain. [S4][S6] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

**Key recurring decision:** **take the quick Explore Hogwarts value, or invest in infrastructure**—Seeker position, Ollivander perks, spell multipliers and Golden Trio locks—for a substantially higher ceiling.

## Danger zones

* **Great Hall ramp reject/airball:** documented setup fault can turn a seemingly clean center-ramp shot into an immediate rollback. **Observed/setup-sensitive.** [S8] ([Pinside][5])
* **Grand Staircase reject:** off-center entries can return straight toward the middle instead of entering the mechanism. **Observed/setup-sensitive.** [S8] ([Pinside][6])
* **Quidditch habit-trail overshoot:** repeated fast upper-playfield loops have been reported to launch onto the slings. **Observed/setup-sensitive.** [S8] ([Pinside][6])
* **Death Eater airballs:** explicitly tracked in the owners’ known-issues list. [S8] ([Pinside][7])
