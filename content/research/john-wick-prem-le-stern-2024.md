## Identity and versions

**John Wick — Limited Edition**, Stern Pinball, 2024. Lead design: **Elliot Eismin**; mechanical engineering: Robert Blakeman; artwork: Randy Martinez. Rules/code credits include Tim Sexton on early software, followed by Mike Vinikour and Joshua Henderson. [S2] Stern also credits Ian McShane with custom narration and Anthrax/Pantera drummer Charlie Benante with the original score. [S4] ([TiltForums][1])

* Editions: Pro, Premium, Limited Edition. Premium/LE add substantial physical mechanisms relevant to play, including the moving car and physical locking behavior used by Deconsecrated and Excommunicado Multiballs. [S2]
* **Rules basis: `code` — LE v1.02, released 2026-07-01.** [S1]
* Six recurring qualifying multiballs: **Helipad Showdown, Taxicab Chase, Motorcycle Pursuit, Bagarre à L’Étoile, Excommunicado, Deconsecrated**. Completing the broader progression can qualify **Red Circle Reckoning**, a timed four-ball wizard multiball. [S1][S2]
* v1.02 added the **MXV Super Skill Shot** and changed each ball to begin with at least five enemies by default. [S1]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Start a Job** at the Weapons Crate/right vertical up-kicker (VUK), ideally selecting a faction whose permanent perk helps the planned stack. [S2]
2. **Use multiballs as scoring/utility windows.** Enemies sitting on Job or multiball shots multiply that shot: one/two/three enemies means **2x/3x/4x**. [S1][S2]
3. **Cash the Job at the left eject** for its faction perk and a 20% Job-score cashout; strong competitive priorities include Multiball Extend, Add-A-Ball, and Lights Out support. [S2]
4. **Exploit Lights Out** for a global 2x during a lucrative Job/multiball or cashout window. [S2]
5. Alternate route: build **Adversary Battles** by defeating ten enemies in single-ball play, then either cash the Battle safely or keep playing to raise its scoring multiplier toward 4x. Long-term completion also advances Red Circle Reckoning. [S1][S2]

**Observed strategy:** strong-player guidance favors stacking Jobs with multiballs—particularly Deconsecrated or the stronger Car Chase multiballs—rather than playing features in isolation. [S3] ([Kineticist][2])

## Core rules and persistence

**Confirmed rule:** Jobs start from the right VUK after sufficient Crate/VUK progress. Base time is **60 seconds**; successful shots add 3 seconds. A Job can earn one additional 30-second extension through Crate/VUK progress. Completing its required work lights the **left eject**, which awards 20% of accumulated Job scoring plus that faction's perk. [S2]

Faction perks persist and can materially reshape later balls: **Security Detail → Multiball Extend; Task a Crew → Add-A-Ball; Heist → Lights Out Motion Sensor; Gather Intel → +20 seconds of Lights Out; Assassination → both outlanes lit for Marker Ball Save; Night Watch → +5 seconds to ball saves; VIP Award → 2x starting bonus multiplier each ball.** [S1][S2]

**Enemies are leverage.** Blue enemy circles on a scoring shot make it 2x/3x/4x with one/two/three enemies during Jobs and the six main multiballs. They do **not** provide that multiplier during Adversary Battles. Only enemy kills during single-ball play count toward the ten needed to qualify a Battle. [S1][S2]

Adversary Battles are long, 200-second modes with generous ball save. Once the Escape Jackpot is available, the player may end the Battle or continue; declining the escape increases Battle scoring, ultimately to **4x**. “Be Seeing You” is worth 25M plus 10M end-of-ball bonus, and the first one qualifies an extra ball under normal settings. [S2]

**Lights Out** lasts 30 seconds and doubles scoring globally. If unused, its qualification carries across balls; partial **YAGA** progress does not. [S2]

Red Circle Reckoning requires **a Job, an Adversary Battle, any Car Multiball, Deconsecrated, and Excommunicado**. It is a 60-second four-ball mode: lit arrows score 10M, the final Red Circle scores 30M, and remaining time pays 1M/second. Lights Out can double this scoring. [S1][S2]

## Skill shots

| Skill shot                        | Execution                                                               | Award / strategic reason                                                                                            |
| --------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Baba Yaga**                     | Clean plunge directly into right VUK with **zero** intervening switches | 1.5M and immediately starts the lit Job, bypassing remaining Job-start progress. [S2]                               |
| **Super Crate**                   | Exactly one switch, then right VUK                                      | Advances Crate progress and starts at 750K; useful when preserving Job setup matters. [S2]                          |
| **Crate Skill Shot**              | More than one switch before VUK                                         | Primarily points, starting at 150K; fallback rather than major strategy. [S2]                                       |
| **VIP Skill Shot**                | Plunge through the backdoor into Red Circle                             | Starts at 650K, growing 250K per success; starts Excommunicado immediately if it is already lit. [S2]               |
| **Car Skill Shot** *(Premium/LE)* | Plunge behind the car                                                   | Starts at 850K, growing 250K; advances the next Car Multiball unless one is already lit. [S2]                       |
| **MXV Super Skill Shot**          | Full plunge, then **Right Orbit → Right Ramp → Left Eject**             | **10M plus 10 additional enemies on the next spawn**—a meaningful future-multiplier setup. Added in v1.02. [S1][S2] |

Skill-shot opportunities can also be re-enabled after certain controlled replunges/features; their practical availability therefore is not limited strictly to Ball 1/2/3 openings. [S1]

## Secondary features

* **Marker Ball Save:** hit the left standups five times initially, with the requirement increasing by two after each use. Flippers move the flashing save between outlanes. Once consumed it must be requalified through an Adversary Battle; Assassination makes both outlanes available. An unused Marker save also substantially extends Battle ball-save time. [S2]
* **Normal ball save:** Stern documented a 9-second default start-of-ball save; it is operator-adjustable. [S1]
* **Action button:** depending on qualified perks, a short press controls Lights Out/Motion Sensor while a long press can use **Add-A-Ball**. Multiball Extend, Add-A-Ball and Swipe-A-Ball are mutually constrained so they cannot all be harvested in one multiball session. [S1]
* **Extra balls:** Stern's Competition Install disables actual extra balls. Current rules award **10M** instead when an extra ball is disabled; Lights Out can double that value. [S1][S2]
* **Tick Tock, Mr. Wick** is now a topper-dependent Challenge Mode and, as of v1.02, is not part of normal main-game progression. [S1]

## What to watch

1. **Blue circles at shot arrows:** one/two/three enemies means the next relevant Job/MB award there is 2x/3x/4x. A ringing phone and flashing circles signal a new enemy spawn. [S2]
2. **Left eject lights:** often means a Job cashout or Adversary opportunity is ready; this frequently explains an abrupt switch from ramps/orbits to the eject. [S2]
3. **Playfield suddenly goes dark:** Lights Out is running—global 2x, with most normal insert information deliberately obscured. [S2]
4. **Car hits after Car Multiball is lit:** the player may be cycling which of the four Car Chase multiballs will start rather than merely taking incidental points. [S2]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:**
**Strategic inference:** favor completed Job cashouts and permanent perks, preserve Marker Ball Save, and take an available Battle Escape rather than unnecessarily pushing its multiplier. Starting a ready multiball can protect scoring progress instead of waiting for a perfect stack.

**Playing behind:**
**Observed strategy:** build a high-ceiling **Job + multiball + enemies + Lights Out** stack. Helipad and especially **Bagarre à L’Étoile** offer substantial Car-Multiball upside; continuing an Adversary Battle instead of escaping can push its multiplier toward 4x. [S2][S3]

**Key recurring decision:**
**Cash out now versus improve the stack.** A good player may deliberately delay a Job cashout, Battle escape, or multiball start to position enemies/multipliers first. Conversely, if only a few enemy kills remain before Battle Ready, doing them **before** multiball matters because multiball kills do not advance Battle qualification. [S2][S3]

## Danger zones

* **Right Orbit:** very fast return; strong-player guidance recommends a parry or controlled flipper response rather than casually catching it. [S3]
* **Car:** behaves like a kicker target; awkward hits can rebound violently. Right-flipper approaches tend to be safer. [S3]
* **Red Circle:** confined target area can produce unpredictable rebounds, especially on Premium/LE hardware. [S3]
* **Left Orbit:** valuable but specifically called out as difficult to recover from cleanly. [S3]
* **Center Ramp:** steep; a controlled right-flipper shot is preferable to an uncontrolled attempt. [S3]
