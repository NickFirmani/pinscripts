## Identity and versions

* **Game of Thrones (Pro)** — Stern Pinball, October 2015; first-generation **SPIKE** platform, DMD, 2 flippers. Scope is the Pro specified in the supplied brief. 
* Design: **Steve Ritchie**; rules/software team led by **Dwight Sullivan**, with Corey Stup, Lonnie Ropp, Mike Kyzivat, Tanio Klyce and Waison Cheng; Pro artwork credited to **Dave Link and Greg Freres**. [S1][S4] ([Pinside][1])
* **Latest verified code: V1.37, July 29, 2019.** This is strategically important: the 2019 updates added unique House action-button powers, made Targaryen selectable, substantially rebalanced multipliers/Houses, and added dedicated Competition Install settings. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* Pro omits the Premium/LE **Castle upper playfield, upper flippers and Iron Throne elevator**; instead it has the direct **Dragon** shot used for Mystery, Extra Ball and Battle for the Wall start. [S1][S4] ([Stern Pinball][3])
* Major multiballs/wizard multiballs useful to commentary: **Blackwater**, **Battle for the Wall**, **Winter Has Come**, **Hand of the King**, and **Iron Throne**. [S4]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Choose a House deliberately.** **Martell** is the straightforward tournament choice because its action button adds a ball once per ball during multiball; **Tyrell** is the higher-scoring alternative because its lit inlane raises shot multipliers. [S4][S5] ([Tilt Forums][4])
2. **Qualify two House Battles before starting them**, then bring both into **Blackwater Multiball**. Battles are qualified by three hits/completions at their associated shots; the left ramp starts up to two together. **Observed strategy:** modes + Blackwater is the basic tournament stack. [S2][S4][S5] ([Primetime Amusements][5])
3. **Build multipliers before the payoff.** Battering Ram hits start **Playfield X up to 5×** once Swords have raised its cap; flowing combos build separate **shot multipliers up to 5×**. They multiply together, making a nominal shot potentially **25×**. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][4])
4. In Blackwater, complete the five major jackpots, then repeatedly hammer the **Battering Ram Super Jackpot** during its 20-second window. Wildfire increases Blackwater jackpot value. [S4]
5. Alternate objective: complete **four Houses for Hand of the King**. A good HOTK can dwarf ordinary mode scoring and is often the deeper tournament payoff. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][4])

## Core rules and persistence

* Each House has an associated shot. Three completions qualify its Battle; the left ramp opens **Choose Your Battle**, where normally up to **two qualified Houses can be stacked**. Greyjoy may only play one at once. Failed Battles remain qualified; some save internal progress while others restart. [S2][S4]
* Completing **3 Houses lights Extra Ball; 4 lights Hand of the King; all Houses light Iron Throne.** [S4] ([Tilt Forums][4])
* **Blackwater:** right 2-bank lights locks; left ramp locks three balls. Five major jackpots light the Battering Ram for repeated timed Super Jackpots. Later rounds require more jackpot hits and raise scoring. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][4])
* **Playfield multiplier:** Ram sequence starts 2× and can reach 5×. Initially capped at **3×**; collected Swords raise the cap to 4× and 5×. Higher multipliers run for less time; Ram hits refresh/advance them. [S4]
* **Shot/combo multipliers** are independent of Playfield X. Making flowing shots raises other shots' multipliers; Tyrell's controllable lit inlane is especially valuable because it raises them without another risky major shot. [S4][S5]
* **Winter Is Coming:** qualifying Houses “ices” their shots. Three iced shots starts a hurry-up; collect four hurry-ups to start **Winter Has Come**, a 4-ball multiball. [S4]
* **Battle for the Wall, Pro:** complete the two top lanes **six times**, then shoot the Dragon to start. Later Wall Multiballs require more lane completions. [S4]
* **Easy commentator trap:** Bonus X, shot multiplier, and Playfield X are three distinct systems.

## Skill shots

* **Pro Skill Shot:** hard-plunge through the currently lit top lane. Award is **500K × ball number plus +1 Bonus X**. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][4])
* Lane choice can be changed with the flipper buttons before the plunge.
* **Observed tournament alternative:** some strong players intentionally soft-plunge without validating the playfield, attempting to gain controlled possession before the normal ball saver starts. This is a control tactic, **not another coded skill shot**. [S5] ([Kineticist][6])

## Secondary features

* **House action button powers, V1.37:** Martell = Add-A-Ball; Stark = instantly complete one running House mode for reduced points; Baratheon = short Lord of Light outlane saves; Lannister = purchase Playfield X with Gold; Tyrell = cash active multipliers into Iron Bank points; Targaryen = freeze non-ball-save timers. Greyjoy steals the most recently defeated House's button power. [S3][S4] ([Pinside][7])
* **Lord of Light:** normally completing the left drop bank lights saves at both outlanes, generally only once from the bank per game. **Competition Install turns this award off.** [S4][S6] ([Tilt Forums][4])
* **ReelPops:** pop bumpers cycle software awards including Wall progress, Wildfire, multiplier increases, Swords and Add-A-Ball. [S4]
* **Mystery / Gold:** completing gold targets lights the Dragon. Gold buys from two offered awards; valuable possibilities include Playfield X, Super Jackpot, mode progress, points and House action powers. [S4]
* **Video mode** can be purchased through Mystery. [S4]
* Factory normal ball save is **5 seconds**. Competition Install instead explicitly sets Blackwater/Wall to **6s**, HOTK to **8s**, and Winter/Iron Throne to **10s**. [S1][S6] ([Betson Enterprises][8])
* **Competition Install disables Extra Balls.** [S6]

## What to watch

* **Two House shields flashing / left ramp lit:** expect a double Battle start; if Blackwater is nearly ready, the player is probably building a stack.
* **2×–5× inserts lit at the Ram:** Playfield X is running. This is when jackpots, Swords, mode collects and Mystery point awards can explode. [S4]
* **Small multiplier numbers beneath major shots:** these are combo/shot multipliers, separate from Playfield X. A 5× shot during 5× playfield is the 25× scoring state.
* **Three balls locked / left ramp ready:** Blackwater is imminent; if the player shoots another qualifying House first, they're deliberately delaying multiball to improve the stack.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** Choose **Martell** for multiball insurance, start already-qualified Battles rather than overbuilding the stack, and cash large scoring opportunities while Playfield X is active. Avoid naked Ram and right-bank shots when the existing stack is enough to win. **Strategic inference.**

**Playing behind:** **Tyrell** or Lannister can produce much larger multiplier leverage. Stack two compatible Battles with Blackwater, raise Playfield X, then attack Blackwater Supers or a large mode cash-out. Tyrell's Iron Bank itself has been documented around the **hundreds-of-millions** ceiling under extreme multiplier states. [S4][S5]

**Key recurring decision:** **Start now or keep stacking?** GoT pays extraordinarily well for Battles + multiball + shot multiplier + Playfield X, but every extra setup shot occurs during dangerous single-ball play.

## Danger zones

* **Right 2-bank:** essential to Blackwater but a hard standup rebound directly above the right outlane. Strong players often exploit initial ball save to hit it. [S5]
* **Left ramp from the left flipper:** looks tempting but weak attempts can reject toward the center drain; right-flipper shots are generally preferred. [S5]
* **Battering Ram:** highly valuable but inherently dangerous as a direct central bash target. [S5]
* **Dragon eject/catch:** the ball can leave the mechanism with enough unpredictability to require immediate reaction; setup strength matters.
* **Pop-bumper/orbit exits:** repeated orbit play is controllable going up, but the return through the pops can rapidly send the ball toward either sling/outlane.

## Trivia

* **Steve Ritchie** designed the playfield; the fast ramp/orbit geometry is particularly characteristic of his style. [S4][S7]
* **Rory McCann**, who played Sandor “The Hound” Clegane on HBO's series, recorded custom speech for the machine. [S7] ([Pinside][1])
* GoT was one of Stern's earliest **SPIKE-system** games. [S1][S7]
* Stern's **ReelPops** use the pop bumpers to spin virtual reels on the DMD and award game resources instead of making the pops purely points-based. [S7] ([Stern Pinball][9])
* The large **2019 V1.36/V1.37 update arrived nearly four years after release** and fundamentally changed House selection/powers rather than merely fixing bugs. [S3]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Old tournament advice is partly stale.** Pre-2019 guides strongly favor Martell/Tyrell under the old shared Iron Bank rules [S2][S5]. V1.36/1.37 gave every House a distinct action ability and made Targaryen selectable, substantially broadening strategy. [S3]
* **Competition Install matters enormously:** it disables Lord of Light from the drop bank, makes multiplier qualification harder, always times out the right 2-bank, restricts Stark/Lannister/Targaryen powers, shortens multiball saves and disables Extra Balls. [S3][S6] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* **Targaryen:** standard V1.37 can begin with all three dragon stages completed, but Competition Install's Hard setting starts with only **two** completed. [S3]
* **Physical feeds** on the Dragon, Ram and target banks depend heavily on pitch, coil strength and post/outlane setup.
