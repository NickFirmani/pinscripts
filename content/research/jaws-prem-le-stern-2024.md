## Identity and versions

* **JAWS — Limited Edition**, Stern, **2024**, SPIKE 2. Design: **Keith Elwin**; code/rules: **Rick Naegele and Elizabeth Gieske**; mechanics: **Harrison Drake**; art: **Michael Barnard**; sound: **Jerry Thompson**. [S1][S3] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Pro, Premium and LE share the core rules. Premium/LE uniquely add the **motorized shark/boat mechanism and Orca upper playfield** with lookout ramp, wheel spinner and radio target. LE is otherwise primarily a presentation/collector upgrade and was limited to **1,000 games**. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* **Rules basis: `code` — v1.02.0, released 2026-07-01.** This is the current all-game release for Pro/Premium/LE. Its gameplay changes are mostly fixes; the large v1.00 update added the finished final wizard mode, **Smile, You Son of A…** [S2] ([Pinside][2])
* Important multiballs: **Jaws Multiballs 1–3**, **Bounty Hunt/Celebration Multiballs**, **Rescue/Search Multiball**, **Night Search**, and later wizard multiballs. [S3]
* Research scope follows the supplied commentator-reference specification.

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed tournament strategy:** on Premium/LE, get **Binoculars** early—often via the Orca wheel’s Light Gear award—then use them to accelerate beachgoer rescue and close beaches toward **Rescue Multiball**. [S4] ([JLP's Pinball Cards][3])
2. Start and complete **Shark Encounters**. Completing one permanently increases the base **Machete shot multiplier**, and encounters are among the game’s largest early scoring opportunities. [S3][S5] ([TiltForums][4])
3. Use **Jaws Multiball 1 as protection**, ideally stacking it with harder encounters such as Night Swim or Beach Panic instead of treating its jackpots as the main objective. [S5] ([Kineticist][5])
4. Time a lit **Machete** for a high-value single shot: Encounter Jackpot, Beach Closed hurry-up, Feeding Frenzy cash-out, or a large chum-line contribution. [S3][S5] ([TiltForums][4])
5. **Alternate route:** start the easy **Mako bounty**, complete its persistent objectives, then capture it in Bounty Hunt Multiball; its reward makes later bounties easier. [S3][S5] ([TiltForums][4])

## Core rules and persistence

**Shark Encounters:** enough boat/shark hits light five encounter starts. On Premium/LE, further shark hits can add time, light Gear, then enable **2× encounter scoring**. Completing the encounter at the fin scores its full Encounter Jackpot and permanently adds **+1× to base Machete**; settling for the backup collect does not. [S3] ([TiltForums][4])

**Machete:** completing the left target bank lights a return lane; crossing it multiplies the **next made shot**, starting at 2×. Base X rises permanently from completed encounters, certain first-attempt bounties and Night Search; temporary boosts can raise it further. Once base X reaches 4×, the harpoon lane is additionally required to requalify it. [S3] ([TiltForums][4])

**Beaches:** three rescued beachgoers at one major shot start a Beach Closed hurry-up. Its collected value permanently boosts scoring at that beach until Rescue/Search Multiball resets it; the fifth hurry-up aggregates prior hurry-up values and then forces Rescue or Search. **The fifth hurry-up cannot stack with another encounter or multiball.** [S3] ([TiltForums][4])

**Bounty Hunts** are untimed background objectives and **persist ball-to-ball**, but their task progress only advances during single-ball play. Completing the task starts a Bounty Hunt Multiball at the reel. Failed capture attempts can be revisited with reduced requirements. [S3] ([TiltForums][4])

**Jaws Multiball:** chum → collect red chum-line shots → load/attach barrel at the fin → shoot a yellow start shot. The first requires one barrel; later versions require two and three. **Jaws MB1/2 can support other scoring objectives; Jaws MB3/Cage Dive is mutually exclusive.** [S3][S5] ([TiltForums][6])

## Skill shots

| Skill shot       | How                                                                                | Award / why choose it                                                    |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Level 1**      | Plunge into pop bumper                                                             | **5M**; simplest option.                                                 |
| **Level 2**      | Plunge through lane above mini-flipper without bumper                              | **10M + Light Gear**; strategically valuable early.                      |
| **Level 3**      | Clean plunge to flippers, then hit flashing left-bank target before another switch | **15M + Light Gear**.                                                    |
| **Secret**       | Clean plunge, then chum bucket or either side of center captive-ball area          | Current skill-shot value; alternate route when normal plunge is awkward. |
| **Super Secret** | Clean plunge directly into **dead center** of captive-ball area                    | Awards **and advances** current skill-shot level.                        |

Every successful normal skill shot also adds **5 seconds of ball save**; level advances after success and there are at most three attempts per ball. Premium/LE shark position can temporarily prevent the captive-ball secret shots. [S3] ([TiltForums][4])

## Secondary features

* **Life Ring:** two hits to its target qualify a manually activated **left-outlane save**. Press the action button shortly before the ball reaches the outlane; current v1.02 includes fixes to its grace-period behavior. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][2])
* **Flip-Lock:** during multiball, hold a ball on the mini-flipper and hold the action button to trap it temporarily. The Thresher bounty perk makes scoring **2× while Flip-Locked**; Tiger can make Flip-Lock reusable. [S3][S5] ([TiltForums][6])
* **Orca Wheel — Premium/LE:** spin the upper wheel to light an award and use the action button to select it. Awards include **Light Gear and +3× temporary Machete**, both potentially major tournament leverage. [S3] ([TiltForums][4])
* **Video mode:** Fish Finder can light a video recreation of the film’s shark arcade game. It can contain an extra ball. [S3][S5] ([Kineticist][5])
* **Extra balls:** awarded at milestones including 8 beachgoers and 3 Quickshots; when disabled they score **15M**, and that compensation can itself be multiplied by Machete. [S3] ([TiltForums][4])

## What to watch

1. **Machete return-lane insert lit:** the next made shot can be multiplied; look for the player to steer the lane and then deliberately cash a hurry-up/jackpot. [S3]
2. **Shark rises before an Encounter:** on LE, repeated shark hits are building Encounter Boosts—most importantly the eventual **2× mode scoring**. [S3]
3. **Orange beachgoer inserts / Beach Closed hurry-up:** this signals the beach-rescue strategy; the fifth closure immediately leads to Rescue/Search MB. [S3]
4. **Red chum line → yellow arrows → raised fin:** Jaws Multiball is being built; a barrel hit is the key transition from setup to ready multiball. [S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** **Strategic inference:** favor Scars/Raft Attack over the harder encounters, take Machete on a known valuable shot rather than building exotic stacks, and use Jaws MB1 as ball protection. Binoculars → Beach closures → Rescue MB is a comparatively structured route. [S4][S5]

**Playing behind:** seek **2× Encounter Boost + high Machete**, or a heavily multiplied Beach Closed hurry-up. Quickshots are another legitimate swing source: strong repeated harpoon/fin combos can reach very large values, though missing resets their internal multiplier. [S5] ([Kineticist][5])

**Key recurring decision:** **cash the scoring opportunity now, or keep setting up multipliers/resources?** JAWS rewards preparation—Gear, Encounter Boosts, Machete, beach values—but its dangerous captive balls and tight reel/right-ramp shots make every extra setup shot costly.

## Danger zones

* **Fin / shark captive-ball area:** ricochets from the settling fin can head directly toward the flippers or center drain. [S5] ([Kineticist][5])
* **Mini-flipper lane exit:** normally feeds the right inlane, but some copies can send it directly toward the **right outlane**. [S5] ([Kineticist][5])
* **Wave-ramp reject:** needs power; weak shots can return rapidly into open play. [S5]
* **Fishing reel:** very tight shot; repeated misses while pursuing a bounty can expose the player to unnecessary center traffic. [S5]
* **Left outlane:** a save may exist, but only if the player remembers to deploy **Life Ring** at the correct moment. [S3][S5]

* Stern integrated material from **all four JAWS films**, not only Spielberg’s original. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* **Richard Dreyfuss** recorded custom speech, alongside John Williams’ famous score. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][7])
* Premium/LE’s shark physically bursts upward through **Ben Gardner’s boat**, while the upper Orca carries its own ramp and horizontal steering-wheel spinner. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][8])
* The 2025 50th-anniversary update finally added **Smile, You Son of A…**, the three-phase final wizard mode; completing it launches Super Celebration Multiball. [S2] ([Pinside][9])
