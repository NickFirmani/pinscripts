## Identity and versions

* **Flash Gordon** — Bally Manufacturing, February 1981; model **1215**, Bally MPU **AS-2518-35** solid-state platform. Design by **Claude Fernandez**, software by **Rehman Merchant**, art by **Kevin O’Connor**. Production: **10,000 units**. [S1][S5] ([Pinside][1])
* Signature layout: **split-level playfield**, three flippers, two spinners, multiple drop-target banks, and a **bidirectional center saucer** that alternately ejects to the upper and lower levels. No multiball. [S2] ([Silverball Rules][2])
* There is no modern official revision history analogous to later games. Stock rules are heavily **DIP-switch/configuration sensitive**, especially bonus/mode memory and Extra Balls. Modern replacement code such as **Flash Gordon 2021/2024** substantially changes the rules, so tournament machines should be checked for stock versus custom code. [S1][S6] ([manualzz.com][3])
* Scope follows the supplied commentator-reference specification. 

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** start on the **right inline drops**. The second target lights **2× Bonus**, the third lights **3×**, and completing the bank lights Extra Ball at the standup behind it if enabled. [S1][S2][S3] 
2. Then complete the **upper 1-2-3 bank for 4× Bonus** and the **lower-left four-bank for 5×**. The lower bank also lights the left spinner on its first completion and the right spinner on its second. [S1][S2] 
3. Build the **50K Mini Bonus** upstairs and/or **100K Super Bonus** downstairs. Those achievements also qualify **2× and 3× playfield scoring** at the center saucer. [S1]
4. If both are ready, shoot the saucer for **15 seconds of 5× playfield scoring**. Lit spinners and an already-prepared **Collect Bonus** become the major cash-out shots. [S1][S2] ([GameEx Online][4])
5. **Safer alternate:** once a spinner is lit, repeatedly shoot it rather than forcing more dangerous target progress. Competitive references explicitly prioritize the lit spinners. [S3][S4] ([Tilt Forums][5])

## Core rules and persistence

* Flash Gordon has **two separate regular bonuses**, upper and lower, each building from **1K to 19K**. The right inline drops advance Bonus X: second inline = 2×, third = 3×; after that the upper 3-bank gives 4× and lower-left four-bank gives 5×. [S1] 
* The **50K Mini Bonus** is earned by completing the flashing 1-2-3 objectives upstairs. The **100K Super Bonus** requires the flashing lower-left drops plus matching-color right targets/inlanes. Both are paid at the drain; expert documentation notes they are **not multiplied by normal Bonus X**. [S1][S2] ([GameEx Online][4])
* Mini Bonus lights **2× playfield**; Super Bonus lights **3× playfield**. Shoot the saucer to start the timer; if both are ready simultaneously the game runs **5× scoring for 15 seconds**. [S1] 
* The upper 3-bank also has repeated-completion progression: first completion lights the upper bumper for 5K, second lights the upper **Collect Bonus** target, third lights Special. [S1]
* The lower-left four-bank progresses: first completion lights left spinner, second lights right spinner, then increasingly valuable saucer awards; fourth completion can light an Extra Ball and fifth lights outlane Specials. [S1] 
* Knocking down the solitary upper drop target lights the **shooter lane to collect the lower bonus**. This is important because a bonus can therefore be cashed during the ball rather than merely waited for at the drain. [S1][S2]
* **Persistence is adjustment-sensitive.** Bonus multipliers, Mini/Super progress, saucer awards, Extra Ball and Specials all have liberal/conservative memory switches. Do not assume progress carries between balls without checking the game. [S1]

## Skill shots

**Upper 1-2-3 Skill Shot** — plunge into the upper playfield and hit the **currently lit target in the 3-bank**; the lit arrow cycles during the plunge. [S2] ([Kineticist][6])

* The useful award is **progress toward the flashing 1-2-3 set**, which ultimately lights the 50K Mini Bonus and 2× playfield qualification.
* A soft/controlled plunge is preferred because it leaves a better attack on the upper targets. **Observed strategy.** [S4] ([Pinball for Mortals!][7])
* No separate verified super, alternate, or secret skill shot exists on **stock code**. Modern Flash Gordon 2021/2024 ROMs add a different explicit shooter-lane skill shot and must not be confused with original rules. [S6] ([Tmurren][8])

## Secondary features

* **Ball save:** none found on stock software.
* **Extra Balls:** completing the right inline bank lights the standup behind it for Extra Ball; the fourth lower-left-bank completion can also light an Extra Ball at the saucer. Original settings allow replay/Extra Ball/novelty-point substitutions, so tournament treatment matters. [S1] 
* The original manual documents Novelty alternatives of **25K or 50K** for several Extra Ball/Special awards depending on operator configuration. [S1]
* **Center saucer:** alternates its eject **lower → upper → lower → upper**; flashing lamps indicate the upcoming direction. This matters both tactically and for anticipating the next dangerous feed. [S2] ([Silverball Rules][2])
* No video mode, action button, lane change, Magna-Save, or other player-controlled utility.

## What to watch

1. **2× / 3× / 4× / 5× Bonus lamps:** quickly tells how far the player has progressed through the target sequence. [S1]
2. **2× and 3× playfield lamps at the saucer:** both lit means a saucer shot starts the huge **5× / 15-second** scoring window. [S1]
3. **Lit spinner:** first lower-left-bank completion lights left spinner; second lights right. A strong player may immediately abandon target progression and start ripping it. [S1][S3]
4. **Collect Bonus lamp in the shooter lane or upper target:** if the player has also built 5× Bonus or a playfield multiplier, that seemingly odd shot may be the biggest scoring opportunity on the ball. [S1][S2]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** get useful Bonus X and a lit spinner, then favor spinner shots and upper-playfield safety rather than forcing every target-bank objective. Avoid chasing the elaborate 100K Super Bonus or perfect 5× playfield stack unless the score demands it. **Strategic inference**, consistent with competitive strategy sources favoring inline multipliers and lit spinners. [S3]

**Playing behind:** build toward the full **5× playfield window**, preferably with a spinner lit and a large Collect Bonus ready. That combination can compress what would normally take many shots into one enormous 15-second scoring burst. [S1][S2]

**Key recurring decision:** **cash the spinner/bonus state already built, or continue shooting dangerous drops to improve the multiplier?** A player at 3× with a lit spinner may sensibly score now; another may gamble on the upper and lower banks to reach 5× and then light playfield X.

## Danger zones

1. **Right-spinner / left-ramp returns:** both can come directly through the middle after crossing between levels. [S2] ([Silverball Rules][2])
2. **Center-saucer downward eject:** can strike the left bumper/rubber sequence and finish in the right outlane or center. [S2]
3. **Pop-bumper exits:** Flash Gordon's confined lower playfield produces drains to **either outlane or straight down the middle**. [S2]
4. **Right inline targets:** extremely valuable, but direct target rebounds are a major drain source. [S2]
5. **Upper-bank ricochets:** the upper field is cramped; proactive nudging is often needed to steer rebounds toward the upper flipper rather than back downstairs uncontrolled. [S2][S3]

## Spoken commentary cues

* “Second inline target—that’s **double bonus**; the third gets them to triple.”
* “Upper bank is done, so they’ve reached **four-times bonus**; the lower-left bank is the last step to five.”
* “Both playfield multipliers are ready—one saucer shot starts **fifteen seconds of five-times scoring**.”
* “The spinner is lit now; that’s why they’ve stopped shooting targets and switched to repeated spinner shots.”
* “That single upper drop just lit the shooter lane to **collect the lower bonus during the ball**.”
* “This saucer is ejecting downward next—that return can be much more dangerous than the upper feed.”

## Trivia

* Flash Gordon was **Bally’s first production split-level pinball machine**. [S5] ([Pinside][1])
* It was the first Bally game to use the **Squawk & Talk** sound board. [S5]
* It was Bally’s **second production game with speech**, following *Xenon*. [S5]
* The backbox originally incorporated a conspicuous **strobe lamp behind the Flash Gordon logo**; some production machines lack it. [S5] ([Pinside][1])
* Despite the unusually deep rules for 1981, the game has **no multiball**—its major scoring leverage comes from stacking bonus and timed playfield multipliers. [S1][S5]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Playfield multiplier timer:** the Bally manual explicitly specifies **15 seconds** [S1]; one secondary mini-guide states **30 seconds** [S4]. The primary manual takes precedence: use **15 seconds** unless the venue machine demonstrates modified/custom behavior. 
* **Persistence is not universal.** The stock game exposes separate memory switches for Bonus X, 1-2-3 progress, lower colored objectives, saucer awards, Extra Balls and Specials. [S1]
* Modern **Flash Gordon 2021/2024** software retains many original concepts but adds new rules, skill shots, hurry-ups and wizard progression. It should be treated as a materially different ruleset. [S6]
* Physical feeds are strongly copy-dependent; tournament reports and expert guides consistently describe Flash Gordon as particularly sensitive to spinner, saucer and target returns. [S2][S4]
