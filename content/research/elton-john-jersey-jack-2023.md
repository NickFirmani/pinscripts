## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** take **Spot Star** on the skill shot, then prioritize the active **song**. Lit notes advance three Wardrobe items; the first gives 30 seconds of 2× song scoring, the second doubles five note advances, and the third opens lucrative Victory Laps. [S3][S4] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)
2. Build **Crocodile Rock MB** as the early protection multiball. Strong-player guidance is to use its ball safety to attack the dangerous **Rocka-Box drops/albums** rather than obsess over Croc jackpots. [S4] [Kineticist](https://www.kineticist.com/news/elton-john-tutorial)
3. If **Rocket Man MB** is close, delay or sequence the starts so Croc + Rocket overlap: they are the only two main multiballs that stack, and **both sets of jackpots become 2×** during the overlap. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)
4. Spell **ELTON JOHN** for Signature Stage. For tournament safety, 1970/1975 offer longer ball saves; the harder 2009/2023 versions trade protection for larger scoring. Looping the side ramp multiplies Signature Stage Super Jackpots. [S3][S4] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)
5. **Long-game alternate:** collect four Milestones to reach **Final Tour**, or use Croc/Rocket MBs to finish albums and Encore Modes toward the very large **Champ Multiball**. [S3][S4] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)

## Core rules and persistence

Songs have three note levels—yellow, orange, red. Each completed level lights a Wardrobe item at **Backstage**. The items are the main song leverage: 30-second Double Song, five shots of double note progress, then Victory Laps worth millions per shot. Collecting all three quickly can carry Double Song into the Victory Laps. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)

**Crocodile Rock:** Tiny Dancer lights Croc locks; first MB needs two locks, later ones three. Croc Supers can be doubled by quickly locking another ball at the Crocodile after the Super. Two Supers—or one doubled Super—earns a Milestone. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)

**Rocket Man:** 500 fuel lights the first MB, but continuing to fuel it before starting adds perks: longer protection, easier Super qualification, another ball/Milestone, and eventually permanently lit jackpots/Super. This creates a real **start-now versus keep-fueling** decision. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)

**Signature Stage:** spell all nine ELTON JOHN stars, then lock three balls at the piano. These multiballs **cannot stack with other multiballs**. 1970 is easiest; 2023 has no normal ball save. All versions eventually light the side ramp for Supers; repeated side-ramp loops raise the Super multiplier. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)

**Milestones:** any **four of ten** light Final Tour; additional Milestones make Final Tour dramatically easier and richer. Final Tour starts at 4M-per-city with four Milestones and can reach a 25M base with all ten. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)

Normal play allows **SUPERSTAR letters from Signature Stage Supers to persist across games**. JJP’s Competition Mode globally disables carry-over features, so do not assume that cross-game progress exists in tournament configuration. [S3][S5] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)

## Skill shots

- **Selectable Skill Shot:** time the plunge over one of five moving awards: **200K, +1 Bonus X, Spot Star, +50 Rocket Fuel, Advance Croc MB**. **Observed strategy:** Spot Star is generally strongest early; fuel is useful when close to a tank threshold. [S3][S4] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)
- **Super Skill Shot:** short plunge to the upper flipper, then make **Backstage or side ramp**. It upgrades the selected award; repeated Supers increase its perk multiplier. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)
- **Rocka-Box Skill Shot:** full plunge resets/lights the center drops; hit any target to **instantly collect the next album**. Particularly attractive later when normal album qualification has become tedious. [S3][S4] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)
- Skill-shot selection gets progressively faster and eventually erratic as successes accumulate. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)

## Secondary features

- **Add-a-ball:** after the multiball saver expires, the left standup lights for add-a-ball. A Super Jackpot can relight it, and collecting during the post-MB grace period can actually **revive the multiball**. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)
- **Honky Cat Kickback:** qualify the hurry-up near the left eject, finish it to bank a left-outlane kickback; up to **three** can be stored. A failed kickback receives compensation save logic. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)
- **Action button:** selects the desired Signature Stage era; in Final Tour it can abort the mode at entry or **cash the currently active region**. If the ball is headed out, mashing it can preserve otherwise-lost Final Tour value. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)
- **Extra Balls:** qualified by piano-ramp progress, Rocka-Box frenzy performance, or five unique combos. I found no authoritative universal tournament point conversion. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)

## What to watch

1. **Wardrobe icons:** one item means Double Song; two means accelerated progress; all three means Victory Laps—the player’s song has become a real scoring window. [S3]
2. **Rocket fuel meter:** a lit Rocket Man start does not mean the player should take it immediately; continued fueling materially upgrades the MB. [S3]
3. **Croc + Rocket both running:** jackpots are now **doubled across both multiballs**. [S3]
4. **Side-ramp E-ticket / Signature Stage Super:** the same difficult upper-flipper shot is either starting a wizard feature or multiplying a Super Jackpot. [S3][S4] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** **Strategic inference:** finish the current song/wardrobe opportunity, take the accessible Croc MB, use its safety for album progress, and prefer 1970/1975 Signature Stage rather than gambling on 2023’s unprotected scoring.

**Playing behind:** push Rocket fuel past the minimum, stack **Rocket + Croc for doubled jackpots**, or choose a harder Signature Stage and loop the side ramp for multiplied Supers. A developed Champ Multiball or Final Tour is easily large enough to reverse a match. [S3][S4] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/jersey-jack/elton-john-rulesheet)

**Key recurring decision:** **cash the ready multiball now, or keep setting up value first?** Rocket fuel, songs/Wardrobe, Croc qualification and Encore progress all reward waiting—but every extra setup shot risks losing the ball.

## Danger zones

- **Side ramp:** difficult late upper-flipper shot; a miss drops the ball out of the controlled upper-flipper sequence. [S4]
- **Rocka-Box drops:** valuable but poor single-ball tournament targets; expert strategy specifically recommends attacking them under multiball protection. [S4]
- **Backstage attempt:** usually best reached from right ramp → upper flipper; missing the early flip sacrifices a valuable song-scoring opportunity. [S4]
- **Rocket return:** the VUK launches around the backboard toward the left flipper—expect an immediate recovery rather than a cradle. [S4]
- **Fast ramp feeds:** both main ramps feed flippers directly rather than gently settling through ordinary inlanes, making this an intentionally fast-flowing Ritchie layout. [S4] [Kineticist](https://www.kineticist.com/news/elton-john-tutorial)
