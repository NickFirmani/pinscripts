## Identity and versions

**Pulp Fiction — Special Edition (SE)** — Chicago Gaming Company / Play Mechanix, **2023**. Retro-styled solid-state game with **13-segment alphanumeric LED displays, two flippers, no ramps, seven drop targets, two spinners, a gun magnet, physical Briefcase lock and Pawn Shop subway lock**. Design: **Mark Ritchie**; rules/software: **Josh Sharpe and George Petro**; mechanical engineering: **Pete Pietroski**; art: **Scott Pikulski**; sound/music: **David Thiel**. [S1][S2]

SE and **Bad Mother Flipper LE** have the **same gameplay mechanisms and rules**. LE adds a topper, shaker, mirror blades, wallet plaque, chrome speaker grille and cosmetics; 1,000 LEs were specified. [S1]

Latest software verified: **v1.0.2**, current on CGC's official update page; Pinside dates it to **November 2024**. v1.01 materially rebalanced several scoring features; v1.02 mainly fixes drop-target, spinner and *The Shot* behavior. [S2][S3]

Major multiballs/objectives: **Briefcase Boogie, Pawn Shop Panic, Cast Chaos, Pulp Fiction Frenzy, The Shot**, then wizard mode **Divine Intervention**. [S1][S4]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Build Big Kahuna playfield X first.** Hit both green standups near the pops, then the Bullseye Magnet target: scoring becomes 2X and can be increased again, persisting for the rest of that ball. [S4]
2. Spend that multiplier on a **Roll Scene**, especially a strong spinner/target mode, then make the critical late-mode **cash-out at the right saucer**. Both the mode build and collect receive playfield X. [S4][S5]
3. Multiballs are useful scoring/protection, but **Scenes do not stack with them—they pause during multiball**. Pawn Shop Panic is a practical early multiball while other objectives develop naturally. [S4][S5]
4. Keep working the five main objectives toward **Divine Intervention**. Completing that wizard mode **doubles the player's entire current score**, making it the ultimate match-swinging payoff. [S4]
5. Alternate route: build **Bonus X + Gun Bonus** through 1-2-3-4/Royale with Cheese; a large Gun Bonus collect is also multiplied by Big Kahuna X. [S4]

## Core rules and persistence

**Big Kahuna:** hit both standups by the bumpers, then the Bullseye Magnet target. Qualification and collection must occur in **single-ball play**. Each completed cycle adds +1X playfield scoring, beginning at 2X; the multiplier lasts until drain. The maximum is operator-adjustable. [S4]

**Roll Scenes:** right drop target qualifies the right saucer. Twist Contest, BMF Wallet, Gold Watch and Clean the Car are **40-second modes**. During the final 10 seconds, the saucer can collect the built value **plus carried base value from previous successful Scene collects**. Big Kahuna multiplies both build scoring and the collect. Multiball pauses the Scene timer rather than allowing simultaneous scoring. [S4]

After all four normal Scenes, **The Shot** becomes an untimed four-ball multiball. Its repeated right-drop payoffs derive from the accumulated Scene-collect base; playing it then resets that accumulated Scene value. [S4]

**Briefcase Boogie:** drop/lock progress is remembered between balls by default. Three locks start 3-ball multiball; reaching at least one Super Payoff completes its main-objective insert. [S4]

**Pawn Shop Panic:** center drops expose the subway lock. Three locks start MB; its **payoff level carries across attempts**, unlike Briefcase Boogie's within-MB phase progress. Locked balls launch directly above the left sling. [S4]

**Cast Chaos:** top saucer qualifies characters; right saucer collects them. Collecting a character lights **Hold Cast** at the left standup—without that hold, character persistence between balls is not automatic. Five collected characters start four-ball Cast Chaos. [S4]

## Skill shots

**Normal Skill Shot**

* Plunge into the **top saucer**.
* Scores 3K per currently lit arrow, plus **100K bonus when all five arrows are lit**.
* Also qualifies a character for later collection at the right saucer.
* Best when character/Cast Chaos progression matters. [S4]

**Super Skill Shot**

* **Hold the left flipper during the plunge**; all Payoff inserts light briefly.
* Hit any lit Payoff shot before time expires for **100K**.
* Trades the straightforward top-saucer objective for a timed flipper shot. [S4]

No additional verified secret skill shot was found.

## Secondary features

**Outlane saves:** first hit on the right Roll Scene drop lights the **left outlane save**; a second hit on the same ball lights the right. With only one lit, sling hits alternate which outlane is protected. Saves reset at drain. [S4]

**Extra Balls:** qualify from a sufficiently developed 1-2-3-4/Bonus-X ladder or a **146+ MPH Bonus**. Competition Mode disables them. [S4]

**Lane change:** flippers move the 1-2-3-4 lane lamps. Completing all four advances Bonus X and lights **Royale with Cheese**; collecting that hurry-up lights Super Spinners and mid-ball **Collect Gun Bonus**. [S4]

**Competition settings:** documented Competition Mode disables ordinary ball save/Extra Balls and several safety compensations, enables **virtual locks**, and fixes character-start behavior. This can make tournament Pulp Fiction substantially harsher than a location game. [S4]

## What to watch

* **Big Kahuna sign showing 2X/3X/etc.:** this is the most important score-state tell; a lit mode is now worth multiples of normal value.
* **Roll Scene saucer flashing late in a mode:** the player is in the cash-out window; missing it can sacrifice most of the mode's leverage.
* **Three balls locked at Pawn Shop or Briefcase:** a multiball is immediately available, but the player may delay it while setting up multiplier/objective progress.
* **Five objective inserts solid:** Divine Intervention is ready; completing it can literally double the entire game score. [S4]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** buy outlane insurance with the right drop, take a **2X or 3X Kahuna** if reasonably available, then spend it rather than chasing one more multiplier cycle. Pawn Shop Panic provides a useful controlled objective without requiring the dangerous Big Kahuna target sequence repeatedly. This is **strategic inference** from the rules plus observed competitive play. [S4][S5]

**Playing behind:** the accepted high-leverage route is **Kahuna X → lucrative Roll Scene → successful cash-out**. Current player reports show multiplied Clean the Car/Twist/BMF collects producing multi-million-point swings. A deep Divine Intervention attempt is the ultimate variance play because completion doubles the entire score. **Observed strategy.** [S5]

**Key recurring decision:** **How much Big Kahuna X do I build before spending the ball on scoring?** Every extra level multiplies nearly everything, but qualification requires more single-ball standup/magnet shots and the entire multiplier disappears at drain.

## Danger zones

* **Big Kahuna standups:** they sit around the pop area; attempting to force them can turn controlled play into unpredictable bumper/outlane action.
* **Bullseye Magnet miss:** the payoff shot is also a direct standup-style center-field rebound; successful catches are controlled, misses are not.
* **Pawn Shop release:** locked balls are explicitly released **above the left slingshot**, creating instant multiball chaos. [S4]
* **Right drop/saucer:** essential for saves, Scenes and characters, but repeated direct shots can send unpredictable rejects back toward the flippers.
* **Pitch sensitivity:** experienced players report noticeably different outlane/shot behavior from fairly small pitch changes; venue-specific feeds matter here more than generic geometry. [S5]

## Trivia

* Chicago Gaming says the project began after **Quentin Tarantino requested an 1980s-style Pulp Fiction pinball machine for his home** and remained involved in development. [S1]
* It was **Mark Ritchie's first new production pinball design in more than two decades**. [S1]
* The deliberately retro game has **no ramps and alphanumeric-style displays**, yet hides modern RGB lighting, physical subway locks, a magnet and deep software underneath. [S1]
* The game includes **250+ film dialogue clips and five licensed soundtrack songs**. [S1]
* LE gameplay is not upgraded over SE; its differences are presentation/feedback features, making the **SE the same tournament rules platform**. [S1]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Big Kahuna maximum:** the published rules describe an adjustable maximum up to **6X** [S4]. Strong owner/developer discussion reports that a later software update changed the **factory default to 3X** without forcing existing adjusted games to reset. Because this radically changes tournament strategy, verify the venue adjustment rather than assuming either value.
* **Release date:** Chicago Gaming markets the game as a 2023 title, Pinside dates its announcement/release to March 2023, while the detailed community rulesheet says the **first production games appeared August 2023**. [S1][S2][S4] This does not affect gameplay.
* **Competition settings:** the current rulesheet labels its Competition-install list as manual-derived but notes it could change in later software. Verify critical settings—especially virtual locks and Big Kahuna cap—on the actual machine. [S4]
* Physical difficulty is strongly pitch/setup-sensitive; treat the danger notes as common tendencies rather than guaranteed feeds. [S5]
