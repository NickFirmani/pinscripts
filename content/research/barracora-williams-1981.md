## Identity and versions

* **Barracora** — Williams Electronics, September 1981; Williams **System 7**, model 510; 4-player solid-state game. Requested scope is the original Williams machine. 
* Design: **Roger Sharpe, Steve Epstein, Barry Oursler**; software/sound: **Ed Suchocki**; artwork: **Doug Watson**. Production: **2,350**. [S4] ([Pinball Archive][1])
* Two multiballs: **2-ball** and **3-ball**. There are no jackpots.
* **Important ROM sensitivity:** stock Barracora does **not** have a universal 2×/3× playfield multiplier. The factory documentation only doubles/triples three switches during 2-/3-ball play: BARRACORA lane rollover, lower eject hole, and upper-right bull's-eye. A later **custom ROM** exists that multiplies all playfield scoring by balls in play; some tournament locations use it. [S1][S5] ([manualzz.com][2])

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Complete A-C-O-R-A on the right first** to advance Bonus X and light the **upper-left ball lock**. Shoot the right orbit into that lock. **Observed strategy:** this locked ball effectively protects the current turn because it is released if the active ball drains. [S1][S2] ([Arcade Archive][3])
2. Complete **B-A-RR** and successive banks to build two multiplicative bonus systems: regular **Bonus X** and persistent **Super Bonus X**, eventually reaching **25×**. [S1]
3. Use the **spinner and lane shots** to build base bonus toward 29K and complete 1-2-3/4-5-6 to light **Collect Bonus** at the upper saucer. A full stock collect is **725K**. [S1][S2]
4. Preserve/re-light the upper lock and repeatedly cash large bonus rather than starting multiball just because it is available. **Observed strategy.** [S2]
5. Alternate route on a brutal setup: start **2-ball multiball** earlier to attack the longer right drop bank more safely. On the custom multiplier ROM, multiball instead becomes a major direct scoring strategy. [S2][S5]

## Core rules and persistence

* B-A-RR and A-C-O-R-A must be hit **in order**. Completing either bank advances regular Bonus X from 2× through 5×; completing both advances Super Bonus X from 2× through 5×. The two multiply together for a maximum **25×**. [S1] 
* **Regular Bonus X resets between balls; Super Bonus X persists.** Drop targets and nearly all lamp-state progress also have memory. [S1]
* Base bonus reaches **29,000**. Thus maximum stock bonus is **29,000 × 25 = 725,000**. Completing all six top-lane numbers lights the upper saucer to collect it; collecting bonus resets the base bonus but retains its multipliers. [S1]
* **2-ball:** complete B-A-RR and enter the lower saucer. **3-ball:** qualify both locks, enter the **upper saucer first**, then lower saucer. Starting at the lower saucer first yields only 2-ball. [S1]
* Crucially, if a ball is locked in the upper saucer and the active ball drains **before 3-ball starts**, the locked ball is released and the same player's turn continues. This is the heart of the tournament strategy, not merely a lock-progress rule. [S1][S2]
* **Target Bonus:** center targets build a base value and multiplier; the upper-right horseshoe collects them. Maximum documented value is **25K × 10 = 250K**, potentially another match-swinging collect. [S2][S3] ([Tilt Forums][4])
* Stock multiball specifically doubles/triples scoring on switches 09, 11 and 33; it does **not** multiply the entire playfield. [S1]

## Skill shots

`None found.`

The plunge feeds the dual 1-2-3 / 4-5-6 top lanes and flipper-controlled lane change can improve the result, but no separate plunge-based skill-shot award is documented in the factory rules. [S1]

## Secondary features

* **Upper-lock rescue:** not a conventional ball save, but an upper-saucer lock prevents the current turn from ending; draining releases that locked ball back into play. It cannot be operator-disabled as a normal ball-save timer. [S1][S2]
* **Lane change:** right flipper rotates **1-2-3**; left flipper rotates **4-5-6**. Completing 1-2-3 increases lower-saucer value; completing 4-5-6 advances the upper-right bull's-eye through 20K, 40K, then Extra Ball. [S1]
* **Extra ball:** factory rules can award one from the 4-5-6 progression. The machine has an adjustment allowing maximum extra balls to be set to zero; tournament setup should therefore be confirmed. [S1] 
* **Bonus Ball:** on factory multiplayer settings, the highest scorer receives **30 seconds of additional play after the game**. The timer is adjustable and can be disabled. This should almost certainly be off for normal tournament use. [S1]
* No mystery, video mode, action button, Magna-Save, or modern timed ball saver exists.

## What to watch

* **Ball sitting in the upper-left saucer:** the player's turn is effectively protected. Starting the lower lock now would convert that protection into 3-ball multiball. [S1][S2]
* **Orange and green multiplier inserts:** regular Bonus X plus Super Bonus X reveal how close the player is to the enormous 25× bonus state. [S1]
* **Collect Bonus lit at upper saucer:** expect the player to prioritize the right-orbit/upper-lock shot, especially with a large bonus built. [S1]
* **Spinner lit for 1K/spin:** completing either drop bank temporarily boosts it from 100 to 1,000 per spin; repeated spinner shots also help build bonus through the BARRACORA lane. [S2]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** Establish the upper lock, leave 3-ball alone, and work bonus/spinner/lane progress while that locked ball protects the turn. Take available bonus collects rather than exposing yourself to center targets or a bad lower-saucer kickout. **Observed strategy.** [S2]

**Playing behind:** Push harder for repeated bank completions and **25×**, where a full 725K Collect Bonus can transform the game. A built Target Bonus can add 250K. On **stock ROM**, multiball is mostly protection/progress; on the custom multiplier ROM, 3-ball becomes dramatically more attractive because all scoring can be tripled. [S1][S2][S5]

**Key recurring decision:** **Keep the upper ball locked as insurance, or cash that insurance into 3-ball multiball?** On stock code, strong players often delay multiball because the protected single-ball state is exceptionally valuable. [S2]

## Danger zones

* **Lower saucer kickout:** notoriously setup-dependent; documented examples kick virtually straight down the middle. [S2][S3]
* **Drop-target rebounds:** both banks can throw balls unpredictably toward center or the outlanes. [S2][S3]
* **Center bull's-eye targets:** tempting 250K setup, but strong players describe them as direct drain-risk shots. [S2]
* **Pop-bumper exit:** going upstairs is relatively safe until the pops send the ball back unpredictably. [S3]
* **Outlanes:** adjustable post position materially changes difficulty; tournament setups may open them substantially. [S2]

## Trivia

* Barracora was designed by **Roger Sharpe, Steve Epstein and Barry Oursler**, with Doug Watson artwork and Ed Suchocki software. [S4]
* Larry DeMar recalls that the project began as **Las Vegas**, became **Jet Orbit**, then was rethemed again as Barracora before release. [S6] ([Pinball Magazine][5])
* Watson's distinctive backglass drew inspiration from **H.R. Giger's *Li I***; the central face was modeled from a young Brooke Shields. [S4] ([Pinball Archive][1])
* Williams produced **2,350** machines. [S4]
* Taito do Brasil later used essentially the same playfield layout for **Titan**, with different art/theme. [S7] ([IPDB][6])

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Maximum bonus typo:** [S3] and some derivative databases state **745K**. The Williams factory manual specifies a 29K maximum bonus and 25× maximum combined multiplier, which mathematically yields **725K**; [S2] independently uses 725K. The final page should use **725K**. [S1][S2][S3]
* **Multiball scoring conflict:** older strategy summaries sometimes state that stock multiball has no multiplied scoring. The factory switch chart explicitly says switches **09, 11 and 33** score double/triple in 2-/3-ball play. Separate custom ROMs additionally apply a universal playfield multiplier. [S1][S2][S5] ([manualzz.com][2])
* **Physical feeds:** lower-saucer kickout, outlane width and target rebounds are strongly copy/setup-dependent. [S2][S3]
