## Identity and versions

**Foo Fighters — Premium**, Stern Pinball, **2023**, SPIKE 2. Lead design **Jack Danger**; software/rules **Raymond Davidson and Tanio Klyce**; mechanical engineering **Harrison Drake**; artwork **Zombie Yeti**; sound **Bob Baffy**. The Premium has **four flippers**, the Area 51 upper playfield, physical Overlord ball lock, and the **Deadpost** left-outlane save. Premium and LE share gameplay; the Pro lacks the upper playfield/Deadpost and uses different Area 51 rules. [S1][S2][S3]

Latest Stern software I could verify is **v1.03.0, November 14, 2024**. [S3]

Major competitive multiballs: **All My Life**, **Monkey Wrench**, **The Pretender** (the three Overlord multiballs), **Area 51**, **Combotron**, and late-game **FooBot Multiball**. [S1][S4]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Build before cashing:** plunge for either Rock-O-Meter progress or a useful **Van Mod**, then work toward both a city mode and **All My Life Multiball**. [S4][S5]
2. Spell **V-A-N**, start a city at the left ramp, and ideally bring that mode into multiball. **Speaker** mods raise scoring; Engine buys time; Bomb shortens the mode. [S1][S5]
3. Before the stack gets lucrative, use the **right orbit / Rock-O-Meter** to start **2X playfield**, then qualify it again during the timer for **3X**. Competition Install shortens that window from 40 to **30 seconds**. [S3][S4]
4. For the huge cash-outs, shatz a **Ray Gun target** for 2X next-shot scoring—or both for **4X**—then hit a valuable mode final, super jackpot, or other major shot. Ray Gun stacks with playfield X and FooBot shot X. [S4]
5. **Alternate Premium route:** build Area 51 on the upper playfield and use its multiball as another mode-protection/scoring window. Under Competition Install, right-ramp shots **do not spot Area 51 progress**, so the upper playfield must actually be played. [S3][S4]

## Core rules and persistence

**Van Modes:** shoot white arrows to spell V-A-N, then left ramp to choose a city. Modes normally start at **50 seconds**; Competition Install uses **40 seconds**. Completed city progress persists, and current code leaves a failed mode ready to restart rather than making the player re-spell VAN. Every city ends with a final shot at the **Overlord**; the final award includes a time-based component, so finishing quickly can be substantial. [S1][S3]

**Van Mods:** complete the three center drops to lock in Engine, Speakers, or Bomb; then hit the exposed paddle repeatedly during its timer to raise that mod. Engine adds time, Speakers adds shot value, Bomb spots progress. The mods apply to the **next** Van Mode. [S1]

**All My Life:** hit the two Overlord-side targets, lock a ball, then bash the captive ball three times to start the first Overlord multiball. In Competition Install the Overlord cannot simply spot those qualification targets. During any Overlord multiball, re-locking a ball creates a **20-second 2X jackpot** window. [S1][S4]

**Multiplier leverage:** 3X playfield × 4X Ray Gun × an active 2X FooBot shot yields **24X** at that shot. If it is also an Overlord jackpot under the 2X re-lock window, the effective stack can reach **48X** its unmultiplied jackpot basis. This is a confirmed interaction, not merely theoretical arithmetic: the rules explicitly say these multipliers stack. [S4]

**FooBot parts:** completing major objectives collects parts that give **2X scoring at one associated shot for the rest of that ball**. Meeting the upgrade criterion makes that multiplier persistent for the **rest of the game**. [S1][S4]

## Skill shots

* **Rock-O-Meter:** soft plunge into the right crossover lane; time the flashing level. Advances toward playfield X and scores more at higher levels. Strong tournament default when multipliers are the plan. [S1][S5]
* **Van Mod-ulator:** short-plunge into the drop bank for **250K + one level** of the flashing Van Mod. Good when prioritizing an immediate city. [S1]
* **Super Sonic Radio:** plunge to the flippers and hit either radio target for **1M**, mystery, and radio-dial progress. [S4]
* **Hi-Five Super Skill Shot:** left crossover, then continue through side ramp/side loop combinations, up to six shots; each continuation also extends initial ball save. [S4]
* **Secret left inlane:** **2.5M + short ball save**.
* **Secret left outlane:** **5M + lights Overdrive**. [S4]

All standard skill-shot successes add ball-save time. [S4]

## Secondary features

**Overdrive:** Premium's left outlane starts lit by default. The ball hits the **Deadpost**; the player holds the left flipper to deflect it back into play, then can make a major shot for an Overdrive bonus. Radio-target completions relight it. Competition Install adds a **2-second compensation save** because the physical rescue is skill-dependent. [S2][S3][S4]

**Right-outlane save:** the first Bot Award lights a conventional save at the right outlane. [S1]

**UFO action button:** captive-ball hits charge up to three uses. Pressing the button automatically collects the currently most valuable lit shot and awards its rules progress, but cannot start a mode/multiball. It receives playfield/FooBot multipliers but **not Ray Gun X**. [S1]

**Mystery:** Competition Mode fixes the radio mystery sequence rather than randomizing it. [S4]

**Extra Ball:** if disabled, the award becomes **10M points**, and that consolation value *is* affected by playfield and Ray Gun multipliers—potentially a meaningful tournament score. [S3][S4]

## What to watch

* **Left ramp flashing / V-A-N complete:** city mode is ready; a player delaying it is probably preparing mods, multiball, or multiplier first.
* **2X/3X playfield running:** scoring urgency changes immediately; expect the player to abandon setup shots and attack valuable awards.
* **Ray Gun target lit/hit:** the next major shot has a brief **2X**, potentially **4X**, window—watch what they aim at next.
* **Ball physically captured in the Overlord:** first Overlord multiball is close; in multiball a re-lock instead means **2X jackpots**. [S1][S4]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** prioritize controllable setup—Overdrive/right-outlane saves, an easy Van Mode, and All My Life—without overextending on Van Mods. Once a valuable stack is ready, take **2X playfield** rather than spending extra time chasing 3X if the game is already dangerous. This is **strategic inference** consistent with tournament guidance emphasizing reliable mode + multiball scoring. [S5]

**Playing behind:** build Speaker mods, get 3X playfield running, bring a city into multiball, and deliberately use Ray Gun 2X/4X on major mode finals or jackpots. Current competitive guidance specifically emphasizes right-orbit multiplier play because it can turn otherwise ordinary features into match-winning awards. [S5][S6]

**Key recurring decision:** **cash the ready mode/multiball now, or spend more controlled shots building Van Mods and playfield X first?** Foo Fighters rewards preparation heavily, but every extra setup shot risks never reaching the payoff.

## Danger zones

* **Overlord side targets:** repeatedly documented as surprisingly dangerous; misses/rebounds can become very fast drains. [S5]
* **Both outlanes:** the game was designed around explicit saves on each side; competitive guidance describes the outlanes as particularly hungry. [S5]
* **Bot targets beside ramps:** can reject an otherwise intended right-ramp or side-ramp shot. [S5]
* **Deadpost rescue:** on Premium the save is physical, not automatic—failing to hold the left flipper can waste a lit Overdrive. [S1]
* **Right-ramp return:** documented as **machine-sensitive**; some copies can throw the return toward the shooter lane/right outlane. Verify this venue game before calling it a universal feed. [S4]

## Trivia

* *Foo Fighters* is **Jack Danger's first full production pinball design** for Stern. [S1][S5]
* The game creates an original fictional cartoon universe, **“Foo Fighters Saturday Morning Action Time!”**, rather than simply presenting concert footage. [S2]
* The Premium's **Deadpost** was marketed by Stern as an industry-first outlane mechanism. [S2]
* Premium/LE's **Area 51** is a genuine second playfield with its own flipper, loops, spinner, standups, diverter and hidden Secret Files target. [S2]
* Dave Grohl and the other band members contributed creative input during the game's more-than-two-year development. [S2]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Latest code:** Stern-derived public software archives currently identify **v1.03.0 (Nov. 14, 2024)** as latest. The community rulesheet header currently says “Code Rev: 1.04,” but I could not verify an official Stern **1.04.0 Foo Fighters release**. Treat v1.03.0 as the latest verified build unless the venue reports otherwise. [S3][S4]
* **Competition Mode vs Competition Install:** these are not equivalent. Competition Mode principally normalizes Mystery; Competition Install additionally shortens playfield/mode timers, alters Overlord qualification, changes Premium Area 51 spotting, provides Deadpost compensation, and virtualizes multiplayer locks. [S3][S4]
* **Right-ramp feed:** player reports show substantial machine-to-machine variation. Do not put a definitive drain arrow on the final page until the venue confirms its copy.
* **Mode choice meta:** current tournament sources agree on building mods/multipliers and stacking modes with multiball, but there is no single universally dominant city; shot geometry on the individual machine matters. [S5][S6]
