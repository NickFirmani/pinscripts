## Identity and versions

**Star Trek — Premium**, Stern Pinball, **2013**, SAM/DMD generation. Design: **Steve Ritchie**; mechanical engineering: **Tom Kopera**; software: **Lonnie D. Ropp, Tanio Klyce, Waison Cheng**; sound/music: **David Thiel**. Premium production began in November 2013. [S1][S2]

Premium/LE share the important upgraded playfield hardware: **physical Vengeance magnetic ball lock/kicker**, a real left-outlane **Rescue kickback**, metal/wireform ramp treatment and Premium lighting/effects. The Pro uses simpler ball-return/Rescue behavior. [S1][S5]

Latest official software verified: **v1.62.0, June 5, 2018**, for Pro/Premium/LE. It is primarily a bug-fix/system release over the mature rules introduced through 1.5x–1.60. [S2]

Major multiballs: **Klingon Multiball, Vengeance Multiball, Kobayashi Maru, Enterprise Amok, Five Year Mission**. [S3]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Start a mission, then start Klingon Multiball.** Missions stack with multiball, so strong tournament play often delays the third lock until a useful mission is running. [S3][S4]
2. Use **combos** aggressively: a combo shot doubles mission, medal and jackpot awards. [S3]
3. Build the **Warp Ramp** toward 60 seconds of **2X scoring**; combine that with Klingon jackpots or a large mission/medal award. [S3]
4. Progress the six Tier-I missions toward **Kobayashi Maru** while collecting good medals; medal quality materially raises the wizard-mode opening bonus. [S3]
5. Alternate comeback route: advance the Vengeance sequence to **Vengeance Battle**, whose final cash-out is **10% of total score**, doubled by combo and again by 2X scoring—potentially **40% of current score**. [S3]

## Core rules and persistence

There are **six mission families**, each with three levels. Tier I usually runs on a timer; Tier II/III become more shot-specific and several later modes preserve progress after timeout/drain. Mission performance earns **bronze/silver/gold medals**. Medals accumulate and are used again when the corresponding wizard mode starts. [S3]

The **Galactic Away Team (GAT)** shot beneath the upper-right flipper changes Tier-I objectives, adds **20 seconds**, and generally concentrates scoring onto fewer, more valuable shots. It is usually good, but not automatically optimal: in *Space Jump I*, taking GAT too early can force the mode to end before a gold medal is available. [S3][S4]

**Klingon Multiball:** complete the three green Light Lock standups, then lock three balls at Vengeance and/or the left scoop. First-time qualification can light multiple locks at once; unused lit locks disappear when the third ball starts MB. Jackpots escalate through Super, Double Jackpot and a **20M Double Super Jackpot** at the Warp Ramp. The Double Super also awards a gold medal. [S3]

**Warp scoring:** repeated Warp Ramp shots eventually award **60 seconds of 2X scoring**; the deepest Warp award also adds a second ball into normal single-ball play. [S3]

**Permanent shot multipliers:** completing any Tier-III mission permanently gives **2X on one specific major shot for the rest of the game**. These multipliers were changed in v1.53 to hold between balls. [S2][S3]

## Skill shots

* **Warp Skill Shot:** soft-plunge to the upper-right flipper, then shoot the **Warp Ramp**. Historically starts at **500K** and builds with repeated successes. [S6]
* **Super Skill Shot:** hold the **left flipper** during launch to feed the left flipper; one major shot is lit for the larger award, historically **1M**. [S6]
* **Super Warp Skill Shot:** hold the **right flipper** during a full plunge so the ball rebounds from the upper gate back toward the upper flipper, then hit Warp. Historically starts around **1.5M** and can build. [S6]
* **Mid-ball/secret Super Skill Shot:** when Mission Start is not lit, shoot the Away Team lane and hold the left flipper during the automatic relaunch; this can generate another Super Skill Shot opportunity. [S6]

Current code changelogs explicitly reference the Super, Warp and random Super Skill Shot systems, but public documentation is less clean on their exact current-value progression. [S2][S6]

## Secondary features

**Rescue — Premium/LE:** the left outlane has a **physical kickback**. Completing the associated left-side blue target bank requalifies it. This is a genuine edition-specific survival advantage versus the Pro's different implementation/feed. [S5]

**Photon Torpedoes:** complete the three orange torpedo standups to earn three shots. Press the lockdown-bar **Fire** button to register Vengeance hits without physically shooting the ship. Torpedoes cannot collect the final Vengeance shot. [S3]

**Multiball Add-A-Ball:** during multiball, qualifying the Black Hole target through Red Matter hits gives one Add-A-Ball. [S3]

**Extra Balls:** awarded through Warp progression and the second Vengeance feature; Warp EB difficulty is adjustable/auto-adjusting. Tournament handling therefore matters. [S2][S3]

## What to watch

* **Mission color + flashing arrows:** tells you which mission is active and what shots currently score.
* **Green Lock lamps at scoop/Vengeance:** Klingon MB is being prepared; if Mission Start is also lit, expect a deliberate mission-before-lock stack.
* **Warp Ramp chase / 2X running:** major cash-outs and jackpots can suddenly double.
* **Vengeance drop target up during Vengeance III:** the player is one precise sequence away from a percentage-of-score award.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** take straightforward Tier-I progress and pair one strong mission with Klingon MB rather than endlessly preparing 2X. Cash a good medal rather than forcing gold if the required shots are dangerous. Keeping Rescue lit on the Premium is worthwhile insurance. **Strategic inference**, consistent with established stacking strategy.

**Playing behind:** build 2X through Warp, then attack a deep Klingon jackpot ladder or high-value mission. Vengeance III is the ultimate desperation lever: the final shot is **10% of total score**, or **20% via combo**, and **40% when combo plus 2X scoring are both active**. [S3]

**Key recurring decision:** **start Klingon MB now, or wait until the right mission/2X state is prepared?** The multiball is valuable by itself, but its ceiling rises dramatically when it protects and multiplies mission scoring.

## Danger zones

* **Mission Start scoop miss:** low on the far left; nearby target rebounds can head to sling, center or outlane. [S7]
* **Vengeance center shot:** repeated direct attacks can return violently; Premium's kicker/magnet hardware makes this feed particularly machine-sensitive.
* **Warp Ramp miss:** failing the upper-flipper shot often dumps the ball back into uncontrolled lower play.
* **Left-ramp reject:** fast geometry means a shallow miss can be immediately back at the flippers.
* **Rescue return:** on Premium the physical kickback saves the ball, but the resulting upper-playfield feed still requires awareness; “saved” does not mean “controlled.”

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Skill-shot exact values:** contemporary player documentation gives 500K / 1M / 1.5M starting values [S6], while later code substantially revised skill-shot presentation and behavior. The existence and shot requirements are well supported; exact current escalators are less authoritatively documented.
* **Warp Extra Ball threshold:** the rulesheet often summarizes it as a fixed Warp milestone [S3], but later code/documentation says the EB threshold can be **auto-adjusting or operator-fixed**, even as high as 18 Warp shots. Ask the venue.
* **Premium versus Pro Rescue feed:** both later support a Rescue concept, but Premium uses a **real kickback** while Pro uses a different software/autolaunch-style implementation. Do not generalize Pro feed behavior to this Premium. [S5]
* **Competition configuration:** I verified operator-adjustable Extra Balls and Warp thresholds, but not a single authoritative modern “Competition Install” profile specific to Star Trek. Treat tournament settings as event-specific rather than assuming one factory package.
