## Identity and versions

**The Uncanny X-Men — Pro**, Stern Pinball, **2024**, SPIKE 2/LCD. Lead design **Jack Danger**; software **Waison Cheng / Mike Kyzivat**; rules **Mike Vinikour**; art **Jeremy Packer (Zombie Yeti)**; sound **Jerry Thompson**; original soundtrack by **Charlie Benante**. [S1][S3]

The Pro has the signature **Danger Room mini-playfield, Cerebro lock/post and articulated Sentinel head**. Premium/LE additionally have the rising Sentinel body/attacking hands and an elevated Beast's Lab captive-ball vari-target; Pro uses simpler hardware for those rules. [S1]

Latest code verified: **v0.98, July 1, 2026**. The current community rulesheet is explicitly based on **0.98**; a separate Pinside machine page still shows the older 0.97, so do not use that stale version listing. [S2][S3]

Major multiballs: **Sentinel Multiball, Save the City Multiball, To Me, My X-Men Multiball, Berzerker Multiball**. [S3]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Rush Sentinel while setting up a Mission.** A strong competitive opener is the Uptown/Cerebro skill shot, then safe Sentinel hits; start the Mission **before** the multiball so they stack. **Observed strategy.** [S3][S4]
2. If comfortable with the lower-left **Danger Room**, train it first: Level 1 makes the next Mission **2X**, while deeper training makes the Future easier/more valuable. [S3]
3. **Sentinel Multiball is the early scoring engine.** Hit the Danger Room exit ramp early to raise jackpots to **2X then 3X**, then cash the Super at the Sentinel; a mouth shot doubles the Super. [S3][S4]
4. A particularly strong first Mission is **Sentinel Facility Raid (Rogue)** because successfully completing it and escaping the Future earns a reusable **multiball Add-A-Ball perk**. **Observed strategy.** [S4]
5. Alternate route: naturally build **Save the City**. It is harder to qualify but can produce enormous jackpots/Supers and is a legitimate game-winning feature. [S4]

## Core rules and persistence

**Missions:** shoot white shots to qualify, then start at the blue **Midtown center ramp or Subway/right loop**. Missions may be stacked with a multiball only if the Mission starts first. After completion, the player travels to the **Future**; success there awards a permanent character perk. [S3]

Easy Missions lead to a Sentinel-based Future. Destroy enough Sentinels, then escape via Midtown/Subway. Current 0.98 code gives the four challenging Missions an **Escape Nimrod** variant instead. [S3]

**Danger Room:** progress is built using its mini-flipper, spinner, targets and exit ramp. Current rules: Level 1 = **2X next Mission**; Level 2 reduces the Future requirement; Level 3 makes Future Sentinels count double and score 2X. Danger Room level **resets when a Mission is played**, although cumulative completed levels eventually award Cyclops' permanent combo perk. [S3]

**Sentinel Multiball:** the first start requires roughly six Sentinel hits. During MB, shots score jackpots; the Danger Room exit raises the jackpot multiplier to **2X/3X**. After enough jackpots, Sentinel lights for a Super; a direct mouth shot scores **2X Super**. [S3][S4]

**Gambit:** three right-pop hits—or an alley pass through that bumper—make the **next mode/multiball shot 2X**. With Gambit's future perk, it becomes **3X**. Saving it for a large Mission finish or Super Jackpot is major leverage. [S3][S4]

**Save the City:** three hits to each of the six City shots qualifies four-ball MB. It can be progressed during other multiballs but cannot start until they finish. A completed jackpot cycle leads to an X-Jet → spinner Super sequence worth **1M per spin** for a timed window. [S3][S4]

## Skill shots

Four verified opening options: [S3][S4]

* **Plunger Skill Shot:** soft plunge onto the switch near the right/highway ramp — **+1 Bonus X**. Low-risk tournament choice.
* **X-Jet Skill Shot:** shoot X-Jet for **+3 Bonus X**. If it cleanly enters the Danger Room, hitting the exit ramp earns the **Super Skill Shot**.
* **Time Loop Skill Shot:** repeatedly loop Downtown, up to five times, then finish at Uptown; full completion also extends initial ball save.
* **Cerebro Skill Shot:** shoot **Uptown** to advance To Me, My X-Men MB by one Cerebro step and feed the left flipper safely.

Competitive guidance favors the **simple plunge or Cerebro/Uptown shot** when reliability matters. [S4]

## Secondary features

**Bishop action-button spot:** enough Bishop bumper hits qualify a use; pressing the action button during a Mission/multiball spots a valuable lit shot. Rogue's Future perk makes qualifying Bishop uses easier. [S3][S4]

**X-Mansion right-outlane save:** four X-Mansion target hits light a save at the **right outlane**. Jean Grey's perk expands when it can be progressed. [S3]

**Extra Balls:** light at the right inlane after **3 and 7 completed Missions**, and from the first Level-3 Beast's Lab award. **Competition Install disables Extra Balls**. [S3]

**Beast's Lab:** mystery-style awards whose level is increased by combos. Pro uses a different physical target implementation than Premium/LE. [S1][S3]

## What to watch

* **Danger Room green/yellow/red:** tells you immediately whether the next Mission is 2X or whether the coming Future has been made easier/more valuable.
* **Sentinel mouth lowered/open:** player is attacking the high-value direct Sentinel shot, especially important for Super Jackpot.
* **Gambit 2X/3X indicator:** the *next* mode or multiball shot is multiplied—watch where the player chooses to spend it.
* **Six City areas green / yellow Midtown:** Save the City is ready, a major scoring transition. [S3][S4]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** favor the **Uptown skill shot → straightforward Mission/Sentinel MB** route and avoid gratuitous Danger Room attempts. Take Sentinel jackpots/Supers rather than spending the ball building a perfect 3X state. **Strategic inference**, consistent with competitive guidance. [S4]

**Playing behind:** train Danger Room, stack a Mission into Sentinel MB, raise jackpots to **3X**, and spend Gambit's **2X/3X** on a Super or Mission finish. Save the City and later **Berzerker MB** are legitimate giant comeback routes: competitive documentation reports Save the City Supers often exceeding **50M** and Berzerker Supers around **30M / 60M doubled**. [S4]

**Key recurring decision:** **go into the Danger Room for leverage, or cash the feature safely now?** The rewards are enormous, but this is literally the game's left-outlane area; one failed mini-flipper shot can erase all the setup.

## Danger zones

* **Danger Room:** missed mini-flipper shots can drain immediately; the highest-value scoring upgrade is also the game's signature death trap. [S4]
* **X-Jet standup:** a near miss can deflect toward the center drain. Shoot it very late from the right flipper. [S4]
* **Midtown ramp reject:** an incomplete ramp can enter the pop-bumper area and threaten the right outlane; experienced players actively nudge this feed. [S4]
* **Subway/right-loop rubber:** can similarly send the ball into the pop/slingshot cluster rather than safely to the flipper. [S4]
* **Sentinel bash/kicker:** center attacks can return the ball violently; backhanding Sentinel from the right flipper is a documented safer technique. [S4]

## Trivia

* The layout was designed by **Jack Danger**, his second Stern cornerstone game after *Foo Fighters*. [S1][S3]
* Stern based the game around the comic-book **“Days of Future Past”** storyline rather than the films or *X-Men '97*. [S1]
* The **Danger Room occupies the lower-left outlane area**, with its own mini-flipper and spinner—one of the most unconventional modern Stern lower-playfield designs. [S1]
* Charlie Benante of **Anthrax/Pantera** composed the game's original soundtrack. [S1]
* The LE production limit of **811** references Earth-811, the dystopian timeline of *Days of Future Past*. [S1]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Current code listing:** the current Tilt code tracker and rulesheet give **0.98 / July 1, 2026** [S2][S3], while Pinside's machine-details page still reports **0.97 / January 29, 2026**. The latter appears stale.
* **Code still incomplete:** the v0.98 rulesheet lists **Master Mold as a placeholder**, so this is still pre-1.0 code and future updates can change the deep-game meta. [S3]
* **Danger Room wording changed over revisions:** older strategy material describes each level as broadly raising mode/multiball scoring; current 0.98 rules are more specific—Level 1 doubles the next Mission, Levels 2/3 modify the Future. Use the current 0.98 interpretation. [S3][S4]
* **Physical difficulty varies heavily:** Midtown/Subway feeds and Danger Room survival are unusually sensitive to setup. Strategy sources explicitly condition aggressive Danger Room play on whether the player is comfortable with that particular machine. [S4]
