## Identity and versions

**Sonic the Hedgehog — Collector’s Edition**, Jersey Jack Pinball, 2026. Lead design: **Steve Ritchie**; software: Bill Grupp, Taylor Snyder, Duncan Brown; art: Darryl Young; audio: Pierce Colbert. [S6]

* Arcade, Special, and Collector’s Editions share the **same playfield, shots, and code**; CE differences are presentation hardware including the mechanical Sonic-vs.-Eggman topper, gold accents and external lighting. [S6]
* **Rules basis: `code` — 0.930, released 2026-08-12.** JJP currently lists 0.930 as the CE full install; its cumulative release history dates it August 12. [S1][S2]
* Two principal multiballs matter competitively: **Chaos Emerald Multiball** and **Sonic Heroes Multiball**. [S4]
* 0.930 substantially revised normal and Super Skill Shot awards and fixed Sonic Heroes and Chaos Emerald Multiballs erroneously running simultaneously. [S3]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Progress Zones while building Chaos Emerald Multiball.** Checkpoints score 1x/2x/3x depending on the chosen shot; upper “Lab” shots are generally the 3x expert route. [S4]
2. **Use Chaos Emerald Multiball primarily to collect Emeralds, not just jackpots.** Each Emerald adds **+15 seconds of Chaos Control** and **+1x to Bash Dash’s starting jackpot multiplier**. [S4]
3. **Build multiple HERO teams before Sonic Heroes Multiball if survival allows.** Completed teams permanently make their associated shot 1.25x and multiple teams create multiplied Super Jackpots. [S4]
4. **Cash the huge stack in Sonic Heroes:** activate Bash Dash, whose multiplier starts at **2x + Emeralds**, hit the held ball to increase it further, then apply that multiplier to the multiplied Super Jackpots. This is the clearest verified tournament blow-up opportunity. [S4][S5]
5. Alternate lower-variance route: simply clear Zones/Boss Battles using safer 1x/2x shots, accumulating Rings and bonus rather than exposing the ball to repeated upper-playfield shots. **Strategic inference.**

## Core rules and persistence

A Zone is selected in the shooter lane. Lit checkpoint shots advance it; harder routes pay 2x/3x, with most 3x shots upstairs. Reaching the Goal Ring finishes the Zone and starts an Eggman Boss Battle. [S4]

Only **one multiball** can run at once. A multiball may coexist with an active Zone, but once the Goal Ring is reached the Zone pauses until single-ball play so Boss Battle cannot begin during multiball. [S4]

**Bash Dash** is the central scoring multiplier. During either main multiball, hit the left standup to capture a ball: jackpot X begins at **2x + Chaos Emeralds held**. Further hits to the captive ball add +1x. Base duration is 10 seconds; normal-play hits to the standup can add four seconds, Rings can relight it during multiball, and Chaos Control pauses its timer. [S4]

In Sonic Heroes, completing a HERO team gives its shot a persistent **1.25x** multiplier. The player can decline the first available multiball via the action button and build more teams; multiple completed teams multiply Super Jackpots together. [S4]

Rings contribute to Zone rank and end-of-ball bonus, light/charge the left-outlane kickback every 20, and light extra ball at 100. A tilt removes **half the Rings earned that ball**, making aggressive saves unusually expensive. [S4]

## Skill shots

* **Ball-1 Eggman Skill Shot:** within 20 seconds, plunge to the center or left ramp, reach the Lab, then hit Eggman. Starts a 5M hurry-up and makes Ball-1 Boss Battles score 2x. Leaving the upper playfield or expiring the timer loses it. [S4]
* **Normal Skill Shot:** on later/locked-ball plunges, choose an award and hit a **white** right-side shot. In 0.930: Chao adds an egg next Chao Garden; Bonus X gives +1x initially then +2x; Checkpoint spots an expert checkpoint plus a small Zone-scoring boost; Hero progresses HERO/team collection; Rings gives 10, increasing by five to 25. [S3][S4]
* **Super Skill Shot:** select the same award but make the **pink** shot. Current rewards are much stronger: Chao doubles spawns for the ball; Bonus X gives +3x plus Hold Bonus X; Checkpoint gives the expert checkpoint, a large Zone boost and removes time from the current level timer; Hero gives 1/2/3 heroes on successive uses; Rings starts at 25, rises to 50 and increases Ring bonus value. [S3]

White versus pink lighting is the useful on-stream tell. [S6]

## Secondary features

* **Chaos Control:** with at least one Emerald, action button freezes relevant timers for **15 seconds per Emerald**; another press resumes them. It freezes Zone, Boss/Tails hurry-up and Bash Dash timers. This can preserve an enormous Bash Dash multiplier. [S4]
* **Kickback:** Rings light/charge the left-outlane save; it kicks the ball toward Battle Zone targets. 0.930 changed default kickback settings to accommodate more ball speeds. [S3][S4]
* **Find Froggy:** every checkpoint lights the far-right target for an equipment-dependent award; high-tier rewards can include **8 seconds of ball save**. [S4]
* **Spin Dash:** qualify at the right ramp; flippers build spin speed and the action button releases in a timed green window. Value depends on Rings. [S4]
* **Extra balls:** three Bosses, 100 Rings, or catching Froggy can light one at the center ramp. When extra balls are disabled, the documented compensation is **10M**. [S4]
* No material standalone video mode was verified.

## What to watch

1. **Blue checkpoint arrows / 1–3 chevrons:** reveal current Zone progression and whether the player is choosing a safer shot or a 3x expert route. [S4][S5]
2. **Chaos Emerald inserts:** every collected Emerald immediately means another +1x starting Bash Dash and 15 more seconds of Chaos Control. [S4]
3. **Completed HERO teams + flashing BASH target:** Sonic Heroes is available; if the player declines it, they are deliberately building a bigger multiball. [S4]
4. **Ball captured at Bash Dash:** the high-leverage window has begun; repeated captive-ball hits raise jackpot X before the player cashes Super Jackpots. [S4]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:**
**Strategic inference:** take 1x/2x Zone progress, start a ready Sonic Heroes Multiball with one team rather than risking additional single-ball setup, preserve the kickback, and avoid unnecessary Lab exposure.

**Playing behind:**
**Observed strategy:** collect Emeralds first, then delay Sonic Heroes while building multiple teams. Use Chaos Control during Bash Dash and chase the multiplied Super Jackpot. Early competitive guides specifically identify **Emeralds → multi-team Heroes → Bash Dash** as the explosive scoring route. [S5]

**Key recurring decision:**
**Start Sonic Heroes now or defer it.** One completed team gives immediate multiball safety; every additional team risks more single-ball play but multiplicatively raises the eventual Super Jackpot ceiling.

## Danger zones

* **Right-ramp return:** the rules include a default SDTM save immediately after this ramp, signaling a recognized dangerous return. [S4]
* **Battle Zone magnet / center ramp:** player reports specifically note possible straight-down-the-middle action. [S8]
* **Spring targets:** documented strategy guidance warns that they fire the ball back with significant pace. [S7]
* **Lab playfield:** fast feeds and multiple rebound surfaces make control noticeably less predictable than downstairs. **Observed physical behavior.** [S8]
* **Aggressive save near tilt:** a tilt also removes half of that ball's Ring gains, on top of losing normal bonus. [S4]
