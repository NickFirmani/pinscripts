## Identity and versions

* **Ghostbusters — Premium/LE**, Stern, **2016**, original SPIKE/DMD platform. Design: **John Trudeau**; software: **Dwight Sullivan, Tanio Klyce, Corey Stup**; mechanics: **Elliot Eismin**; art: **Jeremy Packer / Zombie Yeti**; sound: **Jerry Thompson**. [S1][S2] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Per catalog convention, **Premium and LE are gameplay-equivalent**. Both have horizontally moving Slimer, the **Ecto Goggles Pepper’s Ghost projection**, magnetic “para-normal” slings, different ramp architecture and a physical subway/Storage Facility lock. LE’s additional differences are presentation/collector features; 500 LEs were produced. Pro plays physically differently. [S1][S6] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* **Rules basis: `code` — v1.17, released 2019-10-08.** This remains the latest published Premium/LE software. v1.17 notably reduced the base skill-shot percentage from 10% to **5%**, changed 6× timing and tightened exploits. [S2] ([Pinside][2])
* Major multiballs: **Storage Facility**, **We Came, We Saw, We Kicked Its…**, **Mass Hysteria**, and deep wizard **We’re Ready to Believe You**; *Are You a God?* is the super-wizard sequence. [S3] 

Research scope follows the supplied commentator-reference specification. 

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** use the skill shot to **start a Scene immediately**, then stay mostly within one Scene ladder instead of scattering progress. Completing a ladder qualifies **We Came, We Saw** (WCWS), the most accessible major tournament payoff. [S3][S4][S5] 
2. Loop the **left ramp → left captive ball/Proton Pack targets** for Gear Combos, lighting **2× then 3× playfield**. Run both together for **6×**. [S3] 
3. Try to enter **WCWS or Storage Facility Multiball with playfield X running**. Scenes can run inside Storage, and a completed ladder can even start WCWS while Storage is active. [S3] 
4. At 6×, prioritize the actual scoring window rather than more setup: WCWS jackpots, a strong Scene, Storage jackpots or later **PKE Frenzy** can dwarf ordinary play. [S3][S5] 
5. **Alternate:** simply master the left ramp. Gear Combos, multiplier setup, Loopin’ Supers and multiple important modes all reward accurate repeated ramping. [S5] ([Kineticist][3])

## Core rules and persistence

There are three Scene ladders: **left scoop (2 modes), left ramp (3), right orbit (4)**. Completing any one lights WCWS; the number of balls in WCWS equals the number of modes in that ladder, so the short two-mode route is faster but produces only 2-ball multiball. [S3][S5] 

On standard v1.17 behavior, draining during most Scenes marks the Scene complete but sacrifices its completion points; the **final Scene in each ladder instead resumes where it left off**. Timing out completes the Scene but requires Scenes to be relit. These behaviors are operator-adjustable and strategically important. [S3] 

**Gear Combo / Playfield X:** left ramp starts the combo; more ramp loops build value; captive ball or Proton Pack standups collect it and advance multiplier qualification. 2× and 3× can overlap for **6×**. Individual 2×/3× can be extended; **6× cannot be extended** on current code. [S2][S3] ([Epicenter For All Things Pinball][4])

**Storage Facility MB:** locks qualify from ghost milestones or other awards; three left-ramp locks start 3-ball MB. All ghost shots become jackpots; collecting every ghost lights three Super Jackpots. On Premium/LE these are **physical subway locks**. [S3] 

Ghost milestones materially alter the game: **60 = Loopin’ Supers, 80 = PKE Frenzy, 100 = Mass Hysteria**. Mass Hysteria resets the ghost ladder afterward. [S3] 

## Skill shots

Ghostbusters has **two independently selectable skill-shot components**. Each successful component scores **1M + 5% of current score** plus its specific award. [S2][S3] ([Epicenter For All Things Pinball][4])

* **P-K-E top-lane Skill Shot:** left flipper chooses the lane. **K** is usually the strategic prize because it advances playfield-X qualification; P gives +3 Bonus X, E adds 3M to Super Jackpot. [S3]
* **Playfield Skill Shot:** right flipper chooses a major shot; it remains available until another major shot is made. Left scoop, left ramp and right orbit can **Start Scene** when applicable. Early in tournament play this is usually more important than the percentage score itself. [S3][S5]
* Current v1.17 **does not** offer the old right-eject video-mode skill-shot exploit. [S2][S3]

## Secondary features

* **Opening ball save:** default settings can save **two consecutive drains** while its timer remains active; adjustable from one to five saves. [S3] 
* **Add-a-ball:** all multiballs light one at start under post-1.10 code; in Storage, captive-ball work can light it at the left scoop. [S2][S3]
* **Video mode:** at 40 ghosts, choose **Negative Reinforcement** or **Don’t Cross the Streams**. Competition settings make Negative Reinforcement deterministic based on ghost count rather than random. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][5])
* **Tobin’s Spirit Guide / Slot Pops:** mystery-style awards can grant multiplier progress, locks, mode features and other utilities. Their competition behavior is more normalized than normal play but is not fully specified in a current Stern table. [S3]
* **Extra balls:** when configured as points, current documentation gives **15M**, multiplied by playfield X. [S3] 
* **Premium/LE magnetic slings:** can enter “haunted” unpredictable-throw behavior or briefly **catch** the ball before dropping it to a flipper. [S3] 

## What to watch

1. **2X/3X targets beside the left ramp:** lit = available; flashing = running. Both flashing means **6×**, the most important scoring tell on the game. [S3]
2. **Ghost ladder at 60/80/100:** Loopin’ Supers, PKE Frenzy and Mass Hysteria are coming online. [S3]
3. **A completed Scene ladder:** left ramp will now start **WCWS**; if Storage is already running, that can become the huge stack. [S3]
4. **Mass Hysteria arrows blue vs purple:** blue means the flippers are currently **reversed**; purple means normal orientation. [S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** **Strategic inference:** take the easiest Scene ladder available, cash WCWS rather than forcing 6× perfection, and avoid unnecessary GHOST/Scoleri/captive-ball shots once a comfortable multiplier is already running.

**Playing behind:** prepare **6×**, then bring it into WCWS, Storage + Scene, or a developed PKE Frenzy. Deep-game Mass Hysteria also offers substantial variance, but reaching 100 ghosts is much slower. [S3][S5]

**Key recurring decision:** **start the valuable feature now, or risk dangerous captive-ball/standup shots to bring 2×/3×/6× into it?** That decision explains much of high-level Ghostbusters play.

## Danger zones

* **GHOST target:** misses against the adjacent guide can lose momentum and fall almost straight down the middle. [S3]
* **Scoleri Brothers:** centered target hits are notoriously capable of returning **SDTM or even airballing over the flippers**. [S3]
* **Left-ramp half-shot:** can curl back and take a one-way trip to the **right outlane**. [S3]
* **Premium/LE right ramp:** steep U-turn makes it perhaps the hardest major shot; added flipper power can instead produce huge airballs. [S3]
* **Magnaslings / double inlanes:** magnetic sling behavior plus the lane-divider geometry makes even apparently safe returns capable of finding an outlane. [S3][S5]

## Trivia

* Ghostbusters was Stern’s **final cornerstone game released with a traditional DMD**. [S5] ([Kineticist][3])
* Original cast member **Ernie Hudson** recorded custom speech as Winston Zeddemore. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Premium/LE’s **Ecto Goggles** use a Pepper’s Ghost-style projection over the right orbit. [S3]
* Premium/LE replace ordinary sling coils with under-playfield **electromagnets** capable of throwing or catching the ball. [S1][S3]
* Stern produced **500 Limited Editions**; Premium and LE share the same substantive playfield mechanisms/rules. [S6] ([Stern Pinball][6])
