## Identity and versions

* **Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles — Premium** — Stern Pinball, 2020. Lead design: John Borg; rules/code: Dwight Sullivan; art: Jeremy Packer / Zombie Yeti. [S1][S2]
* Based on the classic animated TMNT incarnation; Stern released Pro, Premium, and Limited Edition models. [S1]
* **Premium/LE gameplay hardware:** physical four-ball TMNT Van lock, player-controlled Glider ramp diverter, animated Krang mechanism, and the Premium/LE-only **Mushrooms** Ninja Pizza topping. Premium and LE share the gameplay layout; LE differences are primarily cosmetics/audio/glass. [S1][S2]
* Two main multiballs: **Ninja Pizza Multiball** and **Turtle Power Multiball**; **Team-Up Multiball** is the important four-Episode mini-wizard. [S3]
* **Rules basis: `code` — v1.59.0, released 2026-07-01.** The latest release is essentially a system/achievement update; the major modern scoring change was v1.53, which doubled Episode scoring and made Neutrino Pizza Party available after one completed Training. [S4]
* The detailed community rulesheet is explicitly based on older code (originally 1.50), so exact values below are used only where later changelogs/tutorials corroborate them. [S3][S4]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** early priority is usually **Ninja Pizza Multiball**: three Pizza Parlor shots. Meanwhile use the two ramps to qualify Episodes, ideally starting an Episode before the multiball so they run together. [S3][S5]
2. In Ninja Pizza, make Foot-Soldier shots → **Mondo Jackpot at the upper loop → Super Jackpot at the LAIR**. The Super starts at a substantial 3M base before multipliers. [S3]
3. **Episodes are the long-game engine:** four played Episodes light **Team-Up Multiball** and re-enable both main multiballs; eight lead toward Final Battle. Completed Episodes also improve Team-Up. [S3]
4. Spell **LAIR** to access Training and then **2× playfield**. Training permanently boosts scoring by 10% per completed level; 2× PF is the major immediately exploitable scoring multiplier. Raphael can eventually raise it to 3×. [S3]
5. Alternate route: complete Training, then qualify **Neutrino Pizza Party**. Its escalating single-ball blue-shot value can become enormous, but it is much higher risk than multiball scoring. [S3][S5]

## Core rules and persistence

* **Episodes:** normally qualify by making both outside ramps, then start at Pizza Parlor. Four played Episodes → Team-Up; eight → Final Battle. Completion is preferable because completed Episodes grant Team-Up perks. [S3]
* Each main multiball is initially available once. After being played, it does **not** simply requalify normally; playing four Episodes and reaching Team-Up restores access to the main multiballs. [S3]
* **2× PF:** every appropriate LAIR completion lights the center target for 30 seconds of 2× scoring; center-target hits while active add 5 seconds. Raphael level 3 makes this **3× PF**. [S3]
* **Training:** completed Training raises the chosen Turtle one level and permanently adds **+10% scoring**, up to +30%. Turtle perks become progressively stronger. [S3]
* Particularly consequential turtle perks include Raphael's 3× PF / 3× Episode scoring and Leonardo's 2× Team-Up/Pizza Party. These can combine multiplicatively with other scoring modifiers. [S3]
* Premium/LE **Glider** can route ramp returns toward a chosen flipper; this is a real gameplay difference from Pro. [S1][S3]
* Player Episode, multiball, Training, etc. progress is individual during normal multiplayer. Insider Connected can preserve Turtle training level between games, making saved-level play materially different from a fresh tournament game. [S3]

## Skill shots

The normal skill shot is **Turtle-dependent** and remains available for roughly 15 seconds after launch. Each successful skill shot increases its award by 250k and lights a Weapon return lane, leading to Weapon Hurry-Up. [S3]

| Turtle       | Skill shot       | Strategic reason                                           |
| ------------ | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Leonardo     | LAIR             | Difficult shot; immediately advances the Weapon route.     |
| Raphael      | Left ramp        | Useful because ramp work overlaps Episode progression.     |
| Michelangelo | Pizza Parlor     | Directly overlaps the high-priority Ninja Pizza lock shot. |
| Donatello    | Center/side ramp | Feeds directly into upper-flipper play.                    |

The separate top-lane plunge skill shot documented for the **Pro is not applicable to Premium/LE**. [S3]

## Secondary features

* **Battle Again:** odd APRIL completions light an outlane rescue; normally slingshots move the lit side. Michelangelo begins with it lit. This is a meaningful survival resource on a notoriously unforgiving layout. [S3][S5]
* **Pizza Eating Contest:** seven pizza slices light the flashing Pizza targets; hit one and rapidly mash the action button, with up to 40 button presses/slices during the short contest. Roughly 2–3M for completion under current documented rules makes it meaningful in this game's relatively compressed scoring. [S5]
* **Extra balls:** can be lit from Episode/pizza progress and Pict-O-Pops, but competition installation can disable extra balls entirely; current Stern tournament defaults specify **NO EXTRA BALLS**. [S3][S4]
* **Pict-O-Pops:** pop-bumper matching awards can include Add-A-Ball, feature lights, points, time, ball save, bonus multipliers, etc.; Competition mode removes random behavior. [S3]
* **Glider:** on Premium/LE the flipper buttons control the diverter over the ramps, allowing the player to influence which flipper receives the return. [S1][S3]

## What to watch

1. **Pizza Parlor lock count / Van balls:** three Parlor shots mean Ninja Pizza is imminent; on Premium the physical Van stores balls. [S1][S3]
2. **White/blue/red Mondo insert at upper loop:** Mondo → Double → Triple Mondo is the key multiball scoring sequence; one Mondo also lights the LAIR Super. [S3]
3. **Center 2× insert:** once flashing, the player has invested LAIR progress into a major scoring multiplier and may deliberately delay a valuable mode until it is running. [S3]
4. **Episode icons/display:** four played means Team-Up is coming; completed Episodes are distinguished from merely played Episodes and improve Team-Up. [S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** Favor control and attainable cash-outs. Ninja Pizza gives ball-save-backed Episode work and concrete Mondo/Super opportunities; don't manufacture dangerous LAIR/center-target shots solely to chase an elaborate multiplier stack. **Strategic inference** based on the game's difficult shot geometry and multiball structure. [S3][S5]

**Playing behind:** Training → 2×/3× PF, Neutrino Pizza Party, or a multiplied Team-Up/Pizza Party creates much higher ceilings. Ninja Pizza's Super Jackpot is also a credible match-swinging cash-out. [S3]

**Key recurring decision:** **progress versus leverage** — take ramps/Parlor now to move Episodes and multiballs forward, or spend dangerous shots building Training/2× PF so the eventual scoring mode is multiplied. A second recurring choice is whether to finish an Episode safely in multiball versus use that multiball primarily for jackpots. [S3][S5]

## Danger zones

* **Pizza Parlor reject:** the apparent opening is guarded by a standup; bad hits can ricochet straight back toward the middle. [S5]
* **Outside ramps:** both are unusually tight; rejects can become immediate scramble situations. [S5]
* **Right-ramp forehand:** expert strategy specifically favors the backhand because a forehand miss into the post can be lethal. [S5]
* **Spinning pizza disc:** target hits can activate it and throw an otherwise controlled ball unpredictably. [S5]
* **LAIR:** lucrative but inherently dangerous because it is an under-flipper shot requiring commitment and recovery. [S3][S5]

## Trivia

* John Borg designed the layout, Dwight Sullivan led the rules/code, and Zombie Yeti supplied the distinctive hand-drawn art package. [S1][S2]
* Stern's game is based on the classic animated incarnation of TMNT rather than the 1990 film used by Data East's earlier TMNT pinball machine. [S5]
* Premium/LE's Van can physically hold **four balls** behind its opening side door. [S1]
* The Premium/LE Glider is explicitly player-controlled and can direct ramp returns toward the player's chosen flipper. [S1]
* All editions use a magnetic spinning pizza disc capable of holding and releasing three balls for Ninja Pizza Multiball. [S1]
