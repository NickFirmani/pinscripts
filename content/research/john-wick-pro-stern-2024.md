## Identity and versions

**John Wick — Pro**, Stern Pinball, **2024**, SPIKE 2/LCD. Lead design **Elliot Eismin**—his first production pinball design; current rules/software credits include **Tim Sexton, Mike Vinikour and Joshua Henderson**; art by **Randy Martinez**. The game includes custom Winston dialogue from **Ian McShane** and an original score by **Charlie Benante**. [S1][S3] ([Stern Pinball][1])

**Pro differences matter:** the Pro has a static car bash toy rather than the Premium/LE motorized drifting car, a static Blood Oath Marker, standup-target Weapons Crate, and **virtual Deconsecrated locks** rather than the Premium/LE physical center-ramp lock. Core Jobs, Battles and scoring systems are shared. [S1] 

Latest software verified: **v1.02.0, July 1, 2026**, for Pro/Premium/LE. [S2][S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][2])

Major multiballs: four **Car Chase Multiballs**—Helipad Showdown, Taxicab Chase, Motorcycle Pursuit, Bagarre à l’Étoile—plus **Excommunicado** and **Deconsecrated**. [S1][S3] 

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Set up a Job and a multiball together.** Jobs start at the Weapons Crate/right VUK; Deconsecrated and Car Chase are common protection/scoring partners. [S3][S4]
2. **Pay attention to blue enemy circles.** During Jobs and multiballs, one/two/three enemies at a lit shot make it **2X/3X/4X**. Those are the game's fundamental scoring multipliers. [S3]
3. Start the **Job first, then multiball**, so the timed Job runs under multiball protection. Competitive guidance particularly values Security Detail/Heist and later Gather Intel/Task a Crew. **Observed strategy.** [S4] ([Kineticist][3])
4. Save **Lights Out** for the stack: it doubles **all scoring for 30 seconds**, so a 4X enemy shot effectively becomes an 8X scoring opportunity before feature-specific scoring effects. [S3]
5. **Alternate comeback route:** kill 10 enemies in single-ball play to qualify an Adversary Battle. Once Escape is available, cash it—or continue and raise Battle scoring as high as **4X**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

## Core rules and persistence

**Jobs:** charge the Weapons Crate/right VUK, choose a faction via its associated major shot, then start the Job at the VUK. Jobs normally run **60 seconds**, gain 3 seconds per scoring shot, and finish at the **left Administration eject**, which awards **20% of the Job total plus a permanent faction perk**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

The most tournament-relevant perks are **Security Detail** (multiball extension), **Heist** (motion sensor during Lights Out), **Gather Intel** (+20 seconds Lights Out), **Task a Crew** (action-button add-a-ball), and **Assassination** (Marker save on both outlanes). [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

**Enemies:** blue circles dynamically spawn around major shots. During Jobs and multiballs they multiply that shot: **1 enemy = 2X, 2 = 3X, 3 = 4X**. Killing enemies during multiball still scores them, but **does not count toward qualifying an Adversary Battle**; the required 10 kills must occur in single-ball play. This is easy to misunderstand on commentary. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][5])

**Adversary Battles:** long single-ball modes that take priority over other features. After sufficient progress, hit the Blood Marker to light Escape at the left eject. Taking Escape ends the Battle and collects the built jackpot; declining it raises subsequent Battle scoring by +1X, up to **4X**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

**Lights Out:** spell Y-A-G-A at the lower lanes, then use the action button for **30 seconds of 2X all scoring** while most inserts go dark. A lit-but-unused Lights Out **carries between balls**; partial YAGA progress does not. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

## Skill shots

* **Baba Yaga:** plunge directly into the right VUK without touching another switch — **1.5M and immediately starts the lit Job**. Excellent when a Job is already prepared.
* **Super Crate:** reach the VUK after exactly one shooter-lane switch — starts at **750K** and advances Crate/Job qualification.
* **Crate:** VUK after additional shooter switches — points only; strategically secondary.
* **VIP Skill Shot:** plunge through the backdoor into the Red Circle — starts at **650K**, increasing 250K per success; starts Excommunicado if already lit.
* **MXV Super Skill Shot:** new/current-code play—full plunge, then combo **right orbit → right ramp → left eject** for **10M and +10 enemies on the next spawn**. The enemy award makes this highly relevant to a planned Job/multiball stack. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

The Premium/LE car skill shot **does not exist on the Pro**. [S3]

## Secondary features

**Marker Ball Save:** work the left Blood Marker standups to light one outlane; flippers move the lit save. Completing Assassination upgrades this to **both outlanes**. Playing an Adversary Battle re-enables qualification after the save has been consumed. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

**Allies:** gold-coin target activity lets the player qualify **Akira, Charon or Katia** for the next Job. Their main competitive purpose is manipulating enemy placement; Charon can make a killed enemy group immediately replenish, while Katia moves enemies toward currently valuable shots. [S3]

**Extra Balls:** several milestones can award one; when disabled they score **10M**. Lights Out doubles that compensation to 20M; enemy multipliers do not. A full Stern Competition Install sets **no Extra Balls**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

No traditional Mystery or video mode is strategically relevant.

## What to watch

* **Blue circles stacked at a lit shot:** one/two/three enemies means 2X/3X/4X during a Job or multiball.
* **Left eject flashing after Job progress:** the player is about to cash **20% of the Job score plus the faction perk**.
* **Playfield suddenly goes dark:** Lights Out is running—everything is doubled, so expect immediate cash-outs rather than setup.
* **Escape lit during an Adversary Battle:** the player now has a visible risk decision—take the guaranteed jackpot or continue for a higher Battle multiplier. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** build a straightforward Job + Deconsecrated/Car stack, use enemy multipliers already available rather than forcing more standups, and **cash Jobs/Battles when ready**. Security Detail is especially attractive because its perk adds later multiball safety. **Strategic inference supported by competitive strategy sources.** [S4]

**Playing behind:** manufacture leverage—move several enemies onto scoring shots, start a lucrative Job under multiball, then fire **Lights Out**. Task a Crew and Gather Intel become especially attractive because add-a-ball and a 50-second Lights Out window can prolong a multiplied scoring burst. Alternatively, continue an Adversary Battle toward 4X rather than taking the first Escape. [S3][S4] ([Kineticist][6])

**Key recurring decision:** **start the multiball now, or delay long enough to improve the stack?** If the player is close to 10 single-ball enemy kills, starting multiball too soon sacrifices Battle qualification; conversely, extra setup shots risk never reaching the protected Job.

## Danger zones

* **Right orbit:** competitive documentation calls it exceptionally fast; clean shots can return violently enough to require a parry or intentional deflection. [S4]
* **Car bash:** safer players often prefer the right-flipper/backhand approach; a hard direct rebound can immediately threaten the flippers. [S4]
* **Red Circle:** slings, bumper and standups deliberately create uncontrolled ball motion.
* **Continental captive ball:** required for Deconsecrated but exposes the player to direct center rebounds.
* **Blood Marker standups:** valuable for survival, but repeatedly attacking close standups to earn the save can create the drain they were meant to prevent.

## Trivia

* **Elliot Eismin**, previously a Stern mechanical engineer, made his production-game design debut with John Wick. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
* Stern built a new **dynamic AI combat system** specifically to move enemy icons in response to player behavior rather than relying only on fixed sequences or random lamps. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][7])
* The game uses footage/audio from **all four John Wick films** and new dialogue from Ian McShane. [S1]
* **Charlie Benante** of Anthrax/Pantera composed an original game soundtrack. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* The Pro deliberately substitutes simpler mechanisms for Premium/LE's motorized car, Blood Marker and physical lock while retaining the same core competitive rules architecture. [S1]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Latest version:** current code references and the official-code archive show **v1.02.0, July 1, 2026** [S2], while some game-archive pages remain stale at v1.01. v1.02 is used here. ([Pinball Rule Sheets][2])
* **First Pro Car Multiball requirement:** the current v1.02 rulesheet is internally inconsistent: its overview still says **four car hits**, while the detailed current Car Chase section says the first requires **two**, with subsequent qualifications increasing toward four. Older tournament strategy material was written for v0.98. Verify the actual machine before printing a number.
* **Competition Mode vs Competition Install:** not synonymous. Competition Mode normalizes rules such as faction/Job behavior; Competition Install also changes broader tournament settings including Extra Balls.
* **Physical feeds:** the right orbit/car danger is well documented, but severity varies significantly with pitch, flipper strength and setup.
