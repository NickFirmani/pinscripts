## Identity and versions

* **Terminator 2: Judgment Day** — Williams Electronics, **July 1991**, model **50013**, early **WPC Dot Matrix**. Design **Steve Ritchie**; software **Dwight Sullivan**; art **Doug Watson**; mechanics **Carl Biagi**; music/sound **Chris Granner**. Production: **15,202**. [S1][S4]
* Three-ball **Skull/Cannon Multiball** is the main multiball. Its defining mechanism is the player-fired oscillating cannon. [S1]
* Latest verified **factory** CPU ROM is **L-8**. A substantial community-developed **L8.4 (2023)** exists; it fixes the notorious PAPA Lost Super Jackpot bug and substantially improves Tournament Mode fairness. [S5][S6]
* Because L8.4 materially changes tournament behavior, identify the installed ROM before publishing the final commentator sheet.

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** for the highest ceiling, hit the **skull drop**, shoot the saucer behind it, then fire the cannon at the lit target to start Multiball. [S1][S3]
2. In Multiball, put one ball in the **skull popper** and try to lock the other two. That creates a **3× Jackpot worth up to 30M**; one extra lock gives a 2× Jackpot. [S1]
3. A 2× or 3× Jackpot qualifies the real blow-up: reload the cannon and hit the **moving target for 50M Super Jackpot**. It can be repeated while 2+ balls remain. [S1]
4. Alternate tournament route: alternate the **left/right ramps** until all ten Security levels are complete, starting **Payback Time**—normally 20 seconds of repeatable **5M major shots**. [S1][S3]
5. A strong right-orbit copy offers a third route: repeated **Chase Loops** build to 5M per loop plus escalating consecutive-loop millions. [S1][S3]

## Core rules and persistence

* **Multiball qualification:** drop the skull target → shoot the ball popper → cannon fires at lit standup target(s). Later Multiballs become harder: more cannon-target hits can be required and the skull/drop opportunity can become timed. These are adjustment-sensitive. [S1][S3]
* During Multiball, loading the skull popper guarantees at least a **1× Jackpot attempt**. Locking another live ball raises it to 2×; locking both raises it to **3× / maximum 30M**. [S1]
* Only a **2× or 3× Jackpot** qualifies Super Jackpot. The Super is fixed at **50M**, requires at least two balls alive, and can be repeated. [S1]
* Payback progress is **game-long**: make five lit Security advances on each ramp. After the first ramp, quick alternating ramps are the efficient route; completed Security progress does not reset each ball. [S1]
* **Payback Time** is operator-adjustable; factory-era documentation describes **20 seconds**. Major ramps/orbits, skull and completed upper lanes score 5M repeatedly. [S1][S2]
* **Escape Route progress persists ball-to-ball.** Complete the red 3-bank to light awards, then collect them sequentially at the upper-left/escape-route hole: Security Pass → Hold Bonus → Light Flipper Lanes → Multiball → Extra Ball → 10M. [S1]
* **Chase Loop** value builds 250K → 5M and stays at 5M for the remainder of that ball; it resets next ball. Consecutive loops add escalating Million-Plus bonuses. [S1]
* Bonus X rises to **8×** via the top lanes. Normally bonus/X reset, but Escape Route **Hold Bonus** carries both to the next ball. [S1]

## Skill shots

**Trigger Skill Shot**

* Pull the gun trigger when the moving white lamp reaches the target the autoplunged ball will hit.
* First success scores **1M**; each later successful skill shot adds another million.
* The lamp movement accelerates after successful skill shots, and its timing is operator-adjustable. [S1][S3]
* On many physical copies the plunge naturally reaches the third or fourth target, so calibration matters. [S3]
* Visible cue: one lamp cycles through the five left standups before launch.

No separate super/secret skill shot found.

## Secondary features

* **Autofire:** drain protection at ball start. The first Multiball also receives a short Autofire period; code analysis of L-8 identifies **7 seconds** for that first Multiball. [S1][S6]
* **Drop-target Autofire:** separate adjustment can briefly protect dangerous skull-target shots. Factory default is Off. [S1]
* **Kickback:** left-outlane survival feature; its starting/memory behavior is difficulty-adjustable. [S1]
* **Database:** skull target lights the lower-left eject for an adaptive pseudo-random award including points, Multiball, Autofire, Hurry-Up, Extra Ball and Video Mode. Stock code considers player performance; **L8.4 Tournament Mode equalizes award sequences among players**. [S1][S6]
* **Video Mode:** qualified through Escape Route/return-lane progression; flipper buttons move the sight to shoot Terminators. [S1]
* **Extra Balls:** available through Escape Route, Bonus-X/drop-target progression, Database and Video Mode. Maximum EBs can be set to **No Extra Ball**. [S1]

## What to watch

1. **Skull target down / “Load Gun” lit:** the central saucer is open; a cannon opportunity is imminent.
2. **Green lock arrows during Multiball:** every extra ball successfully parked raises the upcoming cannon Jackpot from 1× toward **3×**.
3. **“Get Super Jackpot” + moving target lamp:** this is the **50M shot**, repeatable while another ball survives.
4. **Security lamps in front of both ramps:** five on each side means **Payback Time** is about to start.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** if ramp feeds are clean, the **Payback route** offers repeatable, lower-complexity scoring without staking the game on a difficult moving cannon target. A developed 5M Chase Loop is another attractive grind. **Observed strategy.** [S3]

**Playing behind:** force Multiball, trap aggressively, obtain at least a **2× Jackpot**, then repeatedly attack the **50M Super Jackpot**. This is the game's clearest comeback mechanism. [S1][S3]

**Key recurring decision:** **Multiball/Super Jackpot or ramp-flow/Payback?** Modern competitive guidance treats both as legitimate: players with strong trapping/cannon control favor Multiball; strong flow players can simply alternate ramps into Payback. [S3]

## Danger zones

1. **Skull drop target:** the most important single-ball shot is also a direct center standup; rejects can become immediate center drains. [S3]
2. **Cannon calibration:** the same trigger timing does not work identically on every copy; a miss can lose Multiball, Jackpot or a 50M Super.
3. **Super Jackpot distraction:** aiming the oscillating cannon while another ball remains live is deadly unless the surviving ball is trapped first. [S3]
4. **Red Escape Route 3-bank:** later completions are timed, forcing repeated standup attacks and poor rebounds. [S1]
5. **Stock-code ball-popper edge cases:** T2 has documented Multiball/popper state bugs. **L8.4 specifically fixes the PAPA Lost Super Jackpot bug.** [S6]

## Trivia

* *Terminator 2* was the **first Williams game designed around the standard 128×32 dot-matrix display**, although *Gilligan's Island* reached production first. [S4]
* It is generally credited as the **first pinball with a player-fired swing-out cannon**. [S4]
* It also introduced pinball's first widely recognized **DMD video mode**. [S4]
* Arnold Schwarzenegger recorded custom speech for the game; unused/profanity recordings later appeared in home ROM variants. [S4][S5]
* Steve Ritchie had reportedly conceived the rotating-cannon idea years earlier, finally realizing it on T2. [S4]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Code:** **L-8 is the latest factory Williams code** [S5]. **L8.4 is a community update**, not a Williams production release; it nevertheless materially improves tournament fairness and fixes known gameplay bugs. [S6]
* **Payback timer:** original manual/rules documentation gives **20 seconds** and confirms it is adjustable [S1][S2]; the 2026 Pinball Cards page currently says **30 seconds** [S7]. Use the installed machine's timer rather than the secondary shorthand.
* **Autofire:** the original manual's broad wording can imply Multiball protection generally [S1], while later code analysis specifically identifies the automatic 7-second Multiball save as applying to the **player's first Multiball** [S6]. Treat later-MB saves according to observed ROM/configuration.
* **Stock Tournament Play is not fully deterministic.** L8.4 explicitly improves Database, cannon-target and Video Mode fairness for multiplayer Tournament Mode. [S6]
* Cannon alignment and skull/drop feeds are especially machine-dependent.
