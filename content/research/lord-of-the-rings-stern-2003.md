## Identity and versions

* **The Lord of the Rings** — Stern, **2003**; DMD-era modified Whitestar hardware. Design: **George Gomez**; software/rules: **Keith P. Johnson and Dwight Sullivan**; playfield art: **Kevin O’Connor**; cabinet/backglass: **Jerry Vanderstelt**; music/sound: **Chris Granner**. [S1][S2] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* This brief covers the **standard 2003 game**, not the 500-unit 2009 Limited Edition. [S2] ([Kineticist][2])
* **Rules basis: `rom` — CPU 10.00, released 2005-10-28.** This is the latest factory gameplay ROM for the standard cabinet. CPU **10.02 is LE-specific** unless a standard game is hardware-converted with the required PAL/shaker support. [S3] ([Matt's Basement Arcade][3])
* Major multiballs: **Fellowship of the Ring, The Two Towers, Return of the King, and Gollum Multiball**. Ring Multiball and the deep **Valinor Multiball** are also strategically relevant. [S1][S4] ([Stern Pinball][1])

Research scope follows the supplied commentator-reference specification.

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed tournament strategy:** collect **3 Elf Rings**, select **War of the Ents**, and start it at the center Ring. Ents repeatedly feeds Orthanc/Paths of the Dead, simultaneously progressing the mode and building toward Return of the King Multiball. [S4][S5] ([JLP's Pinball Cards][4])
2. Collect **9 Man Rings** and start **Gollum Multiball underneath the mode**. Gollum adds a ball and alternates every seven seconds between **0.5× scoring as Gollum and 2× as Sméagol**. [S4] ([JLP's Pinball Cards][4])
3. Complete a mode/multiball to earn an **Elf Gift**; use the Shire target to select **2× Scoring**, then collect it via the left orbit/Barad-dûr. It doubles essentially everything for **60 seconds**; a lit Palantir can add 30 seconds. [S4][S6] ([JLP's Pinball Cards][4])
4. Bring in **Two Towers Multiball** during that package. Its jackpot multiplier grows both with **balls in play** and with consecutive jackpots, making it the primary explosive tournament scoring route. [S4][S5] ([JLP's Pinball Cards][4])
5. **Alternate route:** keep grinding Ents/Orthanc for Souls and play Return of the King; merely playing all three movie multiballs eventually lights **Destroy the Ring**, even if their individual objectives were not completed. [S6] ([Pinball Nirvana][5])

## Core rules and persistence

Three ring types drive qualification: **3 Elf Rings light a Ring Mode, 7 Dwarf Rings light Mystery at the Shire, and 9 Man Rings light Gollum Multiball**. Ring Modes are exclusive with one another; a completed mode earns an Elf Gift. [S4][S6] ([JLP's Pinball Cards][4])

**Gollum Multiball is deliberately non-exclusive.** A normal mode may run with it, and a movie multiball can subsequently be started. Its 0.5×/2× phases multiply other scoring rather than replacing it. [S6] ([GameFAQs][6])

**Two Towers Multiball:** spell KEEP, then physically lock three balls on the Sword ramp. Jackpot value depends on balls remaining—up to **Triple Jackpot with four balls**—and rapid consecutive jackpots build an additional 1–7 multiplier. With four balls, Sméagol 2× and the Elf Gift 2× running, the theoretical peak is **84× base jackpot**. [S6] ([Pinball Nirvana][5])

Progress within each movie multiball **carries between attempts**. After each of Fellowship, Two Towers and Return of the King has been played, their inserts remain completed toward **Destroy the Ring** even if the player did not finish the multiball’s internal objective. Sword-lock credit is player-specific despite physical balls being stored/released. [S6] ([Pinball Nirvana][5])

**Destroy the Ring is adjustment-sensitive.** Factory ROM 10.00 defaults to a **one-ball finish**: after four lit shots and one Ring shot, the magnet releases the ball and the player must cleanly shoot through the Ring. Adjustment 35 can instead require knocking a held ball free with a second ball. [S3] ([Matt's Basement Arcade][3])

## Skill shots

| Skill shot             | How                                                               | Award / strategic use                                                                                            |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Lane Skill Shot**    | Soft plunge into flashing O-R-C top lane; flippers move the lamp  | **+5× bonus multiplier**; safest conventional plunge.                                                            |
| **Tower Skill Shot**   | Medium plunge directly into Orthanc                               | **250K +50K per prior successful Tower skill shot**, plus a free Paths-of-the-Dead trip and Souls.               |
| **Flipper Skill Shot** | Full plunge around left orbit, then immediately shoot right orbit | Starts at **1.25M** and, on ROM 7+, also awards **one Ring of each type**; highest strategic payoff if reliable. |

Skill shots occur on normal shooter-lane plunges, but not ordinary ball-save relaunches. [S3][S6] ([GameFAQs][6])

## Secondary features

* **Palantir:** light it through Barad-dûr, then hit the white standup. Awards are state-aware; important examples are **Add-A-Ball during multiball**, +30 seconds to 2× scoring, mode time, ball save or progression. Competition code explicitly keeps Palantir deterministic. [S3][S6] ([Matt's Basement Arcade][3])
* **Mystery:** 7 Dwarf Rings light the Shire. Under Competition Mode its categories cycle deterministically through multiball advancement, ring completion and fixed point/bonus awards rather than normal pseudo-random selection. [S3] ([Matt's Basement Arcade][3])
* **Extra balls:** can be lit by Gifts, Barad-dûr and other progress. Stern’s Competition install sets **no extra balls**; their tournament treatment is therefore configuration-dependent. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][7])
* **No video mode, kickback, action button or player-controlled save** is present.

## What to watch

1. **Gollum MB insert pulsing vs solid:** pulsing means **Sméagol / 2×**; solid means **Gollum / 0.5×**. Big cash-outs should ideally occur during the pulse. [S3] ([Pinside][8])
2. **2× Scoring Gift pulsing:** everything important is doubled for the active timer; expect the player to abandon setup and attack jackpots immediately. [S3][S4] ([Pinside][8])
3. **KEEP complete / balls parked on Narsil:** Two Towers is being prepared. Holding it while starting a Ring Mode or Gollum explains why the player is avoiding the right ramp.
4. **Green Paths-of-the-Dead lamps / Soul total:** the player is progressing toward **5,000 Souls and Return of the King**; War of the Ents accelerates this naturally. [S4] ([JLP's Pinball Cards][4])

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** **strategic inference:** take a prepared movie multiball instead of greedily assembling the full Gollum/2× package; use Palantir to extend survival, and avoid naked Ring/Palantir shots when the required value is already secured.

**Playing behind:** pursue the classic **mode + Gollum + 2× + Two Towers** stack. Four-ball Two Towers during Sméagol can multiply an already-growing jackpot sequence enormously. Super Ring Frenzy can add another substantial timed scoring source if it is the available Elf Gift. [S5][S6] ([TiltForums][9])

**Key recurring decision:** **cash a qualified multiball now, or preserve it until Ring Mode/Gollum/2× are prepared?** LOTR’s biggest scores come from layering systems; the counterargument is that the extra setup requires dangerous center and Palantir shots.

## Danger zones

* **Center Ring ramp:** underpowered attempts can fall back into central traffic or drain; Destroy the Ring magnifies this risk. [S6] ([Digital Pinball Fans][10])
* **Palantir from the left flipper:** documented tendency to rebound **straight down the middle**. [S6] ([GameFAQs][6])
* **Inner/right orbit returns:** can arrive at the flippers unexpectedly fast. [S6] ([GameFAQs][6])
* **Sword lock release:** shooting the right ramp while balls are physically parked can release one immediately; commentators should expect sudden two-ball traffic. [S6] ([GameFAQs][6])
* **Balrog:** centered hits must actually pivot the toy; glancing/post hits may reject without rule credit. [S6] ([Pinball Nirvana][5])

## Trivia

* Stern describes LOTR’s rules as **Keith Johnson’s first game after *The Simpsons Pinball Party***, another famously deep rule set. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* **Elijah Wood and John Rhys-Davies** recorded original speech for the machine. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* The physical One Ring contains an **electromagnet that catches the ball in midair** after the center jump ramp. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* A later **2009 LE run of 500** added presentation/hardware differences and CPU 10.02 support; the standard game remains on 10.00. [S2][S3] ([Kineticist][2])
* The deepest wizard, **Valinor**, requires far more than simply reaching Destroy the Ring: all three movie multiballs completed, all six Elf Gifts, There and Back Again, and a successful Ring destruction. [S4] ([Wikipedia][11])
