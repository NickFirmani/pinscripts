## Identity and versions

* **Spider-Man** — Stern Pinball, **June 2007**, Stern **SAM** system. Design by **Steve Ritchie**, software/rules by **Lyman F. Sheats Jr.**, mechanics led by **John Rotharmel**, art by **Kevin O’Connor, Margaret Hudson and Mark Galvez**, sound/music by **David Thiel**. Standard production is listed as **3,760**. [S1][S4]
* The **Black Spider-Man** edition (2007, 500 produced) is mechanically/rules-wise the same game with alternate cosmetics. [S4]
* **Spider-Man Vault Edition (2016)** retains the original gameplay/layout but replaces the Raimi-film presentation with *Ultimate Spider-Man* comic art, renamed modes, new audio/animation and durability improvements. [S5]
* Latest verified original-game software is **2.61**. Pinside dates it August 19, 2011, while the maintained code ledger dates it December 20, 2012; version number agrees, date does not. [S4][S6]
* Major multiballs: **Black Suit Multiball**, three escalating **Doc Ock multiballs**, **Battle Royale**, and deep wizard **Super Hero**. [S2][S3]
* Scope follows the supplied commentator-reference specification.

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** clear the six **white-arrow shots** before starting multiball. The next major shot gets **2× for the rest of the ball**; left orbit/spinner, Venom side ramp and Doc Ock are the favored early choices. [S2][S3]
2. Start the resulting timed mode at either orbit. **Finish it** to earn a **3× shot multiplier**; multipliers are generally more strategically valuable than rushing Black Suit Multiball. [S2][S3]
3. Use **Doc Ock multiball and/or Black Suit Multiball** as protection while attacking dangerous villain shots, especially Sandman. The two multiballs can stack. [S2][S3]
4. Beat **all four villains twice** to light **Battle Royale**. Before starting it, ideally put 2× or 3× on the **right ramp**, where the cumulative Royale Jackpot is collected. [S2][S3]
5. Deep alternate: all villains three times plus all five timed modes qualifies **Super Hero**; its final Spider-Sense shot is worth **250M**. [S3]

## Core rules and persistence

* Six major shots carry white arrows: both orbits, all three ramps and Doc Ock. Clear all six, then deliberately place a **2× multiplier** on the next major shot. Completing the associated timed mode earns a **3× multiplier opportunity**. Multiple different shots can be multiplied; multipliers disappear at drain. [S2][S3]
* You **cannot progress toward another white-arrow cycle while multiball is running**. This is the central reason experts often delay a ready multiball. [S3]
* **Black Suit Multiball:** hit Light Lock, then either orbit for a virtual lock; three locks start 3-ball play. Two jackpots exist at each of six major shots. Clearing all 12 lights two Super Jackpots at the left ramp and two higher-value Supers at the Venom side ramp. [S2]
* **Doc Ock** progresses through Fusion Malfunction, Bank Bust and Armed at the Dock. All are multiballs and can stack with Black Suit. [S2][S3]
* Villain progression is the game's long-term ladder: defeat each villain once → Extra Ball; twice → Battle Royale; three times → Special and progress toward Super Hero. [S1][S2]
* **Venom Value** starts at 250K, can reach 750K, and **persists ball-to-ball**. Spinner spins and Super Skill Shots raise it; ordinary side-ramp shots collect it. [S2]
* Most non-Doc-Ock villain battles continue through a drain until completed; **Goo on You** is the notable timed Venom exception. [S3]
* **Battle Royale:** shoot all four villains, then the right ramp for the cumulative Royale Jackpot. The first two Royale Jackpots add a ball. Shot multipliers apply to Battle Royale scoring. [S2]

## Skill shots

**Normal Skill Shot**

* Plunge through the flashing **W-E-B** top lane.
* Scores **250K +25K per successive skill shot**, adds **+5 Bonus X**, and doubles pop-bumper values for that ball.
* On ball 3, it also gives **2× end-of-ball bonus**. [S2]

**Super Skill Shot**

* Soft plunge to the upper-right flipper, then immediately shoot the **Venom side ramp**.
* Scores **500K +50K per success**, adds **100K to Venom Value**, and advances Venom qualification.
* On ball 3 it gives **3× end-of-ball bonus**.
* Competitive guidance generally prefers the much easier normal skill shot unless the Venom progress or triple bonus is specifically valuable. **Observed strategy.** [S2][S3]

## Secondary features

* **Spider Sense:** complete the three right-side targets, then shoot the lane behind the upper-right flipper. During multiball this can award **Add-A-Ball**; during a timed mode it can add time. Multiple qualifying awards can stack before collection. [S2]
* **Extra Ball:** defeating all four villains once lights it at the left orbit. Additional EBs can come from combo progression. Tournament handling should be verified. [S2][S3]
* **Last Chance:** when Battle Royale or Super Hero is ready on the final ball, the outlanes can provide limited automatic replunges; center drains are not saved. [S2][S3]
* Standard adjustable ball save exists, but its duration is setup-dependent; the Super Skill Shot's associated save is specifically very short. [S2]
* No video mode, Magna-Save or player action button.

## What to watch

1. **All white arrows strobing:** the player's next major shot will become 2× for the rest of the ball—watch where they intentionally place it.
2. **Solid white-arrow shot:** a persistent 2×/3× is already active; this often explains apparently repetitive spinner, Doc Ock or Venom shooting.
3. **Villain inserts:** second completion of all four means Battle Royale is ready; the next strategic question is whether the right ramp has been multiplied.
4. **Battle Royale + flashing right ramp:** all four villain jackpots have been made and the cumulative Royale Jackpot is ready.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** establish one good 2× multiplier, take easy Doc Ock value, and use multiball to safely finish villains. Do not spend excessive single-ball risk trying to manufacture a perfect Battle Royale 3× stack.

**Playing behind:** finish a timed mode for a **3× right ramp**, then reach Battle Royale. Kineticist estimates an ordinary strong Battle Royale around 20–30M, while one tripled Royale Jackpot can itself reach that neighborhood. Deepest comeback route is Super Hero's **250M final collect**. [S3]

**Key recurring decision:** **start multiball now, or postpone it until the white-arrow multiplier is established?** Multiball provides immediate safety, but it locks out white-arrow progress; expert strategy strongly favors getting the multiplier first whenever feasible. [S3]

## Danger zones

1. **Light Lock target:** documented tendency to throw the ball sideways toward the right outlane or through the Spider-Sense bank toward the left outlane. [S3]
2. **Sandman bank:** valuable villain progress but repeated center standup hits are intrinsically dangerous. [S3]
3. **Spider-Sense lane:** can exit onto the top of the right sling and fall directly into the right outlane. [S2][S3]
4. **Venom side ramp:** requires the upper-right flipper; a weak or late flip ends the flow and often creates an uncontrolled return.
5. **Orbit timing near ready multiballs:** an orbit intended for white-arrow progress may instead start Black Suit/Battle Royale and prematurely lock the player out of multiplier setup. [S3]

## Trivia

* The original game combines characters and material from **all three Sam Raimi Spider-Man films**. [S1]
* Stern credits the game concept jointly to **Steve Ritchie and Lyman Sheats**; Sheats wrote the software/rules. [S4]
* The moving Sandman three-bank was deliberately inspired by the attack mechanism in **Attack From Mars**. [S4]
* Doc Ock uses a **magnet to physically capture and throw the ball** during his multiball sequences. [S1]
* Stern rebuilt the title in 2016 as **Spider-Man Vault Edition**, preserving gameplay while completely replacing the film-based visual/audio package with Ultimate Marvel material. [S5]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Code date:** sources agree **2.61** is the latest original Spider-Man code, but Pinside says **August 19, 2011** [S4] while the maintained code ledger says **December 20, 2012** [S6]. Use the version number, not a release date, on the final page.
* Detailed sources conflict slightly in wording around the **3× multiplier**: some describe completion as upgrading the prior 2× shot, while competitive guides and experienced-owner documentation describe the next flashing white shot as the selectable 3× shot. [S2][S3] On actual play, treat the flashing-arrow state as authoritative before announcing the selected shot.
* Spider-Man's **Competition Mode / Competition Install** behavior is poorly documented in public rules sources. Do not assume it automatically handles Extra Balls exactly as the tournament wants.
* The Vault Edition uses renamed modes but Stern explicitly describes it as the **same gameplay**. [S5]
* Light Lock, Spider-Sense, Sandman and upper-flipper feeds are all strongly copy-dependent.
