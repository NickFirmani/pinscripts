## Identity and versions

* **Andromeda** — Game Plan, 1985; model **850**, four-player solid-state game on the **Game Plan MPU-2** platform. Production is listed as **500 units**. [S2][S5] ([Game Plan Pinball][1])
* **Design:** Ed Cebula and Mike Kubin. **Art:** Paul Faris. **Software:** Rehman Merchant. [S2][S5] ([Game Plan Pinball][1])
* One production version found; no Pro/Premium-style variants. One unnamed **2-ball multiball**. [S5] ([Pinside][2])
* No revision-labeled production ROM was verified; Pinside lists latest software as **Unknown**. [S5] ([Pinside][2])

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Build the spinner** by completing the two 3-bank drop-target banks. A full six-target completion scores **50K** and advances spinner value. [S1][S3] ([Game Plan Pinball][3])
2. **Rip the far-left spinner/orbit** for repeatable points while using trips through the top lanes to build the end-of-ball bonus multiplier. Expert documentation gives a maximum **80K bonus × 10X = 800K**. [S3] ([Silverball Rules][4])
3. For multiball: hit the **single black drop target**, lock a ball in the upper-left saucer, then hit the **yellow “Launch Ball” stand-up**. Two-ball multiball doubles **all playfield scoring**. [S1][S4] ([Game Plan Pinball][3])
4. **Observed strategy split:** Bob's Guide favors repeatedly starting multiball and ripping the doubled spinner; Colorado Pinball instead prioritizes spinner + bonus and treats multiball as optional. Both are credible tournament approaches. [S3][S4] ([Silverball Rules][4])

## Core rules and persistence

* **Confirmed rule:** top lanes score 3K and advance bonus; completing the lit top lanes advances Bonus X. Expert documentation gives the progression as **2X → 3X → 4X → 5X → 10X**. [S1][S3] ([Game Plan Pinball][3])
* **Confirmed rule:** a complete six-target drop-bank cycle scores 50K and advances spinner value. The second cycle also lights the right return lane for Extra Ball; later cycles progress Specials. [S1] ([Game Plan Pinball][3])
* **Confirmed rule:** releasing a locked ball with the lit Launch Ball target starts multiball and makes **all playfield scores 2X**. [S1] ([Game Plan Pinball][3])
* **Important persistence adjustments:** DIP S7 controls Bonus-X recall; S22 controls spinner-value recall; S13 controls recall of a lit Extra Ball lane. Spinner starting value itself is adjustable to **100 or 1,000** via S25. [S1] ([Scribd][5])
* **Shared state:** the physical lock is stealable between players. If someone else has left a ball locked, the current player can hit Launch Ball and take that multiball. [S3][S4] ([Silverball Rules][4])
* Easy commentary trap: a ball can shoot **through the lock slot to the top without actually locking**. [S3] ([Silverball Rules][4])

## Skill shots

**None found.** The plunge can reach the three top lanes, and the right flipper can lane-shift their lit state, but no reliable source identifies a distinct normal/super/secret skill-shot award. [S1][S4] ([Scribd][5])

## Secondary features

* **Extra Ball:** complete all six drops a second time to light the **right return lane** for Extra Ball. DIP S16 enables/disables the Extra Ball lane; S13 determines whether the lit lane is recalled. No factory point-conversion rule was found. [S1] ([Scribd][5])
* **Player control:** the **right flipper shifts the lit top-lane insert**, useful for completing lane sets and advancing Bonus X. [S4] 
* No standard ball save, video mode, mystery award, Magna-Save, or comparable utility was found in the available factory/strategy documentation.

## What to watch

1. **Black single drop is down:** the upper-left lock route has been opened; expect a saucer attempt next. [S3][S4] ([Silverball Rules][4])
2. **A ball is physically sitting in the saucer:** Launch Ball can start multiball — even for another player. [S4] 
3. **Central Bonus-X ladder:** reaching 5X makes one more lane completion especially important because the next step is 10X. [S3] ([Silverball Rules][4])
4. **Multiball/lightshow begins:** all normal playfield scoring is now doubled; spinner shots immediately become much more valuable. [S1][S3] ([Game Plan Pinball][3])

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** Favor controlled spinner/top-lane scoring and let incidental hits work on the drop banks. Bob's Guide specifically recommends avoiding deliberate drop-target shots unless the bank is nearly complete. Protect a valuable Bonus X rather than creating unnecessary rebound risk. [S3] ([Silverball Rules][4])

**Playing behind:** Finish the drops, raise the spinner, start multiball and attack the spinner under **2X playfield**. A built spinner plus multiball is the obvious high-variance comeback route. [S3] ([Silverball Rules][4])

**Key recurring decision:** whether to keep harvesting relatively controlled spinner/bonus scoring or invest in the riskier lock/Launch-Ball sequence to double everything. Colorado Pinball explicitly favors the former more strongly than Bob's Guide. [S3][S4] ([Silverball Rules][4])

## Danger zones

* **Launch Ball reject:** an off-center hit can rocket across the playfield toward the right drain. [S3]
* **Drop-bank exits:** balls leaving above/between the banks can come straight down the middle. [S3]
* **Right-side return:** a key machine check is whether this reliably reaches the right flipper or occasionally falls center. [S3]
* **Lower-left pop:** holding the left flipper up while the ball rattles there can guard against a center drain. [S3][S4]
* **Lock saucer:** too much speed can carry the ball through the lock area to the top, wasting the lock attempt. [S3]

([Silverball Rules][4])

## Trivia

* Only **500** Andromeda machines are reported produced. [S2][S5] ([Game Plan Pinball][1])
* The distinctive artwork is by **Paul Faris**; Ed Cebula and Mike Kubin designed the game. [S2] ([Game Plan Pinball][1])
* Despite its 1985 release, Andromeda has **no custom speech**. [S5] ([Pinside][6])
* Game Plan used an otherwise spare driver-board relay for additional playfield general illumination on Andromeda; PinRepair notes Super Nova as the other Game Plan title using that second relay socket. [S6] ([PinRepair][7])

   * A. 1,000
   * B. 100
     This dramatically changes the value of the dominant spinner strategy.

2. **Persistence settings?**

   * Bonus multiplier S7: Held / Not held
   * Spinner value S22: Held / Not held

3. **Extra Balls?**

   * S16 enabled / disabled
   * If enabled: played normally / plunged / other event rule
   * If disabled: any tournament point conversion? Factory documentation gives none.

4. **Ball count:** 3 balls or 5 balls?

5. **Physical setup:** does this particular machine's right-side return reliably reach the right flipper, and does the Launch Ball target or lock saucer have an unusually nasty reject/overshoot?

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Dominant tournament route:** [S3] recommends repeated multiball + doubled spinner; [S4] recommends spinner/Bonus X as the main loop and says “if you must play multiball.” This appears to be a legitimate strategic disagreement rather than a rules conflict.
* **Exact spinner progression:** the factory manual [S1] confirms that drop-bank completions advance spinner value but does not list each value. [S3] describes a 1K→3K jump after the first completion and 4K after the second. Use those exact values as **secondary-source rules**, not factory-confirmed values.
* **ROM revision:** no revision-labelled production code version was verified.
* **Extra-ball conversion:** no factory point conversion was found; tournament handling is venue/event-specific.
