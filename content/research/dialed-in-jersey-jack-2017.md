## Identity and versions

**Dialed In!** — Jersey Jack Pinball, 2017. Original theme, designed by **Pat Lawlor**, artwork by **John Youssi**, software led by **Joe Katz and Ted Estes**, sound/music by **David Thiel**. Three editions exist—Standard, Limited, and Collector’s—and JJP says they share the **same core gameplay**; LE/CE differences are primarily trim, glass, lighting, topper and collectible upgrades. [S1][S2]

Latest software verified: **v2.02, March 25, 2025**. JJP’s current support page still lists 2.02 as the latest download. Recent releases are primarily platform/maintenance changes; the last clearly documented major competition-scoring changes predate 2.02. [S1][S3]

Major features useful to commentary: **Under Attack Multiball**, three **Quick Multiballs**, **Quantum Theater Multiball**, and wizard modes **Armageddon** and **Showdown**. [S4]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Get SIM Cards.** Charge the phone by hitting **QED**, start a Disaster Mode at the Phone Scoop, complete it, then collect the lit SIM Card at the upper-right scoop. [S4][S5]
2. Every collected SIM Card increases the payoff from **all later Disaster Modes**; eight cards spell D-I-A-L-E-D-I-N and qualify **Showdown**. [S4]
3. Build **Big Bang** from the upper-flipper target. During a Disaster Mode it instantly completes the mode and lights its SIM Card—but sacrifices scoring from shots you skipped, making it less attractive once the SIM multiplier is large. [S4][S6]
4. Use multiballs mainly to **protect difficult SIM Card collections and mode progress**; Under Attack itself is usually secondary scoring compared with accumulated SIM-enhanced modes. **Observed tournament strategy.** [S5]
5. Alternate high-end route: advance the four Theater resources—Kilowatts, Spiders, Drones and Transit—to reach **Quantum Theater Multiball** and its potentially large Super Jackpots. [S4]

## Core rules and persistence

**Disaster Modes:** hit QED to charge the phone, then shoot the green-lit Phone Scoop. Qualification becomes progressively harder, from one QED hit early to as many as four. Flipper buttons cycle the available mode selection. Extra QED hits before starting can **Overcharge** the phone above 100%, increasing mode scoring. [S4]

Complete a Disaster Mode to earn a completion bonus and **light a SIM Card**. The card must normally be collected at the SIM Scoop **before draining or it is lost**. Collected SIM Cards persist for the game. [S4]

**SIM multiplier:** instantaneous mode shots score normally; when the mode ends, collected SIM Cards award additional copies of the accumulated mode value. Thus later completed modes become dramatically more valuable. [S4]

**Big Bang:** once qualified, shooting Big Bang during a mode instantly completes its remaining requirements and lights the SIM Card. However, skipped mode shots do **not** become part of the SIM-multiplied mode total. In Competition/League behavior, later code further reduced the value of using Big Bang purely as a mode-completion shortcut. [S3][S4][S6]

**Under Attack Multiball:** the first is available after playing a Disaster Mode and locking a ball. Later attempts require ramp qualifications and additional locks. If Multiball is lit, the player can deliberately defer it to start a Disaster first—an important stacking decision. [S4]

During Under Attack, red shots score jackpots; the side ramp is 2X. Qualified Super Jackpots appear as attacks from QED, Theater or the pop area. If the attack reaches the flippers first, the affected flipper is briefly disabled and the Super is lost. [S4]

## Skill shots

**Normal Skill Shot**

* Short-plunge the ball to stop just behind the Theater and cross the shooter-lane switch at the desired lit award.
* Base scoring starts at **5K** and increases on successive successful skill shots.
* More important than the points are **Hold** awards: Hold Bonus, Drones, Kilowatts, Transit or Spider can preserve valuable resources across balls. [S4]

**Super Skill Shot**

* After a successful normal skill shot feeds the upper flipper, shoot the **side ramp**.
* Improves the corresponding selected award/value. [S4]

**Super Duper Skill Shot**

* From that same upper-flipper feed, shoot the **SIM Card scoop**.
* Offers the strongest version of the selected award; especially useful for Bonus X/resource holds. [S4]

## Secondary features

**Kickback:** complete the lower-left **B-O-B** targets to light the left-side kickback. This is a major survival objective on difficult tournament setups; the 2020 INDISC machine was intentionally configured with it unlit at game start. [S4][S6]

**Extra Ball:** normally lights after three Disaster Modes; other qualifications include high combo totals and some awards. Exact tournament handling is operator/event-dependent; no universal built-in points conversion was verified. [S4]

**Drone Mystery / randomness:** Competition Mode was added to normalize some random behavior. Singularity’s otherwise random final shot, for example, becomes the **left orbit** in Competition Mode. [S3][S4]

**Player controls:** flippers select Disaster Modes and can move which inlane hurry-up is active. The original smartphone-control app is **no longer supported from v1.75 onward**, so do not describe remote Big Bang activation as a current tournament feature. [S3][S4]

## What to watch

* **Phone border glowing green:** a Disaster Mode is ready; watch the phone display to see which mode the player selects. [S4]
* **D-I-A-L-E-D-I-N letters / SIM scoop blinking:** a SIM Card is waiting; because it dies with the ball, this is often the immediate priority. [S4]
* **Big Bang lit:** the player now possesses a guaranteed mode-completion escape hatch. Whether they use it depends heavily on their SIM multiplier. [S4][S6]
* **Colored arrows:** blue = Crazy Mode shots, green = multiball locks, red = multiball jackpots. [S4]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** light the Kickback, pick Disaster Modes you can finish naturally, and **collect every SIM immediately**. With several SIMs already collected, avoiding an unnecessary Big Bang preserves the much larger multiplied value of manually made mode shots. [S5][S6]

**Playing behind:** choose harder/high-value modes, Overcharge the phone, stack a mode with multiball where possible, and use Big Bang when necessary to guarantee completion/SIM progress. A run of later modes with six or seven SIM Cards can create enormous score movement. [S4][S5]

**Key recurring decision:** **finish this Disaster manually for full SIM-multiplied value, or fire Big Bang now to guarantee the SIM Card?** Score position, mode difficulty and remaining time determine the answer.

## Danger zones

* **SIM Card shot from upper flipper:** vital but difficult; a miss can throw away the only chance to collect a card before drain. [S5]
* **QED:** the moving standup is needed constantly but can produce sharp, unpredictable rebounds.
* **Theater return:** normally provides useful upper-flipper access, but tournament setups can make it extremely fast; INDISC specifically documented difficult Theater-to-side-ramp control. [S6]
* **Big Bang access:** the shot becomes especially narrow when QED is parked toward its home position. [S5]
* **Under Attack:** failing to repel an approaching attack can briefly disable a flipper—a very literal warning that a drain may be imminent. [S4]

## Trivia

* *Dialed In!* was Pat Lawlor’s first new pinball design in roughly a decade and reunited him with longtime collaborator **John Youssi**. [S2][S7]
* It was JJP’s **third game** and, unlike *Wizard of Oz* and *The Hobbit*, used an original rather than licensed theme. [S7]
* JJP calls it the first pinball machine with **Bluetooth connectivity, a built-in camera and smartphone integration**. [S1]
* The game deliberately references several earlier Lawlor titles, including *Whirlwind*, *Earthshaker!*, *Road Show*, *No Good Gofers* and *Twilight Zone*. [S2]
* The original working title was reportedly **Killer App** before the name *Dialed In!* was chosen. [S2]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Current Competition Big Bang scoring:** v1.71 changed League/Competition behavior so Big Bang completion receives reduced mode-completion value, and v1.75 says Competition Big Bang points were adjusted again. The public changelog does not provide a complete current formula. [S3]
* **SIM multiplier wording:** older rules documentation presents the SIM award as a separate additional copy of accumulated mode value, while some modern summaries call the effective result “2X/3X/etc.” Both describe the same strategic effect, but avoid quoting a simple multiplier without checking the current display. [S4]
* **Skill-shot sub-award values:** the overall normal/Super/Super-Duper structure and Hold awards are well documented, but older community tables contain tentative exact numerical increments. Those uncertain values are intentionally omitted.
* **Physical feeds:** Theater, SIM and QED behavior vary substantially machine-to-machine; published tournament accounts confirm the importance of setup-specific feeds. [S6]
