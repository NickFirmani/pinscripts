## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Confirmed scoring advice:** before starting an important mode, spell **EATME** and collect the **2×/3×/4× playfield multiplier at the left orbit**. Dutch Pinball explicitly calls this crucial for high-score play. [S2] [Dutch Pinball](https://cms.dutchpinball.com/uploads/AAIW_20_Rulesheet_4e45b2c32e.pdf?v=msqptwo9)
2. Qualify a main story mode with the **main ramp**, then start it at the **scoop**. Pop-bumper hits change which mode is selected. [S2] [Dutch Pinball](https://cms.dutchpinball.com/uploads/AAIW_20_Rulesheet_4e45b2c32e.pdf?v=msqptwo9)
3. For safer progression, attack the main captive ball for **Quick Croquet MB**: five hits starts 2-ball; five jackpots earns a **WONDERS** letter. [S2] [Dutch Pinball](https://cms.dutchpinball.com/uploads/AAIW_20_Rulesheet_4e45b2c32e.pdf?v=msqptwo9)
4. A high-value alternate is rapid ramping: **15 ramp shots within 20 seconds** awards a WONDERS letter plus **2× scoring for the remainder of the ball** in current code. [S3] [Pinside](https://pinside.com/pinball/machine/alices-adventures-in-wonderland/details)
5. Long-game objective: gather all seven **WONDERS**, start all six main modes, play Jabberwocky MB and collect all four Suits to reach **Queen of Hearts**. Reduce her 21-guard requirement beforehand with **Painting the Roses Red / Sneak-In**. [S2][S4] [Dutch Pinball](https://cms.dutchpinball.com/uploads/AAIW_20_Rulesheet_4e45b2c32e.pdf?v=msqptwo9)

## Core rules and persistence

Main modes are qualified at the ramp and started at the scoop. The clock rollover adds **5 seconds** to a running mode, capped at 60 seconds. Completing a main mode can award a WONDERS letter. [S2] [Dutch Pinball](https://cms.dutchpinball.com/uploads/AAIW_20_Rulesheet_4e45b2c32e.pdf?v=msqptwo9)

**Jabberwocky MB:** hit left/right ramp targets to light locks, then lock three balls via the right orbit. Collect all jackpots for Super Jackpot. Importantly, missed Super progress **carries into the next Jabberwocky MB**, and every Jabberwocky start awards a WONDERS letter. [S2] [Dutch Pinball](https://cms.dutchpinball.com/uploads/AAIW_20_Rulesheet_4e45b2c32e.pdf?v=msqptwo9)

**Four Suits:** outside modes, complete the left U-turn 5×, left orbit 5×, right ramp 6× and right orbit 5×. All four suits start a hurry-up and award a WONDERS letter. Suit difficulty is adjustable; v1.00 default is **Easy**. [S2][S3] [Dutch Pinball](https://cms.dutchpinball.com/uploads/AAIW_20_Rulesheet_4e45b2c32e.pdf?v=msqptwo9)

**Queen of Hearts:** requires all 7 WONDERS, Jabberwocky MB, all six main modes started, and all four Suits. The final battle nominally asks for **21 guards**; guards defeated earlier in Painting the Roses Red or Sneak-In reduce that count, to a minimum of four. [S2][S4] [Dutch Pinball](https://cms.dutchpinball.com/uploads/AAIW_20_Rulesheet_4e45b2c32e.pdf?v=msqptwo9)

Easy commentary trap: the official sheet does **not clearly document how long the EATME 2×/3×/4× multiplier persists**. By contrast, the separate 15-ramp reward is explicitly ball-long. Do not casually call EATME “rest-of-ball” without machine verification. [S2][S3] [Dutch Pinball](https://cms.dutchpinball.com/uploads/AAIW_20_Rulesheet_4e45b2c32e.pdf?v=msqptwo9)

## Skill shots

- **Down the Rabbit Hole:** soft plunge, lock in the score shown on-screen, then complete the rabbit-hole path. Hitting a pop bumper or rollover first fails it. [S2] [Dutch Pinball](https://cms.dutchpinball.com/uploads/AAIW_20_Rulesheet_4e45b2c32e.pdf?v=msqptwo9)
- **Super Skill Shot:** plunge directly into the **VUK within 5 seconds**. Awards **1M**, 5 seconds on the mini playfield, then **+1M per upper-playfield target hit**. This is the materially stronger competitive choice if the player can hit it reliably. [S2] [Dutch Pinball](https://cms.dutchpinball.com/uploads/AAIW_20_Rulesheet_4e45b2c32e.pdf?v=msqptwo9)

No other verified normal/secret skill shot found.

## Secondary features

- **Swirling Wonderland outlane saves:** each game begins with two player-controlled saves. Stored duration is color-coded: red 5s, yellow 10s, green 15s. Top flipper buttons activate the left/right spinning discs; using both consumes 10 seconds. Save time can be recharged through the Save target and right-side orbit/ramp. [S2] [Dutch Pinball](https://cms.dutchpinball.com/uploads/AAIW_20_Rulesheet_4e45b2c32e.pdf?v=msqptwo9)
- **Cheshire Cat Mystery:** spell CAT, then scoop. Left flipper takes the offered award; right reveals a secret alternative, which can be better—or **Cat in a Bag**, worth nothing. A WONDERS letter is among the possible awards. [S2] [Dutch Pinball](https://cms.dutchpinball.com/uploads/AAIW_20_Rulesheet_4e45b2c32e.pdf?v=msqptwo9)
- **Extra Ball:** a hidden EB is available from the mini-playfield captive ball during Knave of Hearts. No verified universal tournament point conversion found. [S2][S3] [Dutch Pinball](https://cms.dutchpinball.com/uploads/AAIW_20_Rulesheet_4e45b2c32e.pdf?v=msqptwo9)
- **Magna-Flips:** the mini playfield is controlled by two player-operated electromagnets rather than ordinary flippers. [S1][S5] [Dutch Pinball](https://dutchpinball.com/games/alices-adventures-in-wonderland)
- Conventional video mode: **None verified**.

## What to watch

1. **EATME complete / left orbit flashing:** a major playfield multiplier is ready; expect immediate setup for a mode or multiball. [S2]
2. **WONDERS letters + Suits display:** these reveal whether the player is merely scoring or actually approaching Queen of Hearts. [S2]
3. **Physical balls visible in Jabberwock’s lock:** three starts the 3-ball multiball. [S1][S2]
4. **Red/yellow/green outlane-save state:** tells you how much active disc time the player still owns. [S2] [Dutch Pinball](https://cms.dutchpinball.com/uploads/AAIW_20_Rulesheet_4e45b2c32e.pdf?v=msqptwo9)

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** **Strategic inference:** take Quick Croquet or a prepared story mode once a reasonable multiplier is available; use the outlane discs proactively and avoid forcing the difficult Queen route from scratch.

**Playing behind:** build the largest available EATME multiplier, chase the 15-ramp ball-long 2×, and bring those scoring windows into a story mode or multiball. Midnight Madness is another high-variance route because repeated main-ramp loops build X scoring. [S2][S3]

**Key recurring decision:** **cash the multiplier into a ready feature now, or keep shooting dangerous setup targets for a larger X / deeper wizard progress?**

## Danger zones

- **Main-ramp reject:** current player reports describe poor hits as capable of returning straight down the middle. **Observed/setup-sensitive.** [S7] [Pinside](https://pinside.com/pinball/forum/topic/dutch-pinball-alice-in-wonderland-hype-thread/page/113?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **Right-side lane/orbit:** commonly described as narrow and demanding; misses enter uncontrolled upper traffic. [S7]
- **Mode scoop:** tight close-range shot; rejected attempts can return quickly toward the flippers. [S8] [Pinside](https://pinside.com/pinball/machine/alices-adventures-in-wonderland/ratings?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **Captive-ball/standup work:** central rebounds are the price of Quick Croquet and multiplier setup.
- **Outlanes:** the spinning discs are **player-controlled resources, not automatic kickbacks**; if they are not activated, stored save time does nothing. [S2]

## Trivia

- The design evolved from **John Popadiuk’s unfinished Zidware Alice concept**, with Melvin Brouwer-Williams developing it into the released DPX game. [S5] [Pinball News](https://www.pinballnews.com/site/2024/10/07/alices-adventures-in-wonderland-revealed/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- Production was capped at **500 individually numbered machines**. [S1] [Dutch Pinball](https://dutchpinball.com/games/alices-adventures-in-wonderland)
- The upper playfield revives the **Magna-Flip** idea—player-controlled electromagnets instead of physical flippers. [S5]
- The animated **Jabberwock** is technically from *Through the Looking-Glass*, not the original *Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland*. [S5] [Pinball News](https://www.pinballnews.com/site/2024/10/07/alices-adventures-in-wonderland-revealed/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- DPX wound down as an active brand in 2026, leaving Alice as its sole released title; Dutch Pinball continued supporting/building remaining games. [S9] [The Pinball Archive](https://pinballarchive.com/designers/melvin-brouwer-williams?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
