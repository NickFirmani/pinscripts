## Identity and versions

* **Black Knight: Sword of Rage — Premium/LE**, Stern, **2019**, SPIKE 2. Lead design: **Steve Ritchie**; code/rules: **Tim Sexton**; art/animation includes **Kevin O’Connor and Joshua Clay**; sound: **Jerry Thompson**; music includes **Scott Ian**. [S1][S2] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Per the catalog rule, **Premium and LE belong together**: both have the transparent upper playfield, third flipper and 3-ball **Catapult** lock. The LE’s 600-unit differences are presentation/collector features rather than verified gameplay-rule changes. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Pro is materially different: no upper playfield, Catapult Multiball or Last Chance/Super Catapult system. [S1][S3] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* **Rules basis: `code` — v1.19.0, released 2026-07-01.** Current v1.19 is largely system/service work; the major modern rules package dates to earlier releases, with v1.18/v1.14-era rules still applicable. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][2])
* Major multiballs: **Triple Knights Challenge, Catapult Multiball, KNIGHT Multiball, The King’s Ransom**, plus **RAGE Multiball** after Black Castle. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][3])

Research scope follows the supplied commentator-reference specification. 

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** slow the game down and **backhand nearly everything**. The rubber-heavy shot entrances make uncontrolled forehands unusually dangerous. [S4][S5] ([Pinball for Mortals!][4])
2. Qualify a **Monster Battle** and **Triple Knights Challenge** together, then run the battle during multiball. Both starts live around the shield/scoop area, so the stack is efficient. [S3][S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][3])
3. During the battle, charge **Sword of Rage POWER** and collect it for **2× battle scoring plus an easier version of that battle**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][3])
4. Longer-game scoring engine: build one or more **Super Modes**, boost them toward purple, spell **KNIGHT**, then start KNIGHT Multiball. Its playfield is multiplied by balls in play—up to **3×**—and a successful Magna-Save can cash all active Supers at that multiplier. [S3][S5] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][3])
5. **Alternate Prem/LE route:** develop Catapult Multiball; it can progress or start during Triple Knights, creating a potentially large multi-multiball stack. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][3])

## Core rules and persistence

Monster Battles start by making enough center-area qualification shots, then the ramp, then the saucer behind the raised shield. Battles start around **65 seconds** and **carry between balls**; if little time remains when the player drains, time is restored to a minimum threshold on the next ball. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][3])

Mode base values can be boosted before starting by hitting the flail, but those boosts **reset on drain**. Winning a battle awards a KNIGHT letter and lights Extra Ball; winning all five qualifies **Black Castle**. [S3][S6] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][3])

**Sword of Rage:** right drops, center-target hits and switches fill the POWER meter. Once full, POWER at the center target gives **2× scoring for the rest of the active battle** and simplifies its rules. Default qualification starts at roughly 70% for the first use, then 40%, then 0%. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][5])

**Triple Knights Challenge:** three shield-scoop locks start 3-ball MB. Phase 1 clears three main shots; the ramp hurry-up then sets the Phase-2 jackpot value. Clear the lit jackpots to relight the ramp for a **3× Super Jackpot + KNIGHT letter**. [S3]

**Catapult MB — Prem/LE:** lock three balls upstairs. Lower-playfield jackpots build the Catapult Super; the upper standup can raise its next multiplier up to **4×**. Triple Knights and Catapult can be progressed and activated during each other. [S3]

**KNIGHT Multiball:** KNIGHT comes from battles, WAR hurry-ups and Triple Knights Supers. Starting KNIGHT MB **ends any running timed mode**, so unlike Triple Knights it is a cash-out feature, not a mode-stacking feature. Scoring is multiplied by balls in play, up to 3×. [S3]

## Secondary features

* **Magna-Save:** action button activates the magnet over the right outlane. A successful save also cashes all running Super Modes as a **Super Jackpot**; right-drop completion relights it. This collect inherits KNIGHT MB’s ball-count multiplier. [S3][S5] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][3])
* **Left-outlane ball save:** qualify by completing the left-side Add Time targets; requirements increase after use. [S3]
* **Last Chance — Prem/LE only:** on the last ball, if Catapult MB has not been played, a qualifying outlane drain can launch a timed upper-playfield survival mode. Lower flippers are disabled; lock three balls in the Catapult to earn **Super Catapult Multiball**. **Competition Install disables Last Chance.** [S2] ([Pinside][6])
* **Mystery:** spell RAGE at inlanes/outlanes; relevant awards include Light Lock, KNIGHT letter, Magna-Save, Super, save progress and Extra Ball. Competition Mode removes randomness. [S3][S7] ([Tilt Forums][7])
* No conventional video mode found.

## What to watch

1. **POWER lit at center target:** the battle can now become 2× and easier—often the immediate priority. [S3]
2. **Super icons on the left display:** colored = active; purple means that Super is maximally boosted. [S8] ([System J Pinball][8])
3. **Magna-Save lamp:** if active Supers exist, a right-outlane save is simultaneously a major scoring cash-out. [S3]
4. **Physical balls in Catapult:** Prem/LE Catapult MB progress is visible; under Competition Install the lock requirements are deliberately harder. [S2]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** prioritize control and a Battle + Triple Knights stack, take POWER when ready, and use Magna-Save as survival rather than deliberately fishing for an enormous Super cash-out. **Strategic inference:** Catapult setup becomes less attractive if its upper-playfield shots are not reliable.

**Playing behind:** develop **Super Spinner/other Supers**, boost them before starting KNIGHT MB, then seek a Magna-Save cash-out while three balls remain. Current strategy sources describe this as capable of **nine-figure swings**. [S5][S6] ([Pinball Genie][9])

**Key recurring decision:** **take the safe Battle/Triple-Knights scoring now, or survive long enough to build KNIGHT + Supers for the much larger cash-out?**

## Danger zones

* **Rubber-post shot entrances:** the defining hazard; misses gain lateral energy and frequently head center. Backhands reduce this risk. [S4][S5]
* **Flail:** direct hits can be rejected **straight down the middle**. [S9] ([Tilt Forums][10])
* **Center POWER target:** strategically important but inherently a dangerous straight-on standup.
* **Shield:** raised/lowered geometry and kick behavior can create very fast returns.
* **Upper playfield:** missed Catapult/lock work returns the ball to the lower field without guaranteed control.

## Trivia

* Sword of Rage is the **third Black Knight game**, following Williams’ *Black Knight* (1980) and *Black Knight 2000* (1989), again designed by Steve Ritchie. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][11])
* Premium/LE deliberately revive the earlier games’ **upper-playfield** concept with a full-size third flipper. [S1]
* Stern produced **600 Limited Editions**. [S1]
* Guitarist **Scott Ian of Anthrax** composed/performed major portions of the soundtrack, with Brendon Small also involved. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* **The King’s Ransom** deliberately recreates Black Knight 2000’s audiovisual style and runs as a 60-second, unlimited-ball-save 3-ball multiball. [S2][S3]
