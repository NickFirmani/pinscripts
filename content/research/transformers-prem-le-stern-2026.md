## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** start a favorable Mission—**Autobot Run, Prime Target, or Golden Lagoon** are documented practical choices—while preparing Transformers Multiball. [S4][S5] ([Kineticist][5])
2. Before starting multiball, finish **BUMBLEBEE** at the captive ball for **30 seconds of 2× playfield**. A mode already running can continue through Transformers MB. [S3][S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
3. In Transformers MB, qualify several jackpots simultaneously: the jackpot is multiplied by the **number currently lit**. Three jackpots light the difficult side-ramp Super. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
4. After the normal ball save expires, shoot **Space Bridge for add-a-ball**, then hit the held ball with the captive ball. That creates 2× scoring—or **3× if Bumblebee 2× was already running**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
5. **Alternate high-ceiling route:** collect Tech Specs/Combiner parts. v0.89 made **Tech Spec Mania** a materially large scorer; its first Super Jackpot is about **25M base** and later values scale upward. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][3])

## Core rules and persistence

Five 60-second **Missions** start at Megatron. The first two require one Megatron start; later missions require more qualification. Completing the required objective counts as completing the Mission; the optional **Final Blow** at Megatron awards additional value and assigns **2× scoring to a shot when its Tech Spec is collected**. Failed Mission progress is saved for the next attempt. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

The spinner builds **Energon**. At the next Mission start the player can spend it to make the Mission **Energized**—easier, but generally lower scoring. Completing a normal Mission instead makes the following Mission **Rusted**: harder and substantially more valuable. This is the game's clearest recurring risk/reward choice. [S3][S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

**Transformers MB:** Optimus lights locks; the VUK locks balls. On current settings later MBs require increasing Optimus hits. Three balls start MB. Repeatedly hit a shot through Decepticon → Autobot → Jackpot states; lighting several jackpots before collecting one multiplies it by the number lit. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][3])

**One Shall Fall** lights after two Missions. Playing additional Missions before starting it adds perks/value, so qualification does not imply immediate start. Completing all five Missions qualifies **Rescue Optimus**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])

## Secondary features

* **Add-a-ball / action button:** Space Bridge supplies the once-per-game normal add-a-ball after multiball ball save expires. Mystery can re-light it. The action button also activates 15 seconds of **Tech Spec availability / 2× shot scoring** after Cassette combos and controls Combiner selection. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][6])
* **Left outlane save:** lit at game start; Mystery can re-light it after use. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
* **Countdown to Extinction:** on the final ball, a right-outlane drain gives **18 seconds to make the right ramp and continue**; on Premium/LE the cannon then fires the ball back toward the left flipper. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][3])
* **Cannon Jackpot — Premium/LE:** once per ball outside other modes, right ramp loads Megatron's cannon; right ramp again gives Cannon Combo, then Optimus from the cannon feed scores **14M**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
* Extra balls light from all five Missions or 15 Tech Specs. **No true normal-play video mode found**; *The Movie: The Mode* is a selectable challenge mode. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][3])

## What to watch

1. **Captive ball flashing / BUMBLEBEE completed:** 2× playfield is ready; expect the player to start the valuable mode/multiball stack immediately. [S3]
2. **Space Bridge lit during MB:** add-a-ball is ready; if the ball is held there, watch for the captive-ball shot that converts it into 2×/3× scoring. [S3]
3. **Multiple red/white jackpot arrows:** the player may deliberately avoid collecting one yet—the next jackpot multiplier equals the number currently lit. [S3]
4. **Energon decision before a Mission:** easy/lower-value **Energized** versus harder/higher-value **Rusted** is deliberate strategy, not menu delay. [S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** **strategic inference:** choose Energized Missions when the reduced difficulty matters, take Transformers MB once 2× is prepared, and cash reasonable jackpot multipliers rather than insisting on the side-ramp Super. Space Bridge mystery/add-a-ball is worth taking when lit because further bumper work cannot bank another Mystery first. [S4] ([Kineticist][5])

**Playing behind:** let a strong Mission become **Rusted**, combine it with Transformers MB and pre-started Bumblebee 2×, then convert add-a-ball into **3× playfield**. v0.89's strengthened Tech Spec Mania is another legitimate comeback path. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][3])

**Key recurring decision:** **Energize for safety or accept Cosmic Rust for value?** The second-order decision is whether to start prepared multiball now or risk more setup to bring Mission + 2× into it.

## Danger zones

* **Side-ramp miss:** expert guidance specifically warns that misses can ricochet between the bumper and slings and end a ball quickly. [S4] ([Kineticist][5])
* **Lower-left bumper/slingshot cluster:** the layout intentionally makes this area unusually lethal after left-side misses. [S4] ([Kineticist][5])
* **Megatron/drop-bank rebounds:** necessary mode work puts the ball into direct standup/drop-target traffic near center.
* **Premium/LE cannon return:** spectacular but fast; once the cannon fires, commentary should expect an immediate left-flipper recovery rather than a settled feed. [S1][S3] ([Stern Pinball][2])

## Trivia

* This is Stern's first Transformers game based specifically on the **original Generation 1 cartoon**; its 2011 Transformers machine used the live-action films. [S4][S5] ([Kineticist][7])
* It is **Elliot Eismin's second lead design**, following *John Wick*. [S4] ([Kineticist][5])
* **Peter Cullen** reprises Optimus Prime; **Frank Welker** voices Megatron and Soundwave. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][2])
* The LE is limited to **750** and adds mirrored art, illuminated armor, upgraded expression/speaker lighting and collector treatments. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][2])
* The game celebrates the 40th anniversary of *The Transformers: The Movie* and includes **“The Touch”** by Stan Bush. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][2])
