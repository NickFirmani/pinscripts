## Identity and versions

**The Beatles — Gold Edition**, Stern Pinball / Ka-Pow Pinball, 2018. Lead design **George Gomez**, adapting Mike Kubin’s 1980 *Seawitch* layout; software/rules by **Dean Grover**, mechanical engineering by **Tom Kopera**, art by **Christopher Franchi**. SPIKE 2 platform with LCD, four flippers, 11 drop targets, two spinners, top magnet and magnetic spinning-record disc. [S1][S5] ([Stern Pinball][1])

Gold is one of three cosmetic editions: **1,614 Gold, 250 Platinum, 100 Diamond**. I found no rules or playfield differences between editions; Platinum/Diamond add collectible/cosmetic distinctions. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])

Latest software verified: **v1.27, November 26, 2024**. Its Beatles-specific scoring is unchanged from earlier releases, but its Competition Install explicitly enables Competition Mode and disables extra balls/player game-mode selection. [S2] ([Pinside][2])

Major multiballs: **All My Loving**, **Hard Day’s Night / Beatlemania Multiball**, and **Taxman Multiball**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][3])

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Start an achievable song**—competitive guidance particularly likes *It Won’t Be Long* (pops)—and advance it aggressively. [S4] ([Japan Flipper Pinball Association][4])
2. After a song ends, complete **F-A-B + F-O-U-R**, then shoot the **upper magnet** to start another song. In current Competition Install, get all five songs to **Level 3** to qualify Beatlemania. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][2])
3. **Cash the Song Super Jackpot behind F-A-B or gamble on carrying it:** it is worth the completed mode total, can multiply when several modes are completed on the same ball, and **vanishes on drain**. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][3])
4. For Beatlemania, shoot the upper magnet, use the ~30-second single-ball build to increase the jackpot, then attack jackpots/multiball; current base Beatlemania jackpot is **400K**, boosted 50K per qualifying increment. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][2])
5. **Alternate leverage:** spell BEATLEMANIA with loops to earn **2X spinners, 5X loops, Collect Bonus, then 10X loops**; time those multipliers with the corresponding spinner/loop song. [S2][S3][S4] ([Pinside][2])

## Core rules and persistence

**Confirmed rule:** the four normal timed songs run for 60 seconds and score by loops (*Drive My Car*), drop banks (*I Should Have Known Better*), spinners (*Ticket to Ride*), or pops (*It Won’t Be Long*). **All My Loving** is an untimed 2-ball multiball with doubled scoring. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][3])

Song progression is game-long wizard-mode progress; after Beatlemania or Taxman ends, the modes reset when multiball returns to single-ball play. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][3])

**Song Super Jackpot is the major cash-out decision:** completing a mode lights the target hidden behind F-A-B for that mode’s total. Complete multiple modes on one ball without collecting and earlier values receive escalating multipliers, up to a theoretical 5X sequence. Drain first and the opportunity is lost. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][3])

**Beatlemania qualification:** Level 2 in normal settings, **Level 3 with Competition Install**. It begins with approximately 30 seconds of single-ball jackpot building; drop-bank completions add 50K to the jackpot. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][2])

**Taxman:** default HARD qualification requires all songs at Level 5, with All My Loving requiring three jackpots. EASY reduces this to one All My Loving jackpot plus Level 3 on the other songs. Current Taxman scoring starts at **800K**. [S2] ([Pinside][2])

Bonus reaches **75K × 7X = 525K**; Bonus X carries ball-to-ball, unlike several per-ball drop-bank features. [S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][3])

## Skill shots

* **Normal:** short plunge to the star rollover — **10K**. [S3]
* **Super:** feed the upper-right flipper and shoot the upper loop — **25K**. [S3]
* **Super Duper:** plunge below the upper magnet so the first major contact is the top pop, or shoot that pop from the upper-left flipper — **100K**. Strong tournament choice. [S3][S4]
* **Secret Skill Shot:** after rolling through the left inlane during play, shoot the temporarily lit star rollover — **75K on v1.10+**. The older v1.22 community rulesheet still prints 50K, but Stern's v1.10 release notes explicitly raised it to 75K. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][2])

Repeated successful Super/Super-Duper skill shots increase their values. [S2] ([Pinside][2])

## Secondary features

**Ball saves:** Competition Install sets dedicated multiball saves of **10s All My Loving / 15s Beatlemania / 20s Taxman**. A separate adjustable left-outlane save can return a ball draining shortly after hitting the Super Jackpot target; introduced default is 4 seconds. [S2] ([Pinside][2])

**Extra Ball:** complete all three drop banks on one ball; the award alternates between the inlanes with sling hits. Disabled Extra Balls score **200K**. Current Competition Install sets **NO EXTRA BALLS**. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][2])

**Mystery:** Competition Mode uses a deterministic sequence beginning 25K → Advance Bonus X → 2X Pops → 50K → 2X Left Spinner…. During relevant multiballs/timed songs, Add-A-Ball/Add Time receive priority. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][2])

**Player control:** while a ball is detected in the shooter lane, flipper buttons select the available song. [S2] ([Pinside][2])

## What to watch

* **Song insert flashing quickly:** currently selected song; slow flash means previously played, solid means Level 5 complete. [S3]
* **Super Jackpot target exposed/lit behind F-A-B:** a large, drain-sensitive cash-out is waiting. [S3]
* **BEATLEMANIA letters / upper magnet lit:** loop progress has earned one of the ordered multiplier/bonus awards. [S3]
* **All five songs at the tournament threshold:** upper magnet can start Beatlemania; this is the major scoring breakpoint. [S2][S3] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][3])

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** cash a lit Song Super Jackpot rather than greedily stacking it, take All My Loving when convenient, and emphasize the pop/loop routes over dangerous direct drop shots. **Strategic inference**, supported by competitive descriptions of the playfield’s draininess. [S4] ([Japan Flipper Pinball Association][4])

**Playing behind:** push all five songs to Level 3 for Beatlemania, synchronize BEATLEMANIA’s 2X-spinner/5X-loop awards with their matching song modes, and consider leaving Song Super Jackpot uncollected to build a multiplied cash-out. Taxman is the extreme high-variance ceiling. [S2][S3][S4]

**Key recurring decision:** **cash Song Super Jackpot now, or risk another mode and multiply it?** Drain destroys the uncollected value, so score position should strongly affect that choice. [S3]

## Danger zones

* **F-A-B direct shots:** competitive guidance specifically warns they are drain-prone; exploit early ball save when possible. [S4]
* **F-O-U-R from the upper-left flipper:** documented as a particularly dangerous rebound; players often prefer shooting toward 1-9-6-4 instead. [S4]
* **Super Jackpot return:** can head immediately toward the left outlane; Stern added a dedicated courtesy-save adjustment specifically for this situation. [S2]
* **Left inlane/outlane area:** reverse/backhand feeds can acquire bad spin and sling into the outlane. [S4]
* **Spinning record:** deliberately introduces magnetic chaos; Competition Install limits it to multiball, so don't expect normal single-ball record action on a properly installed tournament setup. [S1][S2] ([Stern Pinball][5])

## Trivia

* The playfield is a deliberate modernization of **Mike Kubin’s 1980 Stern *Seawitch***; Gomez says he modified the geometry so the top orbits could be reached from the lower flippers and added the magnets, skill shots and hidden return. [S5] ([Strictly Pinball][6])
* Exactly **1,964** machines were announced, referencing the Beatles' 1964 American breakthrough; Gold accounts for 1,614. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Despite being a SPIKE 2 LCD game, it intentionally uses **simulated score reels** and a ramp-free, four-flipper retro layout. [S1]
* **Cousin Brucie**, who introduced the Beatles at Shea Stadium, recorded custom game callouts. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Code **v1.22** added **Classic Mode**, with Seawitch-like scoring and no multiballs, magnets or center-disc action. [S2] ([Pinside][2])

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Beatlemania ball count:** Stern's v1.08 release notes explicitly say Beatlemania Multiball **defaults to 3 balls** [S2], while the community rulesheet based on v1.22 describes it as **4-ball multiball** [S3]. I found no later changelog entry clearly documenting that change. Confirm the venue setting before stating a ball count. ([Pinside][2])
* **Secret Skill Shot:** [S3] still lists 50K, but Stern's v1.10 notes explicitly increased it to **75K** [S2]. For v1.10+ use 75K. ([Pinside][2])
* **Taxman qualification** is adjustment-dependent; HARD is the documented default, while EASY materially shortens the route. [S2]
