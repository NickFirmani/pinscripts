## Identity and versions

* **Batman ’66 (Premium)** — Stern Pinball, 2016, on the then-new **SPIKE 2** platform with full-color HD LCD. Lead designer **George Gomez**; code/rules by **Lyman F. Sheats Jr.**; playfield art by **Kevin O’Connor and Christopher Franchi**, with Premium cabinet/translite art by Franchi. [S1][S2] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Premium, LE and Super LE share the core layout/rules; LE/SLE differences are principally trim, art, audio and collector hardware. The later Catwoman Signature Edition is also mechanically based on this game. [S1][S2] ([Stern Pinball][2])
* Competitive multiballs worth recognizing: **Villain Multiball** is the main one; **Shame** begins as a useful 2-ball multiball; three deeper Minor Villain wizard modes are 6-ball multiballs. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* Latest code I could verify: **1.14, July 1, 2026**. The detailed community rulesheet still identifies itself as based on **1.03**, so later-code equivalence is an unresolved verification issue. [S3][S6] ([Tilt Forums][4])
* Scope here is specifically the **Premium** requested in the uploaded brief. 

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed tournament strategy:** qualify **Catwoman** by shooting both ramps, hit the Bat-Phone, and start her Major Villain mode. Catwoman is attractive because the ramps are comparatively flow-oriented and help build useful lane progress. [S3][S4] ([Tilt Forums][3])
2. Before scoring mode shots, the **Penguin scoop → left orbit Batcave Combo** doubles the base value of that villain mode. Commissioner Gordon can also build permanent shot multipliers and increase Villain Multiball scoring. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][5])
3. During the villain mode, hit the turntable lock targets and lock three balls via the **right orbit** for Villain Multiball. **Shame** is a viable safety/stack along the way because its first attempt is 2-ball multiball. [S3][S4]
4. The huge leverage comes from multiplying the same scoring: up to **3× playfield**, permanent **2×–6× shot multipliers**, villain-mode multipliers, and eventually King Tut’s **Main Channel multiplier**. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
5. Alternate/deep strategy: complete **Bookworm → Mr. Freeze → repeated King Tut**. Bookworm makes minor-villain perks persistent, Freeze stops important timers, and later Tuts add Main Channel X. This has a vastly higher ceiling but requires much more survival. **Observed strategy.** [S3][S5] ([Tilt Forums][3])

## Core rules and persistence

* Four Major Villains: **Catwoman = ramps, Joker = Villain-Vision/turntable targets, Penguin = scoop, Riddler = orbits**. Qualify one, then shoot the Bat-Phone hurry-up to start its mode. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* Major Villain shots build a **Super Jackpot (SJP)**. When SJP lights at the scoop, the player can cash out or continue to the next phase for **+1× mode scoring** and a potentially much larger eventual SJP. [S3]
* The **Batcave Combo**—scoop then orange-lit left orbit before the first mode shot—doubles that mode's base scoring. [S3]
* **Shot multipliers persist for the rest of the ball:** three Gordon hits light the first 2×; later multipliers rise through **6×** and are attached to whichever major shot is made next. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* Hit both Batmobile-side targets → Atomic Pile lights for **2× playfield for 30 seconds**; repeat during the timer for **3×**. [S3]
* Minor-villain perks normally last only that ball. **Bookworm** makes them last for the game; **Mr. Freeze** freezes major timers; first King Tut saves minor-villain progress, and subsequent Tut completions increase Main Channel X for that ball. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* Easy commentary trap: stacked modes continue running even when not displayed. Holding the action button changes the **Main Channel**, which matters because King Tut's multiplier applies only to that channel. [S3]

## Skill shots

* **Top-Lane Skill Shot:** plunge when the shooter-lane indicator has the desired top lane(s) lit, then collect the lit rollover. Scores 600K + 200K per prior success and adds **+5 Bonus X**. Bonus can become substantial. [S3][S4] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Penguin Skill Shot:** soft plunge directly into the Penguin scoop—or quickly backhand it after a short plunge. Roughly 1M–1.2M, +5 Bonus X, and it also advances the scoop's normal award. [S3]
* **Super Skill Shot:** hold the **left flipper** while plunging; for five seconds, make a flashing major shot. With no villain running it instantly qualifies that villain; during a villain it awards a **Gadget**. [S3]

## Secondary features

* **Standard ball save:** factory manual lists **5 seconds**, operator-adjustable. Villain Multiball has its own save; completing Shame adds a ball to multiballs and **+10 seconds** to their base save. [S2][S3] ([Stern Pinball][6])
* **Gadgets:** complete GADGET, then tap the action button to spot a mode shot. A Gadget can also route a lit lock onto the turntable from the left-orbit saucer/shooter lane. Holding, rather than tapping, changes TV channel. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Mystery:** complete the return lanes and collect at the left-orbit saucer. Awards are context-sensitive; notably, during multiball Mystery gives **Add-A-Ball**, and during timed modes it can add time. [S3]
* **Extra ball:** normally lights at the left-orbit saucer after two captured Major Villains, at combo thresholds, or via percentage-based Mystery. Tournament treatment must be checked. [S3]
* **Competition Mode** exists as an operator setting; the service manual does not document enough Batman-specific behavioral detail to assume exactly which random awards it normalizes. [S2]

## What to watch

1. **Bat-Phone exposed on the turntable:** a Major Villain is qualified; the next Bat-Phone hit starts it. [S3]
2. **White flashing X at a major shot:** a permanent shot multiplier is assigned there for the rest of the ball; count flashes for its value. [S3]
3. **Green major-shot arrows + balls on the rotating turntable:** Villain Multiball is running; after enough jackpots, watch for the turntable to expose **Atomic Pile SJP**. [S3]
4. **LCD's large TV versus small TVs:** the large TV is the Main Channel. If Tut multipliers exist, a player holding the action button may be deliberately moving the multiplier onto the valuable mode. [S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** use the documented **Catwoman → Shame → Villain Multiball** safety route, take good SJP cash-outs, use ramp flow, and avoid spending an entire ball constructing Bookworm/Freeze/Tut infrastructure. **Observed strategy.** [S4] ([Colorado Pinball][7])

**Playing behind:** invest in stacked multipliers or the deep **Bookworm → Freeze → Tut** engine; alternatively continue Major Villain stages instead of cashing SJP immediately. Batman ’66 can turn one heavily multiplied shot into a match-changing score. **Observed strategy.** [S3][S5] ([Apple Podcasts][8])

**Key recurring decision:** **cash the villain Super Jackpot now, or continue the story for another +1×?** Cashing protects accumulated value; continuing deliberately trades security for multiplicative scoring.

## Danger zones

1. **Turntable magnet release:** reports range from a left-flipper feed to straight-down-the-middle; highly setup-dependent. [S7] ([Pinside][9])
2. **Left-ramp entrance/exit:** early machines in particular were reported to produce airballs if ramp/wireform alignment was poor. [S7] ([Pinside][10])
3. **Right orbit:** can rip around quickly and feed a sling rather than a clean flipper return. [S7] ([Pinside][11])
4. **Atomic Pile/Batmobile area:** narrow center shot with nearby targets; failed attempts can return uncontrolled.
5. **Gordon/TV/GADGET standups:** strategically tempting multiplier/resource targets, but repeated direct standup shooting sacrifices the controlled flow of the ramps.

## Trivia

* Batman ’66 debuted Stern's **SPIKE 2 platform and HD LCD presentation**, using actual footage from the 1966 television series. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Original Batman and Robin actors **Adam West and Burt Ward** supplied speech for the game. [S1]
* The rotating **Action Turntable** is a genuine motorized mini-playfield that alternates between the Bat-Phone/Villain Vision and Batcave mechanisms. [S1][S2] ([Stern Pinball][12])
* Stern released the machine for both its own **30th anniversary** and the television show's **50th anniversary**. [S1]
* The game was inspired by former Stern designer **Joe Kaminkow**, with Gomez and Sheats leading development. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* The best detailed rules reference [S3] says it is based on **v1.03**, while the current-code ledger lists **v1.14 (July 1, 2026)** [S6]. I found no authoritative 1.14 changelog detailed enough to certify every scoring rule above unchanged.

* The manual confirms a **Competition Mode** setting [S2] but does not adequately document Batman-specific normalization; its precise venue behavior should be checked rather than assumed.
* Physical turntable, ramp and orbit danger is demonstrably **machine/setup-dependent**. [S7]
