## Identity and versions

* **Pokémon by Stern Pinball — Pro**, Stern Pinball, **2026**. Officially revealed/released February 12, 2026. Lead design: **George Gomez and Jack Danger**; code/rules credits include **Tanio Klyce, Andrew Wilkening, and Joshua Henderson**. [S1][S4] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* **SPIKE 3** platform. Pro/Premium/LE share the major rules architecture, but the Pro has **static Pikachu, no arena magnet, a static Poké Ball/left-ramp stop instead of the Premium/LE lock, no Squirtle whirlpool, and a Psyduck stand-up instead of the scoop**. These differences matter for feeds and a few awards. [S1][S2] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* **Rules basis: `code` — Pro v0.85.0, 2026-08-07.** Stern distributes downloadable updates; v0.85 added Pokémon Arena and Pikachu vs. Lucario. [S3] 
* Regularly relevant multiballs: **Pikachu vs. Raichu** and **Team Rocket Multiball 1/2**. Deep progression adds **Pikachu vs. Lucario** and **Pikachu vs. Charizard**; an optional topper adds **Magikarp Multiball**. [S3][S4] 

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** take the normal top-lane skill shot; the bumpers also advance Pokémon discovery. Start working the **right ramp three times** toward Pikachu vs. Raichu, the easiest immediately useful multiball. [S4][S5] ([Tilt Forums][2])
2. During Pikachu multiball, exploit the ball save to hit **BATTLE** targets. Completing BATTLE lights a **green Action Button add-a-ball**; meanwhile jackpots lead to Pokédex captive-ball Super Jackpots. [S4][S5] ([Tilt Forums][2])
3. Build **Team Rocket Multiball**: complete BATTLE, hit the lowered Meowth balloon three times, then enter the center lane. **Observed strategy:** stack a single-ball story—especially **Squirtle**—into it. [S4][S5] ([Tilt Forums][2])
4. The major leverage is multiplicative: qualify a **permanent-for-the-ball 2× shot multiplier**, preferably on a valuable shot such as the left ramp, and/or run **20-second 2× Playfield** from the berry targets during a valuable mode or multiball. [S4][S5] ([Tilt Forums][2])
5. Alternate long-ball route: catch Pokémon → play Rival Battles → travel through biomes, or complete all four stories for the Lucario/Charizard mini-wizards. Rival battles and strong story play can produce major scoring without relying solely on Team Rocket. [S3][S4][S5] 

## Core rules and persistence

* Each character story starts after **three shots** to its lane during eligible single-ball play: left orbit/Bulbasaur, left ramp/Squirtle, right ramp/Pikachu, right orbit/Charmander. Completing a story qualifies a shot multiplier; playing all four qualifies the Lucario/Charizard choice at the right ramp. [S3][S4] 
* A qualified **shot multiplier applies to the next major shot made: 2× all scoring from that shot for the rest of the ball**. If every shot already has 2×, the next qualification can create 3×. Easy commentator trap: this is not merely a mode-jackpot multiplier. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* Berry targets start **2× Playfield for 20 seconds**; center-berry hits while active add 5 seconds, capped back at 20 seconds. This can compound with shot X. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* Team Rocket Multiball 1: six jackpots → center-lane Super Jackpot. That Super qualifies shot X and starts a **15-second unlimited-Super-Jackpot window** on the outside major shots under current v0.85 documentation. Later cycles require eight jackpots. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* Discover → scan Pokédex → CATCH hurry-up → final left-ramp catch → Town scoop Rival Battle. Winning lights a **10-second left-ramp Victory Bonus**. Training affects battle strength and adds 20 seconds during timed story modes. [S3][S4] 
* End-of-ball bonus can become substantial on long games: Pokémon caught, berry hits and Poké Balls, all multiplied by Bonus X up to **20×**. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][2])

## Skill shots

* **Standard skill shot:** full plunge through the flashing top lane. Starts at 500K and increases **Bonus X by 2×**, then +1× on subsequent successful skill shots. **Observed strategy:** usually preferred because the resulting bumper action also advances Pokémon discovery. [S4][S5] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* **Super skill shot:** hold the **left flipper** during a full plunge, then shoot a flashing playfield shot; starts at 1M / 2× normal skill-shot value. Center lane instantly completes BATTLE and lowers Meowth; if Team Rocket Multiball is already lit there, it starts MB and lights add-a-ball. Town scoop lights Mystery. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* **Super-secret Psyduck skill shot:** current v0.85 rules documentation gives 3× skill-shot value, starting at 1.5M, for a lucky bounce into Psyduck. **Pro-specific execution is uncertain:** Stern confirms the Pro has a stand-up target where Premium/LE have the Psyduck scoop. [S2][S4] ([Stern Pinball][3])

## Secondary features

* **Pokédex Assist:** captive-ball hits earn up to three assists. A **red** Action Button spots a CATCH or Rival-Battle shot, but cannot award the final Catch or Victory Bonus. [S3][S4] 
* **Multiball add-a-ball:** complete BATTLE during Pikachu vs. Raichu or Team Rocket Multiball; press the Action Button while **green**. This is a major tournament survival resource. [S4][S5] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* **Scan ball save:** scanning a discovered Pokémon starts a short, operator-adjustable save. [S3][S4] 
* **Outlane save:** complete the Town targets; its lit outlane can normally be moved with the flippers. Qualification gets harder after each use. [S3][S4] 
* **Extra balls:** verified qualifications include three stories, 50 Poké Balls, 25 captive-ball hits, or rare Mystery. When unavailable/maxed, the documented award is 2.5M; exact tournament configuration should be checked. [S4] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* **Optional Magikarp topper:** letters earned by catching Pokémon explicitly persist **player-to-player and game-to-game**, making tournament reset policy important. [S3][S6] 

## What to watch

* **Pikachu cheek inserts glowing:** 2× Playfield is live; this is the moment ordinary jackpots can become match-changing. [S4]
* **Action Button green:** add-a-ball is ready. **Red:** a stored Pokédex Assist is usable. [S3][S4]
* **Meowth balloon lowered:** Team Rocket qualification is underway; after enough balloon hits the center lane becomes the multiball start. [S4]
* **“X” insert at a major shot:** that lane has persistent-for-this-ball doubled scoring; watch where the player deliberately places the next qualified multiplier. [S4]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** Favor reachable Pikachu/Team Rocket multiballs, cash Super Jackpots, use the green-button add-a-ball intelligently, and avoid forcing dangerous captive-ball/berry-target setup solely to manufacture a giant multiplier stack. **Strategic inference** based on the current rules and documented feeds.

**Playing behind:** Build **2× Playfield + shot X**, stack Squirtle or another single-ball story with Team Rocket, attack the Super Jackpot/unlimited-Super window, and consider longer story progression toward Lucario/Charizard. These deliberately increase scoring ceiling and variance. [S4][S5] ([Tilt Forums][2])

**Key recurring decision:** **setup versus immediate multiball scoring**—whether to spend ball control on berries, BATTLE, captive-ball progress and a strategically placed shot X, or start/cash the scoring feature already available.

## Danger zones

* **Pokédex captive-ball rebound:** often kicks toward the right-orbit guide/right sling and can threaten the outlane. [S5]
* **Town-scoop eject:** fast, awkward feed toward the left flipper; expert guidance favors being ready for a reflex flip rather than assuming a comfortable dead bounce. [S5]
* **Right-ramp return:** arrives quickly at the right flipper with little automatic settling time. [S5]
* **Left-orbit return:** tends toward the tip of the left flipper; manageable, but feed speed/angle requires attention. [S5]
* **Center stand-ups/BATTLE:** repeated qualification shots invite lateral rebounds around an unusually open lower playfield. **Strategic inference.** ([Kineticist][4])

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Team Rocket unlimited-Super timer:** current v0.85 rulesheet says **15 seconds** [S4]; Noah Crable’s April 2026 strategy guide says **20 seconds** [S5]. The latter predates v0.85, so **15 seconds is the preferred current value**, but this appears revision-sensitive. ([Tilt Forums][2])
* **Magikarp start shot:** Stern’s official topper page says spelling MAGIKARP lights Multiball at the **right ramp** [S6]; the current v0.85 community rulesheet says **left ramp** [S4]. Verify on the installed topper/code. ([Stern Pinball][5])
* **Super-secret skill shot on Pro:** the rulesheet describes a Psyduck “sneak-in” [S4], while Stern’s edition matrix confirms the Pro substitutes a **stand-up target** for the Premium/LE scoop [S2]. The Pro-specific triggering path is not sufficiently documented. ([Tilt Forums][2])
* Stern v0.82 notes state that **Competition install now affects various game features** but do not enumerate enough behavior to safely state how Mystery/random awards or every tournament-sensitive feature is normalized. [S3]
