## Identity and versions

* **Led Zeppelin (Premium)** — Stern Pinball, December 2020; SPIKE 2, LCD, three flippers. Scope is the Premium specified in the supplied brief. 
* Design: **Steve Ritchie**; mechanics: **John Rotharmel**; software: **Tim Sexton, Raymond Davidson, Dean Grover**; art: **Greg Freres and Stephen Jensen**; callouts: **Brendon Small**. [S1][S4] ([Kineticist][1])
* Premium/LE uniquely add the motorized **Electric Magic** device: it rises from beneath the playfield as a spinner, magnetically captures balls, and enables Electric Magic Multiball/Frenzy. They also add the upper-flipper **side ramp** and Expression Lighting; Pro substitutes a standup side shot and lacks Electric Magic. [S1][S2] ([Stern Pinball][2])
* **Latest verified code: V1.20.** The embedded Stern changelog dates it **November 13, 2023**, although Pinside's archive header labels it November 13, 2024; V1.20 is mostly system/competition-install support, not a major gameplay rewrite. [S4]
* Major paths: **Tour Multiballs, Zeppelin Multiball, Electric Magic Multiball/Frenzy**, plus deeper Mothership, World Tour and Top of the Charts. [S2][S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Always work toward a multiball.** Shoot the left eject early to select a Tour; Tour objectives can often be advanced while playing other features. **Observed strategy.** [S3][S5] ([Tilt Forums][3])
2. Simultaneously build **Icarus X**: make combos, finish them into the four target banks, then complete all four banks and hit Icarus. The four bank multipliers are **multiplied together**, so balanced buildup is extremely powerful. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
3. Start **Zeppelin, Tour or Electric Magic Multiball**, then activate Icarus X while many song/jackpot shots are available. This is the primary match-swinging scoring state. **Observed strategy.** [S3][S5] ([Tilt Forums][4])
4. During multiball, hit Icarus three times for **Add-A-Ball**; save three Band Boosts for **Super Band Boost**, which also adds a ball. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
5. Premium alternate: pursue **Electric Magic**. A strong Electric Magic jackpot setup or Frenzy can become enormous, but qualifying it requires dangerous LED/ZEP target work. [S3][S5]

## Core rules and persistence

* A **song mode is always running**. Lit shots change with verses/choruses/solos; 100K sales earns a Gold Record, five Gold Records completes the song, and ten makes it Platinum. Song progress is player-specific and persists when returning to that song. [S3][S5] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* Completing five songs lights **Mothership Multiball**; all ten lights **Top of the Charts**. These are real long-game scoring routes but normally secondary to the immediate multiball/Icarus strategy. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Tours:** left eject selects one of four. First Tour needs one objective; each later Tour adds another objective and another ball to its multiball, up to five balls. Tour jackpot values carry between Tour Multiballs. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Zeppelin Multiball:** first game attempt needs three left-ramp locks; later attempts first require ROCK. Four jackpots each open a short timed double-jackpot combo. Finish all four to light Super Jackpot at left eject and Premium side ramp; the side ramp scores **2×** the easier eject collect. LED/ZEP/ROCK completions can raise the Super multiplier to 4×. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Icarus X:** completing combos into a bank raises that bank by **0.1× per combo shot**. Total Icarus X is the *product* of all four bank values. Complete all banks and hit Icarus for **15 seconds + 5 seconds per Premium side-ramp shot**. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* Example of the leverage: four banks built merely to **2.0× each = 16× playfield scoring**. This is why target-bank setup can matter more than an ordinary jackpot.
* Multiballs generally **do not stack with one another**, though Tour objectives/locks can be advanced during other multiballs. [S5] ([Kineticist][5])

## Skill shots

* **LED Skill Shot:** after selecting the song, plunge into the currently lit moving **L-E-D target**. Award: **500K + 5 seconds of ball save**. Flippers can move the selection. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* No separate Super/Secret skill shot was verified in current rules.

## Secondary features

* **Band Boost / action button:** green button uses one Boost. In single-ball play it cashes the current combo at 5× and advances the lowest bank multiplier. Holding three enables **Super Band Boost**, worth 10× and advancing the two lowest banks. During multiball, normal Boost spots Icarus; Super Boost **adds a ball**. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Left-outlane save:** completing the far-left two-bank qualifies a save; later saves require additional completions. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Zeppelin Awards:** each Premium side-ramp shot cycles Super Spinner, Pops, Slings and Ramps; it also adds **5 seconds to the next/current Icarus X**. Super Spinner starts at +100K/spin for 100 spins, making it a genuine swing feature when combined with Electric Magic/spinner play. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Extra Balls:** several qualifications exist. When disabled they score **10M**, and that 10M is multiplied by Icarus X. Current Competition Install explicitly sets **No Extra Balls**. [S3][S4] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* Factory normal ball save is **5 seconds**. [S2] ([Stern Pinball][6])

## What to watch

* **Four central band-symbol multipliers:** their colors indicate the component values being multiplied into Icarus X; four completed banks means Icarus is ready. [S3]
* **Icarus target flashing:** the player is about to start the critical timed playfield multiplier.
* **Electric Magic physically rises:** Premium-only multiball qualification is active; repeated spinner shots are charging the magnet. [S1][S3]
* **Action button red rather than green:** three saved Band Boosts means **Super Band Boost**—during multiball that is an Add-A-Ball. [S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** Get into whichever multiball is closest and use it as protection for song shots and target-bank work. Start a modest Icarus X rather than continuing risky single-ball combos solely to raise it further. **Strategic inference.**

**Playing behind:** Build the four bank multipliers as evenly as possible, prepare a multiball, then activate **Icarus X during the multiball**. Electric Magic Frenzy plus a strong multiplier, or Zeppelin Super Jackpots with their own multiplier layered under Icarus X, can erase a huge deficit. **Observed strategy.** [S3][S5]

**Key recurring decision:** **Start multiball now or keep building Icarus X first?** Multiball gives survival and many lit shots; every extra pre-multiball combo can multiply the eventual scoring dramatically.

## Danger zones

* **Left-ramp reject:** one of the most common straight-down-the-middle returns. [S5]
* **Premium side ramp:** upper-flipper brick can deflect directly toward either outlane. [S5]
* **LED/ZEP banks:** required for Electric Magic and Icarus, but repeated standup/drop-target attacks create unpredictable lateral rebounds. [S5]
* **Plunge into LED bank:** an uncontrolled opening plunge can ricochet immediately toward the right outlane. [S5]
* **Wide-open lower playfield:** fast horizontal motion above the slings frequently becomes an outlane drain; Led Zeppelin rewards accuracy more than recovery. [S5]

## Trivia

* Led Zeppelin was **Steve Ritchie's final Stern playfield design** before he left for Jersey Jack Pinball. [S5] ([Kineticist][5])
* Stern's software lead **Tim Sexton** describes himself as having led the rules/software development on the SPIKE 2 game. [S6] ([Tim Sexton][7])
* All editions synchronize changing shot patterns to the musical structure of **ten full Led Zeppelin songs**. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][2])
* Premium/LE's Electric Magic mechanism physically **rises from beneath the playfield, spins, magnetically captures a ball, then lowers it out of sight**. [S1]
* The Premium also has **four ramps versus three on Pro**, largely because its upper-flipper side shot is a full return ramp. [S1][S4]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **V1.20 date:** Pinside's current header labels it **November 13, 2024**, but the embedded Stern changelog itself says **“LE v1.20 — November 13, 2023.”** [S4] The version is certain; the displayed release year is conflicting. ([Pinside][8])
* **Rulesheet revision:** the maintained Tilt rulesheet labels itself V1.20 and incorporates the important later rules. [S3] No gameplay changes after V1.20 were found.
* **Competition Install:** current V1.20 install explicitly sets Extra Ball Limit to **No Extra Balls**, but older tournament setups may use Competition Mode without the full install. [S4]
* Physical Electric Magic behavior and side-ramp feeds are highly setup-dependent.
