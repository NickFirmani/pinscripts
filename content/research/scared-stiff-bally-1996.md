## Identity and versions

* **Scared Stiff** — Bally/Midway, **September 1996**, model **50048**, **WPC-95**. Design by **Dennis Nordman and Mark Weyna**; art/design **Greg Freres**; software/design **Mike Boon and Cameron Silver**. Production: **4,028 units**. [S1][S4]
* Latest official game ROM: **1.5, February 7, 1997**. It fixes multiplayer skill-shot scoring, Deadhead scoring, Coffin jams and several Spider/Leaper bugs; tournament copies should preferably run 1.5 or at minimum 1.3+ for improved Coffin handling. [S2]
* Main multiballs: **Stiff in the Coffin** (3-ball), **Terror from the Crate** (2-ball), **Monster Multiball** (4-ball wizard payoff), plus deep **Spider Mania**. [S3][S5]
* Scope follows the supplied commentator-reference specification.

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** soft-plunge into the **Spider hole**, not the normal skill-shot hole, and deliberately stop the backbox Spider on **Coffin** or **Crate**. Those awards advance the corresponding multiball **and give it a free restart after draining to one ball**. [S5]
2. Prioritize **Stiff in the Coffin**: shoot the left ramp to light Lock, then the far-left loop to lock three balls. In multiball, jackpots alternate between the two ramps, starting at **500K**. [S3][S5]
3. If available, collect **Double Trouble** from the Spider first: **2× all scoring for 20 seconds**, ideally overlapping multiball. [S3][S5]
4. Complete all **six Tales of Terror**, then shoot the crate for **Scared Stiff**. Reach Stiff-O-Meter level 10 to score **5M** and start Monster Multiball, where every ramp/crate jackpot is **1M**. [S3][S5]
5. Alternate conservative route: repeatedly loop the left ramp. Its combo value becomes significant and it is safer than deliberately bashing the crate on many copies. **Observed strategy.** [S5]

## Core rules and persistence

* Six Tales: **Stiff in the Coffin**, **Terror from the Crate**, **Monster's Lab** (20 pop hits), **Night of the Leapers** (all three Leaper targets), **Return of the Deadheads** (all three top lanes), and **Eye of the Boney Beast** (three left ramps). [S3]
* Tales are accomplishments, not mutually exclusive timed modes; the two major multiballs cannot run together. [S3]
* **Stiff in the Coffin:** each lock is lit from the left ramp and collected at the left loop. The first multiball requires one left-ramp completion per lock; repeat multiballs require progressively more, capped at five. [S3]
* Coffin jackpots start **500K**. After the first jackpot, ramps alternate; hitting the currently unlit ramp raises the jackpot. [S3]
* **Terror from the Crate:** light all four crate eyes, then enter the crate for 2-ball multiball. Repeated crate starts become progressively harder. [S3]
* After all six Tales, crate starts **Scared Stiff**, a 30-second single-ball challenge. Ramp/crate shots advance the Stiff-O-Meter; reaching level 10 awards 5M and starts Monster Multiball. If the player fails, **Stiff-O-Meter progress persists** to the next Scared Stiff attempt, but the six Tales must be completed again. [S3][S5]
* **Spider awards persist as collected** and cannot normally be taken twice. Coffin/Crate are especially important because their restart benefit remains available until used. [S3][S5]
* Three right-ramp shots award **Hold Bonus**, making large switch bonus from long multiballs relevant on subsequent balls. [S3][S6]

## Skill shots

**Normal Skill Shot**

* Plunge into the upper **Skill Shot hole**.
* Scores **250K**, increasing another 250K for each subsequent successful skill shot. [S3]
* ROM 1.5 fixes a bug where players 2–4 could be denied these points. [S2]

**Spider soft-plunge — observed tournament alternative**

* Plunge just far enough through the upper gates to fall into the **Spin Spider hole**.
* This is **not the coded skill shot**, but competitive guides prefer it because a controlled Coffin, Crate or Double Trouble award is usually worth far more than the skill-shot points. [S5][S7]

## Secondary features

* **Spin the Spider:** right ramp lights the Spider hole; flippers stop the backbox pointer. Important awards include Coffin, Crate, Double Trouble, spotted Deadheads/Lab/Leaper progress, Light Jackpot and Extra Ball progress. [S3]
* **Double Trouble:** **2× all scoring for 20 seconds**. Best used immediately before a multiball or another high-value state. [S3][S5]
* **Saved by the Spell:** complete the three targets above the left outlane, normally twice, to light a **left-outlane drain save**. Production games do not have a physical kickback—the game serves another ball instead. [S3][S5]
* **Telepathetic Power:** right-side target toggles the right-outlane award; collecting it gives an award rather than saving the ball. [S3]
* **Extra Ball:** can be lit from Spider eyeballs, Web/right-ramp progress, Leaper Mania, Bonus X and reaching Scared Stiff; collect at the center passage. Tournament treatment materially changes strategy. [S1][S3]
* **Spider Mania:** collecting every Spider award eventually starts a 4-ball feature with broad scoring lit. It is real but generally too deep to drive ordinary tournament strategy. [S3]

## What to watch

1. **Coffin or Crate Spider insert solid:** that multiball now has a valuable automatic restart waiting.
2. **Left-loop Lock lamp:** the player has done the left-ramp setup and is going for a Coffin lock.
3. **Six Tale inserts lit:** crate now starts Scared Stiff; this is the pivot from setup to wizard payoff.
4. **Stiff-O-Meter:** level approaching 10 means Monster Multiball and its repeated **1M shots** are imminent.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** prioritize **Coffin + Spider restart** and controlled left-ramp/left-loop play. Take straightforward alternating Coffin jackpots and avoid unnecessary crate bashing or Leaper standups.

**Playing behind:** get **Double Trouble** before a multiball, or push all six Tales toward Scared Stiff. Monster Multiball's 5M start plus repeated **1M ramp/crate shots**, potentially doubled, is the clearest comeback package. [S3][S5]

**Key recurring decision:** **farm Coffin Multiball/left-ramp value, or leave that safe scoring loop to finish the remaining Tales?** Coffin is repeatable and relatively controlled; finishing Crate, Deadheads or Lab exposes the player to more varied feeds but unlocks the much larger Monster Multiball ceiling.

## Danger zones

1. **Crate bash:** expert guidance calls it one of the game's most dangerous required shots; rebounds routinely destroy control. [S5]
2. **Failed left ramp:** steep rejects can come straight back toward the center drain. [S5][S6]
3. **Leaper targets:** direct standup rebounds are a common instant-drain threat. [S6]
4. **Spider/upper kickout:** the tight upper-middle eject is specifically described as unpredictable; learn the venue copy before relying on a catch. [S5]
5. **Slings/outlanes:** prolonged side-to-side sling action is especially punishing; the left Spell save is useful but itself dangerous to qualify. [S5][S6]

## Trivia

* Scared Stiff is the second Elvira pinball after **Elvira and the Party Monsters (1989)**; Dennis Nordman later also designed Stern's **Elvira's House of Horrors (2019)**. [S4]
* The backbox's player-controlled **Spin the Spider** was marketed by Williams/Midway as an “industry first” 3-D interactive backbox feature. [S1]
* Cassandra Peterson provides **Elvira's voice**; Tim Kitzrow supplied additional callouts. [S4]
* Several planned production embellishments—including more elaborate bone-flipper/skull effects—were cut during Williams' mid-1990s cost pressure. [S5]
* ROM 1.5 arrived months after production and fixes an unusually long list of practical issues, including Coffin jams, broken Spider logic and a multiplayer skill-shot bug. [S2]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* Some modern sources describe the Spider as having **16 positions/awards**, while the classic Robert Macauley rulesheet describes **14 collectible awards** before Spider Mania. [S3][S5] This distinction does not affect ordinary tournament commentary; say “collect all Spider awards” rather than quote a count.
* Normal Extra Ball thresholds, especially the first right-ramp/Web award and Bonus-X award, are **adjustment-dependent**. [S3]
* ROM **1.0 versus 1.1+** changes Saved by the Spell during multiball; **1.3+** improves Coffin ball-search logic; **1.5** fixes additional gameplay bugs. [S2]
* Whether the Spider soft plunge is easy enough to use as the standard tournament opening is strongly **machine/plunger dependent**.
* Crate, Spider-hole kickout, left-ramp reject and Leaper rebounds are highly copy-dependent.
