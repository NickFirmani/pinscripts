## Identity and versions

* **Deadpool (Pro)** — Stern Pinball, 2018; SPIKE 2 platform. Requested scope is specifically the Pro. 
* Lead design: **George Gomez**; rules/code: **Tanio Klyce**; artwork: **Zombie Yeti**; **Nolan North** voices Deadpool. [S1][S3] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Pro/Premium/LE share Lil' Deadpool, Katana ramp, Battles, Quests and the core progression. Premium/LE add eight drop targets, a motorized disco ball, character toys, Chimichanga truck and moving left-orbit ramp. [S1]
* **Major rule difference:** on Pro, 60 Dazzler-spinner spins qualifies **Disco Multiball**; Premium/LE instead play the single-ball **Disco Loops** feature. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* **Latest code verified: Pro V1.14.0, October 10, 2025.** V1.14 itself mainly adds Expression Topper support, but V1.11 in July 2024 materially increased Berserker Rage scoring and added Berserker BOOM. [S2] ([Pinside][3])
* Important multiballs: **Lil' Deadpool, Ninja, Disco, Mechsuit and Sauron**; later wizard progression adds Clone Multiball. [S3]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Prepare Lil' Deadpool Multiball without starting it:** clear O-O-F, hit Lil' Deadpool until the lock is ready. [S3]
2. **Start a Battle first—usually Juggernaut—then start Lil' Deadpool Multiball inside it.** The extra ball and ball save make Juggernaut's dangerous DEAD/POOL-target phase much safer. This is a well-documented tournament route. **Observed strategy.** [S3][S5] ([Tilt Forums][2])
3. **Whenever possible, hit SNIKT:** ricochet the center-right standup into the Katana ramp to raise timed **Playfield X as high as 5×**. Major Battle finishes, jackpots and other scoring become enormous under multiplier. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][2])
4. Build **Ninja Multiball** by locking three balls at the Katana ramp, preferably starting it during a Battle or Quest rather than alone. [S3][S5]
5. **Alternate:** defeat Sabretooth early with orbits/pops—it is the easiest Battle without heavy setup—then use the safer progression toward Sauron Multiball. [S3][S4] ([Tilt Forums][2])

## Core rules and persistence

* **Battles:** Battle is lit at the start of each ball or by completing DEAD. Shoot scoop, choose Juggernaut, Mystique or Sabretooth, deal damage at the lit shots, then return to the scoop within about **10 seconds** for the Final Shot. Failed Battle progress persists to the next attempt. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* A strong Battle completion can be a major cash-out: the Final Shot approaches roughly **30M** when Deadpool retains most of his health. [S3]
* **Team-Ups persist until used in a Battle.** Two shots initially qualify:

  * Dazzler / left orbit → more health/time.
  * Colossus / U-turn → **2× Battle scoring**.
  * Wolverine / center spinner → **2× damage**.
  * Domino / right orbit → opponent-specific super scoring. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* **Lil' Deadpool MB:** after the OOF bank, hit Lil' Deadpool twice initially and lock a ball. It can start during a Battle, but not during Quests or other multiballs. After playing it, another Battle or Quest must be completed before the next Lil' Deadpool mode can be qualified. [S3]
* **Ninja MB:** three Katana-ramp locks. Its jackpots scale with collected Ninja Stars; completing the jackpot shots lights a right-ramp Super Jackpot equal to the jackpots accumulated in that wave. It can stack into Battles and Quests. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* **Playfield X:** SNIKT supplies temporary multipliers; Lil' Deadpool Bounce can add +1× for the ball; every 100 banked Weapons permanently raises the base multiplier for the rest of the game. Maximum effective Playfield X is **5×**. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* **Bonus carries between balls:** Chimichangas, Ninja Stars and Weapons remain banked, while Bonus X from the BAM lanes resets. [S3]

## Skill shots

* **Lane Skill Shot:** plunge the flashing BAM lane; **2.5M +250K** per previous success. Lane may be changed before launch but locks once the shooter-lane sensor is crossed. [S3]
* **Super Skill Shot:** hold left flipper on plunge, then shoot the **right Katana ramp**; **5M +500K**, while also collecting anything currently lit there. [S3]
* **MXV Skill Shot:** hold left flipper, shoot **right orbit → right ramp** combo; **12.5M +750K** plus the current Ninjapocalypse value. [S3]
* **Super Secret Skill Shot:** hold left flipper, then **SNIKT → right ramp**; **15M +1M per prior skill shot plus +1 Playfield X.** This is the strategically strongest plunge when the player can reliably execute it. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][2])

## Secondary features

* **BOOM button:** spell BOOM at the flipper-controlled return lanes. Pressing the flashing action button spots the highest-value currently lit non-scoop shot. Saving four uses creates **Big BOOM**, which spots all lit shots. [S3]
* **Berserker Rage:** qualify via center-loop shots; a 45-second spinner mode whose value rises through spins/pops. Since V1.11, BOOM also awards the best spin-session value, while Super BOOM awards **4× that Berserker BOOM value** in addition to its normal effect. This can be match-changing. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][3])
* **Colossal Jackpot:** repeated full U-turn shots qualify it; its value grows from relevant switch activity, can be **doubled by making the U-turn cleanly into the target**, then multiplied again by Playfield X. It is a legitimate large-score side objective. [S3]
* **Regeneration:** every third DEADPOOL completion lights the **left outlane** for a ball save. [S3]
* **Mystery:** DEADPOOL completion lights the scoop. During multiball it normally prioritizes Add-A-Ball when available; other awards include Ninja Lock, Weapons/Ninjas and Extra Ball qualification. [S3]
* Factory manual default standard ball save is **5 seconds**; this and multiball saves are adjustment-sensitive. [S4] ([Stern Pinball][4])
* Extra Ball is lit at the scoop for **25 Weapons** or **50 Ninja Stars**, plus a rare Mystery. Tournament handling must be confirmed. [S3]

## What to watch

* **Lil' Deadpool insert solid green:** the utility multiball is staged; if the player shoots the scoop instead, expect them to start a Battle before releasing it.
* **SNIKT / Playfield X inserts flashing:** temporary multiplier is active. A player repeatedly attacking SNIKT during multiball is deliberately trying to turn ordinary jackpots into match-changing scores. [S3]
* **Character Team-Up icon lit:** Dazzler, Colossus, Wolverine or Domino is ready for the next Battle; Wolverine generally signals easier completion, Colossus higher scoring. [S3]
* **Two balls already locked at Katana:** Ninja Multiball is one shot away, making a Battle/Quest start at the scoop especially attractive before the third lock.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** Take the prepared Battle + Lil' Deadpool stack rather than adding unnecessary Team-Up or multiplier setup. Sabretooth is attractive when you want uncomplicated progress; cash lit Final Shots instead of spending their short window chasing SNIKT. **Strategic inference.**

**Playing behind:** Add leverage: Colossus for double Battle scoring, Wolverine for faster completion, start a staged multiball inside the Battle, then attack **SNIKT repeatedly**. A 5× scoring window over Battle finishes, Mechsuit/Ninja jackpots, Colossal Jackpot or Berserker scoring can overturn a very large deficit. **Observed strategy.** [S3][S5] ([System-J Pinball][6])

**Key recurring decision:** **Start the scoring feature now, or keep preparing the stack?** Lil' Deadpool/Ninja Multiball makes a Battle dramatically safer, while Team-Ups and SNIKT make it dramatically more valuable—but every extra setup shot is another single-ball drain opportunity.

## Danger zones

* **Hellhouse scoop kickout:** documented copies can kick over the right flipper into the opposite sling and outlane; coil strength/setup matters. [S6] ([Pinside][5])
* **Right-orbit return:** some setups send it rapidly toward the center drain; pitch changes this substantially. [S6] ([Pinside][7])
* **Lil' Deadpool / OOF rebounds:** repeated direct target work needed for Juggernaut/Lil' DP can create unpredictable lateral feeds—one reason strong players stack the multiball rather than attack Juggernaut naked. [S5]
* **Katana-ramp reject:** a weak Ninja-lock/Super-Jackpot attempt returns rapidly into the lower playfield.
* **Outlanes generally:** owners repeatedly note strong side-drain sensitivity; post position and pitch materially change the game. [S6] ([Pinside][8])

## Trivia

* **George Gomez** designed the machine and **Tanio Klyce** created the core rules/software; the game's visual presentation deliberately mimics an old side-scrolling fighting video game. [S1][S3]
* Deadpool is voiced by **Nolan North**, who has also voiced the character extensively in Marvel video games and animation. [S1]
* Zombie Yeti supplied the hand-drawn artwork across all three editions. [S1]
* The Pro is not merely a mechanically stripped Premium: its **Disco Multiball** is actually a different rules feature from Premium/LE's Disco Loops. [S1][S3]
* In July 2024—almost six years after release—Stern added **Berserker BOOM** and raised Berserker Rage scoring as a tie-in with renewed Wolverine content. [S2]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Rulesheet version label:** the Tilt Forums rulesheet still says “Current Code Rev. 1.10,” despite incorporating the July 2024 Berserker BOOM rule. The current Pro changelog records **1.14.0 dated October 10, 2025**. [S2][S3] ([Pinside][3])
* **Competition behavior:** Stern's factory manual documents separate Competition Mode and Player Competition settings, while later code adds a selectable Competition game mode. I did not find sufficiently authoritative Deadpool-specific documentation establishing every Mystery/Extra-Ball change under the venue's exact “Competition” configuration. [S2][S4] ([Stern Pinball][4])
* **Physical feeds:** scoop, orbit and outlane behavior are strongly affected by pitch, level and coil/post adjustments. [S6]
