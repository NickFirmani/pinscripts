## Identity and versions

**Family Guy** — Stern Pinball, **2007**, Stern **S.A.M.** platform, four-player DMD game. Design: **Pat Lawlor**; software: **Dwight Sullivan, Keith P. Johnson, Lonnie D. Ropp**; artwork: **John Youssi and Margaret Hudson**; sound/music: **David Thiel**. Approximately **2,500** were produced. [S1][S2]

Latest software verified: **v12.0, January 29, 2008**. [S1][S3]

Major competitive features: **Fart Multiball, Lard Multiball, Stewie Multiball**, TV Wizard, and final wizard multiball **Sperm Attack**. [S3]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. Use the **Death target** to raise the center ball-save post before attacking dangerous shots. [S2][S3]
2. Grind the **Lois spinner/right orbit** to light **TV**, then start a TV mode at the scoop. Strong play stacks those modes with **Fart or Lard Multiball** when possible. [S3][S5]
3. Complete the **F-A-R-T bank three times** for the first Fart Multiball; keep progressing Beer Can awards because one of them is the useful **Lard Multiball**. [S2][S3]
4. A major alternate scoring route is **Crazy Chris**: properly built, its lit shots reach **1M each for 20 seconds**, enough to swing a tournament game quickly. [S3][S5]
5. High-variance route: spell **P-I-N-B-A-L-L**, survive Stewie's miniature playfield, complete all five Griffins and start **Stewie Multiball**, where the Super Jackpot can immediately set up a triple-value secret jackpot. [S3]

## Core rules and persistence

**TV modes:** spinner rotations light TV at the scoop. The first qualification requires 10 spins; later qualifications increase by 5, up to 30. Pop bumpers rotate which unplayed TV mode is selected. Completed TV modes persist until all five are finished; the next TV start is **TV Wizard**, after which the TV set resets. [S3]

TV Wizard is a major scoring opportunity: its initial jackpot value is built from shots made during earlier TV modes. Each collected jackpot increases the scoring multiplier; shooting the TV scoop when qualified scores the current value × multiplier, then resets that cycle. [S3]

**Fart Multiball:** first start requires three F-A-R-T-bank completions; later starts require progressively more, to a maximum of six. The first starts with two balls; later starts use three. [S3]

**Stewie Pinball:** captive-ball hits spell P-I-N-B-A-L-L; shoot the TV scoop to transfer play to the miniature pinball machine. Factory minimum save time is **15 seconds**, extendable with Stewie-target hits; the default ending is **Sudden Death**, meaning play can continue after time expires until the mini-ball drains. [S2][S3]

Complete Brian, Meg, Peter, Lois and Chris on the mini-playfield to start **Stewie Multiball**. During that multiball each character must be collected on both playfields; finishing the set lights a **2M Super Jackpot** at the Evil Monkey lane. Collecting it briefly lights the Drunken Clam for the **Triple Super Secret Jackpot**, worth **3× that Super**. [S3]

Bonus X is advanced by completing Peter's three-target bank, max **5X**. It normally resets between balls; Drunken Clam can award **Hold Bonus X**. [S3]

## Skill shots

**Normal Skill Shot**

* Plunge onto the lowest **F** target in the F-A-R-T bank while Skill Shot is lit.
* Scores **500K initially**, increasing by **250K** for each successful Skill Shot that game.
* Also advances the Fart Multiball bank. [S3]

**Secret / community “Super” Skill Shot**

* Preserve the Skill Shot lamp through the plunge and feed the ball to the upper flipper without touching the F-A-R-T or Death targets, then shoot the **Evil Monkey lane**.
* Scores **2× the current Skill Shot value** and advances the next Skill Shot value.
* Added in software v5.0. Competitive players commonly call this the Family Guy “super skill shot.” [S1][S3]

## Secondary features

**Death ball-save post:** hitting the lit Death drop target raises the physical post between the main flippers. Factory time is **9 seconds**, adjustable from 5–20 seconds, and the mechanism can be disabled. Unlike a conventional invisible ball save, commentators can literally see the post protecting the center drain. [S2]

**Drunken Clam / Mystery:** can award points, Bonus X, Collect Bonus, Extra Ball, PINBALL/TV progress, ball-save features, Crazy Chris and other context-aware awards. Competition Mode standardizes random features; Stern's changelog specifically documents a competition-mode Mystery award list. [S1][S2][S3]

**Extra Ball:** left-ramp progress can light Extra Ball at the upper captive ball; Mystery can also light one. A Stern Tournament Game defaults to **no Extra Balls, Specials or bonus credits**. [S2][S3]

**Video mode:** none. Chicken Fight was developed toward a video feature early, but final code implements it as a normal frenzy-style TV mode. [S1]

## What to watch

* **TV flashing at the scoop:** enough spinner work is done; expect an immediate mode start.
* **Center Death post physically raised:** the player has temporary freedom to attack riskier center/upper shots.
* **P-I-N-B-A-L-L complete:** the next scoop starts Stewie Pinball rather than an ordinary TV feature.
* **Drunken Clam flashing immediately after Stewie Super Jackpot:** that's the short-lived **Triple Super Secret Jackpot** opportunity. [S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** raise Death whenever available, take TV modes inside multiball rather than exposing the ball unnecessarily, and favor the repeatable spinner/TV progression. Once Stewie Pinball is qualified, consider whether entering the miniature game is worth abandoning a controlled main-playfield ball.

**Playing behind:** stack a lucrative TV mode or **Crazy Chris** with Fart/Lard Multiball, or pursue Stewie Multiball. The **2M Stewie Super → 6M Triple Super Secret Jackpot** sequence is a particularly visible one-shot swing. [S3][S5]

**Key recurring decision:** **keep grinding predictable TV/multiball scoring, or divert into Stewie Pinball?** Stewie offers the larger jackpot structure and wizard progress but moves the player onto a difficult miniature playfield where survival skill is very different.

## Danger zones

* **Stewie mini-playfield:** its tiny flippers are unforgiving; contemporary play reports specifically note balls jumping over them, especially from the Lois ramp. [S6]
* **Stewie timer at zero:** the temporary unlimited relaunch is gone; the next mini-ball drain ends the feature immediately. [S2][S3]
* **F-A-R-T / Death area:** repeated required direct target hits can produce fast rebounds toward the center.
* **Drunken Clam eject:** returns into the pop-bumper area rather than directly to a controlled flipper. [S3]
* **Death post expiration:** the audible gong countdown means a previously safe center drain is about to become live again. [S3]

## Trivia

* Family Guy was designed by **Pat Lawlor** and was one of his later Stern designs before *CSI*. [S1][S2]
* Stern advertised **exclusive dialogue recorded for the machine by Seth MacFarlane**, with Adult, Moderate and Family speech settings. [S1]
* Stewie's mini-playfield is effectively a **fully miniaturized pinball machine**, with its own smaller ball, flippers, ramp, targets and orbits. [S1][S2]
* Stern reused the basic physical playfield for **Shrek** in 2008, replacing Stewie Pinball with Donkey Pinball and writing new themed software. [S7]
* Final code v12.0 arrived in **January 2008**, less than a year after the initial February 2007 software release. [S1]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Stewie Multiball ball count:** secondary descriptions variously call it four- or five-ball because the separate mini-ball and balls on the main playfield are counted differently. The strategically useful description is **simultaneous main + mini-playfield multiball** rather than quoting a total.
* **Competition Mystery order:** Stern v4.0 verifies that Competition Mode uses a defined Mystery list, but the public changelog/manual reviewed does not expose a reliable final v12.0 award order. [S1][S2]
* **“Super Skill Shot” terminology:** final v12.0 rules documentation calls the Evil Monkey-lane award a **secret Skill Shot**; competitive players commonly call the same difficult upper-flipper attempt the **super skill shot**. [S3][S5]
* Reports of a low-score **ball-3 charity multiball** exist in experienced-player/community sources, but I did not find sufficiently authoritative documentation of its exact trigger. Omitted from the main strategy rather than guessing.
