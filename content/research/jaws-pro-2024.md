## Identity and versions

* **Canonical game:** *JAWS (Pro)* — Stern Pinball, 2024, Spike 2 era. Lead designer **Keith Elwin**; code/rules **Rick Naegele and Elizabeth Gieske**; mechanical engineering **Harrison Drake**; art **Michael Barnard**; sound **Jerry Thompson**. [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* **Pro-specific hardware:** 3 flippers; Shark Cage multidirectional captive ball; 3 stand-up Fish Finder targets; Wave ramp returns to the mini-flipper. Premium/LE instead have the Orca upper playfield, a second mini-flipper, drop targets, and motorized shark/boat mechanism. Core Shark Encounters, Bounty Hunts, Jaws progression, harpoon lane, fin target, reel, and Chum Bucket exist on all editions. [S1] 
* **Latest code verified:** **v1.02.0, July 1, 2026**, an all-game stable release for Pro/Premium/LE. Player-facing changes are mostly fixes, including Life Ring behavior; the major rules completion happened in v1.00.0 in July 2025. [S4][S3] ([Evolution Pinball][2])
* **Major progression:** three Jaws Multiballs — **Reel Him In!, The Chase, Cage Dive** — culminating in the final wizard mode **Smile, You Son of A…**. Bounty Hunt multiballs and Search/Rescue are meaningful parallel scoring paths. [S2][S3] ([Tilt Forums][1])

This brief follows the supplied publishing specification. 

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Light a Shark Encounter** by hitting the center Shark Cage captive ball, preferably taking extra hits first to earn encounter boosts: extra time, gear, and eventually **2× encounter scoring**. **Confirmed rule.** [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])
2. **Build Jaws Multiball in parallel:** hit Chum Bucket → complete the moving red Chum Line → raise the fin → hit the fin to attach the required barrel(s). Later multiballs require more barrels and the left target bank to Ready Barrel. **Confirmed rule.** [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])
3. **Finish Encounters at the fin.** The Encounter Jackpot is both a meaningful cash-out and gives a **permanent +1× to base Machete**, the game's next-shot multiplier. Missing the fin gives a weaker backup award and loses that permanent Machete increase. **Confirmed rule.** [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])
4. **Common tournament route:** stage Jaws Multiball 1 and use it as protection while completing a difficult Encounter—particularly Night Swim or Beach Panic—rather than concentrating on MB1's relatively modest jackpots. **Observed strategy.** [S5] ([Kineticist][3])
5. **Alternate route:** develop Bounty Hunts and gear in the background; Mako first is a documented expert preference because its reward accelerates later bounties. Beach Closed hurry-ups and Quickshots can also become large **Machete cash-outs** without committing to deep Jaws progression. **Observed strategy / confirmed mechanics.** [S2][S5] ([Tilt Forums][1])

## Core rules and persistence

* **Machete:** starts at **2×**. Complete the left target bank to light an inlane; after the lit inlane/return is triggered, the **next shot** is multiplied. Completing Encounters permanently raises base Machete; temporary increases are also possible. It generally cannot be newly lit during multiball, but an already-prepared Machete can be carried into one. **Confirmed rule.** [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* **Encounters:** enough center captive-ball hits light one at any of five beach shots. Completing its required shots lights the fin for the Encounter Jackpot. Extra center hits can yield +15 seconds, gear, then 2× scoring. **Confirmed rule.** [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* **Bounty Hunts persist ball-to-ball** and are untimed during single-ball play. Capturing sharks grants persistent perks; e.g. Mako accelerates later reel qualification, Thresher can produce 2× multiball scoring while Flip-Locked, and Hammerhead preserves Beach Closed values. **Confirmed rule.** [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* **Flip-Lock:** during multiball, a ball held at the mini-flipper can be locked there with the action button for about 20 seconds, reducing the number of actively moving balls. **Confirmed rule.** [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* **Feeding Frenzy is unusually persistent:** its accumulated total carries across the entire game and is awarded again in end-of-ball bonus, subject to bonus multiplier. **Confirmed rule.** [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* If extra balls are disabled, documented rules convert them to **15M**, and that compensation can be multiplied by Machete. Verify tournament configuration. [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* Standard competitive players have their own progression; **Co-op/Team Play deliberately shares** Chum Line, barrels, Jaws MBs, Bounties, Encounters, gear and other progress among teammates. [S3] ([Pinside][4])

## What to watch

1. **White beach arrows → blue mode shots → red/white fin opportunity.** That sequence means an Encounter has gone from available, to active, to its decisive cash-out. [S2]
2. **Lit Machete inlane.** Once the ball passes it, the player's *next scoring shot* is the important decision—watch for a Beach Closed hurry-up, Encounter Jackpot or Quickshot. [S2][S5] ([Kineticist][3])
3. **Chum Line / barrel state.** A completed red Chum Line followed by yellow setup shots means the player is close to attaching a barrel and starting the next Jaws Multiball. [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])
4. **Green action button with a ball on the mini-flipper during multiball.** That's a Flip-Lock opportunity; with Thresher already captured it can also mean 2× multiball scoring. [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** Favor finished value over deeper setup. Cash a prepared Machete on a known-value shot, finish an already-progressed Encounter, exploit MB1 as protection, and let persistent Bounty progress accumulate rather than repeatedly attacking the fin solely to build future potential. **Strategic inference** from the verified persistence/multiplier rules. [S2]

**Playing behind:** Chase combinations that multiply each other: fully boosted Encounter + multiball protection, high-base Machete on an Encounter Jackpot/Beach Closed/Quickshot, or deeper Jaws MB2/MB3 progression. Later Jaws multiballs have substantially more upside than MB1. **Observed strategy / strategic inference.** [S5] ([Kineticist][3])

**Key recurring decision:** **cash now, or preserve setup for a stack?** A good player may deliberately leave Jaws MB1 ready until a difficult Encounter is active; another may start it immediately for safety or score. Likewise, firing a prepared Machete at a modest shot can sacrifice a much larger upcoming cash-out. [S2][S5]

## Danger zones

1. **Fin target:** documented as producing dangerous ricochets back toward or between the flippers; Shark Cage gear gives a one-use fin-hit ball save. [S2][S5] ([Kineticist][3])
2. **Center captive-ball attacks:** repeated Encounter boosting is valuable but means repeatedly firing into a rebound-heavy central mechanism. **Strategic inference.**
3. **Mini-flipper lane:** normally feeds the right inlane, but some individual JAWS copies can leak toward the **right outlane**. Setup-specific. [S5] ([Kineticist][3])
4. **Left outlane:** Life Ring only saves the ball if the action button is timed correctly; v1.02 specifically changed the Easy-setting grace-period behavior. [S2][S4] ([Tilt Forums][1])
5. **Weak Wave-ramp / harpoon attempts:** failed shots surrender control just as the player is often building a high-value mode or fin cash-out. **Observed/strategic inference.** [S5]

## Trivia

* Keith Elwin designed the game; Rick Naegele and Elizabeth Gieske handled code/rules, with Michael Barnard artwork. [S2] ([Tilt Forums][1])
* Pro's distinctive **Shark Cage captive ball** replaces Premium/LE's Ben Gardner's Boat and motorized shark mechanism. [S1] 
* All three editions use the motorized shark-fin target and cross-playfield harpoon lane. [S1]
* Stern licensed footage/audio from **all four JAWS films**, plus custom Richard Dreyfuss speech and John Williams' original theme. [S1] 
* The July 2025 50th-anniversary code update finally added **Smile, You Son of A…**, plus Co-op and Team Play; these were software additions to existing JAWS machines, not just features of the anniversary model. [S3][S6] ([Pinside][4])

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Current code listing:** [S4] documents **v1.02.0 (July 1, 2026)** as the stable all-game Pro/Premium/LE release, while the Pro-specific Pinside archive [S3] still labels **v1.01.0 (July 22, 2025)** as “latest software.” I regard the Pinside headline as stale because its embedded changelog stops at 1.01; verify the physical machine.
* The detailed Tilt rulesheet [S2] identifies itself as **code 1.01**. v1.02's published gameplay changes appear narrow enough that its central rules remain applicable, but any undocumented 1.02 difference would supersede it.
* [S5]'s tournament strategy section was originally developed on earlier code and represents **one expert player's approach**, not universal competitive consensus. Its core MB1-as-utility and Encounter/Bounty priorities remain consistent with the current verified rules.
* Mini-flipper-to-right-outlane behavior is explicitly **machine/setup-specific**, not a universal JAWS Pro characteristic. [S5]
