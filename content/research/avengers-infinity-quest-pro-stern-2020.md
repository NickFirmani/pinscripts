## Identity and versions

* **Avengers: Infinity Quest (Pro)** — Stern Pinball, 2020; SPIKE 2 platform. Lead design **Keith Elwin**; code/rules team includes Rick Naegele, Raymond Davidson, Brett Rubin, Mike Kyzivat, and Elwin; art by **Zombie Yeti (Jeremy Packer)**. [S1][S3] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* This brief is specifically for the **Pro**, as requested.  Premium/LE add the motorized pop-up Portal/subway, Pym VUK and physical Captain Marvel ramp; on Pro, Gem Quests start at the **right ramp**, and Captain Marvel progress comes from spinner hits. [S1][S3] ([Stern Pinball][2])
* Major practical multiballs: **Thor Multiball** and **Iron Man Multiball**; **Black Order Multiball** is a later mini-wizard multiball. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* Latest code version I could verify is **1.10, released July 1, 2026**. Stern's current code page hosts authorized updates, but its public HTML does not expose individual version numbers; the current-version ledger records AIQ 1.10. The detailed community rulesheet is explicitly based on **1.08**, so post-1.08 rules changes should not be assumed absent without checking the venue machine. [S4][S7] ([Stern Pinball][4])

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** build combos early; **4 combos lights the first Portal Lock**, and 10 lights another. Each collected Portal Lock brings another ball into the next Gem Quest and makes it **2× / 3× scoring** while those balls survive. [S3][S5] ([Tilt Forums][3])
2. Get **Thor Multiball nearly ready**—the first takes only three captive-ball hits—then spell STRANGE and start a Gem Quest at the right ramp. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
3. Start Thor (or Iron Man) during the quest. Finish the Gem Quest with 2+ balls alive to enter **Victory Laps**, where major shots score again with a balls-in-play multiplier. [S3][S5] ([Tilt Forums][3])
4. **Reality Gem is the common high-scoring tournament choice:** disc spins build its shot multiplier; eight yellow shots complete it. Competitive sources repeatedly recommend Reality + Portal Locks + multiball for a fast large score. **Observed strategy.** [S3][S5][S6] ([Tilt Forums][3])
5. Alternate route: exploit the **Computer Grid/Super Modes** or build Iron Man Multiball, especially when safe combo-building or captive-ball shots are not behaving well on that machine. **Strategic inference.**

## Core rules and persistence

* Spell **STRANGE** at the spinning disc → right ramp → choose Gem Quest. Quests are not conventionally timed, but their health decreases continuously; draining fails the quest and gives that Gem to Thanos. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* Portal Locks are earned from the secret skill shot, cumulative combos, Hawkeye Challenge, or Computer Grid. **1 lock = 2×; 2 locks = 3× Gem Quest scoring** while the extra balls remain alive. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* Completing the quest with multiple balls still alive starts **Victory Laps**. Disc spins build/relight them; values are multiplied by balls in play, with the pops lane/upper loop receiving another 2×. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* After winning a quest, the right ramp becomes **Collect Gem**; then the player chooses a flashing shot on which to place it. Gem placement materially changes future scoring. [S3]
* **Reality Gem:** 2× shot multiplier normally, increasing to 3×/4× when placed on a level-1/level-2 Avenger. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* Avenger collection persists, but levels normally do **not** persist between balls unless the **Power Gem** is placed on that Avenger. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* Bonus X reaches **25×**; trophies and collected Gems persist into bonus, while several switch/activity components count only the current ball. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])

## Skill shots

* **Normal Skill Shot:** soft plunge into the top rollover by the pops. **250K + 100K per previous success**, plus **3 seconds ball save**. Safe, modest value. [S3]
* **Super Skill Shot:** full plunge, then shoot Avengers Tower. **1M + 150K per success**, **5 seconds ball save**; first success lights Mystery. [S3]
* **Secret Skill Shot #1:** medium plunge so the ball rolls backward through the upper loop. **3M + 200K per success**, **10 seconds ball save**, and immediately lights a **Portal Lock**. This is particularly valuable for the tournament Portal-Lock strategy. [S3][S5]
* **Secret Skill Shot #2:** full plunge then hit the **center Computer Grid drop target**. Same 3M+/10-second save and collects the currently flashing grid square. Useful when a valuable grid award is lined up. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])

## Secondary features

* **Ball saves:** skill shots add 3/5/10 seconds depending on difficulty. The Computer Grid can also award a **30-second Super Ball Saver**. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Mystery:** includes Add-A-Ball, STRANGE progress, THOR/IRON progress, 10M and other awards. In **Competition Mode**, randomness is removed; Competition Install fixes grid/Mystery behavior and disables extra balls. [S3][S8] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Action button:** selects Gem Quests/Gem placement and gains major powers from some Gems. Most importantly, a placed **Mind Gem** can automatically collect lit shots; later Black Order Multiball can risk the Soul Gem for a multiball extender. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* **Extra balls:** several qualification routes exist; tournament treatment is configuration-dependent. [S3][S8]

## What to watch

1. **Disc / STRANGE inserts:** nearing completion means a Gem Quest is about to become available.
2. **Portal Lock inserts / extra balls entering a quest:** immediately signals **2× or 3× quest scoring**. [S3]
3. **Yellow arrows in Reality Gem:** player is shooting multiplied mode shots; repeated disc spins are rebuilding the multiplier. [S3]
4. **Thor captive-ball letters:** if THOR is nearly complete while a quest runs, expect the player to intentionally start multiball and protect the mode. [S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** favor controlled left-ramp/combo work, take the first Portal Lock, and avoid forcing dangerous center-ramp or captive-ball progress solely to create a huge stack. A clean 2× quest is preferable to draining while building an elaborate 3× stack. **Strategic inference.**

**Playing behind:** pursue two Portal Locks plus Thor/Iron Man, preferably into **Reality Gem**. Keeping the three-ball quest alive into Victory Laps produces the most accessible match-swinging scoring package. **Observed strategy.** [S5][S6] ([System-J Pinball][5])

**Key recurring decision:** **start the Gem Quest now, or keep building the stack first?** Starting immediately banks progress; delaying for another Portal Lock/multiball dramatically raises both multiplier and drain risk.

## Danger zones

1. **Gauntlet ramp reject:** short shots can fall back toward the middle and threaten a center drain. [S5]
2. **Left-orbit return:** setup-dependent; players report fast returns capable of going straight down the middle. [S9] ([Pinside][6])
3. **Pop-bumper / Captain America lane exit:** can return centrally and abruptly; feed varies noticeably by setup. [S10] ([Tilt Forums][7])
4. **Black Panther/right-orbit return:** on the Pro it is intended to feed toward the right-flipper/sling area, but speed and post interaction can make control uncertain. [S10]
5. **Thor captive ball:** a strategically tempting repeated shot that leaves less predictable rebounds than the safer left ramp; especially costly when one ball remains in a multiplied Gem Quest. **Strategic inference.**

## Trivia

* AIQ was **Keith Elwin’s third Stern design**, following Iron Maiden and Jurassic Park. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][3])
* Stern based the machine on the **Marvel Comics universe**, rather than the Marvel Cinematic Universe films. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* The Dr. Strange disc detects **direction and velocity** of spins, which the software uses directly in gameplay. [S1]
* Avengers Tower uses a **magnet to hold balls visibly in midair** for Iron Man locks. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][8])
* The Premium/LE Portal physically raises and sends balls into an underground subway; the **Pro intentionally replaces that mechanism with the right-ramp rule**. [S1][S3]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* The detailed rulesheet [S3] currently identifies itself as based on **code 1.08**, while the latest verified release listing is **1.10 (July 1, 2026)** [S7]. I did not find sufficiently detailed authoritative 1.10 release notes to prove that every gameplay rule above is unchanged.
