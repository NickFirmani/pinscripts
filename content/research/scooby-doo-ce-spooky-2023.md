## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** work the **left spinner** to qualify Mystery Machine locks; Fred accelerates this route with an easier first multiball. Current tournament code allows every player to choose the same character. [S1][S3] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/2419-2/)
2. Meanwhile collect **Scooby Snacks**. They increase end-of-ball bonus, refill the Case **Bravery Meter**, and qualify the two apron locks for **Scooby Snack Multiball (SSMB)**. [S2] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)
3. Start a **Case** at the Mystery Machine, then bring SSMB into it. Spooky’s own developers specifically describe this as a major scoring stack because snacks continually refill Bravery, which can reach **4× Case scoring**. [S2][S4] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)
4. Alternatively stack SSMB over **Mystery Machine MB**; SSMB is the game’s universal two-ball overlay and keeps snack shots lit while stacked. [S2] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)
5. **Central decision:** start available protection immediately, or finish Case/SSMB setup first so the same multiball protects a much higher-value scoring window.

## Core rules and persistence

**Cases:** collect white investigation shots, then shoot the Mystery Machine. On Medium difficulty the first Case needs three whites; later Cases add one requirement to a maximum of six. During a Case, **Bravery is both timer and scoring multiplier**: red 1×, orange 1.25×, yellow 1.5×, green 1.75×, flashing green 2×, rainbow 4×. If Bravery reaches zero the Case ends; Scooby Snacks refill it. After collecting the required clues, trap shots multiply the final unmasking bonus before the Mystery Machine cash-out. [S2] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)

**Mystery Machine MB:** spinners qualify three locks. Character-orbit hits build toward jackpots; keeping all five character shots at matching levels lights the Mystery Machine for Super, Double Super or Triple Super. Triple Super also awards Extra Ball under normal settings. [S2] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)

**Scooby Snack MB:** first lock requires four Snacks, with later lock thresholds rising. One ball locks in each physical apron; the two locks start 2-ball MB. It **stacks with Cases, other multiballs and wizard modes**; while stacked, snack inserts do not extinguish after collection. Apron-lock shots score Supers and relight all snacks. [S2] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)

Important multiplayer trap: the **physical apron locks are shared**, so another player can consume a ball you physically locked. [S2] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)

Character progression and Cases persist during the game. End-of-ball bonus is based on a fraction of that ball’s scoring multiplied by collected Scooby Snacks, so early snack collection compounds across later balls. [S2] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)

## Skill shots

- **Drop Targets:** select with flippers, then hit the flashing drop target; awards **four Scooby Snacks**. Usually the most directly useful tournament choice because snacks feed Bravery, SSMB and bonus. [S2] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)
- **Dog Tag Target:** raises the center **Trap Door** save. [S2] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)
- **Character Orbit:** immediately tiers up that character’s benefits. [S2] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)
- **Secret / Shatz skill shot:** select Drop Targets, then flip at the last instant so the ball travels up the opposite inlane and knocks down the bottom drop target. [S2][S3] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)

Skill-shot availability becomes stricter at **Zoinks** difficulty. [S2] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)

## Secondary features

- **Trap Door ball save:** complete the four Dog Tags to raise the physical drop target between the flippers. Multiple saves can be banked on Easy/Medium; Zoinks normally limits this to one, except Fred can hold one extra. [S2] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)
- **Ruh-Roh save:** the two left outlanes light individually after drains; flippers move the lit lane, and with both lit a subsequent left-outlane drain is saved. [S2] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)
- **Skull save:** every **third right-outlane drain** is saved, and this counter is **shared across players** in multiplayer. [S2] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)
- **Mystery:** earned through Scooby Snacks at the Secret Passage. A rare **Reverse Flippers** award lasts through the next mode/multiball but gives **2× scoring**, turning an apparent curse into a high-variance scoring opportunity. [S2] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)
- **Extra Balls:** five villains visited, two unmaskings, 3rd/6th Mystery, or Mystery Machine Triple Super. Current Tournament Mode supports disabling EBs, but no universal point conversion is documented publicly. [S1][S2] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)
- Base CE has no conventional video mode. The optional **aftermarket Bookcase topper** adds *Ghost of Elias Kingston*. [S2] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)

## What to watch

1. **Bravery strip color:** immediate Case multiplier/timer readout; rainbow flashing means **4×**. [S2]
2. **White arrows + flashing Mystery Machine:** a Case is ready; expect the player to stop general setup and start it. [S2]
3. **Balls in the two apron locks:** SSMB is visibly being staged, and those physical locks can be stolen in multiplayer. [S2]
4. **Mystery Machine lock/gas progress:** repeated spinner shooting usually means the player is building the safer main multiball route. [S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** favor **Fred → Mystery Machine MB**, bank Trap Door/Ruh-Roh protection and take a Case at a comfortable Bravery level. **Strategic inference:** do not force awkward upper-playfield apron-lock shots merely to create the perfect stack when ordinary multiball already protects the lead.

**Playing behind:** build SSMB, start a Case, then release the apron locks. With constantly relit snacks maintaining **2×–4× Bravery**, the Case plus SSMB is the game’s clearest documented leverage stack. A Triple Super in Mystery Machine MB is another significant swing. [S2][S4] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Scooby-Doo-Rules.pdf)

**Key recurring decision:** **take a ready multiball now, or keep exposing the ball to drops/upper-playfield shots so Scooby Snack MB and a high-Bravery Case can overlap?**

## Danger zones

- **Center ramp reject:** needs a strong, centered shot; poor makes can fall directly toward the middle. Some machines briefly raise the Trap Door as compensation. **Observed/setup-sensitive.** [S7] [Pinside](https://pinside.com/pinball/forum/topic/scooby-doo-where-are-you-club-scooby-snacks-for-all/page/273?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **Upper-right lanes:** difficult from the bookcase/upper flippers; weak attempts can fall back into uncontrolled traffic. [S7] [Pinside](https://pinside.com/pinball/forum/topic/scooby-doo-where-are-you-club-scooby-snacks-for-all/page/21?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **Drop-target banks:** essential for snacks but inherently produce central rebounds.
- **Mystery Machine:** strategically unavoidable start/cash-out; failed front-door shots surrender the ball near the center.
- **Right outlane:** only every third drain receives the Skull save; the other two are ordinary drains, despite the visible save system. [S2]

- Scooby Snack Multiball uses actual **apron ball locks**, an unusually visible mechanism in modern pinball. [S2]
