## Identity and versions

* **Batman — The Dark Knight** — Stern Pinball, 2008; Stern **S.A.M.** system, dot-matrix display. Scope is the original 2008 Stern game requested here; there is no Pro/Premium/LE split. 
* Design: **George Gomez**; rules/software led by **Lyman F. Sheats Jr.**; artwork by **Kevin O'Connor and Margaret Hudson**; sound by **Ken Hale**. [S1][S7] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Pinside records approximately **3,500 produced**. [S7] ([Pinside][2])
* **Latest code verified: 2.94, May 6, 2010.** The detailed rulesheet used here specifically recommends 2.94; a current-code index independently lists 2.94 as final. Earlier software differs materially, especially Scarecrow Multiball and Bat Signal scoring. [S3][S6] ([Pinside][3])
* Main repeatable multiballs: **Joker, Scarecrow, Bat-Pod**. The deeper **Bat Signal Multiball** is the important high-value payoff.

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Triple Joker at the plunge if possible.** Hold the left flipper for the Super Skill Shot and hit Joker; Joker scoring is then **3× for the rest of that ball**. This is a recognized high-level tournament opening. [S3][S4][S5] 
2. **Add a Mini-Mine shot multiplier to Joker.** Gordon targets or Mystery can light multipliers; placing one on Joker turns its jackpots and Super Jackpots into major match-swinging scores. Experienced players document effective **5× and eventually 6× Joker scoring** when combined with the launch multiplier. [S3][S4] ([Pinside][3])
3. **Get the Scarecrow crane active before starting Joker Multiball.** Then finish the five crane positions while Joker is running, stacking Scarecrow Multiball into it. You cannot begin Scarecrow Attack once another multiball has already started. [S3][S4] ([Pinside][3])
4. After starting Joker + Scarecrow and collecting a Batmobile Hurry-Up, attack **Bat Signal**. Its single-ball build phase lets the player keep increasing the eventual multiball jackpot—or stop risking the ball and start the 4-ball payoff. [S3][S4] ([Pinside][3])
5. **Alternate strategy:** build the **Batmobile Hurry-Up** with right loops, double it once at the left loop, then cash at the center ramp. Its ceiling is enormous, but it asks for repeated single-ball shooting and a dangerous ramp collect. [S3][S5] ([Pinside][3])

## Core rules and persistence

* The three major features—**Joker, Scarecrow, Batmobile**—can receive a plunge-selected feature multiplier for the **entire current ball**: normally 2×, or 3× via Super Skill Shot. [S3] ([Pinside][3])
* **Mini-Mines:** hit the currently indicated Gordon target enough times to light a multiplier; the next of the five major shots hit gets **2× for the rest of the ball**. After all five have been multiplied, another award permits one shot to become **3×**. Mystery can shortcut this setup. [S3] ([Pinside][3])
* **Joker:** knock down the drop target to light locks; lock three balls for Joker Multiball. All five major shots score jackpots; five jackpots light Joker for a Super Jackpot. Later Joker Multiballs require more target work. [S3] ([Pinside][3])
* **Scarecrow:** shoot the scoop to begin Scarecrow Attack, then hit the hanging crane ball at all five indicated positions to start 2-ball Scarecrow Multiball. Five crane jackpots light the scoop for a Super Jackpot equal to the collected jackpots. [S3] ([Pinside][3])
* **Critical stacking rule:** Scarecrow Attack cannot be *started* during multiball, but if the crane is already active, it can be completed during Joker/Bat-Pod/Bat Signal and Scarecrow Multiball will stack. [S3][S4] ([Pinside][3])
* **Bat Signal:** start Joker Multiball, start Scarecrow Multiball and collect a Batmobile Hurry-Up. After completing its initial three-shot challenge, multiball is ready; additional Joker/Batmobile/Scarecrow shots increase the jackpot by **50K each, 100K when doubled**, up to 1M base before starting the 4-ball multiball. [S3] ([Pinside][3])
* **Easy commentator trap:** BATMAN standups qualify 30-second Bat Missions. Modes can run with multiball, but cannot normally be started during it; competitive tips warn that completing BATMAN during multiball can consume/reset the qualification without starting the mode. [S3][S5] ([Pinside][3])

## Skill shots

* **Feature Skill Shot:** time the plunge while **Joker, Scarecrow or Batmobile** is lit in the shooter lane. That feature scores **2× for the entire ball**. Joker is generally the strongest tournament choice. [S3][S5] 
* **Normal Skill Shot:** plunge through the flashing top rollover. Starts at **250K**, increases 25K per success and awards **+5 Bonus X**. [S3] ([Pinside][3])
* **Super Skill Shot:** hold the **left flipper before launching**; then quickly hit Joker, center/Batmobile ramp or Scarecrow. The chosen feature becomes **3× rather than 2× for that ball**. High-level strategy strongly favors Joker when the shot is reliable. [S3][S4] ([Pinside][3])

## Secondary features

* **Ball save:** factory default is **5 seconds**, adjustable from none through 15 seconds or Auto. [S2] ([manualzz.com][4])
* **Alfred:** completing the top lanes or receiving the appropriate Mystery award lights the Alfred standup. During multiball, Alfred can award **Add-A-Ball once**, making it a significant survival resource. [S3] ([Pinside][3])
* **Mystery:** complete the inlane/outlane lamps, then collect at the upper saucer, usually via the left loop. Material awards include **Light Multipliers, Light Lock / Lock Award, Light Alfred, Extra Ball, +2 Bonus X and Hold Bonus X**. [S3] ([Pinside][3])
* Stern's standard **Competition Mode** unrandomizes feature events and standardizes rules, but I did not find authoritative documentation of the exact Batman 2.94 Mystery sequence. [S2] ([manualzz.com][4])
* Extra balls can be earned from score, Batgadgets and Gotham City; the operator can globally set **NO EXTRA BALLS**. [S2][S3] ([manualzz.com][4])
* No player action button, Magna-Save, video mode or conventional kickback is present.

## What to watch

* **An X insert lit at a major shot:** that shot has a persistent-for-the-ball Mini-Mine multiplier. If it's Joker, expect the player to route jackpots there. [S3]
* **Scarecrow crane extended over the playfield:** Scarecrow Attack is active. If Joker is nearly ready, the player is probably setting up the preferred multiball stack. [S3][S4]
* **Joker rotates into view:** Joker Multiball has begun; all five major arrows are jackpot shots. [S3]
* **Bat Signal qualified / left-loop saucer flashing:** the major risk-reward phase is available. Continued shots may mean the player is deliberately building the jackpot instead of immediately starting multiball. [S3][S4]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** Take the reliable multiplier you already have, stack Joker/Scarecrow if prepared, and **start Bat Signal rather than greedily maximizing its jackpot**. Avoid repeated Gordon standups or speculative Batmobile building when a safer cash-out already wins. **Strategic inference** from the verified risk/reward structure. [S3][S5]

**Playing behind:** Chase the **3× Joker Super Skill Shot + Mini-Mine multiplier**, stack Joker and Scarecrow, and build Bat Signal aggressively before starting it. Multiplied Joker Super Jackpots in the **tens of millions** are documented by competitive players. A huge Batmobile Hurry-Up is another high-variance comeback route. **Observed strategy.** [S4][S5] ([Pinside][5])

**Key recurring decision:** **setup versus cash-out.** More multiplier work, another Bat Signal build shot or another right loop raises the ceiling—but all of it happens while one bad shot can end the opportunity.

## Danger zones

* **Center ramp reject:** a weak attempt can return essentially straight down the middle. [S5]
* **Scarecrow crane:** the hanging ball blocks changing parts of the playfield and hard collisions can ricochet directly toward the drain. [S3]
* **Gordon standups:** strategically valuable but specifically avoided by some tournament players because of dangerous direct rebounds. [S5]
* **Left-loop saucer / pop exit:** scoop bounce-outs and the subsequent pop-bumper feed can abruptly remove control. [S3]
* **Scarecrow scoop during multiball:** recent top-lane/pop activity can cause the game not to credit the intended Scarecrow jackpot/Super Jackpot immediately; strong players may trap up and wait for the upper playfield to settle. [S3][S4] ([Pinside][3])

## Trivia

* Stern paired **George Gomez's playfield design** with **Lyman Sheats's rules**; Stern specifically promoted Gomez as the designer of *The Lord of the Rings* when announcing Batman. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* The game includes three unusually visible mechanisms: the **rotating Joker reveal**, **Scarecrow's moving crane with a suspended pinball**, and the teeter-totter **Batmobile Bridge**. [S1]
* The factory manual actually documents **two different Scarecrow crane assemblies**, identifiable by different motor-shaft/hub geometry. [S2] ([manualzz.com][4])
* Stern later described the crane in **Batman '66** as a modern version of the mechanism first used here. ([Stern Pinball][6])
* Pinside's archive records about **3,500 machines produced**. [S7] ([Pinside][2])

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Revision-specific:** current verified code is **2.94**. Earlier software used a **3-ball Scarecrow Multiball** versus 2 balls in the later rulesheet, and older Bat Signal code reportedly increased its build value much more slowly. Installed version therefore materially changes commentary. [S3][S6] ([Pinside][3])
* **Competition-mode Mystery:** Stern's manual confirms Competition Mode standardizes/unrandomizes feature events, but the exact Batman 2.94 Mystery order was not verified. [S2]
* **Multiplier arithmetic:** experienced 2.94 players describe the Super Skill Shot and Mini-Mine multipliers as producing effective **5×**, and eventually **6×**, scoring on one major shot; this is well documented competitively but not spelled out in Stern's service manual. [S4][S5] ([Pinside][5])
* **Physical feeds:** center-ramp, crane, Joker and scoop behavior are substantially machine/setup dependent. [S3][S5]
