## Identity and versions

* **The Addams Family** — Bally/Midway, March 1992; model 20017; Williams **WPC Fliptronics I** platform. Design: **Pat Lawlor and Larry DeMar**; software: **Larry DeMar and Mike Boon**; art: **John Youssi**. Production: **20,270 standard machines**. [S4] ([IPDB][1])
* The 1994 **Special Collector’s Edition / “Gold”** is not merely cosmetic: it adds Cousin It’s Hideout random awards, Super Seance, multiball-rule changes, and buy-in capability. Those rules should **not** be applied to a standard 1992 game. [S5] ([GameFAQs][2])
* Verified Bally ROM revisions include **L-7 and H-4** in IPDB; the installed revision should be checked rather than assuming all surviving machines run identical code. [S4] ([IPDB][1])
* Major multiballs useful to commentary: **Greed/Vault Multiball**, **Quick Multiball**, and **Thing Multiball**. [S2]
* Unless marked otherwise, rules below are **confirmed rules** from the Bally manual or detailed contemporary rulesheet; strategic recommendations from Colorado Pinball are labeled **observed strategy**. This follows the requested research structure. 

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy — “Bear and Chair”:** shoot the **Bear Kick center ramp** (or Million Plus ramp) to light the yellow Electric Chair, then shoot the **Chair or Swamp kickout** to collect the flashing Mansion room. Repeat toward **Tour the Mansion**. [S2][S3] 
2. Collect all 12 normal Mansion rooms, then collect the flashing **“?”** for **Tour the Mansion**: **50M immediately**, plus the major Mansion features are replayed automatically. [S2]
3. **Greed Multiball** is the major alternative: spell GREED at the Bookcase, lock balls, then collect the **Train Wreck Jackpot** and especially the **Million Plus ramp Super Jackpot**. First multiball Super = **2× Jackpot**; later multiballs = **3×**. [S2] ([Wikipedia][3])
4. A separate “chopping wood” route builds the **Million Plus ramp** to as much as 10M per shot, advances THING, and feeds the upper-left flipper for a possible **5× Graveyard** Swamp collect worth as much as **20M**. [S2][S3]
5. **Strategic inference:** when close to Tour, continuing controlled Mansion progression can be preferable to starting Greed Multiball, because Mansion rooms cannot be collected during multiball. When badly behind, jackpot/Super Jackpot multiball offers greater immediate variance. [S2]

## Core rules and persistence

* **Mansion:** yellow-lit Chair or Swamp kickout collects the currently flashing room. Either main ramp permanently relights the Chair; the right inlane lights it briefly. Jet bumpers rotate the flashing Mansion selection. [S2] ([GameFAQs][4])
* **Bear Kicks persist for the game.** At 15, 25, 35, etc., they spot Mansion rooms; the first Bear-Kick extra-ball threshold reflexes, while another is normally at 50. [S2]
* **End-of-ball bonus can become significant:** `(100K × Bear Kicks + 500K × Mansion rooms) × Bonus X`, plus accumulated Mamushka/Cousin It/Raise the Dead totals. Bonus X reaches 5× but resets each ball. [S2] ([GameFAQs][4])
* **Million Plus resets each ball by default**, climbing 1M per side-ramp shot to 10M. Graveyard normally resets to 1M each ball and caps at 4M; earning a Train Wreck holds its current value into the next ball. [S2]
* Greed Multiball Jackpot starts at **10M**, can build to **25M**, and by default carries between multiballs until collected; collecting it resets it to 10M. If multiball collapses before any Jackpot, there is one **20-second Thing’s Eject restart** opportunity. [S2] ([GameFAQs][4])
* During multiball, **Mansion rooms, Stars, 5× Graveyard and Thing Flips cannot be collected.** Physical locks can be displaced by another player, but the game provides that player a Swamp relock opportunity. [S2]
* Easy commentary trap: the regular Mansion **Light Extra Ball** normally stays lit across balls; the extra ball lit by **Tour** is “hard” by default and disappears at that ball’s end. [S2]

## Skill shots

**Thing’s Eject skill shot** — plunge so the ball settles into Thing’s Eject near the upper-right shooter lane. It scores **2M, then 3M, 4M, and 5M maximum**, with the value advancing across successful plunges for the entire game. When Thing’s Eject is simultaneously lit for a lock or feature, that plunge can also advance that state. A hard plunge tends toward the bumpers; too soft may fall toward the Swamp. [S2] ([GameFAQs][4])

No separate verified super, alternate, or secret skill shot found.

## Secondary features

* **Ball save:** no conventional ball-save rule was found in the original Bally documentation/rulesheet.
* **Extra balls:** principally Bear Kick thresholds, the Mansion EB room, Train Wrecks and Tour; tournament setups commonly make their treatment relevant, so venue configuration must be checked. [S2]
* **Player control — The Power:** the under-playfield magnets disturb balls during features including multiball and Seance. Colorado Pinball documents holding both flippers to **briefly disable the magnets**, a useful competitive control technique. **Observed strategy.** [S3] ([Colorado Pinball][5])
* **Thing Flips:** after the far-left inlane lights it, a Bear Ramp shot is diverted to the upper-left mini-flipper and the game itself attempts the Swamp shot, using prior results to calibrate timing. [S1][S2]
* **Random awards:** none material found for standard TAF. **Gold only** adds Cousin It’s Hideout random awards, including large points and possible extra ball/special. [S5]

## What to watch

1. **Yellow Chair lamp:** Mansion room is collectible; an unlit Chair gives the famous “not plugged in yet” non-award instead. [S2]
2. **Flashing Mansion window / flashing “?”:** tells you which room is next; “?” means Tour is ready. [S2]
3. **Bookcase position and green/red lamps:** GREED completed/open Vault means locks are available; red indicates multiball-start/jackpot-related Vault state. [S2]
4. **Lightning bolts/side flashers:** **The Power** magnets are active—expect visibly less predictable ball motion near the flippers. [S2]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** favor repeatable Bear Ramp → Chair progression, controlled kickouts and existing lit awards. Avoid forcing the upper-left-flipper 5× Swamp shot when The Power is active. **Observed strategy.** [S3]

**Playing behind:** Greed Multiball becomes attractive, particularly later multiballs where Super Jackpot is **3×** a Jackpot that can reach 25M. A maximized 5× Graveyard is another legitimate **20M** swing shot. [S2]

**Key recurring decision:** **keep progressing Mansion/Tour or cash into Greed Multiball now?** Mansion progression is unavailable during multiball, while multiball offers much larger immediate volatility. [S2]

## Danger zones

1. **The Power magnets:** deliberately randomize lower-playfield trajectories during important scoring states. [S2]
2. **5× Swamp from the upper-left flipper:** Colorado Pinball explicitly recommends avoiding it while magnets are running. [S3]
3. **Chair kickout:** ideally bounce-pass or drop-catch; setup differences can make it much less friendly. [S3]
4. **Swamp kickout:** normally catchable, but machine setup materially changes speed and angle; bad examples can threaten the center. [S3] ([Wayne's This and That][6])
5. **Side-ramp rejects / Train Wreck or Cousin It ricochets:** often head toward the wide Swamp area; a timely nudge can turn the danger into a controlled Swamp kickout. [S3]

## Trivia

* **20,270** standard units makes TAF the record-setting commercial success of solid-state pinball. [S4] ([IPDB][1])
* The 1994 Gold edition added **1,000** numbered collector machines and materially expanded the rules. [S4][S5]
* **Thing** physically emerges and magnetically picks up the ball; it is not merely animation. [S1][S4]
* **The Power** uses under-playfield electromagnets specifically to distort ball movement. [S1]
* TAF uses the early **WPC Fliptronics I** system, enabling its famous software-controlled **Thing Flips** mini-flipper. [S4]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Tour difficulty is operator-adjustable.** Contemporary documentation notes that 3M/6M room treatment can alter how many actual collections are needed before Tour. [S2]
* **Swamp Mansion collection is adjustable.** Do not assume the Swamp kickout awards a room on every venue game. [S2]
* **Gold rules materially differ from standard TAF**, particularly random Cousin It awards, Super Seance, multiball details and buy-in. [S5]
* The exact installed **ROM revision/configuration** remains unknown and could alter a few rules or introduce a modern ball-save/competition patch.
