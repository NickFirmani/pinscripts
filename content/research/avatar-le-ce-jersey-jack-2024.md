## Identity and versions

* **Avatar: The Battle for Pandora** — Jersey Jack Pinball, 2024. Lead design: **Mark Seiden**; code/rules: **Keith Johnson, Taylor Snyder, Jason Allen**; art: **Lea Faske**; music/sound: **Stemage**. [S5][S8] ([Kineticist][1])
* **Edition:** Limited Edition (LE). LE and Collector’s Edition (CE) use the same documented playfield mechanisms: six flippers, two lower playfields and the Infinity Mirror physical lock. Current material does not establish a tournament-relevant LE rules difference. Launch coverage mentioned an unspecified CE “hidden bonus,” but current JJP documentation does not explain it; do not apply that claim to LE. [S7][S8] ([Jersey Jack Pinball][2])
* **Rules basis: `code` — v1.02, released 2025-07-08.** JJP currently distributes full and delta 1.02 installs; the release date is corroborated by the maintained code index. [S1][S2] ([Jersey Jack Pinball][3])
* Major multiballs: **Battle of the Hallelujah Mountains** (“A1”) and **Battle of Awa’Atlu** (“A2”). **Eywa’Eveng** is the PANDORA mini-wizard multiball. [S3][S5]

Research scope follows the supplied commentator-reference specification.

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** prime a multiball—A1 is particularly convenient because a super skill shot can steal a lock—while getting a **Sivako or Songcord mode** ready. [S5][S6] ([Kineticist][1])
2. **Observed strategy:** start the mode, then bring in the prepared multiball. Current tournament guidance describes the multiballs as unusually lucrative/overweighted. [S6] ([JLP's Pinball Cards][4])
3. **Confirmed rule:** after lit mode/jackpot shots, mash the green-lit action button to replenish **arrows**, preserving the event multiplier up to **4×**. Every flip consumes arrows; depletion drops the multiplier by 0.25×. [S4][S5] ([Tiltforums][5])
4. **Big leverage:** if **Eclipse** joins the stack, concurrent scoring feeds its jackpot. Cash the accumulated value on the **right ramp before Eclipse ends**. [S4][S5] ([Tiltforums][5])
5. **Alternate line:** repeated, comfortable Sivako completions are a lower-complexity source of solid scoring and PANDORA progress; spelling PANDORA lights **Eywa’Eveng**, a three-ball mini-wizard that can itself be a major swing. [S5] ([Kineticist][1])

## Core rules and persistence

**Confirmed rules:** hit any of eight major Sivako starters three times to begin that mode. Sivako/Songcord cannot normally coexist, but either can stack with a multiball; A1 and A2 cannot stack with each other. [S4] ([Tiltforums][5])

Both main multiballs use the same scoring structure: lit “award” shots qualify a moving jackpot; **10 award shots arm an add-a-ball on the next jackpot**, visibly flashing red/green. Five jackpots across attempts qualify the RDA super jackpot. Both multiballs progress through three increasingly difficult stages. [S4][S5] ([Tiltforums][5])

**Eclipse progress persists across balls.** During Eclipse, other scoring—including modes/multiball—contributes to the Eclipse jackpot; the right ramp collects it and uncollected value is lost when Eclipse expires. [S5] ([Kineticist][1])

**Meuia (honor) is ball-scoped:** shots build it during the ball, skill shots can increase a shot’s meuia yield for the remainder of that ball, and Home Tree cashes awards. PANDORA is longer-term progression; completing it lights Eywa’Eveng at the scoop. [S4][S5] ([Tiltforums][5])

Two secondary swing opportunities deserve notice: **Bond Jackpot** is built from all eight bond shots and scales from accumulated combo values; **Left Hand Combo** increases exponentially for consecutive left-flipper shots until the right flipper cashes it. [S4] ([Tiltforums][5])

## Skill shots

**Confirmed common mechanic:** plunge into one of the listed shots. A normal skill shot gives that shot **+1 meuia for the rest of the ball**. Pressing the action button selects the roving **super skill shot**, which also extends ball save and supplies the bonus below. All are available at ball start; expert documentation also reports another choice after physical locks. [S4][S5] ([Tiltforums][5])

| Skill shot                          | Super award / strategic use                                                  |

| Left outlane                        | Adds a stackable kickback charge.                                            |
| Left inlane                         | Lights a lane award.                                                         |

## Secondary features

* **Ball saves:** super skill shots extend the ordinary save, but no reliable current source gives a universal timer. Left outlane kickback charges can stack. **Skill Save** is stranger: when lit, the player has 10 seconds to hit a randomly lit shot or the ball ends. [S4] ([Tiltforums][5])
* **Lane awards:** Max Toxin sets the next event to 4×; Unlimited Arrows prevents multiplier decay for the next event and carries through a stack; Left Hand Combo is the exponential left-flipper scoring opportunity above. [S4] ([Tiltforums][5])
* **Player controls:** action button selects the super skill shot, replenishes arrows after lit event shots and manages Songcord tokens. Flippers cycle lane awards and selected lower-playfield targets. [S4][S5] ([Tiltforums][5])
* **Extra balls:** Home Tree can light one from 50 meuia, three PANDORA letters, Tulkun progress or mystery. Tournament conversion is not a fixed universal rules value. **No true video mode found.** [S4] ([Tiltforums][5])

## What to watch

1. **Green action button + yellow inlane arrows:** player is replenishing arrows to protect scoring X; rapid button presses are deliberate. [S5] ([Kineticist][1])
2. **Playfield lighting suddenly darkens:** Eclipse has started; watch the right ramp and any concurrent mode/multiball scoring. [S5] ([Kineticist][1])
3. **Red/green jackpot insert:** ten multiball awards have been made; that jackpot also adds a ball. [S4] ([Tiltforums][5])
4. **Infinity Mirror / green orbit lock lights:** A1 is being prepared; three physical locks start Hallelujah Mountains multiball. [S4][S5] ([Tiltforums][5])

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead — strategic inference:** favor a reliable Sivako starter, take multiball when the complementary mode is ready, maintain arrows rather than over-flipping, and **collect Eclipse rather than greedily building it**. Lane awards such as Unlimited Arrows or Skill Save become more attractive than forcing Tulkun/A2 precision.

**Playing behind — strategic inference:** seek the high-ceiling **mode + multiball + Eclipse** stack at 4×, push add-a-ball qualification, and consider letting the Eclipse pot grow before the right-ramp cash-out. If PANDORA is already close, Eywa’Eveng becomes a legitimate comeback route.

**Key recurring decision:** **when to start the prepared multiball.** Good players may deliberately leave it ready while qualifying a favorable mode or waiting for Eclipse; starting it immediately gives safety, but sacrifices the biggest stacking leverage. This matches published tournament strategy. [S5][S6] ([Kineticist][1])

## Danger zones

* **Home Tree VUK eject:** high-speed upper-right-flipper feed; missed transfer can go straight down the middle. **Observed.** [S5] ([Kineticist][1])
* **Right orbit:** tight shot returning quickly to the upper-left flipper; commentator should expect immediate reflex play. **Confirmed feed; control risk inferred.** [S5] ([Kineticist][1])
* **Tulkun/center mechanism:** extremely precise upper-flipper shot surrounded by center traffic; misses are inherently higher variance. **Observed difficulty.** [S5] ([Kineticist][1])
* **Left outlane:** check whether kickback charges exist before calling the drain inevitable; several rules can add charges. [S4] ([Tiltforums][5])
* **Device releases generally:** expert guidance specifically warns that Avatar has many unusual release directions and that failing to use the indicated flipper can make center drains very likely. [S5] ([Kineticist][1])

## Trivia

* This was **Mark Seiden’s first solo pinball design** and JJP’s tenth title. [S8] ([Jersey Jack Pinball][6])
* The game uses material from both *Avatar* and *Avatar: The Way of Water*. [S5][S8] ([Kineticist][1])
* **Stephen Lang** recorded custom Colonel Quaritch voice work. [S7][S8] ([Jersey Jack Pinball][2])
* JJP describes the game as having an **industry-first dual lower playfield**; there are six flippers across its three playfield levels. [S7] ([Jersey Jack Pinball][2])
* LE and CE differ visibly in topper, cabinet treatment and UV presentation; their core playfield mechanisms are documented as common to both. [S7][S8] ([Jersey Jack Pinball][2])
