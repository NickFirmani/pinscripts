## Identity and versions

* **JAWS (Pro)** — Stern Pinball, 2024; SPIKE 2. Lead design: **Keith Elwin**; code credited to **Rick Naegele and Elizabeth Gieske**; mechanics Harrison Drake; art Michael Barnard; sound/music Jerry Thompson. [S4][S6] ([Kineticist][1])
* Models: Pro, Premium, LE, plus later 50th Anniversary trim. The **Pro is mechanically important**: 3 flippers, no Orca upper playfield, no motorized shark bash toy, stand-up Fish Finder targets, and a Wave-ramp return to the mini-flipper. [S2] 
* **Latest verified Pro code: v1.02.0, July 1, 2026.** It includes Life Ring fixes and runs Stern System 4.28. [S1] 
* Major competitive multiball families: **Jaws Multiball 1–3, Bounty Hunt multiballs, Search/Rescue, Night Search**, plus late-game Great White/Super Celebration. [S2][S3] 

Scope follows the supplied commentator-research brief. 

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Start Shark Encounters.** Hit the Shark Cage/captive-ball area to light them; another hit on Pro enables Encounter Boosts, eventually including **2× mode scoring**. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][2])
2. **Finish the Encounter and hit the fin jackpot.** A successful Encounter Jackpot permanently adds **+1× to base Machete** for the rest of the game. Missing it gives a lesser backup award and **no Machete increase**. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][2])
3. **Weaponize Machete.** Complete the left target bank, collect the lit return lane/ramp feed, then deliberately spend the multiplier on a big shot: Encounter Jackpot, Beach Closed Hurry-Up, or a valuable Quickshot. [S3][S4] ([Tilt Forums][2])
4. **Alternate route:** use **Jaws Multiball 1 as a utility multiball** to safely work an Encounter or other objectives rather than relying on its base jackpots. This is documented expert strategy, not a confirmed rule priority. [S4] ([Kineticist][1])

## Core rules and persistence

* **Confirmed rule — Machete:** starts at 2×; Encounter completions, first-attempt non-Mako Bounty captures, and Night Search completion can permanently increase it. A lit Machete multiplies the next qualifying shot. It generally cannot be newly lit during multiball/wizards, but can be carried into them. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* **Confirmed rule — Beach Closed:** rescue three beachgoers at one major shot to start a hurry-up beginning at 15M plus modifiers. **Machete multiplies the cash-out itself**, making this a major tournament swing. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* **Confirmed rule — Quickshot:** harpoon lane → timed fin. Consecutive success can build Quickshot to **7×**, while a miss resets that multiplier; Machete also applies. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* **Confirmed rule — Feeding Frenzy:** its accumulated score is **held across the entire game and paid again in end-of-ball bonus each ball**, then multiplied by bonus X. This can quietly become enormous. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* Normal multiplayer progress is player-specific; explicit shared progress was added only for Co-op/Team play. [S1] 

## Skill shots

| Skill shot   | Execution                                                                   | Award / reason                                     |
| ------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- |
| Level 1      | Plunge to pop bumper                                                        | 5M; safest.                                        |
| Level 2      | Plunge through lane above mini-flipper without bumper                       | 10M + **Light Gear**.                              |
| Level 3      | Reach flippers cleanly, hit flashing left-bank target before another switch | 15M + **Light Gear**.                              |
| Secret       | Chum bucket, or either side of captive-ball area, with no prior switch      | Current skill-shot value.                          |
| Super Secret | Dead-center captive-ball area, no prior switch                              | Awards **and advances** current normal skill shot. |

Every normal skill shot adds **5 seconds of ball save**; up to three attempts are available per ball. The normal level advances after prior success. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][2])

## Secondary features

* **Life Ring:** qualify by hitting its target, then again while flashing. Press the action button at the right moment to save a **left-outlane** drain. Its usable window becomes less forgiving after successful uses. v1.02 fixed the Easy-setting grace period. [S1][S3] 
* **Cheap-drain saves:** v1.01 added configurable protection specifically for a bad **Quickshot-lane → outlane** exit and **right-inlane-gate → outlane** failure. Defaults were enabled. [S1] 
* **Extra balls:** Pro qualifications include 8 beachgoers, third Quickshot, and the Shark Hunter video-mode icon. When disabled they score **15M**, and that conversion can be Machete-multiplied. Stern Competition Install disables extra balls. [S1][S3] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* **Flip-Lock:** during multiball, trap a ball on the mini-flipper and press Action to hold the flipper up temporarily, reducing chaos. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* **Gear:** right flipper selects the currently offered gear before collecting it at the right ramp. Gear provides substantial survivability/scoring perks; selling all six also unlocks Great White. [S3][S4] ([Tilt Forums][2])
* **Mystery/competition:** Stern specifically fixed Competition Mode so players receive equivalent Mystery sequencing. [S1] 

## What to watch

1. **Machete popup + lit inlane:** player is preparing one multiplied shot; the next choice is highly intentional. [S1][S3]
2. **Fin raised / Encounter Jackpot lit:** completing it is not merely points—it permanently raises Machete. [S3]
3. **Harpoon/Quickshot lighting:** a player repeatedly returning to harpoon → fin may be protecting a growing Quickshot multiplier. [S3]
4. **Orange Life Ring / Action-button attention:** a left-outlane rescue is armed, but timing matters. [S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:**
**Strategic inference:** take prepared value rather than overbuild it. Cash a ready Machete on an Encounter Jackpot or Beach Hurry-Up, prioritize survivability gear/Life Ring, and use early Jaws Multiball as a control window.

**Playing behind:**
Higher variance is justified: fully boost an Encounter to 2×, stack it with multiball, build a high Quickshot multiplier and combine it with Machete, or pursue Bounty/Gear progression. Quickshots into Machete have documented potential for extremely large single awards. [S3][S4] ([Tilt Forums][2])

**Key recurring decision:**
**Build versus spend Machete.** A good player may either cash the multiplier on the strong award already available or continue setting up a much bigger but less certain target.

## Danger zones

* **Fin / center captive-ball rebounds:** direct, central hits can return violently toward the flippers. [S4] ([Kineticist][1])
* **Quickshot-lane exit:** Stern considered the outlane feed problematic enough to add a dedicated software ball save. [S1] 
* **Right-inlane gate failure:** likewise has a dedicated configurable cheap-drain save. [S1] 
* **Mini-flipper feed:** some physical copies can send balls past the mini-flipper toward the right outlane; highly machine-dependent. [S4] ([Kineticist][1])
* **Life Ring:** the rescue is only useful on the left outlane and its timing gets tighter after use; pressing early can waste the opportunity. [S3] ([Tilt Forums][2])

## Trivia

* Stern launched JAWS at **CES in January 2024**, drawing footage/audio from all four original films. [S5] ([Stern Pinball][3])
* The game includes custom speech recorded by **Richard Dreyfuss** and John Williams’ iconic film theme. [S2] 
* Pro substitutes a static Shark Cage/captive-ball assembly and Wave-ramp mini-flipper return for Premium/LE's motorized shark and Orca upper playfield. [S2] 
* JAWS was Keith Elwin's **sixth Stern design**. [S4] ([Kineticist][1])
* The final wizard mode and Super Celebration were substantial post-launch additions rather than launch-code features. [S1]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Feeding Frenzy qualification count:** the later community rulesheet says the second Frenzy requires five target sets [S3], while Stern's official revision history records a default change from five to **three** subsequent sets [S1]. Treat the actual threshold as adjustment/version-sensitive. ([Tilt Forums][2])
* **Strategy evidence:** Noah Crable's detailed tournament recommendations were originally developed on **v0.98**. The important underlying mechanics remain documented in the newer v1.01 rulesheet/current Stern revision history, but “Jaws MB1 as utility” should remain labeled **observed strategy**, not current manufacturer guidance. [S3][S4] ([Kineticist][1])
* Physical drain tendencies are strongly copy/setup-dependent; Stern's dedicated feed-saver adjustments provide stronger evidence than anecdotal descriptions of any particular machine.
