## Identity and versions

**Godzilla — Premium**, Stern Pinball, **2021**, SPIKE 2. Lead design **Keith Elwin**; lead software **Rick Naegele**; mechanical engineering **Harrison Drake**; art **Jeremy Packer / Zombie Yeti**; sound **Jerry Thompson**. [S1]

Premium/LE add the **motorized collapsing skyscraper with physical locks, collapsing bridge ramp, and rotating Mechagodzilla target bank/jump ramp with magnetic capture**. Pro lacks those mechanisms, so some shot geometry and qualification differ. [S1]

Latest code verified: **v1.16.0, August 26, 2026**. Current sources characterize it as a minor firmware/behind-the-scenes update; no scoring rewrite was identified. [S2]

Most relevant multiballs: **Godzilla Multiball, Mechagodzilla Multiball, Bridge Attack Multiball, Tank Attack Multiball**. [S1][S3]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. Shoot ramps to qualify a **Kaiju Battle**; **Gigan first** is a common tournament route because its required ramp shots simultaneously damage the building toward Godzilla Multiball. [S4]
2. After the first battle, change cities—**London** is a popular choice because conquering it raises Destruction Jackpot—and prepare **Titanosaurus inside a multiball** rather than attacking its dangerous standups single-ball. [S4]
3. Destroy the building, then on Premium **physically lock three balls** to start Godzilla Multiball. Six jackpots light the building Super Jackpot. [S3]
4. Collect **Rodan** when a large scoring window is ready: it is **60 seconds of 2X playfield scoring**. Heat Ray can then collect multiple lit shots at once. [S3]
5. Do not ignore a fat **Destruction Jackpot**: its base grows from scoring elsewhere and its separate multiplier can climb repeatedly. That single upper-loop cash-out can swing a match. [S3]

## Core rules and persistence

**Kaiju Battles:** first battle is qualified by two ramp shots; later battles require **both ramps**. Battle progress persists after failure, but replayed shot values and the final time bonus are reduced. Tier-1 Battles can stack with multiballs if the Battle is started **first**. [S3]

**Godzilla Multiball:** ramp/building hits reduce building stability. At 0%, Premium requires three physical locks. During 3-ball MB, jackpots advance in value; six light the building Super. Completing all jackpot shots repeatedly can multiply the Super up to 3X, plus a one-time Wallop increase. [S3]

**Mechagodzilla MB:** charge the right spinner, defeat the rotating three-target Neo Barrier, then return to Mechagodzilla. After six jackpots, spinners build a timed Super: **1X at right spinner or 3X via the upper-flipper Tail Whip**. [S3]

**Destruction Jackpot:** major city objectives light it. Its value incorporates percentages of accumulated mode/multiball scoring; its multiplier increases through conquered Kaiju, trains, loops, fighters, Monster Rampage and other objectives. **Changing cities wipes its temporary base value**, creating a real collect-now-versus-move-on decision. [S3]

**Rodan** is a true 2X playfield multiplier. **Anguirus** stores an action-button add-a-ball; **Mothra** lights a left-outlane save. [S3]

## Skill shots

* **Short-plunge lanes:** several shooter-lane/UFO switches score **250K–750K × unique skill shots** and add **3–5 seconds of ball save**. [S3]
* **Left spinner lane:** plunge behind the upper flipper for **5M +1M per unique skill shot** and **8 seconds ball save**. Excellent tournament default when makeable. [S3]
* **Upper-loop Secret Skill Shot:** plunge to the upper flipper, then reflex the upper loop for **4M × unique skill shots** plus 5 seconds save. [S3]
* **Left-inlane Secret Skill Shot:** direct plunge to left inlane for **5M +1M × unique skill shots**, plus 5 seconds save. [S3]
* **Super Secret:** make the left-spinner-lane plunge, then immediately reflex the **scoop**: **10M, +2 Power-Up progress, and a lit Ally is immediately collected**. [S3]

Skill-shot ball-save time is strategically significant if the venue has otherwise shortened/disabled normal plunge saves.

## Secondary features

**Heat Ray:** center-spinner progress lights the action button. Hold it to 100% to collect lit playfield shots simultaneously; it cannot collect Destruction Jackpot and is restricted in some late modes. It also enables a one-rip **Super Spinner** afterward. [S3]

**Mothra:** left-outlane ball save. **Anguirus:** stored add-a-ball used with the action button. **Rodan:** 60-second 2X playfield. The right flipper chooses among currently available Allies. [S3]

**Oxygen Destroyer:** on the last ball, a right-outlane drain can trigger a one-time rescue challenge: a replacement ball is served and must be shot **through the building within 12 seconds** or the game kills the flippers. [S3]

**Extra Balls:** earned from multiple milestones including two conquered cities, three saucers and ten fighters. Current Competition Install sets **NO EXTRA BALLS**. [S3][S5]

## What to watch

* **Battle flashing at scoop:** player has finished ramp qualification and is choosing the next Kaiju.
* **Green building/lock state:** building is destroyed; on Premium, watch balls physically accumulate on its roof.
* **Rodan active:** all scoring is doubled for a minute; this is when already-lit jackpots become much more dangerous to the opponent. [S3]
* **Building grabs ball, then drops it to upper flipper:** **Destruction Jackpot** is live; the player gets a brief upper-loop cash-out attempt. [S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** use the reliable **Gigan → city change → prepared multiball** route, prioritize Mothra before greedier Allies, and take a mature Destruction Jackpot before changing cities erases its temporary base. **Observed strategy plus rules-derived inference.** [S3][S4]

**Playing behind:** create leverage: start Titanosaurus inside Godzilla/Tank/Bridge MB, choose **Rodan** for 2X scoring, use Heat Ray when several valuable shots are lit, or push a multiplied Destruction Jackpot. Mechagodzilla's **3X Tail Whip Super** is another concentrated comeback shot. [S3][S4]

**Key recurring decision:** **cash accumulated value now or keep building?** Godzilla constantly offers this choice through Destruction Jackpot, city changes, Allies, Heat Ray, and multiball setup.

## Danger zones

* **Battle/City scoop:** sharp, late left-flipper shot; experienced players describe it as dangerous even when dialed in. [S4][S6]
* **Right-ramp rejects:** a short ramp shot can return rapidly toward center; strongly machine/setup-dependent. [S6]
* **Building misses/reverse exits:** central rebounds can become straight-down-the-middle feeds on poorly leveled copies. [S6]
* **UFO pop:** strategically useful but inherently uncontrolled; Kineticist specifically notes it can throw the ball out of control. [S4]
* **Titanosaurus targets:** direct standups are substantially safer when attacked during multiball; this is why tournament guides recommend stacking it. [S4]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Latest code:** the current JLP/PinballCards reference and Pinside owners-club index identify **v1.16.0 (August 26, 2026)** [S2], while Pinside's main game archive still displays **v1.15.0 (July 28, 2026)** [S5]. The v1.16 post describes a minor firmware/behind-the-scenes update; no gameplay-rule change was found.
* **Competition Mode vs Competition Install:** they are not synonymous. The Install enables Competition Mode **and** sets no Extra Balls plus several generic tournament settings. [S5]
* **Physical feeds:** scoop, ramp and building returns vary significantly with pitch/level and mechanism adjustment. Treat the danger descriptions above as common observed behavior, not guaranteed geometry.
* The detailed community rulesheet is still labeled as being based on **code 1.08**, despite having later edits. Current late-code changes after 1.08 appear mostly system/bug-fix related, but venue code should still be confirmed. [S3][S5]
