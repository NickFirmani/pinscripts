## Thirty-second game plan

1. Collect **three white Film Reel arrows**, then shoot the **ACME Crate or Vault** to start an Episode. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/spooky/looney-tunes-rulesheet)
2. **Observed strategy:** prepare Anvil or Rocket multiball, but **start the Episode first**; normal multiballs block new Episode starts, while an already-running Episode continues through multiball. [S4] [Flutter Catch](https://fluttercatch.com/machine/looney-tunes?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
3. Build **Playfield X** through the hidden Blueprint lane; repeated qualifications reach **6×**, then activate it through the lit return lane for a 30-second scoring window. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/spooky/looney-tunes-rulesheet?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
4. Finish the Episode under multiball protection, then collect its **Encore at the Vault**; a clean Vault shot without clipping the surrounding targets doubles the Encore. [S3][S4] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/spooky/looney-tunes-rulesheet?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
5. **Alternate/high-variance route:** the second Rocket cycle becomes **Rocket Skates**, whose final Super can reach a **36× multiplier** and can swing a match immediately. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/spooky/looney-tunes-rulesheet)

## Core rules and persistence

Episodes are the primary modes. They are generally not conventionally timed, though individual phases may be. Completing one lights Encore for **10% of that Episode’s score**, doubled on a direct clean Vault shot. Episode starts/completions accumulate toward Extra Ball, Special and ultimately Duck Amuck. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/spooky/looney-tunes-rulesheet)

**Anvil Frenzy:** left ramp lights an Anvil hurry-up at the Crate; collecting two of these banks two virtual locks and starts 2-ball multiball. Major-shot jackpots lead to a Crate Super, with a skillful magnet collision capable of producing the Mega Jackpot. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/spooky/looney-tunes-rulesheet)

**Rocket route:** current v2026.10.16 defaults to just **one captive-ball hit for the first Rocket MB qualification, five thereafter**; three right-orbit locks start the multiball. Giant Rocket requires five jackpots before a declining captive-ball Super. The next Rocket cycle alternates to Rocket Skates. [S2][S3] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/2405-2/)

**Rocket Skates:** after the third lock, three extra physical-lock attempts can raise it from 3 to 6 balls. Collect yellow jackpots, revisit three for 2× jackpots, then the Crate Super is multiplied by **balls in play × number of 2× jackpots**, max **36×**. Collecting the Super **immediately ends the multiball**. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/spooky/looney-tunes-rulesheet)

Main multiballs are mutually exclusive. **TNT Add-A-Ball is the exception**: it can coexist with Episodes and other multiballs, making it unusually valuable as protection. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/spooky/looney-tunes-rulesheet?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

Blueprint Playfield X lasts 30 seconds and can reach **6×**. Qualified multipliers persist between balls; separately, each completed Factory Mayhem requirement adds **10% scoring**. [S2][S3] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/2405-2/)

## Skill shots

- **Standard Skill Shot:** time the plunge into the **strobing ACME Vault target**. Useful as a low-risk opening award and Vault progress cue. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/spooky/looney-tunes-rulesheet?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **Super Skill Shot:** press the **Action button before plunging**, then make the Wile E. Coyote upper/inner loop from the upper flipper immediately after launch. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/spooky/looney-tunes-rulesheet?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## Secondary features

- **Hare Restore:** five carrot-target hits light saves at both outlanes; later saves require more carrots. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/spooky/looney-tunes-rulesheet?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **TNT Add-A-Ball:** complete TNT, then shoot the hidden lane below the upper-right flipper. Holding one ball there can double the frenzy value while it is trapped. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/spooky/looney-tunes-rulesheet?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **ACME Items:** Crate awards include shot spotting, 3× ramp/orbit/spinner boosts, ball save, Extra Ball qualification and Blueprint Playfield X. Action button uses the selected item; secondary flipper buttons cycle held items. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/spooky/looney-tunes-rulesheet)
- **Extra Ball:** lights at the right orbit from three completed Episodes, 200 MPH Roadrunner speed, or using the Giant Magnet item. No authoritative universal tournament point conversion found. [S3] [The Pinball Archive](https://pinballarchive.com/machines/looney-tunes-spooky?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- The software exposes **Tournament Mode**, but current public documentation does not explain its complete normalization behavior. [S6] [Notice Facile](https://www.notice-facile.com/en/manual/1718070/spooky%2Bpinball%2Blooney-tunes?utm_source=chatgpt.com)

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** **Strategic inference:** take an Episode + Anvil stack, use Hare Restore defensively, and collect Encore rather than overbuilding a difficult 6× setup or forcing Rocket Skates.

**Playing behind:** prepare Playfield X, bring it into an Episode + Rocket multiball, and on the second Rocket cycle preserve as many balls as possible for the **Rocket Skates 36×-capable Super**. Factory Mayhem’s accumulated 10% scoring boosts also become increasingly relevant in a long game. [S2][S3] [Spooky Pinball](https://www.spookypinball.com/2405-2/)

**Key recurring decision:** **start the prepared multiball now for safety, or first start an Episode/build multiplier so the same multiball protects much larger scoring?**

## Danger zones

- **Right outlane:** multiple owners independently describe it as unusually hungry even with the post in its easier position. [S7] [Pinside](https://pinside.com/pinball/forum/topic/whats-up-doc-its-the-looney-tunes-owners-club/page/41?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **Center-adjacent ramps:** less-than-square hits can reject or airball instead of completing the ramp. [S7] [Pinside](https://pinside.com/pinball/machine/looney-tunes-collectors-edition/ratings/page/2?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
- **Crate/captive-ball bashes:** valuable setup shots return energy toward the middle instead of guaranteeing a controlled feed.
- **Upper-flipper setup:** missing Vault, Blueprint or loop shots often drops the ball back into uncontrolled traffic.
- **Rocket Skates Super:** collecting it too early is a strategic drain—it **ends the multiball immediately**, even if an Episode or larger multiplier opportunity remains. [S3] [Pinball Rule Sheets](https://pinballrulesheets.com/spooky/looney-tunes-rulesheet)
