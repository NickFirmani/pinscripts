## Identity and versions

* **Radical!** — Bally/Midway, **1990**, model **2015**, Williams **System 11C**. Design by **Dan Langlois and Peter Perry**, software by **Dan Lee**, art by **John Youssi**, music/sound by **Paul Heitsch**. Approximately 1,300 games were produced; archival counts disagree slightly. [S1][S5] ([Pinside][1])
* Four flippers, three ramps, two physical balls, six drop targets, two spinners and a left-outlane kickback. The single major feature is **2-ball Multiball / Mega-Million**. [S1][S2] 
* Original domestic production code is **L-1**, dated **April 9, 1990** in the factory handbook. [S1] 
* A licensed **Revision 3.0 (2020)** by Soren Worre/Planetary Pinball materially improves tournament fairness: player-specific lock progress, no hard lock stealing, deterministic Snake Run in Contest mode, and Mega-Million sequence **4M → 6M → 8M → 10M**. [S4] ([Planetary Pinball][2])
* Scope follows the supplied commentator-reference specification. 

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** spell **R-A-D-I-C-A-L** by making seven different major shots. Completing it lights **Skate or Die**, i.e. the first lock opportunity. [S1][S2][S3] ([GameEx Online][3])
2. Lock one ball, then spell **RADICAL again** and lock the second to start 2-ball Multiball. [S1][S2]
3. In Multiball, shoot the **left ramp for Mega-Million**—stock code randomly awards **3M–10M**, often dwarfing everything else on the game. [S1][S2] ([Pinball Nirvana][4])
4. After collecting Mega-Million, keep spelling RADICAL during Multiball; another virtual lock can **relight the jackpot**, permitting repeated Mega-Millions. [S2]
5. Alternate scoring: fast RADICAL letters can reach **500K/1M each**, and Top Million, Bottom Million, Snake Run and maximum **2M end-of-ball bonus** can materially swing a game even without Multiball. [S2][S6] ([Pinball Nirvana][5])

## Core rules and persistence

* Each RADICAL letter corresponds to a major shot: **R Snake Lane; A Vertical Ramp; D left orbit/top spinner; I right ramp; C lower drops; A right orbit/spinner; L left ramp.** Letters can also be spotted by the skill shot and lit hidden target. [S1][S2] ([Pinball Nirvana][4])
* Letter value rises **20K → 40K → 60K → 80K → 100K → 500K → 1M** as letters are collected, but higher values time out. Fast, accurate shotmaking can therefore make the RADICAL build itself worth well over 1M. [S2]
* Completing RADICAL also relights the **kickback**, lights Bottom Million, and adds **100K to outhole bonus**. [S1][S2] ([Pinball Nirvana][5])
* Top drop-bank completions advance **Bonus X to 5×**. RADICAL letters build base outhole bonus to **400K**; maximum end-of-ball bonus is therefore **2M**. Base bonus normally carries ball-to-ball but is adjustable. [S2] ([Pinball Nirvana][5])
* **Stock L-1 multiplayer warning:** the physical lock can effectively be **stolen/shared between players**. Rev 3.0 instead tracks each player's lock achievement independently. This can fundamentally change tournament strategy. [S2][S4] ([Pinball Nirvana][5])
* Under Rev 3 Contest mode, jackpot progression is deterministic **4M/6M/8M/10M**, rather than stock's random 3M–10M. [S4]

## Skill shots

**RADICAL Skill Shot** — plunge the ball up the **left ramp** while the RADICAL lamps cycle.

* Spots whichever RADICAL letter was lit when the ramp entry registered. [S1][S2] ([GameEx Online][3])
* Particularly valuable on the first RADICAL because it removes one unique shot from the qualification cycle.
* The launch guide is physically adjustment-sensitive; owner documentation notes that misalignment can make the skill shot impossible. [S7] ([Pinside][6])
* No separate super/secret skill shot verified.

## Secondary features

* **Kickback:** left outlane; normally lit at ball start and relit by completing RADICAL. It is a genuine survival resource. [S2] ([Pinball Nirvana][5])
* **Extra Ball:** completing **S-N-A-K-E**, sufficient Vertical Ramp progress, or receiving the Snake Run award can qualify/award it. The shot-based EB is a short hurry-up that moves from Vertical Ramp to right outlane. Tournament treatment should be verified. [S1][S2]
* **Snake Run:** complete the upper drop bank, then collect the timed Snake Run for a random award: Skate or Die, Extra Ball, 1M, mystery score, Tail Sliders or Verticals. [S1][S2] ([Pinball Nirvana][5])
* **Rev 3 Contest mode** removes that randomness with a fixed player-specific award sequence and fixes Mystery at 100K. [S4]
* No conventional timed ball save, action button, lane change or video mode.

## What to watch

1. **RADICAL letters:** immediately show how close the player is to lighting the next physical lock.
2. **“Skate or Die” callout / lock lamps:** RADICAL is complete; any of three lock routes can now bank the ball.
3. **Mega-Million lamp on left ramp:** this is the match-swinging Multiball cash-out—3M–10M stock, deterministic under Rev 3 Contest.
4. **500K/1M letter-value lamp:** a player moving unusually fast between distinct shots may be scoring huge points before Multiball even begins.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** keep taking distinct RADICAL shots and value the kickback. Once Multiball starts, cash Mega-Million rather than overcomplicating the ball with perfect relight preparation. **Strategic inference** based on the jackpot's dominant value.

**Playing behind:** maximize variance: push letter values quickly toward **500K/1M**, collect Mega-Million, then immediately rebuild RADICAL during Multiball for another virtual lock and jackpot relight. [S2]

**Key recurring decision:** **shoot the safest missing RADICAL letter, or use the lower drop/Spot Letter shortcut?** Directly completing seven distinct shots preserves flow; hammering drops can shortcut an awkward letter but is one of the few parts of Radical! that deliberately gives up ball control. [S2][S3]

## Danger zones

1. **Lower drop targets:** explicitly one of the main places where controlled flow stops; rebounds can become immediate drain threats. [S2]
2. **Weak left-ramp shot:** can fall directly into the pop bumpers rather than completing the intended ramp/feed. [S2]
3. **Snake Lane:** very narrow and immediately beside the left outlane—valuable but unforgiving.
4. **Right outlane:** described in the detailed rulesheet as particularly harsh; unlike the left, it has no normal kickback. [S2]
5. **Upper-flipper combos:** the right ramp and several letters depend on high-speed orbit feeds; a weak feed leaves little time or angle for the required upper-flipper shot. [S2]

## Trivia

* Radical! was a very early-1990s celebration of **skateboarding culture**, complete with ramps, “Skate or Die” speech and deliberately period slang. [S1][S8] ([World Radio History][7])
* Its playfield is unusually flow-oriented: four flippers and multiple diverters route successful shots directly toward another flipper rather than stopping the ball. [S2]
* The game uses **only two physical balls** and no conventional under-playfield ball trough; its physical lock is therefore central to game state. [S1][S2]
* Radical! straddles eras: physical stealable locks and random jackpots coexist with elaborate ramp diverters, timed features and stackable Multiball rules. [S2]
* The licensed **2020 Rev 3** software was specifically rewritten to make Radical! substantially fairer for modern tournament play. [S4]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Production count:** Pinside gives **1,315**, while IPSND/Kineticist give approximately **1,328**. [S5] Use “about 1,300.”
* **Stock versus Rev 3 is a major rules split.** Original L-1 uses random 3M–10M jackpots and permits multiplayer lock interaction; Rev 3 Contest uses player-specific locks and deterministic 4M/6M/8M/10M jackpots. [S2][S4]
* The exact **Mega-Million grace period** after returning to single-ball play is operator-adjustable. [S2]
* Base outhole-bonus persistence and several letter/Extra-Ball behaviors are also adjustable. [S1][S2]
* The detailed 2002 rulesheet leaves the exact Vertical-Ramp Extra-Ball threshold uncertain; the factory rules state only **multiple Vertical Ramp shots**. Do not announce an exact count without checking the machine. [S1][S2]
* Many critical returns are mechanical/diverter dependent; Radical! is especially sensitive to ramp and shooter-lane adjustment.
