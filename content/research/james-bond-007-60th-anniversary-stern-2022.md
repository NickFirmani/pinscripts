## Identity and versions

**James Bond 007 60th Anniversary Limited Edition** — Stern Pinball, 2023. Design: **Keith Elwin**; software/rules: **Mark Penacho**; artwork: **Kevin O’Connor**; engineering credits include Harrison Drake and Robert Blakeman. SPIKE 2, two flippers, no ramps, four optical spinners, ten drop targets, mechanical score reels plus an in-playfield LCD. [S2][S5]

* Limited to **500 machines** globally. [S2]
* This is **not an edition of George Gomez’s 2022 James Bond Pro/Premium/LE**. It is a completely different Keith Elwin layout and ruleset. [S2][S7]
* **Rules basis: `code` — v1.11.0, released 2026-07-01.** [S1][S5]
* Four principal multiballs: **Mission, Villain, Gadget, Bond**. Completing the latter three categories qualifies **007 Mode / 007 Multiball**. [S3]
* The current community rulesheet remains incomplete in several low-level areas, so this brief avoids inventing missing mode values. [S3]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Current tournament focus: Mission Multiball.** Knock down the three inline targets, then shoot through them to the right scoop to start the 2-ball phase. Current competitive guidance specifically labels this the multiball focus. **Observed strategy.** [S4]
2. During Mission MB, **lock one ball in each scoop** within the timers to reach 3-ball play. Then lock balls again to light the captive ball for Jackpot/Double Jackpot. [S3]
3. After the captive-ball Jackpot, shoot **M** for the **1,500-point Super Jackpot** and restart the sequence. [S3]
4. Outside Mission MB, work **BOND → Gadgets/Bonds** and **MI6 → Villain modes**. Completing all six Bonds, all four Gadgets, and all six Villains eventually produces their respective multiballs. [S3]
5. Long-game alternate: start **Villain + Gadget + Bond Multiballs** to qualify 007 Mode. That route has much higher setup risk but leads to the final 007 Multiball. [S3]

## Core rules and persistence

**Mission Multiball:** complete the inline drop bank to light the right scoop. It starts as 2-ball; shooting either scoop begins a **15-second window** to lock the other ball in the opposite scoop. Lock both to launch Ball 3. During 3-ball play, one locked ball lights the captive-ball Jackpot for 500; balls locked at both scoops qualify the Double Jackpot. Jackpot → M scores a **1,500-point Super Jackpot** and restarts the sequence. If the first 2-ball phase ends prematurely, the right scoop can light briefly for restart. [S3]

**Villains:** complete MI6 to enable Oddjob’s disc and choose/start the current villain. Completing all six qualifies Villain Multiball, where all four spinners become major scoring shots. Villain-mode progress can survive an unfinished attempt according to experienced-owner documentation. [S3][S6]

**Gadgets:** BOND progress enables the disc’s Gadget Meter. The four awards are **Jetpack = Hold Bonus; Spy Camera = action button can light Mission lock; Gondola Hovercraft = Underwater Escape outlane saves; X-Ray Glasses = Gadget Multiball.** [S3]

**Bonds:** BOND completion and starting villain modes can light the left scoop to Collect Bond. Collect all six Bond actors to start Bond Multiball. [S3]

**007 Mode:** start Villain, Gadget and Bond Multiballs. The left scoop then starts a single-ball bomb-defusing phase: clear the three inline drops and reach the right scoop before time expires. Success starts **007 Multiball**. Progress resets afterward, so another 007 Mode is theoretically possible in the same game. [S3]

**Bonus matters:** A-B-C completions advance Bonus X to **3x**. M can collect the current bonus before drain, and the Jetpack gadget holds bonus. [S3]

## Skill shots

* **007 Lane Skill Shot:** plunge through the flashing 0/0/7 top lane. The lit lane **cannot be moved with the flippers**. It collects the currently indicated back-panel award and advances the top-lane feature. [S3][S4]
* **Mission Multiball / inline-drop Super Skill Shot:** hold the left flipper, plunge past the top lanes, then shoot through the inline-drop lane. It clears the bank and immediately lights Mission Multiball lock. [S3][S4] Current owner reports indicate later code limits a successful use to **once per game by default**, with an adjustment available. [S6]
* The current rulesheet also documents **short-plunge → Q** and **left-flipper/full-plunge → M** launch sequences, but it does **not document their awards**. Their strategic value is therefore **uncertain** rather than guessed. [S3]

## Secondary features

* **Underwater Escape:** Gondola Hovercraft gadget lights the outlanes for a rescue/ball save. [S3]
* **Spy Camera/action button:** after Spy Camera is earned, the green action button can light the Mission Multiball lock without re-completing the inline drops. [S3]
* **Extra Ball:** the third 007-lane award lights Extra Ball at the captive ball. When extra balls are disabled, the documented conversion is **500 points**. [S3]
* **Spinner boost:** either inlane lights the opposite-side spinner for **10 points per spin** instead of 1, making long spinner shots meaningful at this game's deliberately small scoring scale. [S3]
* No conventional random Mystery award or separate video mode materially affecting tournament strategy was verified.

## What to watch

1. **Right lock scoop lit behind the inline drops:** the player is one shot from Mission Multiball—the most common competitive objective. [S4]
2. **A ball parked in each scoop during Mission MB:** the player is trying to convert 2-ball play into 3-ball play or light the Double Jackpot. [S3]
3. **Oddjob disc / MI6 lighting:** either a villain is being selected/played or gadget progress is being manipulated; check which drop bank the player just completed. [S3]
4. **Back-panel Bond/Gadget/Villain progress nearly complete:** this can signal a pivot away from short-term Mission MB toward the category multiballs and 007 Mode.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:**
**Observed strategy:** stay focused on Mission Multiball. Use the safer backhand into the inline drops, convert to three balls, and take the Jackpot/Super sequence rather than deliberately building all three long-game category multiballs. [S4]

**Playing behind:**
Push broader progression: finish villain modes, Bonds and Gadgets, and pursue **007 Mode**. During a villain mode, exploiting its scoring multiplier/victory-lap state adds another higher-variance avenue. **Strategic inference:** the long route is appropriate when Mission MB alone is unlikely to erase the deficit.

**Key recurring decision:**
**Mission Multiball now versus category progression.** Mission is comparatively fast and repeatable; BOND/MI6 work has more dangerous target exposure but moves the player toward three separate multiballs and the wizard mode.

## Danger zones

* **Inline drop bank:** known to brick hard shots on some machines; rebounds can turn the primary strategy into instant danger. [S6]
* **Right scoop:** owner reports specifically document strong rejection/bounce-out behavior when not well adjusted. [S6]
* **Left scoop feed:** the orbit-to-saucer path is especially sensitive to machine setup and can miss the lock entirely. [S6]
* **Oddjob disc:** a central spinning object makes post-hit direction difficult to predict.
* **Overall shot geometry:** experienced players describe the game as unusually fast and brutal; there are many narrow shots and very few passive safe areas. [S6]

## Trivia

* Stern produced only **500** 60th Anniversary machines worldwide. [S2]
* Keith Elwin’s game has **zero ramps, two flippers, four optical spinners and ten drop targets**, deliberately evoking late-1970s/early-1980s solid-state design. [S2][S7]
* Scores are intentionally tiny by modern standards and displayed on **real mechanical score reels**, supplemented by illuminated rollover indicators for higher totals. [S7]
* The machine represents **all six film Bonds and all 25 Bond films released through the anniversary**, unlike the separate Connery-focused Gomez game. [S2]
* Since code **1.05.0**, QR codes can switch among multiple historical chime/sound packages, including deliberately stripped-down “Vintage” presentations. [S8]
