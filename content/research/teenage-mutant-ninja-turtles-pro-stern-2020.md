## Identity and versions

* **Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles — Pro**, Stern, **2020**, SPIKE 2. Design: **John Borg**; lead rules/software: **Dwight Sullivan**; art: **Jeremy Packer / Zombie Yeti**; sound: **Jerry Thompson**. [S1][S5] ([Stern Pinball][1])
* Pro shares the core rules with Premium/LE but lacks their sculpted 4-ball van lock, player-controlled Glider diverter, animated Krang and Premium-only **Mushrooms** pizza topping. Pro instead stages Ninja Pizza locks beneath the Dimension X ramp and uses six installed balls rather than eight. [S1]
* **Rules basis: `code` — v1.58, released 2024-11-26.** Current Stern-derived changelog identifies 1.58 as latest; it is mainly a system update. Important later gameplay corrections include the v1.55 Team-Up fix and v1.57 Insider Connected Training-save fix. [S2] ([Pinside][2])
* Primary multiballs: **Ninja Pizza Multiball** and **Turtle Power Multiball**. **Team-Up Multiball** is the four-Episode mini-wizard; **Final Battle** and **Cowabunga Multiball** are deeper goals. [S3]
* Research scope follows the supplied commentator-reference specification.

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed tournament route:** pick **Raphael** for an immediately lit Episode, or Michelangelo on an exceptionally brutal game for the lit outlane save. [S4][S5] ([System J Pinball][3])
2. Prioritize **Ninja Pizza Multiball (NPMB)**: three Pizza Parlor shots lock/start it. If possible, bank **2× Playfield** first and trigger it immediately before multiball. [S3][S4] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][4])
3. In NPMB, defeat six Foot Soldiers → **Mondo Jackpot** at the upper Krang loop → repeat it for Double/Triple Mondo; the first Mondo also lights **Jackpoto Grande** at the LAIR, starting at roughly **3M + 500K per Mondo**. [S3]
4. Run **Episodes underneath multiball** when possible. Four Episodes qualify Team-Up and reopen the path to the two main multiballs. [S3][S5]
5. Alternate strategy: Training → **Neutrino Pizza Party** can produce large single-ball scoring; Donatello offers immediate Turtle Power Multiball as a safer utility route. [S3][S5]

## Core rules and persistence

Shoot both main ramps to light **Start Episode** at the Pizza Parlor. Episodes are generally timed; completing one gives Team-Up perks. Four played Episodes light **Team-Up Multiball**; all eight light **Final Battle**. [S3]

**Ninja Pizza Multiball:** three Parlor shots start it. Each lock freezes more pizza toppings—2, then 2, then 1—which modify the multiball. On the Pro, Competition Mode has been documented with **Octopus + Fudge** first; the strongest add-ball/instant-jackpot toppings are removed from that competition pool. Treat that exact sequence as observed competition behavior, not a manufacturer rule specification. [S3][S4] ([System J Pinball][3])

**Turtle Power Multiball:** fourth right-ramp shot starts it, unless Donatello’s level-1 perk lights it immediately. Four green jackpots light Mondo; Mondo lights the LAIR Super. Completing all four Turtle shots lights an add-a-ball at the right ramp. [S3][S5]

**Training / scoring leverage:** odd LAIR completions light Training; even completions light **30 seconds of 2× Playfield**, extendable 5 seconds per center-target hit. Each completed Training raises all future scoring by **10%**, up to +30%, and unlocks the next turtle perk. Raphael level 3 upgrades the playfield multiplier to **3×**. [S3]

Turtle level/perks normally persist for the game. Insider Connected can save trained levels **between games**, but Stern’s rules programmer confirmed that **Competition Mode ignores saved Training and starts players at level 1**, a major tournament-fairness distinction. [S3][S7] ([TiltForums][5])

A major late swing: Final Battle’s start award is based on accumulated Episode performance and **is multiplied by active 2×/3× Playfield**. Expert play reports single-shot swings of tens of millions here. [S4] ([System J Pinball][3])

## Skill shots

| Skill shot                         | How                                                                                                                                                                    | Award / why                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Top-lane Skill Shot — Pro only** | Plunge through the flashing top lane                                                                                                                                   | **+1× Bonus multiplier.** Holding left flipper lets the ball bypass the pops toward the upper flipper.                                            |
| **Turtle Skill Shot**              | Within ~15 seconds, hit the shot corresponding to the selected turtle: Leonardo = LAIR, Raphael = left ramp, Michelangelo = Pizza Parlor, Donatello = center/side ramp | Starts at **250K** and increases with successive skill shots; lights an inlane for Weapon Hurry-Up. Often the strategically more important award. |

The two can occur sequentially on the same plunge: if the ball goes through the pops first, the turtle-shot timer begins afterward. No separate super/secret skill shot was verified. [S3][S5] ([Pinball Rule Sheets][6])

## Secondary features

* **Battle Again:** odd APRIL completions light one outlane for a save; by default slingshots move which side is protected. [S3]
* **Pict-O-Pops:** matching bumper images awards utilities such as Add-A-Ball during multiball, timer extension, Battle Again, Episode qualification and Bonus X. Standard play contains randomness; Competition removes/normalizes random elements. [S3]
* **Action button:** during the Pizza Eating Contest, mash it for up to 40 pizza slices in roughly six seconds. On the Pro it has no Glider-steering function. [S3]
* **Extra balls:** can light after two Episodes, pizza-slice progress or Pict-O-Pops. Current Stern Competition Install sets **No Extra Balls**; I did not verify a fixed TMNT point conversion. [S2][S3]
* **Neutrino Pizza Party:** current code includes a ball save; it is a one-per-game, 30-second single-ball scoring mode whose blue collect can build toward **5M**. [S2][S3]
* **Video mode:** None found.

## What to watch

1. **Center 2X target lit:** the player has banked playfield X; expect them to save it for NPMB, Team-Up or Final Battle. [S3]
2. **Pizza topping icons:** these are not decoration—they define NPMB rules. Each Parlor lock freezes more of the displayed set. [S3]
3. **White/blue/red upper-loop jackpot lighting:** Mondo → Double → Triple Mondo; after the first Mondo, LAIR is the Super/Jackpoto Grande. [S3]
4. **Four Episodes played / left ramp flashing:** Team-Up is ready, which also represents the game’s main route back toward additional main multiballs. [S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** **strategic inference:** take NPMB once reasonably prepared rather than risking extra topping manipulation; cash the first Mondo/Grande cycle, use easy Episodes toward Team-Up, and avoid low-value hurry-ups unless they overlap another needed shot.

**Playing behind:** bank **2×**, then bring it into NPMB and chase repeated Mondo/Triple Mondo plus Jackpoto Grande. If Training progress is available, Raphael’s higher-level multiplier/Episode perks or Neutrino Pizza Party offer much larger variance. Final Battle with playfield X active is another genuine match-flipping collect. [S4]

**Key recurring decision:** **start Ninja Pizza now or spend additional risky shots improving its multiplier/toppings first?** Current strategy sources broadly agree NPMB is the game’s central scoring engine; the disagreement is how much setup risk is justified before taking it. [S4][S5] ([System J Pinball][8])

## Danger zones

* **Pizza Parlor guard target:** mistimed shots can reject almost immediately toward the drain. [S5]
* **Spinning pizza disc:** target hits can activate it and throw otherwise-controlled balls unpredictably. [S5]
* **Right-ramp forehand:** clipping the nearby post can be disastrous; strong players often prefer the backhand. [S5]
* **Left-ramp reject:** one of the tightest required shots on the game; misses return fast into open play. [S5]
* **LAIR feed:** documented as capable of bouncing toward the left outlane on some setups; venue-specific geometry matters. [S4]

## Trivia

* Stern’s game uses the **1987 animated-series** presentation rather than the 1990 movie used by Data East’s earlier TMNT pinball. [S1][S5]
* The layout is by **John Borg**, with rules led by **Dwight Sullivan** and hand-drawn art by Zombie Yeti. [S1]
* Every model has a magnetic spinning pizza disc capable of holding and throwing three balls for NPMB. [S1]
* Premium/LE received the physical four-ball Turtle Van, while the Pro uses a simpler staged lock system. [S1]
* TMNT supports unusually varied built-in play formats: Standard, Competition, Cooperative, Coop+Competition, Impossible, Monster and team play. [S3]
