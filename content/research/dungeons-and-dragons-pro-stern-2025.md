## Thirty-second game plan

1. Observed strategy: choose a character and starting town. Wizard Helenir offers a longer shield; Paladin Ardor makes defeating Rath easier. Arabel is a strong opening for its accessible Retrieve a Potion for Nighdaar quest. [S4]
2. Prioritize Gelatinous Cube Multiball: left orbit lowers the ramp; shoot the ramp until the magnet locks a ball. Its jackpots grow by 3M each, reaching 25M, with a 20M Super Jackpot. This is a primary scoring engine. [S3][S4]
3. Start Dungeon Crawl: qualify with orange targets/spinner, then hit the spinner again on Pro. Progress through orange/purple shots and win battles for substantial points. [S3]
4. Qualify Dragon Multiball: spell DRAGON at the three-bank, start at right orbit/side ramp, defeat Rath and collect Celebration jackpots. Starting both multiballs plus Dungeon Crawl qualifies Tavern Brawl, the major tournament objective. [S3][S4]
5. Scoring leverage: activate the Hidden Temple's timed 2× scoring before lucrative jackpots; alternatively pursue Tiny's Dice Game, where the Pro spinner can reach 500k per spin. [S3][S4]

## Core rules and persistence

- Campaign: pick character with right flipper, town with left. Travel normally requires three blue shots; draining also finishes travel. In town, yellow shots let players select quests. Red shots damage enemies, cyan completes phases. [S3]
- Battles: each red shot rolls damage for the four-person party. Enemies attack periodically. The action button's ranged attack and two captive-ball hits (Critical Hit) can substitute for dangerous physical shots. [S3]
- Dungeon Crawl: starts with approximately 20 seconds of ball save, operator-adjustable. Procedurally generated levels have battles, treasure, traps and stairs. Progress survives drains within the game; Competition mode limits starts to once per ball. [S3]
- Dragon Multiball: spell DRAGON and start at the right orbit or side ramp. Shoot the targets to lower Rath, then hit his head. The multiball has unlimited ball save, but a finite drain allowance: failing to defeat Rath ends it. Victory immediately launches Town Celebration. [S3]
- Gelatinous Cube: required ramp hits vary from one to four (or by adjustment). Additional locks increase the multiball's starting value. After playing it, completing a map mode requalifies it. [S3]
- Tavern Brawl: start both main multiballs and Dungeon Crawl; collect at center spinner when otherwise available. The spinner qualifies left-ramp looping jackpots with 1×, 2×, 3× awards, while DRAGON completion enables an add-a-ball and Super Jackpot route. [S3]
- 2× scoring: complete the Secret Door/Hidden Temple sequence, then hit Hidden Temple for 20 seconds of doubled scoring; subsequent hits extend it by five seconds. [S3]
- Persistence: Standard Insider Connected play saves character level, inventory and campaign progress between games. Competition mode disables saved progression, legendary equipment and persistent trinket advantages, using standardized randomness. Each player maintains their own progress in normal multiplayer; weekly Dungeon Crawl layouts are shared. [S3][S5]

## Skill shots

The rulesheet verifies four opportunities. Normal skill shots score 2M × ball number (extra balls increment the multiplier); Super and MXV start at 6M. [S3]

| Name             | Execution                                                                        | Value / reason                   |
| ---------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------- |
| Upper Loop       | Short plunge, then upper-loop shot                                               | Standard skill-shot points       |
| Cube Switch      | Short plunge into the switch near Gelatinous Cube, directly or via shortcut loop | Alternative standard skill shot  |
| Super Skill Shot | Left eject → side ramp → right ramp                                              | Higher-value, difficult sequence |
| MXV Skill Shot   | Hidden Temple lane behind upper flipper                                          | Higher-value alternate           |

Values increase on later balls; the documented awards are not limited to ball one. Extra-ball adjustment can indirectly affect the multiplier. The source does not establish a separate skill-shot selection-button requirement. [S3]

## Secondary features

- Shield: pressing the flashing action button raises a physical bar between the flippers, saving center drains. Return-lane completions replenish uses, requiring progressively more completions (up to five). The shield can also deliberately stage balls for otherwise difficult shots. [S3][S4]
- Ranged attacks: hold the action button during battle, wait for the blue targeting arrow to reach the intended shot, then release to deal damage. Reload by shooting a lane/ramp. [S3]
- Critical Hits: hit the captive ball twice within 15 seconds. Spots battle progress, Dragon letters, Cube progression or Hidden Temple qualification depending on state. [S3]
- Fizmo's Shop: visit the left eject between modes to spend earned gold on useful items, including Dragon letters, Dungeon Crawl access, 2× scoring and sometimes Tavern Brawl. Stock changes weekly; Competition mode disables trinkets. [S3][S4]
- Extra Balls: lit at the right ramp from two map modes started, Dungeon Crawl awards, three Dragon Multiballs, 50 gems or Fizmo purchases. Tournament availability/conversion depends on installation; no specific point conversion was verified. [S3]
- Mimic Hurry-Up: a treasure chest can reveal a Mimic and trigger a hurry-up starting at 50M, a meaningful independent score swing. This is separate from topper-based Mimic Mania. [S3]
- Darkhold Goblin Portal: completing a Town Dungeon unlocks an infinite-goblin battle with continuous ball save until party defeat or tilt. Expert guidance reports 100M+ potential, although unlocking it requires substantial progression. [S3][S4]
- Tiny's Dice Game: three location modes started (or Fizmo purchase) qualify this spinner-based mini-wizard; raising the die to 20 makes spins worth 500k each. Especially productive on Pro. [S3][S4]

## What to watch

1. Character/town selection: Wizard signals defensive shield play; Paladin signals easier Rath battles. Arabel suggests the quick potion-quest route. [S4]
2. Orange Dungeon spinner: flashing orange signifies Dungeon Crawl qualification; orange movement shots and purple intersection choices follow. [S3]
3. Rath lowering / DRAGON letters: three-bank progression qualifies Dragon Multiball; lowered dragon head is the damage opportunity. The display's remaining-drains counter is crucial. [S3]
4. Teal Cube jackpots / Hidden Temple 2×: escalating teal jackpot arrows signal Cube cash-outs; doubled scoring can dramatically magnify them. [S3]

## Match strategy

Playing ahead: Prefer attainable Cube jackpots, safer Dungeon Crawl scoring, and use the shield defensively rather than spending it to stage ambitious shots. Critical Hits and ranged attacks can reduce unnecessary exposure during battles. Strategic inference, supported by documented rules and expert shot guidance. [S3][S4]

Playing behind: Pursue Tavern Brawl, build repeated Cube jackpots, time Hidden Temple 2× with a multiball, or exploit Tiny's Dice spinner. Advanced campaign players may instead pursue Darkhold's extended ball-save scoring. [S3][S4]

Key recurring decision: Take the immediate scoring feature or invest in prerequisites for Tavern Brawl? Strong players may also disagree about prioritizing Dragon Multiball versus Cube: Dragon is easier to qualify but Cube has stronger ordinary jackpot potential. [S4]

## Danger zones

- Rath three-bank: misses and direct target rebounds can send a fast ball toward the lower playfield. Strategic inference.
- Left orbit/Cube ramp: the orbit is narrow; the ramp generally requires a controlled right-flipper shot. [S4]
- Right ramp: difficult entrance; failed shots can produce abrupt rebounds. Strategic inference.
- Fizmo eject: returns toward the upper flipper; missing the catch can surrender control. [S4]
- Shield delay: button activation is not instantaneous. A last-moment press may fail to rescue a center drain. [S4]

## Trivia

- This is Brian Eddy's fourth Stern design since returning to the company, following his work on games including Venom. [S3]
- It commemorates the 50th anniversary of Dungeons & Dragons, originally released in 1974. [S1]
- Michael Dorn voices Rath; the cast also includes Matthew Mercer, Laura Bailey, Marisha Ray and Luke Gygax. [S1]
- Stern's PinSave system lets players resume a D&D campaign over multiple sessions — unusually extensive progression for a pinball machine. [S1]
- Bally previously produced a Dungeons & Dragons pinball machine in 1987, almost four decades earlier. [S4]
