## Identity and versions

**Toy Story 4 — Collector’s Edition**, Jersey Jack Pinball, 2022. Designed by **Pat Lawlor**; lead software/rules by **Joe Katz**; artwork by **John Youssi**. [S6] The game uses three flippers, a physical Road Trip lock, Duke Caboom jump ramp, spinning Take A Chance wheel, and a 10-inch playfield LCD.

* Only **Limited Edition (5,000)** and **Collector’s Edition (1,000)** models were produced. The CE adds presentation features including sparkle playfield accents, extensive cabinet lighting, mirrored backglass, Bo Peep speech, and the mechanical/fiber-optic Duke Caboom topper; no consequential CE-only gameplay rule difference was verified. [S6]
* **Rules basis: `code` — v2.04, released 2025-03-31.** JJP currently distributes 2.04 as the latest CE full install. [S1][S2]
* Major multiballs useful to commentary: **Road Trip, Buzz Quick, Carnival, Gabby Gabby, Tiki Party**, plus mini-wizard **Take A Chance Multiball**. [S3]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Make the soft-plunge Woody skill shot whenever possible.** It adds **+5x bonus**, and repeated successes can make end-of-ball bonus match-changing. [S3][S5]
2. **Get to Road Trip Multiball quickly:** drop target → three locks. If practical, start a Carnival mode first so multiball chaos advances the mode for free. **Observed strategy.** [S5]
3. In Road Trip, **add balls before collecting the first Mega Jackpot**, exploit **Shot X** for a 2x–4x jackpot multiplier, and work toward the Super → Mega Jackpot sequence. [S3][S5]
4. The nuclear setup is **Super X + Road Trip multipliers + Shot X**. Super X provides 2x playfield and can be raised to **4x**; current 2.04 tournament guidance demonstrates single-shot scores above 10M from layering these multipliers. **Observed strategy.** [S3][S5]
5. Alternate route: deliberately grow **Bonus X**, then obtain **Hold Bonus X**. Long-game examples show bonus becoming a huge percentage of total score. **Observed strategy.** [S4][S7]

## Core rules and persistence

The overall objective is to **start seven Scenes** to qualify **Meet Me At The Carousel** at the scoop. Finishing a Scene is not required for qualification, but each completed Scene adds **+1x to Carousel scoring, up to 7x**. [S3]

**Road Trip Multiball:** hit the left drop target and lock three balls. Lit jackpots have 1x–3x base values according to shot difficulty. Enough jackpots light the drop target for Super Jackpot; collecting it lights the lock for a hurry-up **Mega Jackpot beginning at 1M plus prior jackpot value**. [S3]

During Road Trip, the left ramp lights **Shot X**; the captive ball locks in a displayed 2x/3x/4x multiplier for the next Jackpot, Super or Mega Jackpot within 15 seconds. Spinner work can improve the available X. Add-a-balls remain available at the lock until the first Mega Jackpot. [S3]

**Current 2.04 strategy documentation** additionally describes extra Road Trip balls increasing a temporary playfield multiplier, which falls as balls drain; keeping more than three balls alive is therefore particularly valuable before the first Mega. [S5]

**Super X:** starts at 2x playfield for 30 seconds. Left inlanes plus Duke Caboom jumps add 10 seconds and increase the multiplier up to **4x**. It can be started before another mode and carried into that feature. [S3]

**Bonus X is major scoring leverage.** Skill shots add +5x; inlane completions add +1x; Super Loops also add +1x per loop. Hold Bonus X from the wheel carries the multiplier to the next ball; collecting Hold Bonus X on the last ball doubles that ball’s total bonus instead. [S3]

## Skill shots

* **Skill Shot:** soft-plunge directly into the **Woody saucer**. The player gets up to three attempts before the game auto-launches. Award: **50 tickets, 50K increasing by 10K per subsequent make, and +5x Bonus X**. Three successful skill shots light extra ball. [S3]
* **Super Skill Shot:** after a successful Woody plunge, repeatedly shoot the **upper loop** with the upper flipper. Each loop scores 10K plus **+1x Bonus X**. The saucer feeds the upper-flipper opportunity directly. [S3][S5]
* **Tiki Party top-lane skill opportunity:** while entering Tiki Party, completing all four top lanes—three can be spotted by an adjustment-dependent initial lane award—gives **2x scoring for the ensuing Tiki Party Multiball**. This is feature-specific rather than the normal plunge skill shot. [S3]

## Secondary features

* **Kickback:** completing FORKY and starting Rescue Forky enables the left-outlane save. The wheel’s **Unlimited Kickback** normally keeps it available for the rest of the ball; the original Competition preset instead limited this to 30 seconds. [S3][S4]
* **Tiki Party Pinball:** a display-controlled virtual pinball challenge started at the left ramp. Make 30 virtual switches to reach Tiki Party Multiball. Once its initial ball-save period expires, a drain causes Sudden Death. [S3]
* **Take A Chance Wheel:** captive-ball hits qualify ALIEN and nudge the wheel; the upper loop spins it. Awards include Super X, Super Loops, Tiki Party, Unlimited Kickback, Hold Bonus X and Extra Ball. The highlighted award is only *qualified*—the player must then make its designated collection shot. [S3]
* **Mystery:** normal play has numerous state-sensitive/random awards, including ball save, bonus holds and Super-X help. Joe Katz documented a predetermined alternative award table for Competition settings. [S3][S4]
* **Extra balls:** lit by three skill shots, Caboom-jump progress or the wheel. No universal 2.04 tournament point-conversion value was verified; event policy should not be assumed. [S3]

## What to watch

1. **Center Scene inserts:** blinking means played/active; solid means completed. Once all seven are active, Carousel becomes available. [S5]
2. **Small playfield LCD during Road Trip:** shows the moving Shot X. A captive-ball hit freezes that multiplier for the next major jackpot. [S5]
3. **Super X insert/timer:** 2x–4x playfield is active; if Road Trip is also running, a major score swing is imminent. [S3]
4. **Huge Bonus X on the display:** a player repeatedly soft-plunging Woody or looping upstairs may be building the end-of-ball bonus rather than merely taking small immediate points. [S3][S7]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:**
**Strategic inference:** favor a quick Road Trip start, take available jackpot multipliers rather than over-building Super X, and protect a large accumulated Bonus X. Avoid repeated Road Trip-lock attempts if the shot is rejecting badly.

**Playing behind:**
**Observed strategy:** build two Road Trip locks, qualify **Super X**, raise it toward 4x with Caboom, then start Road Trip. Add balls before the Mega Jackpot and combine playfield X with Shot X for the highest-variance cashout. [S5]

**Key recurring decision:**
**Cash Road Trip now or keep building multipliers/add-a-balls.** The first Mega Jackpot is lucrative, but collecting it closes the unlimited add-a-ball phase; waiting increases ceiling while exposing more balls and more difficult shots. [S3]

## Danger zones

* **Road Trip lock misses:** narrow far-left shot; experienced players specifically describe misses as punishing and outlane-prone. [S7]
* **Pop-bumper exits:** owner observations explicitly report classic Lawlor “pops-to-drain” behavior. [S7]
* **Left outlane:** the game’s own Unlimited Kickback is exceptionally valuable because this side is a frequent drain route; Joe Katz also described tournament setup as deliberately making it dangerous. [S4]
* **Scoop miss/reject:** another narrow central-right attempt that can turn progression into immediate danger. [S7]
* **Upper-loop failure:** ends a controlled Woody-saucer feed and costs the Super Skill/Bo Peep opportunity.

## Trivia

* **Toy Story 4 was Pat Lawlor’s final pinball design before retirement.** [S6]
* The game deliberately uses ideas reminiscent of earlier Lawlor designs: a spinning award wheel and a mechanically raised stunt/jump ramp. [S6]
* The CE was limited to **1,000 units** and reportedly sold out within minutes of launch. [S6]
* CE-exclusive presentation includes a **fiber-optic fireworks topper with flying Duke Caboom mechanism**. [S6]
* The physical game contains a playable virtual pinball machine—**Tiki Party Pinball**—on its secondary LCD. [S3][S6]
