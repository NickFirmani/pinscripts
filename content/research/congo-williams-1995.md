## Identity and versions

* **Congo** — Williams Electronic Games, November 1995; model **50050**, **WPC-95**. Design by **John Trudeau**, software by **Bill Grupp and Dean Grover**, art by **Kevin O’Connor**, music/sound by **Vince Pontarelli**. Production: **2,129 units**. [S1][S5] ([IPDB][1])
* Williams sold Congo both as a complete WPC-95 game and as a conversion kit for earlier narrow-body WPC Security cabinets. Revision 2.0 added automatic WPC/WPC-95 hardware detection. [S4][S5] ([Planetary Pinball][2])
* Latest verified production software: **2.1, October 7, 1996**. It fixed the ball-save adjustment and a Skill Fire display issue, among other service changes. [S4] ([Planetary Pinball][2])
* Major commentary-relevant multiballs: **Volcano Multiball** (standard 3-ball), **Diamond Hunt Multiball**, **Ghost Tribe Multiball**, and **Super Multiball** at 100 diamonds. [S1][S2] ([Scribd][3])
* Scope follows the supplied one-page research specification. 

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** hammer the **G-R-A-Y lane**. Each letter is 5M; completing GRAY starts the lower gorilla playfield. Modern player tips explicitly summarize Congo as “shoot the GRAY loop.” [S2][S3] ([GameFAQs][4])
2. Beat the gorilla: the **first GRAY Attack awards 20 diamonds**; the **second awards Multiball directly**. Meanwhile diamonds qualify Volcano locks, normally at **4, 8, 12...** diamonds. Collect a lit lock promptly so you can work toward the next. [S2][S3] ([GameFAQs][4])
3. In **Volcano Multiball**, shoot either ramp for jackpots. Hit the **AMY saucer** to light Super Jackpot for ~20 seconds, then return through the upper-upper orbit for **2× Jackpot**. [S2] ([GameFAQs][4])
4. Keep the **left kickback** lit. Congo rewards survival and steady progress more than a single dominant shot. [S1][S2] 
5. Viable alternate: work the safer **left ramp → MAP** progression for 25M awards, diamond relights, Jungle/Ghost Tribe features, Diamond Hunt Multiball, and eventually **Skill Fire**, which has genuine match-swinging potential. [S2] ([GameFAQs][4])

## Core rules and persistence

* Diamonds are the main progression currency. Lit shots and mode awards collect them; **100 diamonds starts Super Multiball**. [S1][S2] ([Scribd][3])
* Factory-medium Volcano Multiball settings begin with only **one lock available at a time**; harder/easier settings materially change available locks and whether diamond lamps are lit at ball start. [S1] 
* Volcano Multiball jackpot value depends on diamonds held when multiball starts. The detailed rulesheet documents values rising from the mid-teens of millions into the 20M+ range; exact formula remains unresolved. [S2] ([GameFAQs][4])
* **GRAY progress normally carries between balls** through Easy–Hard; Extra Hard disables GRAY memory. [S1] 
* End-of-ball bonus is **500K per diamond × Bonus X**. Factory Medium does **not** hold Bonus X between balls; easier settings can. [S1][S2] ([GameFAQs][4])
* **Skill Fire:** a MAP award can light it at the MAP saucer. The normal ball is deliberately drained and the player gets 20 seconds of repeated skill-shot attempts: regular shots begin at 10M, supers at 20M, with escalating values up to documented 50M/100M caps. This is one of Congo's few true blow-up scoring opportunities. [S2] ([GameFAQs][4])
* **Super Multiball:** Williams advertises it as “2-level, 5-ball”; the detailed rulesheet describes four main-playfield balls while the separate lower gorilla playfield is simultaneously active. Ramps/lower saucers score 10M jackpots and defeating the gorilla scores a 50M Super Jackpot. [S1][S2] ([Scribd][3])

## Skill shots

**Normal Skill Shot** — plunge toward the three left-side kickback targets and hit the currently lit target. Value starts at **10M**, increasing by 5M for subsequent successes; the cycling lamp speeds up as successes accumulate. [S2] ([GameFAQs][4])

**Super Skill Shot** — deliberately plunge into the **left outlane** for **25M**. The kickback fires for the Super Skill Shot even if it was not previously lit. [S2][S3] ([GameFAQs][5])

Unusual exploit/feature: if the plunge reaches **no switch at all**, the normal ball-save logic has not yet activated; the player can let the ball drain and receive another plunge. [S2] ([GameFAQs][4])

## Secondary features

* **Ball save:** factory setting is **one saved drain per game**, with a **4-second** timer; adjustable Off/1–5 saves and 2–15 seconds. Revision 2.1 fixed the ability to disable it. [S1][S4] ([Arcade Archive][6])
* **Kickback:** complete the left three-bank to relight the left-outlane kickback. Its starting state and memory vary strongly with difficulty; keeping it lit is strategically significant. [S1] 
* **Mystery:** spell Z-I-N-J, then collect at the Mystery saucer. Awards include 25M, lock, diamonds, Extra Ball and multiballs. Tournament Mode puts random awards into a fixed sequence; it does **not** inherently disable extra balls. [S2] ([GameFAQs][4])
* **Mine Shaft:** upper-upper orbit starts a choice-based display mode. Wrong routes fail; successful stages give points/diamonds, and progress persists to the next Mine Shaft attempt. [S2] ([GameFAQs][4])
* **Video Mode:** complete the hieroglyphics and collect Mystery; steer the boat with flipper buttons around rocks and hippos. Strategically secondary. [S2] ([GameFAQs][4])
* **Extra balls:** numerous and highly adjustment-sensitive; Volcano-ramp EB qualification is percentage/reflex controlled. Tournament treatment should be checked. [S1][S2] 

## What to watch

1. **G-R-A-Y letters:** near completion means the player is one loop from the lower gorilla feature—and potentially 20 diamonds or direct multiball. [S2]
2. **LOCK lamps:** once a Volcano lock is lit, expect an immediate lock attempt rather than more diamond farming. [S1][S3]
3. **MAP completed / diverter active:** the left ramp starts feeding the left flipper for a shot at the MAP saucer instead of its normal return. [S2]
4. **AMY saucer during multiball:** collecting it lights the timed Super Jackpot; watch for the player to aim immediately at the upper-upper orbit. [S2]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** stay on repeatable GRAY/left-ramp flow, collect lit locks promptly, keep the kickback active, and take ordinary Volcano Multiballs rather than chasing 100 diamonds or deep MAP awards. **Strategic inference** based on the documented progression.

**Playing behind:** chase the higher-ceiling states: Skill Fire, repeated Volcano jackpots/Super Jackpots, or rapid diamond acquisition toward Super Multiball. A completed first GRAY Attack's 20 diamonds can accelerate all of these. [S2]

**Key recurring decision:** **GRAY versus MAP.** GRAY is the short, direct tournament route toward 20 diamonds and a second-completion multiball; MAP is a longer but safer-flow progression that can yield major awards such as Diamond Hunt, Ghost Tribe and Skill Fire. Good players may choose differently based on the specific feeds and match deficit. [S2][S3]

## Danger zones

1. **Pop-bumper exits:** AMY, Mine Shaft and right-orbit play repeatedly send the ball into the pops; owner reports specifically flag pop exits as a common outlane threat. [S2][S6] ([Pinside][7])
2. **Right/Volcano route:** attractive for jackpots and HIPPO, but a miss often means more uncontrolled bumper action. [S2][S6]
3. **Saucer kickouts:** the contemporary rulesheet explicitly notes Congo is fair *unless the saucer kicks are misaligned*—very copy-dependent. [S2] ([GameFAQs][4])
4. **Right outlane:** unlike the left, it has no kickback; the rulesheet describes it as effectively unrecoverable once entered. [S2]
5. **Kickback unlit:** changes left-outlane shots from intentional Super Skill Shot/survival territory into a normal drain risk. [S1][S2]

## Trivia

* Congo was based on Paramount's **1995 film**, but the pinball machine did not reach production until November. [S5]
* The game uses a mechanically operated **GRAY gorilla mini-playfield**: the flipper buttons move the gorilla's arms to bat a separate ball at C-O-N-G-O targets. [S1][S2]
* It was one of Williams' earliest **WPC-95** releases. [S5]
* Williams also produced Congo as a **conversion kit** for compatible WPC Security cabinets; software 2.0 was specifically written to support both hardware generations. [S4][S5]
* The contemporary rulesheet records Congo as the **last pinball title produced at Williams' California Avenue plant** before production moved. [S2] ([GameFAQs][4])

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* The exact **Volcano jackpot formula** is not resolved in the detailed rulesheet; it is clearly diamond-dependent, but do not announce a precise formula. [S2]
* The manual calls Super Multiball a **“2 Level, 5 Ball”** feature while the rulesheet describes **four released main-playfield balls** plus the active lower gorilla playfield. These appear reconcilable rather than contradictory, but commentary should simply call it Super Multiball. [S1][S2]
* A player-contributed tournament tip reports that the first Tournament-Mode Mystery is **Diamond Hunt Multiball if collected before normal multiball**. [S3] This is useful but not sufficiently authoritative to put in the core game plan without venue verification.
* Skill Fire has historically been **ROM-sensitive**; the famous billion-point anecdotes in [S2] should not be presented as a guaranteed 2.1 scoring ceiling.
