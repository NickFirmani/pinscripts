## Identity and versions

* **Winchester Mystery House**, Barrels of Fun, released/revealed October 13, **2025**; FAST Pinball platform, LCD, four flippers, two magnets, rotating eight-position hallway/turntable, collapsing tower, physical three-ball Séance lock, and Pepper’s Ghost “Ghost Box.” [S1][S6]
* Design: **Karl DeAngelo**; software Eric Priepke; art Brad Albright; sound Jeff Dodson; animation Joshua Clay/Julian Brown; mechanical engineering Paul Sulisz/Luke Underwood. [S3]
* Single limited production run of **525 machines**; no Pro/Premium-style rules split found. [S1]
* Two primary multiballs: **Séance Multiball** and **Wheelbarrow Ghost Multiball**. [S1][S3]
* Latest code verified: **2026.08.20**. It changed travel-value scaling and fixed/adjusted several Séance Multiball/turntable interactions. [S2]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Observed strategy:** tour **Kitchen** early; its untimed two-ball room mode can feed directly toward Séance Multiball. [S3][S4]
2. Qualify Séance locks via the inline drops—or more safely light the captive ball green, hit it to lower the drops, then lock. **Start another room before starting Séance** so the room stacks into multiball. [S3][S4]
3. Build **O-P-E-N → Wheelbarrow Ghost Multiball**, preferably with another room running. [S3][S4]
4. The biggest general leverage is **2×/3× Playfield X**: scoring is stolen into a multiplied bank, then must be reclaimed at a Spider Web/right-orbit shot. **Drain before collecting and the whole bank disappears.** [S3]
5. Alternate long-ball route: build several **Thirteens** together for simultaneous 2×/3×+ scoring and eventually Thirteenths; this is higher setup than the multiball route. [S3]

## Core rules and persistence

* Tour flow is **select room → travel → start room**. White shots during travel build that room’s value; combos build it faster. A key can unlock a room or skip the room at the door and continue traveling while retaining accumulated value. [S3]
* A travel drain carries the player to the room next ball but cuts the earned room value in half. Active tour paths now persist through drains, preventing doubling back. [S2][S3]
* **Séance MB:** modes must generally be started **before** the multiball; Hungering Flame/Kitchen does not stack with it. Default ball-3 pity behavior lowers the inline drops and allows one Séance shot to start MB if it has not yet been played. [S3]
* **Wheelbarrow:** complete OPEN, hit a cyan ramp, then the cyan hallway/Stairs shot. Default first successful Wheelbarrow lock starts MB; subsequent MBs require three. [S3]
* **Playfield X:** fill Spirit Energy with a lit spinner; X target starts 2×, another X boosts to 3×. The bank must later be collected; current rules document a **20-second** collect window. [S3]
* **Stair Madness** is another bank/cash-out opportunity: third right ramp starts it; cashing via Left Ramp → Right Ramp doubles it, Center/Side → Left → Right gives **4×**. [S3]

## Skill shots

**Rotating drop-bank skill shot.** Plunge so the first switch is the currently flashing center drop. Verified opening awards are: complete **OPEN**, max **Spirit Energy**, **+3 to every Thirteen category**, or—when all three are lit—a points award. [S3]

**Observed strategy:** Advance 13 (+3 to all five categories) is commonly recommended; OPEN accelerates Wheelbarrow, while max Energy immediately sets up Playfield X. [S4]

No separate super/alternate/secret skill shot was verified. Later-plunge award changes are not adequately documented.

## Secondary features

* **Run:** hold Action during travel to sprint or escape Angry Spirits. One use is ready initially; requalification difficulty is operator-adjustable. Running from a Spirit encounter sacrifices scoring/opportunity. [S2][S3]
* **Help target:** once per room, sends a spirit to spot progress; during either primary multiball it provides **add-a-ball**. [S3]
* **Return kickback:** left-outlane rescue; Mystery can light it. Current code also supports disabling the physical kickback and using a virtual save instead. [S2][S3]
* **Mystery:** tiered, partly hidden awards can supply keys, OPEN, room value, Spirit Energy, timer extensions, kickback and other progression. Competition-mode normalization was not verified. [S3]
* **Extra ball:** the Kitchen memento is verified to light one; Mystery may also do so, but its documented qualification is uncertain. Tournament conversion behavior is venue-dependent. [S3]
* **Major swing:** a qualified Wildcard Memento can randomly be the **Signature Quill for 99M**. [S3]
* No standalone video mode found.

## What to watch

* **Whole playfield turns blue + score freezes:** Playfield X is running; every point is now at risk until the bank is collected. [S3]
* **Apron Spirit Board countdown:** shows short timers, including multiplier/room timers, without requiring the commentator to read the LCD. [S3]
* **Captive ball green / red:** green can lower Séance inline drops; red during Séance MB advances toward the Super Jackpot shot. [S3]
* **Red dots on the mansion map:** failed rooms have released Angry Spirits; three force a Reckoning. [S3]

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** **Strategic inference:** favor starting reachable rooms rather than repeatedly spending keys to inflate value; use Run to avoid hazardous travel encounters; take straightforward Stair Madness cash-outs; do not activate Playfield X unless confident of surviving through the collection shot.

**Playing behind:** stack a room + Séance or Wheelbarrow, activate 2×/3× around valuable scoring, extend travel value with keys, and pursue multiple simultaneous Thirteens. These create much greater variance. [S3][S4]

**Key recurring decision:** **build versus cash out.** Keys can extend room-value building instead of starting the room; Stair Madness rewards extra combo setup; Playfield X literally puts accumulated scoring at risk for a multiplier.

## Danger zones

* **Left outlane:** intentionally protected by Return, but still a major setup-sensitive danger.
* **Upper-left hallway return:** recent owner reports say the nominal left-inlane feed can hit a post and cross toward the right outlane on some setups. **Observed/setup-specific.** [S7]
* **Séance inline drops:** direct lock qualification means firing repeatedly into a close drop bank; misses/rebounds are control-risky. **Strategic inference.**
* **Rotating hallway:** ball paths genuinely change with turntable position; expect nonstandard feeds rather than assuming a fixed orbit/ramp return. [S1]
* **Playfield-X collect phase:** any drain before the Spider Web collect destroys the entire multiplied bank. [S3]

## Trivia

* Designer **Karl DeAngelo**, best known competitively through IE Pinball, made his commercial game-design debut with Winchester Mystery House. [S6]
* The run is **525 machines**, matching the real mansion’s address, **525 S. Winchester Blvd.** [S3]
* The Ghost Box uses a **Pepper’s Ghost** illusion so digital spirits appear within the physical playfield. [S1]
* The revolving hallway has **three entrances and eight positions**, allowing the same physical structure to route balls differently. [S1]
* The collapsing tower references the tower lost during the **1906 San Francisco earthquake**. [S1]
