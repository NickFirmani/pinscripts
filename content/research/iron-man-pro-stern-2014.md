## Identity and versions

* **Iron Man (Pro Vault Edition)** — Stern Pinball, 2014; **S.A.M. system**, DMD, two flippers. The supplied brief specifically requests the Pro Vault Edition. 
* Design: **John Borg**; software: **Lonnie D. Ropp and Lyman F. Sheats Jr.**; animation: Mark Galvez; sound/music: David Thiel. [S2] ([Pinside][1])
* The Vault Edition is fundamentally the **2010 Iron Man Pro rules/layout re-release**, not a new rules edition. Stern added all-LED playfield lighting, a modern backbox/cabinet package and stronger one-piece Iron Monger, War Machine and Whiplash figures. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][2])
* Stern's 2014 V1.83 notes explicitly state **no rule difference between V1.83 and V1.83_VE**. [S2] ([Pinside][3])
* **Latest verified code: V1.86.0, April 28, 2020.** V1.85 materially changed Whiplash scoring, added an Extra Ball path and introduced a second skill shot. [S2] ([Pinside][1])
* Main multiballs: **War Machine, Iron Monger, Whiplash**, plus the extremely deep **Do or Die Multiball**. [S2][S3]

## Thirty-second game plan

1. **Prepare War Machine plus either Monger or Whiplash.** Light War Machine from the four Drone targets; meanwhile raise Monger with spinner work or bring Whiplash near completion. [S3][S6]
2. **Start War Machine first.** It can stack with Monger or Whiplash; those two cannot stack with each other. This is the key tournament setup rule. **Observed strategy.** [S6] ([Pinside][4])
3. Before multiball, preferably start **2X Scoring and/or We Got a Bogey**. Those timed features can run underneath the multiball; by default they cannot be started afterward. [S2][S6]
4. For the largest short-game swing, start all five character objectives **on the same ball** to light **Do or Die**, a center hurry-up starting at **35M**; active 2X can make that effectively 70M. [S2][S6] ([Pinside][3])
5. Alternate safer line: repeatedly qualify **Bogey** through the two ramps and take flowing ramp scoring rather than exposing the ball to all the standups and toys needed for Do or Die. **Observed strategy.** [S5]

## Core rules and persistence

* **Iron Monger:** spinner shots spell MONGER and raise the toy; repeated direct hits defeat him and start Iron Monger Multiball. During multiball, hit Monger repeatedly to light the center-spinner Super Jackpot. [S3] 
* **Whiplash:** repeated Whiplash standups qualify its multiball. Current V1.85+ scoring gives jackpots starting at **350K**, a **2.5M halfway collect**, and a **5M Super Jackpot** at Whiplash. [S2] ([Pinside][1])
* **War Machine:** complete the Ground/Air/Tactical/Sea Drone targets to light the War Machine kicker and start its multiball. [S3]
* **Stacking:** Whiplash and Monger are mutually exclusive. War Machine can be paired with either **only when War Machine is started first**; if Monger is already raised, War Machine → Monger is the available stack. [S6] ([Pinside][4])
* **Iron Man standups** cycle timed scoring features including Fast Scoring, 2X Scoring and a hurry-up. V1.85 added an adjustment allowing a ready Iron Man feature to start during multiball, but **factory default is OFF**. [S2] ([Pinside][1])
* **We Got a Bogey:** repeated left/right ramps start a timed ramp-scoring round. Later Bogeys can require balanced ramp work rather than simply repeating one ramp, depending on difficulty. [S3][S5] ([Pinside][5])
* **Do or Die Hurry-Up is not Do or Die Multiball.** The former is a one-ball achievement requiring all five character objectives to be started on that ball; the latter is the far deeper final wizard multiball. [S2][S6]
* Jericho progress is another long-game path: collect **Mark I–VI suits** to reach **Jericho Missile Mayhem**. Whether Jericho itself carries between balls is operator-adjustable. [S3][S5]

## Skill shots

* **Normal Skill Shot:** plunge into the flashing top rollover lane. The left-orbit up-post normally helps feed the top lanes; flippers control lane selection. [S5]
* **Alternate Skill Shot — added V1.85:** **hold the left flipper before plunging**. The orbit post stays down and the normal skill shot is disabled; either top lane awards **1M +250K for each additional successful skill shot** in the game. [S2] ([Pinside][1])
* Why choose the alternate: much larger immediate points and better flow if the player prefers the orbit post down, at the cost of losing the normal controlled plunge behavior.
* If the venue has the orbit post disabled globally, the practical plunge strategy changes materially.

## Secondary features

* **S.H.I.E.L.D. Mystery:** complete the two top lanes plus four lower lanes, then collect at War Machine. [S3] 
* **Extra Ball:** V1.85 added an EB for completing the Iron Man standups; factory default qualification is **two completions**. V1.86 also added separate enable/disable adjustments for S.H.I.E.L.D. Extra Ball and Special. [S2] ([Pinside][1])
* **Ball save:** factory standard is **5 seconds**. Competition Mode exists but is factory-off; exact tournament behavior should be confirmed on the venue machine rather than assumed. [S4] ([Stern Pinball][6])
* **No video mode or action button** was found. [S2]
* Pinside catalogs a **kickback**, but I did not verify its qualification logic from primary rules documentation; do not print a specific activation rule without checking the machine. [S2]

## What to watch

* **Iron Monger rises:** Monger Battle is active; if War Machine is already running, the player is attempting the valuable stacked multiball.
* **War Machine insert lit:** the four Drone objectives are done and the utility multiball is ready.
* **Iron Man targets pulsing:** a timed Iron Man scoring feature is qualified; if multiball is imminent, the player may deliberately start it first.
* **All five character inserts lit on the current ball:** Do or Die hurry-up is imminent; the center spinner becomes a potentially enormous match-swinging shot.

## Match strategy

**Playing ahead:** Take the prepared War Machine → Monger/Whiplash stack without insisting on 2X or Do or Die setup. Flowing ramp/Bogey scoring is attractive when the opponent only needs to be covered. **Strategic inference.**

**Playing behind:** Prepare multiple characters, light **2X**, start War Machine, layer Monger or Whiplash, and pursue the same-ball **35M Do or Die hurry-up**. This is the highest-leverage practical comeback route short of the deep wizard modes. **Observed strategy.** [S6]

**Key recurring decision:** **Cash the ready multiball now, or leave it staged while preparing 2X/Bogey/Do-or-Die progress?** Every extra setup shot increases both the multiplier potential and the chance the ball ends before the stack exists.

## Danger zones

* **Iron Monger:** repeated center bashes can rebound directly toward either outlane or the middle.
* **Whiplash magnet:** catches and accelerates/throws the ball; possession becomes unpredictable by design. [S2]
* **War Machine kicker:** ejects forcefully back down the playfield; be ready rather than assuming a cradleable feed. [S2]
* **Ramp rejects:** Iron Man is fast and shallow-looking; weak left/right ramp attempts frequently return immediately to the danger area.
* **Left outlane:** competitive/owner reports consistently regard it as especially hungry; early nudging is often required. **Observed physical behavior.**

## Trivia

* The 2014 Iron Man was **Stern's first announced Vault Edition**, bringing a 2010 title back into production. [S1] ([Stern Pinball][2])
* The Vault's rule set was intentionally retained; Stern's V1.83 notes say the VE/non-VE builds had **no game-rule differences**. [S2]
* The three major toys were remolded as stronger **one-piece figures** for the Vault Edition. [S1]
* The Whiplash mechanism literally catches and **accelerates the ball magnetically**, while War Machine fires it back at the player. [S2]
* Iron Man received a substantial rules update in **2020, almost ten years after the original release**, including a new skill shot and rebalanced Whiplash Multiball. [S2]

### Uncertainties and conflicts

* **Vault vs. original rules:** no meaningful rules difference existed at the 2014 release [S2], but later **V1.85/1.86** changes apply to both compatible versions and materially alter current play.
* **Iron Man modes during multiball:** pre-2020 behavior forbids starting them once multiball begins; V1.85 makes this adjustable, **default OFF**. [S2]
* **Jericho carryover:** explicitly operator-adjustable in older software documentation; venue setup determines whether a failed attempt persists. [S5]
* **Kickback:** machine databases list one [S2], but a reliable factory rule describing how it is lit was not located during this pass.
* **Physical feeds:** Whiplash magnet strength, War Machine eject angle, Monger rebounds and the orbit post can all substantially change tournament difficulty.
